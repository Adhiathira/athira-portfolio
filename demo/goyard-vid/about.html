Figma for AI video.

Making a video used to mean a crew, a timeline, and a budget that eliminated most of the people with something worth saying. The tools existed, but access didn't.

Solo creators today have more software than they can learn and less time than they need. Switching between a dozen disconnected apps — for scripting, recording, editing, sound, and export — burns the hours that should go into making.

The production quality gap between a professional studio and an individual creator is closing. The collaboration gap is not. That's the problem we're here to fix.

Where this goes.

  1. Now — Shared editing Real-time co-editing for video projects. Two people, one timeline, zero merge conflicts.
  2. Next — AI assist layer Suggested cuts, auto-captioning, and scene analysis that stays out of the way until you need it.
  3. Later — Creator marketplace A place to find collaborators, share templates, and get credit for the work you've shaped.
  4. Vision — Platform of platforms Infrastructure for the next generation of video tools, built on open collaboration standards.
