Our mission

Figma did it for design.
We're doing it for video.

Video production belongs to everyone — not just studios, not just those with expensive equipment, not just those who can afford to hire a team. AI makes the tools accessible. Community makes them powerful.

Video production today is broken.

A solo creator with a story to tell faces weeks of learning, expensive tools, and a blank timeline with no help. The result: most people never make the thing they imagined. Not because they lack creative vision — because the production infrastructure was never designed for them.

When Figma launched, design became multiplayer. When GitHub launched, code became collaborative. Video production is still waiting for its multiplayer moment.

We're building that moment.

Built by people who believe it.
