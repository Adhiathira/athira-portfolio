## Prompt

A crisp, modern enterprise-SaaS dashboard screenshot for CentralHub — the product's main overview screen shown as a single unified interface on a clean white ground (`#ffffff`). The layout reads as one command center that clearly combines five domains at a glance: a left CRM pipeline column with stacked deal cards moving through stages, a marketing-campaign stats panel with a small return-on-spend chart and KPI tiles, a call-activity feed with recent-call rows and durations, and a website-status card with an uptime indicator — all arranged in a calm bento grid of light surface panels (`#f0f0f4`) separated by hairline borders (`#cccce1`). Use navy ink UI text (`#1b1b37`) for headings and figures, slate gray (`#7d8794`) for secondary labels, and a single deep teal accent (`#2b8a80`) — not blue — reserved only for active controls, one small trend line, and the primary action button; everything else stays achromatic. Render all microtext as blurred illegible, softly blurred lines so nothing is readable, keep small avatar dots and simple bar/line chart glyphs, and light the scene as a flat, evenly-lit screen capture with no glare. Straight-on flat UI render, generous white margins, uncluttered and precise — the whole frame should read as one product replacing five separate tool logins. Avoid blue accent buttons entirely — the only accent color is deep teal (`#2b8a80`); no green or mint accents, no blue, no pink or magenta, no dark backgrounds.
