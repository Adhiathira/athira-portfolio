## Apple Design System Aesthetic Reference

The Apple visual identity operates on **stillness as luxury**. Pure black (`#000000`) hero sections treat product as cultural artifact — stage-lit in darkness, floating with generous negative space, trusted to carry emotional weight without motion spectacle. The emotional tone is "flagship retail store at off-hours: hushed, cool, spotlit." Nothing moves unless it means something.

The blue (`#0071e3`) is a confirmation color — it signals action, not atmosphere. It never floods a scene. The palette is black, white, and near-black charcoal (`#1d1d1f`, `#424245`) — no neon, no gradient, no ambient color wash.

## Generation Prompt

Pure black void (`#000000`), absolute darkness with no ambient fill. Three short-form video frames appear sequentially — not simultaneously — each treated as a precision-machined object floating on an invisible pedestal in the void. The first frame: a beauty product against a soft white-lit surface, shot with studio diffused lighting, completely still. The second: a tech product reveal — dark background, single cool-white directional key light from above-left, sharp shadow edge, no fill. The third: overhead food styling, warm white light from directly above, perfect symmetry.

Each frame materializes slowly from black — a 0.3s ease-in fade with a barely perceptible scale from 98% to 100%, as if coming into focus rather than appearing. It holds on screen for 2.5 seconds with complete stillness — no wobble, no float, no particle drift. Then it fades back into black at the same ease. The camera does not move. There is no orbital drift, no push-in, no parallax. The stillness is the statement.

The sole accent color: a single thin white progress bar below the active frame — 1px height, clean white (`#ffffff`), slowly filling left to right across the frame width at the same pace as each hold. When the frame transitions, the bar resets. This is the only motion in the lower third. No waveforms, no UI chrome, no floating panels.

Lighting is cool and directional — soft key light from upper-left, no fill, no rim. Color temperature: 5500K daylight studio. Shadows are soft but defined. The overall frame is dark: the video frames themselves are the only bright elements in the composition, floating against the black with the same quiet confidence as a MacBook Pro on a black stage.

**Negative prompt:** no neon, no colored rim light, no glows, no particle effects, no lens flare, no camera movement, no orbital drift, no fast cuts, no waveforms, no UI panels, no floating elements other than the video frames, no text overlays, no watermarks, no people, no faces, no cyberpunk aesthetic, no busy backgrounds, no ambient color wash, no gradients, no grain or film texture

**Loop:** Seamless loop — the third frame fades to black, a beat of pure silence, then the first frame materializes. 9–10 second total cycle. The transition is a hold-to-black, not a crossfade.
