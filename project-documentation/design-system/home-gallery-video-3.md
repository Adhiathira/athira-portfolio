**Gallery card**: Card 3 — Creator Gallery section, kinetic-vid homepage

**Tool**: Kling AI video generation

## Prompt

A three-cut cross-location montage: Cut 1 (2 seconds) — a slow-motion ocean wave breaking from right to left, shot at water level, the foam luminous and cold blue-grey; Cut 2 (2 seconds) — a busy city street at midday with pedestrians in motion blur, concrete and glass, a cool neutral grade; Cut 3 (2–3 seconds) — a rooftop at dusk looking over a city skyline, warm amber and dusty rose in the sky, silhouetted foreground objects. Each cut begins with a fast whip-style transition from the previous. The three locations are unified by a consistent grade: cool desaturated shadows across all three, with the warmth only permitted in highlights, creating a coherent visual thread despite the location jumps. The final frame fades almost imperceptibly back into the ocean shot for the loop.

## Specifications

- **Duration**: 6–8 seconds, seamlessly loopable
- **Aspect ratio**: 16:9 (1280×720 or 1920×1080)
- **Style**: Editorial / Cross-location montage
- **Motion**: Fast whip transitions between three cuts; slow-motion wave, pedestrian blur, static rooftop wide shot
- **Color grade**: Cool desaturated shadows throughout; warmth restricted to highlights; coherent cross-scene palette
- **Mood**: Expansive, kinetic
