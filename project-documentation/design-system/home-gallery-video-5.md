## Prompt

An abstract motion graphics piece on a pure black background, driven by kinetic typography and geometric shape morphing. A single word — "KINETIC" — is set in a wide, light-weight sans-serif typeface at center frame. On each implied beat (4 pulses across the clip), the letterforms fragment into component geometric strokes — rectangles, diagonal lines, circular arcs — that scatter outward radially then contract back into a slightly different typographic arrangement. In the intervals between pulses, thin electric violet lines trace outlines of geometric shapes — hexagons, triangles — that expand slowly and fade at their edges. The overall palette is deep navy (#0a0f2e) and electric violet (#7c3aed) against black, with brief white flashes on each beat impact. The loop closes when the shapes return to the original "KINETIC" composition.
