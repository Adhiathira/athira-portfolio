**Tool:** Nano Banana (image generation)
**Usage:** Generate portrait photographs for the three GoyardVid co-founder bios in `about.html` Section 3.
**Target:** Replace `.about-avatar` placeholder divs once images are generated.
**Spec:** Square crop, portrait orientation. Save as `about-avatar-mia-chen.jpg`, `about-avatar-reuben-okafor.jpg`, `about-avatar-soraya-naimi.jpg` in `demo/goyard-vid/img/team/`.
