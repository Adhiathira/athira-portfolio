# Brand Name — Layout Brief

---

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page follows a long-form editorial scroll, organized into roughly **8–10 distinct sections** that alternate between light (white/off-white) and dark (deep navy/indigo) backgrounds. This rhythm creates a breathing cadence — the eye is never visually fatigued. The overall width is constrained at approximately 1280px with generous horizontal padding, but full-bleed backgrounds break out to the viewport edge constantly.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

A **fixed, full-width top navigation bar** with a clean white background. The layout is a classic three-zone structure:

- **Left zone:** Brand wordmark logo (compact, dark)
- **Center zone:** Five primary nav items (each with a dropdown chevron — evenly spaced, medium-weight type)
- **Right zone:** Two CTA buttons — Secondary CTA (ghost/outlined, light border) and Primary CTA (solid dark, pill-shaped with a trailing arrow icon)

The bar is slim and unobtrusive. There's no mega-menu visible at rest — just text links. The overall tone is professional restraint.

---

## 3. Hero Section

A **full-bleed, asymmetric composition** that is one of the most distinctive layout decisions on the page.

- The **left half** is a clean white column containing the headline, a live counter stat above it, and two CTA buttons stacked horizontally below the headline
- The **right half** bleeds edge-to-edge with an abstract, painterly gradient that occupies the full right portion of the viewport and overflows the top edge

The gradient is not contained in a box — it **flows freely** and overlaps into the nav area slightly, creating a sense of depth and motion without any imagery. The headline is large (display scale), left-aligned, and the secondary copy sits beneath in a muted state.

Below this, a **social proof strip** — a single row of well-known partner/client logos — sits centered at full width on white, separated by a subtle top border. This acts as a visual palate cleanser and trust signal.

---

## 4. Content Sections Below the Hero

### Section: Product Feature Cards
A **multi-card horizontal layout** presenting two product tiles side by side. Each card is a large, rounded-corner container with:
- A headline + description in the upper-left
- A full-bleed illustrated/screenshot mockup filling the rest of the card
- A small expand/fullscreen icon in the upper-right corner

The cards feel like interactive demo windows. The gradient bleeds into the card backgrounds, reinforcing the brand warmth.

### Section: Three-Column Feature Grid
Below the cards, a **three-equal-column grid** presents individual product capabilities. Each column contains:
- A headline at top
- A richly illustrated or UI screenshot visual filling the center
- No explicit card borders — just white space separating the columns

### Section: Stats / Dark Hero Interlude
A **full-bleed dark background section** with a large centered headline — purely typographic, maximum weight. Below it, a **four-column stat grid** displays key metrics ([Stat], [Stat], [Stat], [Stat]). Each stat follows the same pattern: oversized number, small descriptor text beneath. The background transitions from white to a deep dark gradient with an ambient animation beneath — creating a dramatic full-bleed visual moment.

### Section: Editorial Two-Column
Back to white. A **classic left-right two-column layout** — headline + CTA button on the left, supporting body copy on the right. This is a lean, text-forward row with no imagery. Below it, customer case studies are listed as **expandable accordion rows** — icon, company name, headline claim, and a "+" button on the right edge.

A full-bleed **photography image** acts as a section break — spanning the full width with no margins, it carries metadata in a three-column bar beneath it.

### Section: Case Study Cards (Carousel)
A **horizontally scrollable card row** featuring customer stories. Each card is a tall square with a dark background image, company logo overlaid, and a short story headline + "Read story" link beneath the card. Navigation arrows (← →) float to the right of the section header, indicating carousel behavior. The four visible cards have equal widths with tight gaps.

### Section: Two-Up Promo Tiles
Two **equal-width cards side by side** — "Service A" and "Service B." Each card has:
- Text (bold label + description + link) on the left
- A decorative geometric shape/gradient illustration on the right, partially cropped

These feel like feature callout banners rather than full cards.

### Section: Platform Dashboard Showcase
A **full-bleed warm gradient background** containing a large **floating browser mockup** centered on screen — a simulated dashboard UI. Below the browser frame, a tooltip/code snippet annotation overlays the lower-left corner, demonstrating a developer or technical context. This is a layered composition: background gradient → browser frame → UI screenshot → annotation overlay.

## 5. Footer

Not fully visible in the screenshots, but the dark sections suggest a **multi-column footer** is forthcoming — typical for this pattern: dense link grid organized by product category, legal links, and country/region selector at the bottom.

---

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

**Full-bleed backgrounds everywhere.** The brand treats the background as a primary design element — gradients, dark panels, and photography all extend to the viewport edge while content stays within the max-width column.

**Floating / overlapping mockups.** Browser and mobile device frames don't sit neatly within containers — they float, partially crop, and overflow their parent sections, creating cinematic depth.

**Sticky navigation.** The header is fixed and always present, maintaining access to primary navigation throughout the scroll.

**Alternating light/dark rhythm.** Sections alternate between white and dark backgrounds, creating visual breathing room and preventing monotony on a long scroll page.

**Generous vertical spacing.** Section padding is very large — the page never feels cramped. Headlines get room to breathe, and transitions between sections feel intentional rather than abrupt.

**Typographic scale as hierarchy.** Display-scale headlines dominate section openers, with supporting body copy significantly smaller — scale contrast rather than decoration establishes hierarchy.

**Accent color as motion.** A signature gradient recurs across sections as backgrounds, card fills, and button fills — it's the primary visual thread tying the page together.
