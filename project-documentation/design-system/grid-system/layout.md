# Brand Name Homepage — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page follows a single-column scroll narrative with approximately 10–12 distinct content sections stacked vertically. There's a clear alternating rhythm between full-bleed dark sections and light/white sections, creating strong visual contrast as the user scrolls. The page reads as a curated editorial sequence — each product gets its moment before yielding to the next. Spacing between sections is tight at boundaries, with visual cuts rather than padding-based transitions.

---

## Header / Navigation

The global nav is a fixed, full-width bar that persists across the entire scroll. It's ultra-minimal: a single horizontal strip with very low height. The brand logo anchors the far left, a bag icon sits far right alongside a search icon, and product category links are evenly distributed across the center with generous letter-spacing. The background is semi-transparent dark (near-black) with a subtle blur effect — it reads as floating above content rather than attached to the page edge. Text is small and tight; the nav never dominates.

---

## Hero Section

The hero is a full-bleed, center-aligned editorial panel — no split columns, no floating boxes. Product name in large, bold serif-adjacent headline sits at the top center, followed by a short descriptive line, a small availability note, and two CTA buttons side-by-side (one filled, one outlined). Below the text, the product image occupies the majority of the vertical space — photographed on a dark background, centered, with generous negative space around it. The effect is stage-like: the product is lit and placed on a pedestal. Proportionally, the image takes roughly 60% of the section height.

---

## Content Sections Below the Hero

### Full-Bleed Single Product (Hero-style repeats)
Several sections replicate the hero formula — centered text hierarchy (headline → tagline → date info → dual CTAs) above a large centered product image. These alternate between dark and light backgrounds, reinforcing the light/dark rhythm. Product 1 and Product 2 sections follow this pattern exactly.

### Two-Column Split Cards (50/50)
The page transitions into a grid of paired product cards — two equal-width panels sitting side by side, edge to edge with no gutter. Each cell is self-contained with its own background color (dark, light gray, or white), product headline, tagline, CTAs, and a product image below. These panels read as tiles in a grid. The vertical rhythm is consistent: text block at top, image bleeds toward the bottom of the cell. This layout appears 2–3 times in sequence, creating a catalog-browsing feel.

### Full-Width Editorial Banner
A single centered headline in very large weight on a clean light section — no image, just typographic breathing room. This acts as a section divider, resetting visual momentum before the content below.

### Horizontal Scroll Carousel (Media/Services)
Below the divider heading, there's a full-width horizontal carousel of media cards. Each card is roughly square-ish, featuring a rich photographic thumbnail. Cards are equal size, equally spaced, and the rightmost card bleeds slightly off-screen to signal scroll affordance. A pagination dot strip sits below the carousel, centered. A small forward-arrow sits at the far right edge.

### Three-Column Services Grid
Three cards sit in a row — Service A, Service B, Service C — each with a photographic background, a service logo (top right), content name (bottom left), and a CTA button (bottom right). Equal columns, equal height, consistent internal layout.

---

## Footer

The footer is a multi-column link directory on a light gray background. It's organized into 5–6 labeled columns, each with a bold column header and vertically stacked plain text links below. The columns are evenly distributed across the full page width. Below the link grid, a thin divider separates a slim legal bar: copyright left-aligned, policy links centered, country selector right-aligned. The footer is information-dense but restrained — no graphics, no color, no imagery.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Sticky Navigation:** The nav bar remains fixed at the top throughout the entire scroll, always accessible without visual noise.

**Full-Bleed Color Sections:** Section boundaries are defined entirely by background color changes — no padding or whitespace separates sections. The cuts are hard, creating a tile-book effect when scrolling.

**Centered Typographic Hierarchy:** Every text block follows the same vertical stack: large headline → smaller tagline → supporting micro-copy → CTA row. This is religiously consistent across all product sections.

**Product Image as Focal Point:** In hero-style sections, the product image is always centered and floats with negative space. It never bleeds to the edge; it reads as a studio photograph, maintaining premium framing.

**Two-Column Grid as Catalog Rhythm:** The 50/50 split card layout appears multiple times to efficiently compare or present secondary products side by side — it's the workhorse pattern of the mid-page content.

**Typographic Section Dividers:** Single large headlines on clean backgrounds serve as visual chapter breaks, adding spatial rhythm without using decorative elements.

**Carousel with Bleed:** The media carousel uses a partial bleed on the trailing card to signal horizontal scrollability — a common pattern that keeps the grid tight while hinting at more content.
