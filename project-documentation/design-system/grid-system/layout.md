# Brand Homepage — Layout Brief

---

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page flows through **six distinct sections**, each with its own spatial logic:

1. Dual-band header (icon bar + navigation bar)
2. Full-bleed video hero
3. Featured products carousel (light ground)
4. Editorial split — "Explore the World of [Brand Name]" (asymmetric two-column)
5. Split editorial — "Feature A" + layered product imagery
6. "The Seasons of [Brand Name]" — centered editorial with product trio
7. Newsletter sign-up — "[Brand Publication]" (two-column)
8. Locations carousel (dark full-bleed)
9. Footer (dark, four-column)

The page alternates between **light/off-white backgrounds** and **deep dark backgrounds**, creating a cadenced rhythm of light → dark → light → dark that feels deliberate and ceremonial rather than utilitarian.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

The header is a **two-tier horizontal band**, very lean in height — roughly 80–90px total.

- **Top tier** (darker background, ~40px): Sparse utility row. A location pin icon sits flush left. A search (magnifier) icon sits just beside it. On the far right, a user/account icon. Center: the **Brand Name** logotype — spaced-capital wordmark with a secondary descriptor in smaller tracking below. This band is the brand's primary identity anchor.

- **Bottom tier** (slightly lighter background, ~40px): Four centered navigation links in all-caps, wide letter-spacing — Category 1, Category 2, Category 3, Category 4. Equal spacing between items, typographically consistent. No dropdowns visible in static state.

Together the header reads as a **thin, prestigious band** — restrained and symmetrical, never competing with content below.

---

## 3. Hero Section

A **full-viewport-width, full-height video hero**. The image/video bleeds edge-to-edge with no margin or container.

- The visual: a close-up lifestyle shot — intimate subject matter with warm, slightly blurred background giving cinematic depth.
- **Text overlay** sits center-screen, vertically centered: a three-line stacked headline in large white serif/spaced capitals — "Primary CTA / Line 2 / Line 3". Each line is independently centered, creating a stacked pyramid hierarchy.
- Beneath the headline: a small action link in tight spaced caps, underlined in an accent color — a subtle color accent that catches the eye against white on dark.
- **Bottom-left corner**: small playback controls (pause + volume icons) — minimal, semi-transparent, non-intrusive.
- **Bottom-right corner**: a decorative brand badge — circular, rotates text around the edge, acts as a floating decorative anchor.

The hero has **zero padding from the header** — the nav floats directly above the video, creating an immersive full-screen moment.

---

## 4. Content Sections Below the Hero

### Section A — Featured Products Carousel (Light Ground)

Background shifts to **pale light/off-white**. Three products are arranged in a **centered three-column equal grid**, each column showing:
- Product image (floating, no box or card container — images appear isolated on the light ground)
- All-caps product name in small tracking
- Secondary action link in a lighter weight below

The grid has generous white space above and below. Images are approximately equal in size, centered within their columns. The carousel is implied (partial cut-off on edges suggests lateral scroll). The section feels **airy and curatorial** — like objects in a gallery vitrine.

---

### Section B — "Explore the World of [Brand Name]" (Asymmetric Two-Column)

A bold asymmetric editorial layout:

- **Section heading** centered at the top in spaced caps
- Below: a **two-column split at roughly 40/60** — the left column is narrower and sits lower, the right column is taller and starts higher
- Left: a square-format lifestyle image with a social handle caption and an underlined link below it — suggests social integration, intimate scale
- Right: a larger, taller portrait/editorial photograph filling the column

The **vertical offset** between the two images creates a staggered, overlapping visual rhythm. They share the same horizontal zone but sit at different heights, generating tension and energy. Light background continues.

---

### Section C — Editorial Split Panels (Two Alternating Full-Width Blocks)

Two **full-bleed horizontal panels** stacked vertically, each occupying roughly half-viewport height:

**First panel** (light): Left ~40% is a text block — headline stacked vertically in dark serif caps ("Feature A / Line 2 / Line 3"), a subtitle in smaller spaced caps, and an underlined action link. Right ~60% is a large product photograph bleeding to the edge. Classic editorial **text-left, image-right** split.

**Second panel** (begins as light, transitions to neutral): A **vertically cropped editorial photograph** bleeds full-width — a partial figure, cropped deliberately to show only a fragment, creating mystery. This transitions directly into the next section.

---

### Section D — "The Seasons of [Brand Name]" (Centered, Three-Column)

Light ground returns. **Centered section title** in spaced caps with generous top margin.

Below: a **three-product grid** — centered, equally spaced, same proportional logic as the earlier product carousel. Products here are more artistic/seasonal. Same format: isolated product on light ground, all-caps name beneath.

The section uses **vertical white space generously** — the products breathe, never crowded.

---

### Section E — "[Brand Publication]" Newsletter (Two-Column, Warm Ground)

A **horizontal two-column panel** at roughly 50/50 split:

- **Left**: centered text content — section title in spaced caps, a two-line descriptor, an email input field (minimal, near-invisible border), and a Primary CTA button
- **Right**: an editorial photograph with a publication or archival motif

The background shifts to a **warm off-white to light gradient**. The input field and button are very restrained — form elements almost disappear into the layout, keeping the aesthetic luxury-forward.

---

### Section F — Locations Carousel (Full-Bleed Dark Background)

Background becomes **deep dark** — dramatic tonal shift.

A **horizontal carousel of location photographs** spans the full width. The center image is larger and slightly elevated within its frame (spotlight treatment), flanked by partial images on left and right that are visually dimmed/receded. Left/right chevron arrows indicate scrollability.

Below the imagery: centered light text — location name in spaced caps, address in lighter weight. A spaced-caps action link with an underline sits centered beneath.

---

## 5. Footer

Still on **dark ground**, the footer sits directly beneath the locations section with a clear horizontal rule dividing them.

**Four equal columns**, horizontally centered in a wide container:
- CUSTOMER SERVICE — stacked links
- COMPANY — stacked links
- LEGAL INFORMATION — stacked links
- ACCESSIBILITY — two toggle switches (Decrease animations / Increase contrasts) + Sitemap link

Below the four columns: a **centered stacked logo block** — the brand emblem, full wordmark, founding descriptor, and location. Generous vertical space above and below.

Below the logo: a **row of social icons** (relevant platforms) — circular icon buttons, evenly spaced, light on transparent.

The very bottom bar: left-aligned locale selector (region | language dropdown), right-aligned copyright notice — lean, utilitarian, restrained.

In the bottom-left corner: the **brand mascot or decorative illustration** reappears — large, whimsical, partially cropped. A signature brand eccentricity that bookends the page.

---

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

**Full-bleed sections alternating with contained content** — the page never settles into one mode; hero and dark sections bleed edge-to-edge while product and editorial sections use an implied inner container.

**Persistent sticky scroll-to-top button** — a thin circle with an up-arrow appears consistently at the bottom-right corner across every section. It's light and unobtrusive, integrated into the layout rhythm rather than fighting it.

**Floating decorative elements** — the brand signature mark appears twice (hero bottom-right, footer bottom-left), functioning as a recurring brand signature rather than UI.

**Vertical image staggering** in the editorial two-column creates layered depth without overlapping — a spatial relationship defined by offset rather than z-index.

**Typography as layout driver** — all section titles use identical treatment (spaced all-caps, centered or left-aligned) and act as horizontal dividers that give the page its measured, ceremonial pacing. No section begins without this title ceremony.

**Color-ground rhythm** — light → light → light → dark → light → light → dark → dark creates a deliberate crescendo toward the dark footer, like a closing curtain.
