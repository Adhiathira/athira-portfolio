## Visual Identity

The site employs a deep forest green and cream palette, offset by signature golden amber accents. Elegant sans-serif headlines contrast with the serif body copy, evoking a classic yet modern feel. Motion is used sparingly, emphasizing subtle transitions and a hero video that provides entry to the brand’s world.

## Hero Section

The hero section features a full-viewport-width video background with overlaid, centered, large headline text. A single underlined CTA link sits below the headline. The video is set to loop.

## Content Sections

The body features several layout patterns: equal-width product cards, editorial splits, and full-width image sections. The color palette is used to create clear section dividers.  Whitespace is used thoughtfully to provide a breathable interface.

## Footer Section

The footer incorporates four equal-width columns of centered text links, each under an uppercase heading. The footer features a centered logo, social media icons, and a language/locale selector at the bottom left, set against a dark green field. Accessibility toggles are included in the rightmost column.

## Design Principles

*   Emphasis on a limited color palette with bold contrasts
*   Strategic use of full-bleed photography intermixed with structured editorial grids
*   Use of a proprietary headline font for a premium feel
*   Considerate use of whitespace to create visual calm
*   Subtle but high-impact motion through video and transitions.

## Distinctive Qualities

The use of a proprietary "Goyard" font throughout headlines, titles, and navigation creates strong, distinguished typography. In addition, the choice of golden amber as a signature accent color against the deep forest green provides a strong visual motif. The scroll-triggered carousel of interior photography in the boutique section uniquely visualizes the physical brand experience in a digital setting.
