# ink-burst — Concept Summary (Image)

## Visual Identity

ink-burst is a **Comic/Pop-Art design system** that channels the energy of Roy Lichtenstein, vintage comic books, and Ben-Day dot printing into a digital product design system.

## Core Visual Language

**Palette:** Pure primary comic palette — white page (#ffffff), black ink (#000000), action red (#ff1744), highlight yellow (#ffeb3b), Ben-Day cyan (#00bcd4), and soft panel tints (#fff9c4, #e3f2fd).

**Borders:** Everything has a 3px solid black border. No softness. Panels, cards, buttons, inputs — all outlined in #000000. This is the ink, the defining feature of the style.

**Box Shadow:** Hard offset shadows only — 4px 4px 0 #000000 with zero blur. No soft shadows, no drop shadows. The shadow suggests depth the way a comic book suggests 3D — through displacement, not gradation.

**Ben-Day Dot Texture:** Background texture using CSS radial-gradient circles (1.5px dots on 8px grid) at 0.08 opacity. The defining texture of comic printing — dots that approximate color and form.

**Typography:** Bangers for all headings and display text — the definitive comic book display font. Comic Neue for body copy — humanist, readable, cheerfully informal. Letter-spacing of 0.04–0.08em is mandatory on Bangers for legibility.

**Layout:** Irregular panel grid inspired by comic book page layout. Thin gutters (4–8px) between panels like comic gutters. Asymmetric spans — some panels are feature-wide, others narrow. Container 1280px with 32px padding.

**Color blocking:** Sections use flat bold fills — yellow hero, white content, cyan accent, red action. No gradients. Color transitions happen at hard panel borders.

**Speech bubbles:** CSS-only speech bubble elements for testimonials, callouts, and emphasis text. Triangular tail, bold 3px border, white or yellow fill.

**Icons and badges:** ZAP!, POW!, BURST! — onomatopoeia as design elements. Rotated 3–8 degrees, in red or yellow fills.

## Design Inspiration

Roy Lichtenstein's panel paintings. Jack Kirby's Fantastic Four. Silver Age Marvel composition. Ben-Day dot printing. Bold declarative typography that demands to be read.
