Got Questions?

Quick Answers

Can I really start for free?

Yes — Starter is free forever. No credit card required, no trial period. Publish up to 10 comics and grow your first 100 subscribers before spending a cent.

What's the revenue share?

Pro creators keep 92% of all sales. Studio creators keep 95%. We take 8% or 5% respectively to keep the platform running. Payment processing fees are separate (Stripe charges ~2.9% + 30c).

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel any time with no fees or penalties. Your comics stay live and your subscribers stay yours — you can export everything.

Do I need design skills?

None required. BURST is built for creators who draw, not for designers. If you can drag and drop, you can build a professional-looking comic page.

What formats can I upload?

PNG, JPEG, SVG, GIF, WebP, and Procreate .procreate files (with automatic panel extraction). Max 50MB per page on Pro and Studio plans.

Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes — pay annually and save 20%. Pro drops to $9.60/mo billed annually. Studio drops to $31.20/mo billed annually.
