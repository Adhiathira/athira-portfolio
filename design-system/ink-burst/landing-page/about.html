Our Story

Built by
Comic Creators

BURST started in 2023 when three independent comic artists got tired of cobbling together Photoshop, Google Docs, and Mailchimp to run their one-person comic empires. There had to be a better way.

Our Mission

Why We Exist

Comics are one of the most powerful storytelling formats humans have ever invented. They're visual, immediate, emotional, and funny. But the tools to make and publish them have been either too expensive, too complex, or built for corporations instead of independent creators.

BURST exists to fix that. We believe every creator with a story to tell should have a professional-grade tool — and a direct line to an audience who loves their work.

Our Values

01

Creators First

Every product decision starts with one question: does this help creators make better comics and reach more readers? If not, we don't build it.

02

No Complexity Tax

Professional tools don't have to be complicated. We obsess over making hard things simple — from panel layout to subscriber management.

03

Fair Revenue

Creators do the work. They should keep most of the money. Our 92% revenue share isn't a feature — it's a commitment to the people who make BURST worth using.
