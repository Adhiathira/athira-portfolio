## Prompt

A slow drift across a working darkroom surface — warm morning light raking at a low angle across uncoated paper and chemical trays. A developing reel rests at the edge of frame. The camera barely moves; what changes is the quality of light as a cloud passes outside a high window. Shot at approximately 3200K colour temperature. Amber and deep sienna tones dominate. The motion is in the light, not the frame. Close enough to show the grain of the paper and the meniscus of liquid in a shallow tray. No hands, no faces. The room is in a state of readiness.
