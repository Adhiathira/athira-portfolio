The method

How the chemistry works.

Development is not a service. It is a controlled chemical reaction. We describe it here in full so you can decide whether our precision matches your film.

Submission

Before you send us anything.

We process film only when we have enough information to develop it correctly. The intake form is not bureaucracy — it is the first step in the process.

01

Complete the submission form

Film type, format, ISO, exposure index, push/pull preference, and any special handling instructions. If you do not know the exposure index, tell us what you saw when you metered it. That is enough.

02

Package the film

35mm: do not rewind into the canister unless you prefer uncut negatives. 120: in a lightproof bag inside a rigid container. Sheet film: in holders, wrapped in black paper, inside a labeled envelope. Nothing loose.

03

Drop off or ship

We accept drop-offs Monday–Friday at Goudsesingel 44. For shipping: use a tracked service with the word FILM written on the outer packaging. We do not process film that arrives damaged.

Development

How we run the chemistry.

Each chemistry type runs on a dedicated machine with its own maintenance schedule. We do not cross-contaminate processes or share baths between formats.

04

Batch calculation

Development time is calculated from the chemistry's current seasoning level, not from a manufacturer chart. We log every run. If the chemistry behaves unexpectedly, we stop and retest before proceeding.

05

Temperature control

Developer temperature is maintained to ±0.1°C throughout the run. All baths — developer, stop, fix, wash — are pre-conditioned before any film enters the machine. We use calibrated thermometers, not processor readouts.

06

Development run

Film is loaded in absolute darkness. Timing begins when chemistry contacts the emulsion. Agitation cycles are constant and mechanical. Manual development is available for large-format sheet film only.

07

Wash and dry

Archival wash: 8 cycles at 20°C with a final Kodak Hypo Clearing Agent rinse. Drying in a dust-controlled cabinet at 35°C. Film is not returned until fully dry.

Delivery

After development.

08

Sleeving

35mm is cut into strips of 5 or 6 frames and sleeved in archival polyethylene. 120 is sleeved in individual strips. Sheet film is returned in individual archival envelopes, labeled with frame number and development data.

09

Scanning (optional)

Flatbed scanning to 4000 dpi optical, 16-bit TIFF, colour-corrected or raw as you prefer. Delivered via secure download link. We do not use AI enhancement or sharpening tools.

10

Collection or return shipping

Standard turnaround: 5 working days from receipt. Rush: 48 hours at additional cost. We ship using DHL Express with full tracking. No exceptions on chemistry time regardless of turnaround.

Chemistry supported
C-41

35mm and 120 colour negative. Push/pull ±3 stops.

E-6

35mm and 120 colour reversal (slide film). Push/pull ±2 stops.

ECN-2

16mm motion picture film (Kodak Vision stocks). Remjet removal included.

B&W

All formats. Developer selection (D-76, HC-110, Rodinal) on consultation.
