The method

Precision is not a feature. It is the minimum.

We run a closed chemical loop. Temperature is held to ±0.1°C. Development times are calculated per batch, not per stock. The result is consistent — not because we are careful, but because we have removed every variable we can remove.

Services

What the lab offers.

Development

All chemistry in-house. C-41, E-6, ECN-2, B&W. Push/pull on request.

Scanning

Drum and flatbed. Up to 8000 dpi. 16-bit TIFF delivery.

Printing

Optical contact prints. Enlargements up to 50×60cm on fibre.

Archival

Cold storage, sleeving, and humidity-controlled long-term custody for working archives.

Consultation

Film selection, exposure guidance, and post-processing workflow review.

Turnaround

Standard: 5 working days. Rush: 48 hours. No exceptions to chemistry time.
