# Brand Name — Layout Brief

---

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately **8 major sections**, alternating dramatically between dark charcoal/near-black backgrounds and a warm off-white/pale grey. This alternation is the primary rhythm driver — it creates a push-pull tension that keeps the reader moving. The sequence is:

1. Hero (dark)
2. App preview / product showcase (dark-to-light transition)
3. Brand manifesto (light)
4. Collaboration pitch (dark)
5. Feature carousel (light-to-dark transition)
6. Values / philosophy section (light)
7. Social proof / CTA (light)
8. Closing CTA + Footer (dark)

The page is **full-viewport-width** throughout. No constrained reading columns — content breathes across the full canvas.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

A **fixed, full-width top bar** — very shallow in height, roughly 56–64px. It sits cleanly in two zones:

- **Left:** Brand wordmark — a distinctive, stylized logotype. Left-aligned, modest in size.
- **Center:** Three navigation items with dropdown chevrons ("Feature A ∨", "Feature B ∨", "About ∨") — horizontally centered in the bar with generous spacing between them.
- **Right:** Two CTAs — "Log in" (outlined/ghost button) and "Sign up" (filled, high-contrast dark button with rounded pill form). Tight to the right edge.

The nav sits on the same warm off-white as the hero background in light sections but reads as floating when over dark sections. It's transparent/no visible divider — it blends into whatever background is beneath it.

---

## 3. Hero Section

**Full-viewport-height, centered single column.**

The hero is entirely dark — near-black charcoal. The core content is vertically and horizontally centered in the viewport:

- A **large editorial headline** — massive serif type, roughly 72–80px, dead center. A trailing cursor "|" is a live typing animation detail.
- Below: a short **two-line subtitle** in a lighter-weight sans-serif, roughly 18px, centered, with relaxed line spacing.
- Below that: a single **Primary CTA pill button** — light/white fill, dark text, softly rounded. Centered.

What makes this hero distinctive are the **floating typographic elements** scattered across the entire canvas — individual lowercase letters drift at various sizes and low opacity across the background like floating debris in zero gravity. They're positioned seemingly at random: top-left, top-right, bottom edges, mid-sides. This creates immense spatial depth without any imagery.

Two additional **floating decorative elements** break the centeredness:
- Bottom-left: a hand-drawn line illustration (white/light outline, roughly 15% viewport width)
- Right-center: a stylized icon in **vivid accent color** — the only chromatic accent in the entire hero, which makes it visually electric.

The hero is **approximately 95–100vh**, consuming the full first viewport.

---

## 4. Content Sections

### Section 2 — App Preview / Product Showcase

A **dark-background full-bleed section** that transitions from the hero. The centerpiece is a large **dashboard UI mockup** — a floating, slightly inset window showing the product's primary screen. This fills roughly 80–85% of the viewport width, centered, with modest padding on sides.

Layered on top of this dashboard, in the bottom-left quadrant, are **two overlapping device mockups** — a tablet and two smartphones at slightly different scales — positioned to overlap both each other and the dashboard behind them. This creates a three-layer depth stack: background → dashboard → devices. The devices break the bounding box of the dashboard intentionally.

The right side of the viewport behind the devices shows **faint sketched pen-drawing illustrations**, adding texture without competing.

This section is heavy on visual weight — it's meant to establish the product as real and substantial.

---

### Section 3 — Brand Manifesto

**Light background (warm off-white), single asymmetric column.**

A dramatic shift in pace. Two large paragraph blocks of editorial body copy, roughly 55–60pt in size, left-aligned:

- First block: a bold differentiating statement — with a key phrase in **italic serif**, visually differentiated.
- Second block: a brand mission statement — with inline **color emphasis** on key phrases: one phrase underlined in the accent color, another circled in a contrasting stroke.

The text spans roughly **60% of the viewport width**, left-offset — not centered, but weighted left. The right side of the viewport holds scattered **hand-drawn illustration elements** floating in the white space, positioned diagonally from one another, creating a loose triangular framing around the text.

Small annotated **hand-lettered callout arrows** point to elements with short brand value statements.

This section is the most literary and typographically expressive section on the page.

---

### Section 4 — Collaboration Section

**Full-bleed dark background**, returning to near-black. A bold **wavy SVG divider** (a smooth curve) separates this from the section above — the curve feels organic, like a rolling hill or wave, not a hard edge.

Content is **center-aligned**:

- Small annotation at upper-left: overlapping avatar circles with an arrow and short caption — a charming, human-scale detail that contrasts with the large type below.
- Headline in massive serif: a key collaboration or value proposition — roughly 64–72px, centered, two lines.
- Beneath: a short paragraph body copy in smaller grey-white, roughly 18px, centered, with a contained max-width (~50% of viewport).

**Flanking this centered text block** are two large pen-drawing illustrations of objects. These are positioned symmetrically, creating a "stage" or "theatre wings" spatial relationship around the central copy.

The lower portion of this dark section shows the **tops of three rounded-corner cards** beginning to emerge from the bottom — a visual preview/teaser of the next section below.

---

### Section 5 — Feature Cards / Carousel

**Light background with a horizontal scrolling or overflow pattern.** Three large, equal-width cards are visible simultaneously — each occupies roughly 30% of the viewport width. They're tall (full-section-height) with subtle rounded corners and a softly colored background (cream-white, cool tone, and complementary tone).

Cards appear to be slightly **cut off at left and right edges** — implying there are more cards outside the viewport (a carousel pattern). Content within each card is an inner-product UI showing key feature states. Cards suggest the product's core interaction model.

This section is the densest information section — pure product, no illustration.

---

### Section 6 — Philosophy / Values

**Light background, asymmetric two-column layout.**

- Left: large display serif headline (a brand positioning statement), roughly 60px, taking up ~50% of the viewport width.
- Right: a smaller body copy block (roughly 300px wide) in a right-offset column position. The text is center-aligned within that column.

**Annotated callout elements** float between and around the columns:
- A key brand value statement in accent color italic with a hand-drawn arrow
- A differentiating stance statement, positioned lower-right

Below this text treatment: large line-art illustrations of objects **float freely across the section**, not constrained to any column or row. They're scattered with apparent randomness but with enough visual breathing room to feel intentional.

This is the most spatially free section — the closest to an editorial magazine spread.

---

### Section 7 — Social Proof / Closing CTA (Light)

**Light background, full-width, vertically centered single column.**

Very minimal. Large illustration sketches in the upper portion, transitioning into pure typography:

- Massive centered headline: a social proof statement with a user count — roughly 64–72px serif, center-aligned, two lines.
- Single **Primary CTA pill button** below — dark fill, white text, pill-shaped. Centered.

This is the cleanest, most open section — maximal whitespace around the type. The illustrations above leave a gap before the text, giving the message room to land.

---

### Section 8 — Pre-Footer CTA Dark Panel

**Dark background, two-column asymmetric layout.**

A transitional section before the footer proper:

- Left: headline with a final action-oriented statement, with a ghost/outlined **Secondary CTA button** below
- Right: a large hand-drawn line illustration taking up roughly 40–45% of the section width

The bottom edge of this dark panel curves or flows downward via an SVG wave divider into the footer below.

---

## 5. Footer Layout

**Dark/near-black full-bleed footer**, divided into two clear halves:

- **Left column (~30% width):** The brand logotype (large, white) stacked above a vertical list of navigation links in small caps — standard utility and informational links. Very generous line-height between links.
- **Right column (~55% width):** A single large editorial pull-quote in large serif — with the typing cursor animation again. Attribution line below.

The two columns are separated by generous gutters; no divider line. The quote occupies more vertical space than the nav links, creating a slight vertical misalignment (top-aligned, not center-aligned) that feels intentional — the quote is the dominant element.

**Sub-footer row** at the very bottom:
- Far left: a row of social icons — spaced evenly
- Far right: legal links and a physical address

The sub-footer uses very small type (~11–12px) and sits in a slightly darker band, creating a tertiary tier.

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

### Organic Wave Dividers
Section transitions between dark and light are frequently handled with **smooth SVG wave curves** rather than hard horizontal lines. These give the page an organic, hand-drawn quality consistent with the illustration style.

### Full-Bleed Section Alternation
Every major section is **100vw** — the alternation between dark and light backgrounds does all the section work. No cards, boxes, or containers divide the page vertically; background color is the section indicator.

### Floating Illustrative Elements
Throughout the page, **hand-drawn line illustrations** appear to float freely — they are not contained within columns or cards. They overlap text zones, bleed toward edges, and create the sense that objects are scattered on a studio desk. This is the dominant decorative vocabulary.

### Typographic Annotation System
A recurring pattern: inline text emphasis via **color, underline, or hand-drawn oval/circle strokes** marks key phrases. Combined with **handwritten callout text + arrow illustrations**, this creates an informal annotation layer that sits above the primary copy hierarchy.

### Three-Layer Depth in Product Section
The product preview section deliberately stacks three visual layers: background color → large UI screenshot → device mockups overlapping the UI. This Z-axis layering is the most spatially complex moment on the page.

### Isolated Accent Color
A **vivid accent color** appears exactly once in the hero and in subtle underline accents in the manifesto section. Its scarcity makes it feel like a brand signature rather than a design system color.

### Typing Cursor Motif
The blinking text cursor "|" appears in both the hero headline and the footer quote — an editorial signature that reinforces the product identity and creates narrative bookends.
