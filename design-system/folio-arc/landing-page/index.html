What Fieldset Does

One Platform,
Three Disciplines

Research data is messy. Observations get lost in notebooks, spreadsheets, and disconnected tools. Fieldset imposes structure without friction — your team works naturally, the data becomes queryable.

01

Structured Capture

Define observation schemas before fieldwork begins. Every data point collected — measurements, annotations, location markers, media — is captured against a pre-defined structure that survives the chaos of real-world collection environments.

02

Validation Pipeline

Automated and peer-review validation rules flag anomalies before they compound. Your data arrives clean — not cleaned after the fact.

03

Query & Export

Run structured queries across your entire observation archive. Export to standard formats: JSON, CSV, NetCDF, or pipe directly to your analysis environment.

04

Audit Trail

Every edit, every annotation change, every schema revision is versioned. You can reconstruct any state of any dataset at any point in its history.

The Methodology

From Raw Observation
to Trusted Finding

Step 01

Define Your Schema

Before any data collection begins, your team defines what will be measured, in what format, and against what reference. Fieldset enforces this schema across every collector on every device — in the field or in the lab.

Step 02

Collect at Source

Fieldset runs offline. Observations are captured locally and synced when connectivity is restored. Collectors see validation feedback in real time — invalid entries are flagged before they leave the collection device.

Step 03

Validate & Reconcile

Submitted observations pass through your configured validation pipeline — automated range checks, cross-reference validation, and peer-review workflows for high-consequence measurements. Discrepancies surface as discrete, resolvable issues, not data entropy.

Step 04

Query & Publish

Validated data is immediately queryable. Run ad hoc analysis, build persistent reports, export to your analysis stack, or publish a citable dataset to a journal archive — with provenance metadata intact.
