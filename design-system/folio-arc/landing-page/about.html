Fieldset Est. 2022

Research data is the most painstaking product of human inquiry. It deserves infrastructure as rigorous as the science it records.

01
Origin

Built by Researchers,
For Researchers

Fieldset was founded in 2022 by a team that had spent the previous decade doing observational fieldwork across ecology, hydrology, and archaeological survey. We knew what it meant to lose a season of data to a corrupted spreadsheet. We knew the particular frustration of discovering, during analysis, that three collectors had been using different unit conventions for the same measurement.

We built Fieldset because we couldn't find a tool that treated data integrity as a first principle rather than an afterthought. Most field data software is designed around ease of capture — and then leaves the hard work of validation and reconciliation to the analyst, after the fact.

We inverted the constraint: structure at capture, so that analysis doesn't begin with reconstruction.

02
Principles

How We Think
About Data

Structure Before Capture

A schema defined in advance is not a bureaucratic constraint — it is the most important act of research design. Fieldset treats schema authorship as a first-class activity, not configuration overhead.

Validation at Source

Errors found at collection cost minutes. Errors found at analysis cost weeks. Errors found at publication cost careers. We validate as early as physically possible.

Provenance is Non-Negotiable

Every observation has a collector, a timestamp, a schema version, and a validation history. These are not metadata — they are the observation. We never lose them.

Analysis Environments are Downstream

We do not replace R, Python, QGIS, or your preferred analysis stack. We make certain that what arrives there is trustworthy — structured, validated, and accompanied by its full lineage.
