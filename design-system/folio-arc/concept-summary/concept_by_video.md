# Folio Arc — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads with the hero fully rendered — the deep forest green field at full opacity, the headline at the bottom-left, CTAs visible. No entrance sequence. The distinction: the green field is not static in the way a flat color is static. There is a living quality to it — a barely-perceptible ambient motion, as though current is running through the field rather than it simply existing as background. The effect is not decorative; it is atmospheric. The page does not announce itself. It operates.

## Hero Experience

The headline text reveal is a fade-up over 910ms (the system's expressive duration), deceleration easing. The deliberate pacing — inherited from the ×1.3 motion scaling — gives the text the weight of a formal declaration rather than a UI animation. Words arrive as if each one has considered its entrance. The hero field breathes around them: no loop, no restless motion — a single pulse of atmospheric depth that settles before the CTAs appear.

The hero is a candidate for ambient video background: **`hero-ambient.mp4`** — a slow visualization of structural forms accumulating in deep forest green space, as if architectural blueprints are being drawn from nothing. The motion is geological in pace: 10s loop, imperceptible at any single frame, legible only over time. The effect is that the page does not sit still — it is in the process of becoming.

## Section Transitions

Section boundaries are clean and unhesitant. The transition from deep green to light gray reads as a material shift — not a fade or a blend, but the change that happens when you turn the page in a book printed on two different stocks. Each section arrives complete. The 390ms standard duration provides a measured rhythm to scroll-triggered reveals: not leisurely, not abrupt, but considered.

## Content Rhythm

Individual content elements enter with the system's stagger-fade-up pattern: 910ms per element, 100ms stagger between cards in a row. The pacing is more deliberate than typical SaaS — it asks the reader to wait a fraction longer than expected, which has the effect of making each arriving element feel considered rather than mechanical. Data elements (percentage indicators, graph connections, roadmap nodes) animate with slightly faster, more precise draw-ins, establishing a dual rhythm: prose arrives slowly; evidence arrives quickly.

## Footer Approach

The footer arrives as the most grounded moment of the page — the return to the deep forest green that opened the experience. No entrance animation is needed. The wordmark settles into its final position as the user arrives. There is a brief illumination: a single-pass horizontal brightness sweep across the logotype over 300ms, as though the field is acknowledging the closing bracket. Then stillness. The platform does not wave goodbye — it acknowledges completion.

## Motion Principles

The motion philosophy is measured, structurally deliberate, and charged with quiet authority. The ×1.3 duration scaling from the source system creates a pace that reads as considered rather than slow — the difference between a signature and a scrawl. Primary durations: 910ms expressive (page-level reveals), 390ms standard (nav, buttons), 195ms quick (menu opacity). Easing: deceleration-only curves throughout — no acceleration phase, which means no jank, no stutter, no excitement. Elements arrive; they do not perform. The voltage energy is expressed through precision and weight, not through speed or enthusiasm. One-time signals for primary interactive elements. No loops on UI elements — loops belong to ambient video only.

## Video Placements

Three sections of this design would be meaningfully served by ambient looping video:

**1. Hero Background — Structural formation ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `hero-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: Slow accumulation of architectural line structures in deep forest green space — grid lines drawing in, connective nodes appearing, structural forms building from nothing into a system; all rendered in the design's palette (#003620 ground, white and #00693e line elements); motion at the pace of geological formation, not animation
- Duration: 10s loop
- Why video: The design's "infrastructure as identity" principle is most powerful when the hero field itself is alive. A structural formation loop — in the primary color world — gives the viewer the felt experience of inhabiting a platform that is actively building its own architecture. The viewer is not watching a demo; they are inside the construction.

**2. Product Feature Showcase — Data intelligence ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `product-system-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: Abstract representation of the evaluation system in operation: data streams flowing between nodes, skill graphs populating with evidence, capability indicators resolving from uncertainty to precision; shot style is diagnostic — clean, cool light, no depth of field drama; 8s loop
- Duration: 8s loop
- Why video: The product sections carry the "proof over promise" principle. Static UI mockups show the output; video can show the process — the intelligence operating, the system evaluating. The temporal dimension is the product's core differentiator: not a score, but a living assessment.

**3. Ecosystem Section — Community and connection ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `ecosystem-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A slow-drift visualization of a professional network forming — nodes labeled with domain categories (ML Engineering, Product, Research, Design) connecting and reconnecting in a gentle, purposeful topology; rendered in light against a dark green background; the motion is organic but not random — connections form with intention, edges have weight, the network breathes
- Duration: 10s loop
- Why video: The "ecosystem" section makes a structural claim about three distinct audiences (businesses, individuals, community). A network-formation loop makes that claim spatial and tangible: the viewer can see the ecosystem as a live entity, not a product diagram. The three columns gain depth when the background is in motion — each column becomes a window into a different part of the same living system.
