— Method

A measurement is only as good as the method that produces it.

01

Sample Preparation

Each test sample is conditioned at 23°C and 50% RH for a minimum of 4 hours prior to measurement. This eliminates moisture-content variables that would otherwise compromise fold endurance readings by up to 18%.

02

Instrument Calibration

The QP-12 is zeroed against a 250µm reference gauge before each session. The QP-7's fold tension is calibrated to the specified load (0.5kN standard, 1kN optional) using the included dead-weight set. Verification is traceable to national standards.

03

Measurement Cycle

The QP-12 completes a full thickness reading cycle in 2.2 seconds. The QP-7 counts each complete double-fold and automatically stops at fracture. Results are logged directly onto the QP data sheet or export-ready CSV if used with the optional QP DataLogger.

04

Reporting

QP results are reported against ISO 5626 (fold endurance) and ISO 534 (thickness). Conservation studios typically report QP-7 values alongside Betti number readings. Binderies use QP-12 caliper data to select appropriate scoring blades for first-edition work.

— The Studio

Instrument-making for people who make by hand.

Quill Plane Instrument Co. was founded in 1971 to serve the British hand-papermaking revival — a moment when craftspeople needed tools that matched the precision of their practice. The QP instruments were never designed for mass production. They were designed for the bench: for the space between the vat and the press, between the stone and the forme, where a 0.3mm variance in caliper can determine whether a first-edition binding holds for fifty years or fails in ten.

The instruments remain in production today for conservation studios, archival binderies, and the handful of European mills still using cylinder mould formation. Acquired by private trust in 1998 and never modernised.
