— Founded 1971, Stroud, Gloucestershire

Instrument-making for people who make by hand.

"The instruments were never designed for mass production. They were designed for the bench."

Quill Plane Instrument Co. was founded in 1971 to serve the British hand-papermaking revival — a moment when craftspeople needed measurement tools that matched the precision of their practice. The first QP instruments were made for the space between the vat and the press, between the stone and the forme, where a 0.3mm variance in caliper can determine whether a first-edition binding holds for fifty years or fails in ten.

The instruments remain in production today for conservation studios, archival binderies, and the handful of European mills still using cylinder mould formation. In 1998 the studio was acquired by a private trust that committed to maintaining production without modernisation. The QP-7 and QP-12 have not changed in specification since 1971 — only the reference standards they are calibrated to have been updated as ISO superseded older national equivalents.

— Studio History

  1. 1971

    Studio founded, Stroud

    The QP-7 Fold Endurance Tester is completed and put into production. First instruments delivered to three British hand-paper mills and one conservation bindery. The design brief: instrument weight under 1kg, compatible with existing paper mill bench dimensions, calibrated to the prevailing BS standard.

  2. 1974

    QP-12 Thickness Comparator introduced

    A second instrument — the QP-12 — is developed in response to requests from cylinder mould mills for a caliper gauge with audible zero indication. The stainless contact-face design is established. Both instruments share the same body casting, finishing, and weight specification.

  3. 1987

    ISO 5626 recalibration

    As ISO 5626 supersedes the British Standard BS 4432, QP-7 calibration is updated. This is the first specification change in the instrument's history. No changes are made to the body, mechanism, or contact geometry — only the reference standard used during factory calibration.

  4. 1998

    Acquisition by private trust

    The studio is acquired by a private conservation instrument trust. The founding condition of the acquisition is that production continues unchanged and that the instruments are not redesigned for industrial manufacturing. The trust commits to maintaining hand-assembly and individual certification for each instrument.

  5. 2003

    QP DataLogger accessory

    A serial interface unit is introduced for QP-7, enabling CSV export of fold count data per sample. This is the first electronic accessory in the QP range. The instrument itself remains mechanical; the DataLogger is an external peripheral. TAPPI T511 support is added alongside ISO 5626.

  6. 2024

    53 years in continuous production

    The QP-7 and QP-12 remain in production, unchanged in specification. Current active instruments in field service span 47 conservation studios and European paper mills. The oldest QP-7 in documented service was assembled in 1973 — it remains calibrated and in regular use at a Gloucestershire archive.

— Material Specification

Every material chosen for service life, not appearance.

The QP body specification has not changed since 1971. BS 10 B 17 is a natural grey powder coat that resists chemical splash common in papermaking studios. Cast aluminium at 870g provides enough mass to sit stably on a mill bench without external clamping. Stainless contact faces resist corrosion in high-humidity mill environments where mild steel would pit within a season.

Cast Aluminium Body

870g / LM6 / precision cast

High-silicon LM6 aluminium alloy. Precision cast for dimensional consistency across batches. Machined contact surfaces. 870g provides bench stability without requiring clamps.

BS 10 B 17 Powder Coat

Natural grey / chemical resistant

British Standard 10 B 17 is a neutral warm grey selected for legibility in low-light studio conditions. The powder coat resists weak acids and alkalis common in papermaking vats and conservation baths.

304 Stainless Contact Faces

QP-12 / factory-lapped pair

Austenitic 304 stainless for corrosion resistance in high-humidity mill environments. Contact faces are factory lapped to 0.1µm flatness and supplied as calibrated pairs — matching is critical to measurement geometry.

Hardened Spring Mechanism

QP-7 / phosphor bronze / 18-year service life

The fold mechanism spring is phosphor bronze, chosen for consistent elastic return over millions of cycles. Rated for 18-year average service life before recalibration-interval reduction is recommended.
