— Model QP-7

Fold Endurance Tester

QP-7 / ISO 5626 / TAPPI T511

Measures the number of double-folds a paper sample can endure before fracture at a specified tensile load. Designed for use in conservation studios, archival binderies, and certification laboratories where fold life must be quantified against an international standard.

Test Standard

ISO 5626 / TAPPI T511

Method

Double-fold, adjustable tension

Load Options

0.5 kN (standard) / 1.0 kN

Range

1 to 100,000 folds

Body

870g cast aluminium, BS 10 B 17 powder coat

Auto-stop

At fracture detection

Optional

QP DataLogger (CSV export)

— Model QP-12

Thickness Comparator

QP-12 / ISO 534 / Caliper

Measures caliper of single sheets from 20 to 1,200 microns. Contact faces are 304 stainless steel with audible click at zero. Cylinder mould mill compatible. Used by European hand-papermakers and production mills where thickness consistency determines scoring blade selection and fold quality in bookbinding.

Test Standard

ISO 534 (Thickness)

Range

20 to 1,200 µm

Resolution

1 µm

Contact Faces

304 stainless steel

Body

870g cast aluminium, BS 10 B 17 powder coat

Cycle Time

2.2 seconds per reading

Zero Indication

Audible click at contact

— Accessories & Consumables

Supporting equipment for complete calibration readiness.

A01

Reference Weight Set

Dead-weight calibration set for QP-7 tension verification. Four weights traceable to NPL standards. Supplied in aluminium carry case.

A02

Replacement Contact Faces

Matched pair of 304 stainless contact faces for QP-12. Factory lapped to 0.1µm flatness. Supplied as a calibrated pair — never individual.

A03

Reference Gauge Block

250µm hardened steel gauge block for QP-12 zero-point verification. Certification traceable to ISO 3650 Grade K. One block per instrument.

A04

QP DataLogger

Serial interface unit for QP-7. Logs fold count per sample, exports as ISO-formatted CSV. Compatible with Windows 10/11 and macOS Sonoma via RS-232 adaptor.

A05

Tension Calibration Tool

Dedicated torque wrench and spring scale for QP-7 fold tension adjustment. Required for annual recertification. Marked 0.5 kN and 1.0 kN.

A06

QP Field Carry Case

Extruded aluminium case in BS 10 B 17 powder coat matching the instrument body. Foam-lined for QP-7 + QP-12 together with weight set. Lockable.
