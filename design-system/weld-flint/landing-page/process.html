Quality System

The Ferren Process

Four documented stages from raw material to certified component. Nothing ships without a measurement record.

Four Stages

Stage 01

Material Verification

Every incoming blank is hardness-tested using a Rockwell tester before it enters the production queue. Dimensional verification confirms that the blank stock is within 0.005″ of nominal on all critical dimensions. Material that fails either check is rejected and returned to the supplier — it is not reworked, not repurposed, and not incorporated into production.

We maintain material traceability records linking every finished component to its source stock lot. When a quality concern arises in the field — even years later — we can identify and isolate all components from the same material batch.

Stage 02

5-Axis Precision Machining

All critical surfaces are machined in a single setup on temperature-controlled Makino D500 5-axis machining centers. Single-setup machining eliminates repositioning errors — the largest source of tolerance stack in multi-setup production. Our facility is held at 68°F ±1°F during machining hours. Thermal variation in the machine structure and workpiece is accounted for in our datum procedures.

Toolpaths for all Ferren catalog items are fixed and version-controlled. A program change requires a sign-off from our quality manager and a re-verification of the first article before production resumes.

Machine capability data
→ assets/process-machining.jpeg

Stage 03

CMM Inspection

Every component — not a sample, every one — is measured on a Zeiss Contura CMM to the full GD&T callout before surface finishing or shipping. The CMM operates in a 68°F isolated measurement room. Probing strategies for all catalog items are fixed and stored in the CMM controller. Measurement data is not rounded and is not manually transcribed: the CMM prints its report directly.

Components that fail any GD&T callout are segregated immediately. They are not re-measured under more favorable conditions and they are not released on a concession. They are scrapped.

Sample CMM report
→ assets/process-cmm.jpeg

Stage 04

Serialization & Documentation

Every component that passes CMM inspection receives a unique serial number, laser-engraved on a non-critical surface. The serial number links permanently to the CMM report for that specific component and to the material lot from Stage 01. When your order ships, the serialization data and certificate of conformance ship with it — not as an option or an upgrade.

We retain documentation for each component for a minimum of ten years. If you need to reconstruct the quality history of a Ferren component — for an audit, an investigation, or a warranty claim — the records are available.
