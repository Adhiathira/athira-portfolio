Series A — Base Plate System

Specifications

Cross-Series Compatibility

All three series share one interface standard.

  1. Unified locating standard

    The Ferren 25mm bolt pattern and precision locating pin format is identical across Series A, B, and C. Components from different series assemble without adapters.

  2. Tolerance stack is documented

    When you combine Series A base plates with Series B jaws, the combined tolerance stack is published and certified — not calculated from nominal values.

  3. No proprietary lock-in

    Ferren uses open bolt patterns and standard locating pin diameters. Your Ferren components work with industry-standard tooling. Your investment is not captive to our catalog.
