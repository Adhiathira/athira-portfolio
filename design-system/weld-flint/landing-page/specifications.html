Specifications

Published dimensional data for all Ferren product families. These are verified values, not nominal claims.

Series A — Modular Base Plate System

Bolt pattern 25mm × 25mm grid
Locating surface flatness ±0.0001″ from datum
Locating pin diameter 12.000mm +0.000 / −0.002mm
Base material Cast iron, grade FC250
Surface hardness 200–240 HB (per ASTM E10)
Available sizes 200×200, 300×300, 400×400, 500×500mm
Weight (400×400) 4.8 kg
Clamping force capacity 12,000 N per clamping bolt (M12 grade 8.8)
Inspection certificate Full CMM report, included standard
Lead time (standard stock) 48 hours

Series B — Precision Vise Jaw System

Jaw material D2 tool steel, 60–62 HRC
Jaw face flatness 0.0002″ per 6-inch length
Parallelism, jaw faces (to vise datum) 0.0003″ per pair
Quick-swap interface Dovetail (45°), 0.0002″ datum repeatability
Changeover time < 60 seconds (no re-tramming required)
Jaw widths available 75mm, 100mm, 125mm, 150mm, 200mm
Service life (locating interface) ≥ 50,000 changeover cycles
Inspection certificate Full CMM report, included standard

Series C — Tombstone & Pallet Fixture System

Material Grey cast iron, stress-relieved
Locating surface squareness 0.0005″/12″ to base datum
T-slot width 14H7 (per DIN 650)
Pallet locating interface Zero-point coupling, 12kN pull-down force
Tombstone heights available 300mm, 400mm, 500mm, 600mm
Multi-face configurations 2-face, 4-face (standard); 6-face (custom)
Inspection certificate Full CMM report, included standard
Lead time (custom configuration) 15 business days
