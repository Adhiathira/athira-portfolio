How We Work

From raw material to certified fixture in four documented stages.

  1. Material verification

    Every blank is hardness-tested and dimensionally verified before machining begins. Out-of-spec material is rejected, not reworked.

  2. 5-axis precision machining

    All critical surfaces are machined in a single setup on temperature-controlled Makino 5-axis centers. No repositioning. No re-datum errors.

  3. CMM inspection

    Every component is measured on a Zeiss CMM to a full GD&T callout before finishing. Measurement data is retained and ships with each order.

  4. Serialization & documentation

    Each component receives a unique serial number and a traceable certificate of conformance. You know exactly what you received and when it was verified.
