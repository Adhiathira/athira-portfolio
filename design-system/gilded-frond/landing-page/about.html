About
Flora Carta

Founded in 2019 to preserve the illustrated botanical folio.

The illustrated botanical monograph flourished for two centuries and nearly vanished within a generation. Flora Carta is a response to that disappearance.

Flora Carta was founded in 2019 by a small group of botanical illustrators, natural history librarians, and conservationists who shared a concern about the degradation of the illustrated botanical record. The concern was not nostalgic — it was practical. Botanical illustration at the level of scientific accuracy requires a form of sustained observation that photography cannot replicate, and that level of observation had become economically unsustainable within the publishing industry.

"The plant is a patient subject. It asks only that the illustrator look long enough to understand what they are seeing."

The solution was a subscription model that funds illustration directly, removes the publisher as an intermediary, and restores the folio — rather than the monograph — as the unit of publication. A folio of twelve plates per season is achievable by a small team of committed illustrators working alongside scientific advisers. It is not achievable by a single publisher operating at commercial print economics.

In 2019, Flora Carta issued its first edition to thirty-two founding subscribers. By 2021 the subscriber list had grown to 400. The current edition distributes to 1,400 subscribers in 47 countries. The archive now numbers twenty-two folios and 264 plates. Every plate made since the founding is held in the physical archive at the Chelsea Physic Garden and in the digital reference library available to subscribers.

We have no plans to grow beyond the point at which the editorial rigour of the process is sustainable. When we reach the limit of what a small team can produce at this standard, we will stop expanding and continue publishing within that constraint.

Our Illustrators

Fourteen associate illustrators. All working practitioners.

Flora Carta works with a stable roster of fourteen associate illustrators, all of whom hold current membership of the Society of Botanical Artists or equivalent professional accreditation. New illustrators are added to the roster only when a member retires or a specific technical specialism is required that the existing roster cannot provide.

Lead Illustrator · Editions I–XXII

Helen Marchetti

Specialism: temperate woodland species. SBA Fellow. 18 years practice. Led the Roots & Dormancy folio and three previous editions. Trained at the Royal Botanic Garden Edinburgh.

Associate Illustrator · Copperplate

James Osei-Bonsu

Specialism: copperplate engraving and intaglio technique. Trained at Camberwell College of Arts. Led the Fungal Architectures folio (Edition XXI) and the Night-Blooming Species folio (Edition XVIII).

Associate Illustrator · Aquatic

Catriona MacPherson

Specialism: coastal and aquatic species, transparent watercolour. Led the Coastal Seaweeds of the Outer Hebrides folio (Edition XX). 24 years practice.

Scientific Adviser · Chelsea Physic Garden

Dr. Sarah Okonkwo

Royal Horticultural Society Wisley, formerly Chelsea Physic Garden. Botanical accuracy reviewer for all twenty-two editions. Specialism: British native flora and herbarium documentation methods.

Associate Illustrator · Orchidaceae

Priya Natarajan

Specialism: Orchidaceae and Ericaceae. Led the Orchids of the British Limestone Pavement folio (Edition XIX). SBA Gold Medal 2022. Trained at the Royal College of Art.

Editorial Director

Tom Whitfield

Formerly Kew Publishing. Founded Flora Carta in 2019. Responsible for species selection, thematic briefs, and print production oversight for all twenty-two editions.

Contact

We welcome correspondence on botanical subjects, subscription enquiries, and institutional licensing.

Flora Carta operates from London. All correspondence is answered personally. Response time for subscription enquiries: within two working days. Response time for botanical and archive enquiries: within five working days.
