Current Edition
Edition XXII · Winter 2024–2025

Roots & Dormancy: Underground Structures of the British Hedgerow

In autumn 2024, twenty-two illustrators from the Flora Carta associate roster spent six weeks excavating root systems along three miles of Kentish hedgerow boundary near Faversham. The resulting twelve plates document rhizomes, taproots, mycorrhizal networks, and bulb-forming species in cross-section — forms rarely depicted in botanical illustration because the act of documentation requires the destruction of the subject.

Lead illustrator: Helen Marchetti. Technical review: Dr. Sarah Okonkwo, RHS Wisley. Printed in warm sepia tones on 300gsm cold-press Hahnemühle. Bound in sage-green cloth with gold foil titleing.

Edition XXI · Autumn 2024

Fungal Architectures

Twelve mycelium and fruiting body studies using the copperplate engraving technique, hand-finished in gold ink. The most technically demanding folio in the series to date — each plate required between sixty and ninety hours to complete.

Edition XX · Summer 2024

Coastal Seaweeds of the Outer Hebrides

A survey of sublittoral and littoral algae on six-colour printing process. This folio departed from the Hahnemühle substrate for the first time, using Fabriano Artistico 300gsm hot-press to capture the translucent quality of kelp and bladder wrack.

Edition XIX · Spring 2024

Orchids of the British Limestone Pavement

Rare habitat documentation in delicate transparent watercolour washes. All twelve plates made on-site during the spring flowering season at limestone pavement Sites of Special Scientific Interest in the Yorkshire Dales.
