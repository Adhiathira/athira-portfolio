Calibration Services

Six Disciplines.
One Accreditation.

Every Forma service is performed against NIST-traceable reference standards and documented to ISO/IEC 17025:2017 requirements.

Instrument Disciplines

Select a discipline below for scope detail, instrumentation ranges, and standard turnaround times.

01

Gravimetric

Analytical balances, semi-micro balances, precision scales, and mass comparators. Calibrated to 0.01 mg resolution with OIML Class E2/F1 reference weights. On-site service available for instruments above 50 kg capacity.

Range

0.1 µg – 60 kg

02

Thermal

Laboratory ovens, incubators, refrigerated cabinets, environmental chambers, autoclaves, freeze-dryers, and temperature data loggers. ITS-90 traceable thermometry throughout. Mapping services for multi-probe uniformity qualification.

Range

–80 °C to 1200 °C

03

Volumetric

Single-channel and multi-channel pipettes, repeat-dispensers, burettes, graduated cylinders, and volumetric flasks. ISO 8655 compliant. Gravimetric method using A&D BM-20 balance, 20 °C controlled environment, and density correction.

Range

0.1 µL – 25 mL

04

Pressure

Barometers, Bourdon gauges, electronic pressure transmitters, manometers, and vacuum gauges. Fluke 2700G reference standard. Gauge, absolute, and differential pressure modes. Leak-test option for pneumatic instruments.

Range

0.1 Pa – 700 bar

05

Electrical

DMMs, oscilloscopes, LCR meters, power analyzers, clamp meters, insulation testers, and pH/conductivity meters. Calibrated using Fluke 5522A multi-product calibrator with 8.5-digit reference DVM. Guard-band methodology applied to all tolerances.

Accuracy

0.001% to 0.1% typical

06

Dimensional

Calipers, micrometers, height gauges, dial indicators, CMM probes, optical comparators, and laser measuring systems. Temperature-stabilized CMM bay, Mitutoyo gauge block reference set to Grade K. ISO/ASME standards applied throughout.

Resolution

0.1 µm typical
