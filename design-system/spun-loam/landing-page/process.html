The Forma Process

Four stages. Every instrument. Every time. No deviations — because a calibration that shortcuts the methodology is not a calibration.

Four Stages to Traceability

From the moment an instrument arrives to the moment the accredited certificate is issued, every step is documented, witnessed, and retained in our quality management system.

  1. 01

    Intake & Assessment

    Each instrument is received, condition-photographed, logged into the Forma LIMS, and assessed against the requested calibration scope. Out-of-tolerance pre-inspection readings are recorded as-found data. No repairs are performed — only calibration. If an instrument cannot be safely calibrated, the client is notified before any measurement is taken.

    Intake Form As-Found Record Scope Confirmation
  2. 02

    Reference Standard Selection

    The calibration method and reference standard are selected from Forma's accredited scope. Every reference standard carries a current NVLAP-accredited calibration certificate with uncertainty budget. The uncertainty ratio between the reference standard and the instrument under test is confirmed to be ≥ 4:1 before measurement proceeds.

    Method Selection Record Reference Certificate 4:1 Ratio Verification
  3. 03

    Measurement Execution

    Measurements are performed per the applicable ISO, ASTM, or OIML standard, or Forma's internally-developed and peer-reviewed SOPs where standards do not exist. Repeatability readings and environmental conditions (temperature, humidity, barometric pressure) are recorded for every calibration. Raw data is retained for ten years in Forma's document control system.

    Raw Data Worksheet Environmental Log Repeatability Data
  4. 04

    Certificate Issuance

    The calibration certificate is generated from measured data — never from nominal values. Certificates include as-found and as-left readings, expanded uncertainty (k=2, 95% confidence), environmental conditions, method references, and technician identification. Certificates are issued only by Forma staff designated as authorized signatories in our quality management system.

    Accredited Certificate Uncertainty Statement As-Left Data

Measurement Uncertainty — Not Just a Footnote

Every Forma certificate includes a full expanded uncertainty statement calculated per JCGM 100:2008 (GUM). This is not a compliance checkbox. Measurement uncertainty is the language your quality system uses to determine whether an instrument actually meets its specification — without it, a calibration result is just a number.

Uncertainty budgets include: reference standard uncertainty, repeatability, resolution, thermal drift, and measurement system bias. Each source is quantified, not estimated.

Guard-band methodology applied to all pass/fail decisions. An instrument reading within tolerance but outside the guard-band is flagged — not passed — because the measurement uncertainty allows for OOT.

Uncertainty ratios are reviewed annually as reference standard uncertainties are updated. If a ratio degrades below 4:1, affected calibrations are re-evaluated before the next cycle.

All uncertainty calculations are available for client review upon request. Forma does not treat measurement uncertainty as proprietary — your quality decision deserves the full picture.
