About Forma

Est. 2003 — Ann Arbor, MI ISO 17025 Accredited Since 2004

Mission

A measurement that cannot be traced is not a measurement. It is a reading.

Forma was founded on a single premise: that analytical laboratories deserve calibration services that hold themselves to the same standards they hold their instruments to. Every result is traceable to NIST. Every uncertainty is calculated, not assumed. Every certificate is signed by a metrologist, not a technician following a checklist. This is not our quality program — it is our operating assumption. It cannot be waived for a tighter turnaround or a lower price point. It is what Forma is.

Twenty-One Years of Traceability

Forma Calibration Services was incorporated in 2003 by Dr. Miriam Kessler, following six years as a staff metrologist at NIST's Physical Measurement Laboratory. The founding intention was narrow: build the calibration laboratory that analytical chemists actually want — technically rigorous, documentarily complete, and staffed by people who understand what a measurement uncertainty statement means in a regulatory context.

NVLAP accreditation was achieved in 2004, eleven months after incorporation. The scope has expanded through seventeen accreditation cycles without a single major nonconformance finding. The Ann Arbor facility was expanded in 2011 to accommodate CMM and thermal mapping services. A satellite on-site service program was established in 2016, now serving clients within a 200-mile radius.

Forma has not pursued acquisition, franchise, or franchise-adjacent growth models. The laboratory is privately held, debt-free, and operates at a scale that allows every calibration to receive the attention of an authorized metrologist before the certificate leaves the building.
