# Brand Name — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The site flows through approximately 7 distinct sections: announcement bar → sticky nav → hero → "How it works" → Privacy → Testimonials → Pricing → CTA strip → Footer. The spatial rhythm is generous — each section commands full viewport height or close to it, creating a slow, confident scroll pace. The dark background unifies everything into one continuous canvas, with sections differentiated by content arrangement rather than background color changes.

---

## Announcement Bar

A slim, full-width accent-colored strip anchors the very top. Single centered text line. It acts as a visual "cap" — the only warm color element that bleeds edge-to-edge at page open. Proportionally it's approximately 5% of the viewport height.

---

## Header / Navigation

Sticky top nav, transparent over the dark background. Two-zone horizontal split:
- **Left:** Logo mark (icon) + wordmark lockup, sitting at roughly 1/6 from left
- **Center:** Five text links spaced with generous gutters — primary navigation items
- **Right:** Two CTA elements — a ghost-style text link and a solid pill button "Primary CTA ›" with an accent border and slight glow

The nav feels airy — lots of negative space between items. The pill CTA has visible rounded corners and a subtle right-arrow character, making it the only hard-edged call to action in the nav.

---

## Hero Section

**Full-viewport asymmetric composition with floating overlapping elements.** This is the most complex layout on the page.

- **Background:** Dark base with large radial gradient blobs — accent colors bleeding in from left and right edges, creating atmospheric depth
- **Left column (~40% width):** Vertically centered text block. Large display type in two lines, a subtitle paragraph, then a single pill CTA button
- **Center (~30%):** A device mockup (card-shaped) floated to the vertical center. It shows a product UI with a glowing animated indicator, a status element, and action buttons. The card has a subtle drop shadow and appears to sit slightly elevated above the background
- **Right column (~30%):** Two or three content transcript cards floating at staggered vertical positions — they overlap the device mockup slightly, creating a layered spatial effect. Cards have semi-transparent dark backgrounds with light text and role labels

The hero has **no hard container edge** — elements bleed organically into the atmospheric background. The floating device + content cards create a depth illusion: foreground device, mid-layer cards, background glow.

---

## "How It Works" Section

**Left-right two-column split, roughly 40/60 proportion.**

- **Left column:** Vertically stacked numbered steps (1, 2, 3). Each step has a bold number in accent color, a thin horizontal rule above it, and a short description below. The numbers are large and use a different color treatment for emphasis. Vertical rhythm is even — equal spacing between each step
- **Right column:** A large product screenshot/UI mockup, styled as a browser window with chrome-style controls. The screenshot fills the right half generously, nearly touching the section edges
- **Top:** Centered section label badge ("HOW IT WORKS" in a small pill/tag) followed by a large centered heading spanning roughly 70% of the viewport width, then a centered subtext paragraph

The section reads top-down: label → headline → two-column content below.

---

## Trust / Assurance Section

**Full-width center-focused layout with an illustration above, text below.**

- **Top half:** A large illustrative graphic, glowing with accent color, centered and roughly 50% of viewport height. It feels icon-like but hero-scale
- **Below the illustration:** Centered section badge pill, then a large two-line heading centered on the page
- **Below the heading:** A centered paragraph, constrained to roughly 60% of viewport width
- **Bottom:** Three equal-width cards in a horizontal row. Each card has a dark semi-transparent background, a square icon top-left, and a sentence with **bold lead words**. The three cards sit in a clean 3-column grid with even gutters

The illustration creates strong visual hierarchy before the grid.

---

## Testimonials Section

**Asymmetric two-column layout: media left, stacked cards right.**

- **Left (~50%):** A large portrait media card with rounded corners, with an overlaid name/title caption at the bottom and a circular play button centered over the image
- **Right (~50%):** Two testimonial quote cards stacked vertically with a gap. Each card has a dark semi-transparent background, large accent-colored quotation marks at top-left, paragraph body text, and a muted attribution line (name + title) at the bottom

Above both columns: centered "TESTIMONIALS" badge + section heading.

The left image and right cards are roughly equal height combined — the image is taller than one card but shorter than both stacked, so there's a satisfying optical balance.

---

## Pricing Section

**Four equal-column card grid.**

- Each plan card has a dark card background with rounded corners and generous internal padding
- Cards share identical structure: plan name → price + currency/cadence → descriptor line → CTA pill button → feature list with checkmarks
- The **recommended** card has a subtle border/glow treatment and a "RECOMMENDED" tag badge above it, making it slightly taller and visually elevated above its siblings
- The **enterprise** card uses an accent color variant for its title
- Four cards sit in a single horizontal row with equal gutters — no card is wider than another except the slight vertical extension of the recommended card

Above: section badge + centered heading.

---

## CTA Strip

**Full-bleed card inset — not a section, but a contained card near the bottom.**

A large rounded-corner card spanning most of the viewport width (with visible margins on sides) with an atmospheric background (gradient blobs on dark base). Contents are fully centered vertically and horizontally:
- Logo icon mark at top
- Two-line large display heading
- Single pill CTA button centered

This creates a "contained full-bleed" feel — the card has breathing room from the page edges, suggesting it's a distinct call-to-action unit rather than a true section.

---

## Footer

**Minimal two-zone horizontal layout.**

- **Left:** Logo mark + wordmark lockup
- **Right:** Three text links spaced with generous gaps, right-aligned

The footer sits flush below the CTA card with ample vertical padding. No social icons, no multi-column link hierarchy — deliberately spare.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

- **Atmospheric layering:** The dark background with radial gradient blobs creates a consistent sense of atmospheric depth across every section. Elements float on this field rather than sitting in containers
- **Full-viewport sections:** Most sections occupy close to 100vh, creating a cinematic scroll experience
- **Floating / overlapping cards:** The hero uses overlapping content cards over the device mockup — depth through z-axis layering rather than flat placement
- **Pill/badge labels:** Every section opens with a small uppercase pill label acting as a section identifier before the heading. These are consistently positioned center-top of each section
- **Sticky nav throughout:** The nav persists at the top through all sections, always visible against the dark background
- **Asymmetric illustration anchoring:** The hero and assurance sections use large central illustrations as focal anchors before supporting content radiates outward
