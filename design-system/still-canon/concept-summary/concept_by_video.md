## Scroll Opening

On page load, the viewport is entirely occupied by the full-bleed video background before any UI chrome resolves. The navigation bar does not flash in — it materializes at approximately 400ms, sliding down from a zero-height clip without opacity change, as if a heavy lid is being raised. The wordmark and primary navigation links cross-fade in at 600ms, staggered left to right at 80ms intervals. The hero text block fades in last, at 900ms, centered and still, giving the video a full second to establish its world before language arrives. There is no bounce, no spring — only a single deliberate deceleration curve on every element.

## Hero Experience

The hero is a video background candidate. The video does not announce itself; the brand text simply floats above it. The subject is a craftsperson's hands working a piece of coated canvas on a workshop table — morning side-light, close but not macro, showing material texture without becoming a product shot. The camera holds nearly still, with only a slow, barely perceptible drift downward of approximately two millimeters per second, as though the lens is settling into the scene. The video should read as a window, not as a presentation. Minimal UI controls — a muted play/pause and a discreet volume toggle — sit at the lower edge of the frame, appearing only after the initial reveal sequence completes, so the first impression is unbroken atmospheric depth.

## Section Transitions

Transitions between sections are not animated. The scroll is vertical and uninterrupted — no parallax displacement, no section-to-section crossfade, no sticky pinning. The visual boundary between the dark navigation bar and the warm off-white content area below it is a hard horizontal edge, and that hardness is the transition. The only concession to motion at the boundary between the category header and the product grid is the thin horizontal rule, which does not animate but acts as a visual punctuation mark that the eye crosses cleanly. Restraint here is the principle: the sections do not perform their separateness; they simply are separate.

## Content Rhythm

Individual product cells in the grid enter the viewport with a fade-in and a short upward translate — approximately 12px over 320ms, eased out. Rows animate as a unit rather than as individual staggered items: the first row of visible products resolves together, then the next row together as it enters the frame. This preserves the grid's structural integrity during scroll and avoids the visual noise of cascade-staggered individual cards. Editorial text sections — the category headline, the supporting paragraph — resolve as a single block, fading in over 280ms with no translate. The hierarchy of the type does the work; motion does not need to amplify it.

## Footer Approach

The footer does not slide in or crossfade. It is simply revealed as the user scrolls past the last content row, its hard top edge entering from the bottom of the viewport in the same motion as any other scroll event. The multi-column link layout is static on arrival. The only motion in the footer is a subtle 200ms opacity transition when the lower band bearing the copyright line becomes visible. This is the closing of the page: quiet, resolved, without ceremony.

## Motion Principles

The timing philosophy is languid but not slow. Durations sit in the 280–400ms range for reveals, and 600–900ms for the initial page load sequence. Easing is exclusively ease-out or custom deceleration curves — nothing springs, nothing overshoots. The principle is that every animated element is arriving, never departing; departure is instantaneous (cuts and hard edges) and arrival is weighted. This mirrors the physical vocabulary of the aesthetic register: objects of quality do not snap into your hand, they settle.

## Video Placements

**Placement 1 — Hero**
- Section role: Full-viewport hero background
- Suggested asset filename: hero-bg.mp4
- What the video shows: A craftsperson's hands pressing and smoothing material on a wooden surface. The camera is positioned low and close, catching the texture of the material and the grain of the work surface. Motion quality is slow drift — camera barely moving, approximately 1–2mm downward per second. Morning side-light from the left. The hands remain partially out of frame; the material surface and the act of working it are the subject.
- Duration: 10s loop, with a clean cut point at the end where the movement can repeat without obvious discontinuity
- Why video: A still image can communicate quality, but only video communicates the deliberateness of the gesture — the weight placed on the material, the pace of working it. A 10s loop at this pace creates an ambient sense of craft and care that no photograph can sustain across a user's dwell time on the hero.

**Placement 2 — Services/Practice Areas**
- Section role: Editorial two-column section introducing the firm's areas of practice
- Suggested asset filename: services-ambient.mp4
- What the video shows: A single document or book resting on a stone or marble surface. The camera is static. A shaft of natural light moves slowly across the surface over the duration of the loop, revealing texture in sequence — darker at the start, catching the light at mid-clip, settling into a warm tone at the end. No human presence. No movement of the object.
- Duration: 5s loop — the light progression is the only event
- Why video: A still photograph captures one reading of a document's surface. A 5s ambient video where light travels across it communicates depth and the sense that these materials carry weight — which is precisely the register for legal practice area descriptions.

**Placement 3 — NRI Services / Trust Section**
- Section role: Dedicated section addressing Non-Resident Indian clients, emphasizing remote representation and credibility
- Suggested asset filename: nri-trust-ambient.mp4
- What the video shows: Interior of a formal workspace or reading room — bookshelves or filing systems visible in the background, shallow depth of field. The camera is on a very slow horizontal drift, moving left to right at approximately 3px per second. Nothing is in motion except the camera. The light is warm and directional, consistent with a north-facing window. The space communicates institutional permanence without displaying any identifying information.
- Duration: 10s loop, with a crossfade cut at the seam
- Why video: This section carries the interpretive weight of trust-building — it is where the service transitions from capability to credibility. A slowly drifting video of a stable, ordered professional interior is an invitation to trust, which is the correct register for a section asking an overseas client to rely on an advocate they have not yet met in person.
