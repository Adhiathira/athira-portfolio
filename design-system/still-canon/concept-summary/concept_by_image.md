## Visual Identity

The site's color palette is based on deep forest greens, warm off-whites, and charcoal grays for a muted, natural feel. A typeface pairing of a classic serif (Newsreader) for body copy and a proprietary sans-serif (Goyard) for headings imparts a feeling of vintage luxury, limited by the display font's lack of web accessibility. Motion is subtle, with slow transitions and a full-bleed looping background video.

## Hero Section

The hero uses a full-bleed looping video background that dominates the viewport. Centered white text overlays the video, creating contrast between the brand's name and the title text. Two minimalistic buttons with the options to play, pause, and increase the video's volume are set at the bottom of the screen. The overall design has a clean feel with minimal elements competing for attention.

## Content Sections

A grid layout is employed, arranging three product images per row, with each item displayed on a background of a warm, off-white color. Following this are additional sections showcasing two-column divisions featuring a product image alongside descriptive text. A newsletter sign-up is contained within a rectangular section, accompanied by another image on the right. The page employs the Newsreader font for body text and a proprietary font, Goyard, for headings across each section.

## Footer Section

The footer utilizes a forest-green color scheme. It features a multi-column layout organizing links into distinct categories. Social media icons are presented as outlined circular glyphs. A copyright disclaimer is then added to the page where the bottom footer transitions into a lighter shade of green.

## Design Principles

The design operates like a well-made leather object: the color is drawn from a single hide — that limited palette is not a constraint but the material itself. The serif and sans-serif typefaces sit together the way waxed thread sits against canvas, each serving a different grain of information. Animations do not decorate; they are the weight of the page, the resistance one feels opening a quality clasp. Full-bleed media is the lining — invisible until you are inside it, then total. The grid is the underlying stitching pattern: invisible in the finished surface, responsible for everything that holds.

## Distinctive Qualities

The use of a full-bleed video hero with minimal controls overlaid creates a cinematic, restrained impression. The limited color palette and high-contrast typography establish a clear visual hierarchy.
