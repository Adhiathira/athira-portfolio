# Brand Name — Layout Brief

---

## Overall Page Structure

The page is built on a **deep black canvas** (#000 or near-black) with a clear rhythmic alternation between full-bleed dark sections and inset "card" panels that feel lighter and more contained. There are roughly **8–10 distinct content sections** flowing vertically, each with generous breathing room between them. The spatial rhythm is slow and editorial — lots of negative space, large type, and deliberate pacing that feels more like a luxury brand than a SaaS product. The page culminates in a white rounded-rectangle footer that floats against the black background.

---

## 1. Header / Navigation

A **slim, full-width top bar** pinned to the viewport top. It sits on the black background with no visible border or separator — it bleeds into the page content below.

- **Left:** Wordmark with a small "By Company" sub-label directly below it — stacked, tight, small
- **Center:** Horizontal nav links ("Nav Item 1 ▾", "Nav Item 2", "Nav Item 3", "Nav Item 4", "Nav Item 5") — evenly spaced, modest weight, no underlines
- **Right:** "Sign in" text link + a solid accent-color pill button ("Primary CTA") — the accent CTA is the only chromatic color in an otherwise monochrome bar

The nav has very little height — it's a ribbon, not a slab. Nothing competes with the hero below it.

---

## 2. Hero Section

**Full-viewport, full-bleed dark.** The hero is a massive typographic statement — no imagery, no product screenshot, just oversized display type dominating the space.

- The headline runs across nearly the full width in two lines, with extremely large type (~140–160px feel)
- **Color split within the headline itself:** The first phrase renders in white; the rest fades to a medium grey — a within-line contrast trick that creates emphasis without layout change
- Small sparkle/star glyphs (✦) float above and to the left of the headline, providing a subtle decorative note
- Below the headline there is a large empty band of black — the hero intentionally withholds a CTA at this stage, letting the type breathe

The hero is purely typographic. No columns, no floating product images. The hierarchy is entirely through scale and color contrast within the text itself.

---

## 3. Section 2 — Hero Continuation / Interactive Demo

This scrolls directly beneath the hero on the same black background. It reveals:

- A text input bar — a live-typing simulation — spanning roughly 60% of the width on the left
- A dark pill button ("Action Button") to the right of the input
- Below and centered: a large circular play button with "Play teaser video" label underneath — this floats over the section transition

A custom illustrated **hand cursor icon** appears large in the lower-right corner, pointing at the interactive elements — a playful directional device.

This section functions as an **interactive hero extension**, still full-bleed black, but it introduces an inset light-grey rounded-rectangle that bleeds in from the bottom — a transitional device hinting at the next section.

---

## 4. Section 3 — Split Two-Column: Key Differentiator Feature

**Asymmetric two-column layout** on a black background:

- **Left (~40% width):** Large headline text stacked vertically, followed by two action controls: a template picker dropdown and an accent-color "Primary CTA" button, then a small "Secondary CTA" text link below
- **Right (~55% width):** A rounded-rectangle video/preview card — a person's face fills this card with an overlaid conversational UI bubble and a white pill "Action Button" centered on the face

The two columns don't align to a rigid grid — the left column is more compact and sits slightly higher; the right card is taller and crops at top and bottom. This creates a slight **vertical offset / stagger** between the two sides.

---

## 5. Section 4 — Full-Width Feature: Feature A

Back to full-bleed black, but now with a **large inset white rounded-rectangle card** on the left occupying roughly 50% width. Inside it sits a form UI with an input and action button. This card is tall and clean — lots of internal whitespace.

To the right of this card, positioned mid-column on the black background:
- Large display headline describing Feature A
- Again using the same **colour-split headline technique** — primary words white, secondary words grey
- An accent-color "Primary CTA" below the headline

Spatial rhythm here: card left, text right. The card and text don't vertically align — the text sits at mid-height relative to the card, creating a floating relationship rather than a locked grid alignment.

---

## 6. Section 5 — Feature B (Global Reach)

A **reverse of the previous section** — text on the left, visual on the right:

- Left: Large display headline describing Feature B with the same white-to-grey split across lines + an accent-color "Primary CTA" pill button
- Right: A large vivid **accent-color rounded-rectangle panel** containing a UI example — the accent color here makes this the most visually striking panel on the page

Both elements appear to float on the black background. The accent panel is taller than the text column, reinforcing that the imagery is the dominant element on this side.

---

## 7. Section 6 — Feature C (Embed / Integration)

Continues the alternating pattern — card left, text right:

- Left: A dark grey rounded-rectangle showing a code snippet — very readable, syntax-highlighted
- Right: Display headline describing Feature C — white/grey split — and an accent-color CTA

Below this, still on the same black band, a second sub-section shows:
- Left: A light-grey card showing format options as small icon tiles in a row
- Right: Same headline / CTA relationship

These two "card + text" pairs are stacked vertically within the same section, with minimal gap between them — they read as a single feature block with two illustrative examples.

---

## 8. Section 7 — Social Proof / Testimonials

A **large asymmetric two-column layout** on black:

- **Left (~40%):** Customer name headline in the same white/grey display type — large, editorial
- Below the headline: numbered pagination dots (1 2 3 4 5) indicating a carousel of case studies

- **Right (~55%):** A dark rounded-rectangle testimonial card containing a long quote in medium-weight white text, followed by a byline and a live accent-color link

Below this pair, a full-width section introduces a product dashboard screenshot — a macOS window frame housing the product editor interface — this screenshot is inset into a **contrasting-color full-bleed background** (the first major background color change in the entire page). The rounded corners on the screenshot match the panel language used throughout.

---

## 9. Section 8 — "Collect Deeper Data"

Back to pure black background. A **full-width type treatment** — extremely large display text centered horizontally, spanning nearly edge to edge. This is a typographic break, a section divider expressed through scale rather than a line or separator.

A circular floating video pip (a person speaking, in a small circle portrait format) overlaps from the right side — it sits partially outside the type block, floating as an overlay.

---

## 10. Footer

The footer is a **large rounded-rectangle white card** that floats on a black background — matching the inset card language used for hero sections earlier, but now as an ending flourish.

**Internal layout:**
- **Far left:** Logo lockup (icon mark in a light rounded square, with wordmark below) — vertically centered in the footer
- **Right of logo:** Four link columns in a horizontal row — "Column 1", "Column 2", "Column 3", "Column 4" — each with 4–8 text links stacked below the column header
- **Bottom center of the card:** "Company / © [Year]" — small, centered, separated from the columns by generous white space

The white card has very generous internal padding — it doesn't feel dense. The footer itself is the largest single "inset" card on the page.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Inset floating cards:** Throughout the page, content panels are contained inside rounded rectangles that sit against the black background — they never bleed full-edge. This creates a consistent sense of "modules floating in space" rather than sections stacked wall-to-wall.

**Background color as section signal:** The overwhelming majority of the page is black. The one deviation — a contrasting background for the dashboard screenshot — signals a major product moment.

**Within-headline colour splitting:** Headlines consistently use white for the emphasized phrase and grey for the secondary phrase, all within the same type block. This does typographic work that layout normally does, letting sections remain visually unified while maintaining hierarchy.

**Stagger and vertical offset:** Left and right columns in two-column sections are rarely top-aligned. Cards and text float at different vertical positions, giving the layout an organic, asymmetric quality.

**Directional flourishes:** The custom hand cursor, sparkle icons, and circular pip portraits are small floating elements that break the grid intentionally — they act as the "handcrafted" notes in an otherwise very clean layout.

**Spatial rhythm:** The page breathes heavily. Sections are separated by large bands of empty black. The scroll experience is slow and deliberate — closer to an editorial magazine than a feature-dense SaaS page.
