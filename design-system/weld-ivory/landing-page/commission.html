Commission

Begin a conversation

We take commissions for poetry pamphlets, artists' books, fine stationery, wedding invitations, and limited-edition documents. The work begins with conversation — tell us what you have in mind.

How we work

  1. Conversation

    We discuss the work — what it is, what you need, what constraints exist.

  2. Proposal

    We prepare a detailed proposal: paper selection, typefaces, printing method, edition size, cost, and timeline.

  3. Making

    The work is printed. You receive proofs at each stage if wanted.

  4. Delivery

    Copies shipped from Edinburgh, signed by the printer. Colophon included in every edition.

Typical timeline

6–14 weeks

From agreement to delivery. Rush work is not possible.

Current availability

3 slots in 2024

We work with a small number of commissions each year.
