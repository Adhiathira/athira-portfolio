The Process

Type set by hand.
Ink mixed by eye.

Letterpress printing demands patience. Each forme is set one character at a time from a compositor's case of handcast metal type. Ink is rolled, paper dampened, impression adjusted until the bite is right — just enough to leave a felt memory in the sheet.

We work in small editions. Twenty copies is a large run. Most books leave the press in editions of eight to twelve.
