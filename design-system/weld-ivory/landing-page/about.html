The Studio

Plinth Press

Edinburgh letterpress studio, founded 2009.

→ assets/studio-interior.jpeg

A single press. A small number of books each year.

Plinth Press is a one-printer letterpress studio in the Leith district of Edinburgh. We operate a Vandercook Universal III proofing press, four cases of handset metal type, and a small collection of photopolymer equipment for image work.

We print under one constraint: every page of every book we make must be an object worth keeping. If a commission or edition doesn't meet that, we don't take it or print it.

The press opened in 2009. In that time we have printed thirty-one titles, taken forty-two commissions, and used approximately eight kilometres of paper.

Equipment

The tools

  • Vandercook Universal III

    Proofing press, cylinder, 1958. Restored 2011. Used for all production printing.

  • Metal type collection

    Bembo 12pt, Caslon 10pt and 14pt, Gill Sans 10pt, Plantin 12pt. All handcast.

  • Photopolymer system

    Used for image reproduction and custom typefaces. Plates processed in-house.

  • Dampening cabinet

    Cotton and mould-made papers dampened before printing for deeper impression.

Materials

Papers we work with

We select paper for each commission and edition. These are stocks we return to.

Zerkall Mould-Made

250gsm and 300gsm. German, rag content, beautiful impression. Our first choice for fine books.

Fabriano Artistico

300gsm hot and cold press. Italian cotton. Used for photographic and image-led work.

Hahnemühle Nostalgie

190gsm. German, naturally aged tone. Excellent for literary and archival work.

Rives BFK

250gsm, French cotton. The cleanest white in the studio. Used for high-contrast work.

Crane's Lettra

220gsm. American cotton, outstanding surface for deep impression. For stationery work.

Bespoke selection

For commissions, we can source almost any sheet. If you have a preference, name it.
