# Weld Ivory — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A fashion model's eponymous beauty product brand. The site moves between two registers — editorial storytelling and product commerce — and the design system bridges these through a consistent typographic authority and a distinctive structural signature: an organic drip-shaped section divider that appears at the bottom of every light informational section, causing the content to visually "melt" into the dark transitional zones below. The brand operates at the intersection of personal voice and product category. The site does not read as a generic beauty e-commerce platform — it reads as the extension of a specific person's aesthetic world, where the product object (a 3D rendered bottle) navigates the page as a visual protagonist throughout the scroll journey.

## Color Architecture

Near-white (#ffffff) is the base surface for all informational and product content sections. Near-black (#1d1d1f) handles body text throughout. Heading text is rendered in a restrained gray (#6e6e73) — not the darkest element on the page — which gives headings a sculptural quality rather than a declarative one. The page alternates deliberately between the light (#ffffff) informational register and dark background sections that serve as transitional and typographic emphasis zones. A single vivid accent color (Apple blue #0071e3) is reserved for primary CTA buttons — appearing as the most chromatically active element in an otherwise near-achromatic system. The color system is minimal in declaration but high-contrast in effect, using the light/dark alternation as the primary pacing device.

## Typography System

The typeface pairing is a study in contrasting material weights. EB Garamond (italic 500) handles all headings and display-scale text — carrying its letters like fine pressed paper, each character slightly compressed in the italic cut, with the open tracking (0.06em letter-spacing) adding an airy suspension between glyphs. The physical sensation this evokes is something between typewriter ribbon and engraved stationery: text that has been pressed into a surface rather than projected from it. Manrope handles body copy, navigation, and all interface labels — a geometric sans-serif that is warm without being casual, its rounded forms softening the precision of the Apple-derived structural system. The CTA text, set in Manrope, reads as an invitation rather than a command. Overpass Mono provides a tertiary register for technical data or accent labels — its monospaced rhythm creates a second visual tempo beneath the body text. The scale has been pushed upward by 15% from the source system: headings arrive with presence, not timidity.

## Spatial System

The page operates with deliberate generosity. The hero carries approximately 88px of padding in all directions — room for the composition to breathe before content begins. Section-level padding in the primary areas reaches nearly 210px at the bottom edge, so each content zone ends with a long exhalation before the next section arrives. The grid gap between columns runs at 47px — substantial enough that left and right columns feel like separate objects placed in proximity rather than halves of a divided rectangle. CTAs use a 58px horizontal padding and a 23px border-radius — the pill shape is derived from the spacing token, not imposed on it.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

Photography is lifestyle-focused, compositionally aware, and uses negative space to balance against text columns. The 3D product object as scroll actor — a rendered beauty product — appears as a continuous thread from the hero through subsequent sections, alternately occupying the foreground and background of split-media compositions. The product is the recurring protagonist; the photography grounds it in a livable world.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar is present throughout the scroll. Over the hero section it is transparent. The nav carries pronounced letter spacing (0.10em) — each character-gap a beat of whitespace, the nav items reading as measured, unhurried typography rather than quick signage. A single CTA button (the accent color) anchors the far right. Nav links are set in Manrope at 12px, inheriting the body font system.

## Overall Design Character

Weld Ivory is an editorial beauty system built on white space and material type. The core tension is between Apple-derived structural precision — the grid, the spacing tokens, the near-achromatic palette — and the warmth injected by EB Garamond's italic editorial presence and Manrope's geometric softness. Motion is languid (0.45s base duration), giving the page a breathing pace that refuses urgency. The letter-spaced headings, the generous section padding, and the tightened 1149px container all signal a system designed to be read rather than scanned.
