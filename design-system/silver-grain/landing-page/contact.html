Fellowship Application

Access by nomination.

Fourteen annual fellowships allow working photographers, darkroom printers, and photographic conservators direct access to our coating room and chemistry archive. There are no application fees. There is a waiting list.

What fellowship provides.

Fellowship holders may visit the coating room and chemistry preparation area during production runs. You may observe every stage of the process and request explanations of any step. We will answer honestly.

Fellows may request custom paper grades outside our standard three. Custom grades require a minimum order of 200 sheets and at least six months lead time. We do not guarantee custom formulations will succeed.

Fellows receive one batch of each standard paper grade per year at no charge. All other materials are available at cost price, not retail price.
