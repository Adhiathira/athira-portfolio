Chemistry

The complete darkroom from one address.

Film

D-76 Formula

The Palacios formulation of D-76 uses a phenidone concentration not published in standard references. The result in Tri-X and HP5 produces a grain structure that is cellular rather than clumped at 100% magnification. Available in 1L concentrate.

Paper

LV-30 Developer

A slow-developing paper formula designed for controlled contrast building in the tray. Working time 90–120 seconds at 20°C. Produces a warm-black tone on all three Loam & Silver grades. Available in 1L concentrate.

Archival

Archival Fixer

Sodium thiosulfate plain fixer, deliberately slow-clearing. Full fixation of FB paper requires 6 minutes at 20°C working strength. This is not a deficiency — a slower-clearing fixer produces more complete fixation and reduces the wash time required for archival permanence.
