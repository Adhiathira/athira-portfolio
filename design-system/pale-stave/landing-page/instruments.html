The Instruments

Four tools.
One complete record.

Each Calliper instrument is designed around a specific documentation act — Survey, Condition, Archive, Report. Together they produce a record that is structured enough to query and precise enough to cite.

01

Survey

Spatial measurement and annotation — the foundational act of any documentation project.

Survey captures dimension strings, reference datums, and field sketches linked to precise coordinates and timestamps. Entry is optimized for a tablet held with one hand: dimension fields accept tape measure readings directly, and reference codes are assigned before recording begins so records from different visits can be compared without reconciliation.

Output: dimensioned floor plan overlay, coordinate-tagged entries, datum record.

02

Condition

Material state recording with a controlled vocabulary that holds across surveyors and visits.

Condition documents surface finish, substrate state, failure modes, and environmental exposure. The vocabulary is structured — not free text — so condition records from different surveyors on different projects are comparable. Dropdowns are ordered by frequency of use at a conservation site, not alphabetically.

Output: condition schedule, failure mode register, material state timeline.

03

Archive

Historical record linkage — the instrument that turns a survey into a cumulative building record.

Archive attaches archival documents, prior survey records, and primary sources to any element by reference code. Each attachment carries a provenance note, a date, and a source type. Records accumulate: the building's documentation history grows with each visit, and every entry can trace its relationship to prior records.

Output: provenance chain per element, source register, linked document archive.

04

Report

Structured output for submission — condition assessments, survey summaries, and maintenance schedules generated from field entries.

Report outputs are structured for conservation practice, not general project management. Condition assessment format follows standard heritage documentation conventions. Survey summary includes dimensioned drawings and annotated photographs. Maintenance schedule is drawn from condition records and linked to element reference codes.

Output: condition assessment PDF, survey summary, maintenance schedule, submission package.
