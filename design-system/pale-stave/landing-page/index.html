Field Documentation System

Measure what endures.

Calliper gives architects and conservators a precise system for recording spatial conditions in the field — dimensions, material states, anomalies, and histories — before anything changes.

Explore Instruments See the Method

The Problem

Field conditions change.
Records should not.

Most documentation systems were built for the office, not the site. Calliper is built for the moment when you are standing in front of a wall with a tape measure and a question you cannot answer later from a photograph.

It captures dimension, material, condition, and date in a single entry — structured enough to query, flexible enough to be completed in the field, and precise enough to be cited in a conservation report ten years later.

Built for the Field

The record is as close as your hand.

Calliper runs offline. No connection required on site. Measurements captured on paper or voice can be transferred via the Calliper field app before you leave the building — no office processing step.

The entry form is designed for a tablet held with one hand. Every field is reachable with the thumb you have free. Controlled vocabulary dropdowns are ordered by frequency of use at a conservation site, not alphabetically.

Read the Method

The Method

Observation. Record. Link. Report.

Calliper is organized around four acts of documentation — not features. Each act has a defined output, so records taken by different surveyors on different visits can be assembled without reconciliation.

I.

Observe

Walk the space. Note what requires documentation. Assign reference codes to elements before recording begins — this is the practice that separates a survey from a set of photographs.

II.

Record

Enter dimensions, conditions, and anomalies by element reference code. Voice input and paper transfer are both supported. The record is timestamped and geolocated on save.

III.

Link

Attach prior surveys, archival drawings, and material specifications to each element. The record accumulates over the life of the building — each visit adds to a continuous thread.

IV.

Report

Generate condition assessments, maintenance schedules, and submission-ready documentation from your field entries — structured for conservation practice, not general project management.
