# Pale Stave — Concept by Image

## Visual Identity

The palette is built on two anchors: a pure white (`#ffffff`) that saturates the page background, and a near-charcoal (`#363636`) that grounds all body text in legibility without the absolute pressure of black — a soft dark, like graphite on uncoated stock. Typography is split between Plus Jakarta Sans (a geometric humanist sans) at functional scales and Lora (a classical editorial serif with roots in letterpress proportions) for display moments — creating a deliberate friction between system clarity and archival warmth. This is the same underlying grammar as a well-made form: the ruled lines hold everything in place, but the content carries temperature. Motion is languid and considered: all transition durations are scaled 1.5× beyond their source counterparts, so interactions arrive unhurried — the UI breathes at the pace of someone reading, not tapping.

## Hero Section

The hero is conceived as a full-viewport-width composition that bleeds edge to edge. No overlay gradient, no CTA pressure in the first frame. Type placement anchors at the lower-left quadrant — a single Lora serif label, italic if the content warrants it, set against the white field with nothing competing for attention. The absence of animation in the hero is intentional: the design does not announce itself with movement. It places an image and waits.

## Material Qualities

The design evokes the tactile register of ruled stationery — the kind printed on cotton-weave stock that accepts ink with a slight tooth, where the line between printed rule and written mark feels collaborative rather than mechanical. The `#ffffff` background is not the white of a screen default but of a surface that has been chosen for its neutrality: off-white would carry warmth, cream would carry age; this white is the absence of those signals. The IBM Plex Mono uppercase navigation sits on the page the way a registration mark sits on a proof sheet — functional, precise, not decorative. Letter-spacing at 0.08em distributes each character across the field with the measured breath of a typesetter setting metal type by hand.

## Content Sections

Content sections alternate between full-bleed image fields and structured text columns — the same unbroken white background carrying both without visual interruption. Section margins are generous (269px vertical rhythm from the spacing system), creating the sense that each section has been placed on the page deliberately rather than stacked in sequence. The grid inherits griting-ellipsus's full-bleed editorial structure but with gaps expanded 1.4× — more air between columns, wider gutters, more silence around each element. Photography is edge-to-edge or structured in the 60/40 editorial split; no contained thumbnails, no card borders, no box shadows.

## Footer Section

The footer continues the white field — no dark terminus, no color break. The close of the page is signaled by the content ending, not by a visual boundary. Link groups use the same Plus Jakarta Sans scale as the body; hierarchy emerges from spacing and case treatment rather than size or weight. The footer reads as a closing page of a document rather than a navigational utility.

## Design Principles

- **Typographic binary with material memory:** Every decision routes through either Plus Jakarta Sans (clarity, system) or Lora (warmth, depth) — the serif carries the memory of physical type, the sans carries the present moment.
- **White as a chosen surface:** The `#ffffff` background is an active choice, not a default — it is the white of a technical drawing page, neutral and receptive rather than expressive.
- **Languid timing as editorial pacing:** All interaction durations scaled 1.5× create a site that moves at reading speed, not tapping speed — the pacing is closer to turning a page than clicking a button.
- **Letter spacing as spatial typography:** The 0.08em tracking on nav links and 0.06em heading tracking treat letter spacing not as decoration but as a structural decision about how words occupy horizontal space.
- **Opacity softness on hover:** All hover states reduce to 0.75 opacity rather than inverting or changing color — a muting rather than a replacement, preserving legibility while signaling responsiveness.

## Distinctive Qualities

The most unusual pairing in this system is the IBM Plex Mono uppercase nav (tracking 0.08em) against Plus Jakarta Sans body text and Lora editorial headings — three distinct type registers occupying the same page without hierarchy confusion, because each is strictly assigned to its domain. The monospaced nav reads as infrastructure; the humanist sans reads as prose; the serif reads as authority.

The 1.5× duration scaling is perceptible but not conspicuous: hover states at 0.3s (rather than the typical 0.2s), page transitions at 0.9s, the ticker scrolling at 38s rather than 25s. None of these crossings the threshold into slowness — they sit in the register of deliberateness rather than delay.
