# Art Bruno — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

Art Bruno presents itself as a European-register interior design and architecture studio operating at the intersection of spatial precision and editorial restraint. The site does not announce its services — it demonstrates them. Every page functions as a curated exhibition: the work is the argument, the layout is the frame, and the interface recedes to the degree that the photography can speak without competition. The underlying communication is that the studio's understanding of space extends to the screen itself.

## Color Architecture

The palette is a studied monochrome. White and near-white carry the dominant field, creating a gallery-blank ground against which the photography delivers all warmth, texture, and color. Near-black and dark gray provide mass — in footer zones, in typographic anchors, and as scrims behind full-bleed video where text must remain legible over complex visual material. There are no accent colors. The deliberate refusal of a brand hue signals that the studio considers color a material choice belonging to each project, not a marketing device belonging to the studio's identity. The color system's only statement is its silence.

## Typography System

The type hierarchy is built on a single-family sans-serif system — assertive at display scale, clean and compact at body scale. The primary headline font carries weight and geometric structure that reads as architectural rather than editorial: its proportions suggest a draftsman's precision rather than a publisher's warmth. All-caps labeling appears at the category and navigation level, providing an additional tier of differentiation without requiring a second typeface. The system maintains hierarchy through scale, weight, and case alone — no serifs, no script elements, no ornamental disruption. This typographic economy is itself a design argument: the studio's voice is defined by what it does not add.

## Spatial System

The spatial logic is generous and architectural. Content sections breathe with substantial vertical margins, creating the sense that each section occupies its own discrete plane on the page rather than flowing continuously from one to the next. The grid is a flexible multi-column structure for alignment, but the section layouts are consistently composition-driven rather than modular — elements are placed for visual balance, not mechanical repetition. The homepage hero deploys a collage-like arrangement of cards at different scales, creating a curated window onto multiple projects simultaneously. Within sections, spacing is managed with the same precision as the architectural plans the studio likely produces for clients.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

The photography is the site's primary medium of communication. Interior spaces are shot with controlled, soft lighting — long exposures that smooth textures, careful compositions that reveal spatial depth without distortion, and art direction that places primary visual interest away from text-safe zones. Images function simultaneously as decoration, portfolio, and proof of concept: the quality of the photography is a proxy for the quality of the work. Full-bleed video sections take this further, allowing a space to move — subtle slow-motion or timelapse light shifts that reveal the temporal quality of the architecture, a dimension that still photography cannot capture. The editorial voice throughout is confident and restrained: no captions explain the imagery, trusting the viewer to understand.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar is a transparency-first system. On pages with full-bleed media heroes, the nav sits transparently over the imagery, holding logo and links in white against the photograph without asserting a background. On scroll, it transitions to a solid light field with a hairline bottom border — the minimal change required to maintain legibility as the hero recedes behind. This behavior treats the navigation as a contextual layer rather than a permanent fixture: present when needed, invisible when not. The navigation structure is restrained: a central cluster of primary links and a right-aligned secondary group, providing directional options without fragmentation.

## Overall Design Character

Art Bruno is a monochromatic, composition-driven portfolio site where the design system disappears in service of the work. Its intelligence lies in what it withholds: no accent colors, no decorative borders, no visual noise between the user and the photography. The asymmetric compositions, the full-bleed video heroes with precision scrims, the diegetic hotspot showcase, and the horizontal gallery all serve a single goal — to present interior space as the primary subject. The design argument is architectural: this studio thinks spatially on screen as it does in the rooms it builds.
