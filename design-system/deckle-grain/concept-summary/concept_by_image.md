This design system is built around a logic of **declarative impact over managed transition**. The dominant organizational principle is percussive density — sections arrive as hard cuts, not dissolves. Each boundary is a structural event rather than a visual handoff. The cool off-white surface (#f2f2f2) against pure black type creates a temperature that reads as technical and precise, a substrate that holds information the way matte-coated paper holds ink — cleanly, without bleed or warmth, with a fidelity that demands attention rather than invites comfort.

---

## Section Design Patterns

Three structural patterns define the visual grammar:

1. **Full-bleed media overlay** — hero section; photography or video consumes the viewport with UI elements placed compositionally within the image, not adjacent to it
2. **Equal-column card grid** — mid-page editorial panels; minimal gutters creating visual fusion, panels reading as a panoramic band
3. **Multi-column link taxonomy** — footer navigation; clean horizontal taxonomy at reduced type scale

---

## Typography Architecture

The type system pairs **Figtree** (rounded geometric sans-serif, headings) with **Nunito** (rounded humanist sans, body and UI). The pairing achieves productive harmony, but its character is best understood as a difference in material weight. Figtree at display sizes — 48px and above — carries a density that pushes into the off-white background the way a letterpress block pushes into dampened paper: not a deposited mark but an impressed one. The strokes have mass; the rounded terminals feel machined rather than drawn, like the radiused edges of precision tooling rather than the soft closures of a calligraphic hand. Where Figtree impresses, Nunito rests on the surface. Its body-weight strokes are lighter, the forms more elastic — the difference in reading experience is like moving from a tool steel component to a anodized housing: same family, different temper.

The letter-spacing injection on headings (0.06em) is a material addition: it adds the quality of text set with deliberate interval, of characters given room to be read individually before they form words. In large display type, 0.06em creates a texture that the eye must traverse rather than simply scan. It belongs to the craft tradition of printing that uses intercharacter spacing as a signal of authority — proclamations, mastheads, book spines — translated here into geometric sans rather than the usual serif context. The tension between the font's modern roundness and the historical tracking creates a specific register: technological authority that has not forgotten the body.

## Grid and Spatial Logic

The container is held at 1220px — a modest reduction from a standard 1440 that increases lateral margin pressure on wide viewports without creating the extreme narrowness of an editorial column. This is a structural choice: the contained column keeps content away from the browser edge, creating a reading environment with clear physical boundaries. The hero opens with generous vertical breathing (209px top padding) — an entrance that communicates scale without requiring visual complexity.

---

## Color Register

The palette operates at the neutral extremes. Cool off-white (#f2f2f2) — not warm cream but the grey-inflected white of machined surfaces, laboratory coats, drafting paper — occupies the background. Pure black (#000000) and near-black (#202020) carry all text and structural weight. This is a palette of precision instruments: the colors of a calibration card, a technical manual, a quality-control surface. No accent color is introduced. The contrast is total and binary.

---

## Motion and Interaction

The motion system extends transition durations by a measured 1.3x factor (1.04s primary), creating a pacing that reads as deliberate without approaching languid. Hover interactions hold at 0.75s — long enough to feel considered, short enough to remain responsive. The easing throughout defaults to a simple `ease` — not the material standard cubic-bezier, not spring physics, but the default cadence of a system that trusts its transitions not to require explanation. Motion here is the minimum duration necessary to confirm that an action has been registered.

---

## Focus and Accessibility

The navigation carries 0.08em letter spacing — a consistent tracking that creates interval between characters without fragmenting words. Nav items are spaced at 59px, giving generous horizontal room that matches the vertical openness of the hero entrance. The overall navigation register: measured, unhurried, confident.

---

## Design Character

The assembled system reads as **precision under restraint**. It would be at home for a technical instruments brand, a precision software tool, a professional services firm, or an advanced manufacturing company. The layout vocabulary borrows from technical documentation; the type from precision publishing; the motion from systems that value clarity above personality. The whole achieves a register that is simultaneously modern and deliberate: made with intent, built to last.
