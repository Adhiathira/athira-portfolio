The Studio
Chapter I

The weight of a letter pressed into dampened cotton

In a converted print house on Canongate, three Vandercook presses stand in a line. Each weighs more than a small car. Each has been printing since the 1960s. The oldest — a Universal III from 1958 — still pulls the cleanest impression in the shop.

Deckle & Thread was founded on the belief that the act of printing is not a production step but a design decision. The depth of an impression, the texture of the substrate, the way ink pools in the counter of a lowercase 'e' — these are not incidental qualities. They are the work.

We print limited editions for poets, artists, and institutions who understand that a book is an object before it is a text. Our editions are small — rarely more than 150 copies. Our paper is dampened the morning of the press run. Our type is hand-set or photopolymer from original drawings.

"The impression is the argument. Everything else is annotation."

Publisher's Note
