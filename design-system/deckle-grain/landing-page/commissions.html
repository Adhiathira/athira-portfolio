Process
How It Works

The commission process

I

Initial Conversation

We discuss your text, your ambitions for the finished object, your intended audience, and your budget. Most conversations happen by email or telephone. Studio visits are welcome by appointment.

II

Proposal & Estimate

We prepare a detailed proposal specifying typeface, paper, format, edition size, binding, and timeline. Estimates include all materials and binding costs. A 50% deposit secures your place in the schedule.

III

Proofing

We set the text and pull proof sheets on the intended paper. You receive physical proofs by post. Corrections and adjustments are made until the text reads as it should. Most editions require two rounds of proofing.

IV

Production & Delivery

The edition is printed, dried, pressed, collated, and bound. Typical lead time is 8–16 weeks from approved proof to delivery. Copies are shipped in custom-built protective packaging.
