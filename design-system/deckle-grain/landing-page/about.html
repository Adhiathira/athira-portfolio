About
The Studio

A converted print house on the Canongate

Deckle & Thread occupies the ground floor of a former commercial printer at 14 Canongate Close, a narrow passage off Edinburgh's Royal Mile. The building has housed presses since the 1890s. We moved in during 2009, inheriting the stone floors, the iron ceiling hooks where sheets once hung to dry, and the faint smell of solvent that no amount of cleaning has removed.

The studio was founded by Ewan Brodie, a typographer trained at the Glasgow School of Art and later at the Letterpress Workshop in San Francisco under the late Robert Bringhurst. He returned to Scotland in 2008 with a Vandercook SP-15, a small collection of Monotype matrices, and the conviction that letterpress printing was not a heritage craft but a living design medium.

Today the studio operates three Vandercook cylinder presses and maintains an inventory of metal type sufficient for most text composition. We source photopolymer plates from Boxcar Press for work that requires custom-drawn letterforms or reproduction of original artwork.

Book artist and compositor. Royal College of Art, printmaking. Manages studio operations, composition of text work, and coordinates with binders and paper suppliers.
