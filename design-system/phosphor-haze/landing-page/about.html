Manifesto Published 2024

Why We
Built This.

Every streaming platform you've used in the last ten years was built by engineers trying to maximize engagement. The metric is time-on-platform. The tool is the algorithm. The result is music selected not for its quality but for its stickiness — for its ability to keep you listening to the next song, not this one.

We built Neon Reverie because we wanted to listen to something that felt like it had been chosen. Not optimized. Not A/B tested. Not ranked by skip rate. Chosen by a person who sat with it and decided it belonged in a room with other things they loved.

Lo-fi, vaporwave, and synthwave are genres built on the idea that music can be atmospheric — that a track doesn't need lyrics or a hook to earn its place. It can just be a texture, a mood, a frequency you inhabit. That's what we're building: a place to inhabit frequencies.

" Music selected not for its quality but for its stickiness. We're doing the opposite. "

The name comes from a concept: a reverie is a daydream — a state of pleasant absorption that isn't quite sleep and isn't quite waking. Neon refers to what the music sounds like: electronic, luminous, synthetic, but warm. Neon warmth. That's the contradiction at the heart of lo-fi and vaporwave — the sound of digital things made by hand, for people who want to feel something.

Process

How We
Curate.

01

Submission

Artists submit directly. One track, a short note about the work, and a contact address. We accept submissions quarterly — no rolling admissions, no upload buttons. Batched review means every track gets equal attention.

02

Listening

Three members of the curation team listen independently. We listen without notes — first impressions only. Then we compare. A track needs at least two votes to advance. The third vote decides ambiguous cases.

03

Placement

Accepted tracks are assigned to playlists by feel, not genre tag alone. A lo-fi track that belongs next to ambient goes there. A synthwave track that belongs next to lo-fi goes there. The playlist is the unit, not the genre.
