Defining the
Sound.

01
Vaporwave · Tokyo, JP

Macintosh Plus

The artist who named the aesthetic and left it behind. Floral Shoppe (2011) remains the most listened-to vaporwave record — slowed-down Lisa Stansfield over mall ambience, turned into something that defies description without being described.

47 Tracks Vaporwave
02
Lo-Fi Hip Hop · Tokyo, JP

Nujabes

Jun Seba (1974–2010) produced music of impossible warmth — jazz samples cut into hip hop with the care of someone who knew he was making something that would outlast him. Each track a meditation. The lo-fi genre owes its emotional core to Nujabes.

83 Tracks Lo-Fi
03
Synthwave · Paris, FR

Kavinsky

A zombie racing driver who makes film music for car chases that never happened. Odd Looks and Nightcall are synthwave compositions disguised as genre exercises — meticulous sound design, cinematic scale, the feeling of speed in a car with the top down on a highway that doesn't exist.
