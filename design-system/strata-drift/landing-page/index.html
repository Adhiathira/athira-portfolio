Platform features

Depth-first infrastructure intelligence

Every cloud stack has layers. Strata renders them physically — so you can see what's upstream, downstream, and laterally adjacent before making changes that cascade.

Layer topology

Auto-discovers your infrastructure layers from AWS, GCP, and Azure. Renders compute at ground plane, networking in mid-ground, application at the front — spatially accurate.

Depth signals

Cost, latency, and error-rate signals float in their correct layer. See a latency spike at the network layer — and immediately understand what's above and below it.

Stack drilling

Click into any layer and drill down without losing spatial context. Adjacent components stay visible in their planes, dimmed but legible — depth never collapses to flat.

Change impact preview

Propose a change and Strata previews cascade paths across all layers — highlighting which dependent planes will be affected before a single resource is touched.

Multi-region depth

Layer stacks across regions appear side by side in the same spatial frame. Compare us-east-1 and eu-west-1 depth configurations without tab-switching.

Team depth context

Share a depth snapshot with your team. Everyone navigates the same spatial frame — no more talking past each other about which layer a problem lives in.

How it works

Three steps to depth clarity

No agent installs. No YAML. Connect your cloud provider and Strata builds the depth model automatically.

01

Connect your cloud

OAuth connect to AWS, GCP, or Azure. Strata reads your resource graph via read-only IAM — nothing is written, nothing is touched. Your infrastructure stays intact.

02

Strata builds the layers

The engine classifies each resource into its correct depth plane — compute at base, network in mid-ground, application at front — and renders the full spatial stack automatically.

03

Navigate and share

Drill into any layer, inspect signals in context, preview change cascades, and share living depth snapshots with your team. No screenshots — full interactive depth frames.
