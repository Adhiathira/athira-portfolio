# strata-drift — Concept by Image

## Visual Concept

Parallax depth aesthetic — multi-layer scrolling with each z-level traveling at a different rate. The visual system creates a sense of physical space: a deep indigo-black void, with distinct geological strata revealed as you descend. Not a website surface — a depth environment.

## Color & Light

The palette reads as deep underwater or deep atmosphere: indigo-to-black base with luminous accent signals piercing through. The accent purple (#7c6ce4) functions as a bioluminescent signal in the depth — it glows rather than merely colors. The teal (#4fb8a0) echoes cold oceanic light at pressure depth. There is no white space — only depth gradient variations.

## Typography in Space

Space Grotesk holds the spatial tension of the display plane. The extreme negative tracking (-0.03em on h1) compresses letterforms into taut geometric tension — as if typography has been compressed by depth pressure. Inter as body text recedes cleanly beneath the display type, playing its role as the legible functional layer rather than the visual anchor.

## Geometric Layer Indicators

Decorative elements are not illustrative but geological: large circular field forms, abstract blob shapes, thin line geometries. These elements are positioned at discrete z-index layers and scroll at different speeds to physicalize the depth stack. Their purpose is spatial orientation — showing the viewer which layer they occupy.

## Depth Signal vs. Depth Shadow

Unlike standard dark-mode design, strata-drift reverses shadow logic: shadows point forward, not backward. Box-shadow depth glows (rgba(124,108,228,0.25)) indicate elements floating toward the viewer, not receding. This creates a depth inversion where purple light emanates from the near plane.

## Card Treatment

Feature cards on dark backgrounds carry minimal visible borders — the depth is implied through box-shadow glow on hover. At rest they are nearly flush with the background layer. On hover they lift (scale(1.04) + translateY(-4px) + purple glow) toward the viewer plane — a physical z-elevation.

## Structural Rhythm

Sections breathe at 120px spacing — enough room for parallax elements to travel through the viewport. The hero is full 100vh minimum, the depth stage allows all three scroll-speed layers to operate independently. Section transitions are smooth z-fades rather than hard cuts.

## Product Identity

The product is Strata — infrastructure visualization tool for cloud architects. The spatial depth system is a direct metaphor for infrastructure layer stacks: compute at the base, networking in the mid-ground, application layer in the foreground. The aesthetic is not decorative — it is conceptually linked to the product domain.
