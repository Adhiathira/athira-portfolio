# Pedro Kalk — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A personal brand and creative portfolio site for a designer or creative professional. The design positions its subject through compositional confidence rather than conventional portfolio grid conventions: the hero is built around a cutout full-body character image that dictates the entire layout, and the portfolio section uses an asymmetric masonry grid with a text block embedded among the photographs. The site communicates taste through formal restraint — one typeface pair, no rounded corners, no decorative dividers, no gradient overlays — and through the editorial quality of its compositional decisions.

## Color Architecture

The palette is deliberately warm and minimal. The primary surface is a light dusty pinkish-white (`#f6f1f1`) — not pure white, but a soft, slightly warm off-white that gives the page a linen-like quality. All body text and headings use dark charcoal (`#373a40`), which reads as almost-black with a subtle warmth. The footer inverts this entirely to a near-black or dark charcoal background with light text — a hard full-stop contrast bookend. A single terracotta or warm amber accent color appears as an override on the final CTA headline — the only warm chromatic assertion in the system, used precisely once to signal the page's conversion moment. Navigation and UI borders are thin, solid black outlines. The color system works through warmth and restraint rather than vibrancy or contrast maximization.

## Typography System

Two typefaces, two roles. Cormorant (Google Font, 300 light weight) is the editorial and display typeface: primary wordmark and section headline at 56px, with h2 at 80px and +4.8px wide letter-spacing — the oversized display at wide tracking creates an airy, high-fashion tension between scale and openness. A text block within the masonry grid also uses Cormorant, maintaining its editorial character in smaller-scale running copy. Outfit (Google Font, 400) serves all functional text: the data table in the hero, button labels, nav content, and footer — clean, monolinear, and efficiently legible. The serif-editorial / sans-functional split is the system's primary typographic distinction.

## Spatial System

The page moves through four distinct zones. Hero: two-column asymmetric composition — editorial text content (introductory phrase, large wordmark, key-value data table, CTA button) anchored left, large cutout character image serving as the right zone's visual mass; a horizontal logo marquee strip at the section's bottom. Section title divider: centered large serif headline + full-width thin horizontal rule — a typographic pause before the portfolio. Masonry media grid: column-based grid with varying item heights, consistent thin visible borders between cells, a single text block embedded as one grid cell among the photographs. Minimal CTA: centered, two-element (headline + contact link), maximum whitespace. Footer: dark background, centered large wordmark, horizontal rule, two-column sub-footer. All section boundaries are straight horizontal edges throughout.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

All media — hero cutout, portfolio photographs, logo strip — uses sharp 90° rectangular frames with no corner radius. This is the site's most disciplined and consistent shape decision: in a design landscape where rounded corners have become the default, the strict rectilinear system signals deliberate intent. The portfolio masonry grid places photographs of varying aspect ratios (portrait, landscape, square) within a grid demarcated by thin light-gray borders — an architectural container for documentary-quality photography. The embedded text cell within the grid is the editorial flourish: it elevates the section from a gallery to a curated collection.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar is fixed throughout — not merely sticky on scroll but anchored at the top from page load. A minimal three-zone structure: a pill-shaped central element containing wordmark and social icon, flanked by a CTA on the right. The nav background is solid and light — slightly differentiated from the page background — and does not start transparent or change on scroll. The CTA is an outline button (consistent with the hero's outline CTA style): thin black border, no fill at rest, black fill with white text on hover. The nav carries no adaptive behavior; its identity is constant from the first frame.

## Overall Design Character

The site stakes out a formal, composition-driven position within the personal portfolio genre. Its decisions are conspicuous by their absence: no rounded corners, no animated section dividers, no scroll actors, no color fills on large surfaces, no gradient elements. What remains is Cormorant's wide-tracked display scale, a cutout character integrated directly into the layout's composition, masonry photography with grid-border structure, and a single warm accent in the final CTA. The formal restraint makes the warmth of the palette — the dusty pinkish-white, the terracotta accent — feel considered rather than arbitrary. This is a portfolio site that trusts its content enough to let the architecture step back.
