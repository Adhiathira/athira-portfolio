Research foundation

The problem with how field data is currently managed.

Most data quality failures in ecology and plant science happen before the analysis stage. They happen in the field, in the transcription, and in the format conversion. Hawthorn was built to close those gaps at the architectural level.

The field data problem

Precision in the field. Noise in the pipeline.

A typical multi-season field ecology study involves observations recorded across multiple devices, multiple formats, and multiple observers. Before analysis can begin, investigators spend weeks — sometimes months — cleaning, reformatting, and linking data that should have been linkable from the moment of collection.

This is not a workflow problem. It is a data model problem. Observations collected without a formal structural connection to their protocol, observer, and session conditions cannot be fully validated. The cleaning process introduces its own errors. The audit trail becomes fragmented or disappears entirely.

Hawthorn removes the fragility by making provenance a property of the observation record itself — not an afterthought added during cleaning.

References

Foundational research.

2024 Survey

Data management practices in field ecology: a discipline-wide assessment

Annual Review of Ecology, Evolution, and Systematics, 55(1)

Found that 67% of field ecology studies required major data cleaning before analysis; identified lack of formal protocol definitions as the primary driver of downstream quality loss.

2023 Methods

Offline-first data architectures for remote field research

Methods in Ecology and Evolution, 14(8), 1884–1899

Establishes design requirements for field data systems operating in intermittent-connectivity conditions; defines session-linking and cryptographic provenance as best-practice requirements.

2022 Retraction analysis

Provenance failures in ecological and botanical literature: a retraction analysis

PLOS ONE, 17(3), e0265104

Examined 412 retracted ecology and plant science papers; 22% cited data provenance issues. Majority traced to transcription errors during post-field processing.

2021 Protocol design

Protocol-driven observation: a framework for reproducible field research in plant science

New Phytologist, 229(6), 3021–3034

Proposes formal protocol definition as a prerequisite for reproducible field research; introduces the concept of protocol-as-schema, in which data collection instruments are generated from protocol definitions rather than constructed independently.
