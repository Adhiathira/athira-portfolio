Origin

Built from seasons of data we could not trust.

Hawthorn began in a wetland in northern Finland in 2018, where a team of three researchers spent two field seasons collecting detailed plant community observations — and then a further eight months cleaning the data before they could run their first analysis.

The problem was not their fieldwork. It was every decision made after the observation: the spreadsheets, the format conversions, the hand-transcribed GPS coordinates, the observer ID in a separate file with a different naming convention. The data was good. The pipeline destroyed its integrity.

Hawthorn is what that team wished had existed. A platform where the structural decisions that preserve data integrity are made once — in the architecture — and then never have to be made again in the field.
