Platform

Three tools. One evidentiary chain.

Hawthorn is structured around the three phases of field research: protocol design, field observation, and analysis. Each module is purpose-built for its phase — and all three share a single data model so nothing is lost in translation.

01 Protocol Studio

Design studies that hold up to peer review.

Protocol Studio is where every Hawthorn study begins. Define your target taxa, spatial transect geometry, sampling intervals, environmental variables, and required media. The protocol becomes the schema — observation forms and analysis pipelines are generated from it.

Version-controlled. Shareable with collaborators. Exportable as a methodology appendix in the format your journal requires.

Taxonomic hierarchy builder with offline species lookup
Spatial transect geometry with GPS waypoint integration
Protocol version history and diff comparison
Automated methodology section generation

02 Field Observer
→ assets/field-observer-tablet.jpeg

The observation form that works where you work.

Field Observer presents observation forms built directly from your protocol. There is no mapping, no reformatting. Required fields are enforced. Works at zero signal. Cryptographic session linking ensures every record can be traced to its observer, device, location, and protocol version.

Offline-first architecture — full functionality at zero signal
Protocol-driven form generation with no manual field mapping
Automatic GPS, timestamp, and environmental variable capture
Media attachment with cryptographic session linking

03 Analysis Engine

From structured observation to citable output.

Analysis Engine reads directly from your structured observation records. No export, no format conversion. Define your statistical model, run analysis, and output publication-ready figures and tables.

→ assets/analysis-stats-panel.jpeg

Statistical analysis without the setup

Select your statistical method, define your grouping variables, and run. Results are linked back to source observations — every number has a provenance trail.

Journal-format output

Figures at target resolution. Tables in the format specified by your journal. Methods section auto-generated with correct software citations and version numbers.

Collaborative review

Share analysis runs with co-investigators. Every reviewer sees the same data, configuration, and output — no local-environment dependencies.
