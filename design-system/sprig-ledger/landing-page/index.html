Why Hawthorn

The problem isn't in the analysis. It's in the collection.

Most data quality failures in ecology and plant science happen before the analysis stage — in the field, in the transcription, in the format conversion. By the time investigators reach their statistical software, provenance is already compromised.

Hawthorn removes the fragility at the architectural level. Every observation carries an unforgeable reference to its protocol version, observer, device, session state, and GPS context. Not as an audit log bolted on top — as a structural property of the data model itself.

Field Observer

Works at zero signal.

The Field Observer app runs entirely on-device. Taxonomic lookup tables cached locally. Full GPS availability independent of network. All forms functional offline. On reconnect, a delta-sync protocol uploads only what's new — including all cryptographic session references intact.

No observation can be entered without a valid session reference. The structural link between observation and protocol is enforced at collection time, not reconstructed during cleaning.

Analysis Engine

From data to publication without leaving the platform.

Analysis Engine reads directly from your structured observation records. Select your statistical method, define grouping variables, and run. All parameters are saved with the analysis run — every result is fully reproducible by any co-investigator.

Output figures at target journal resolution. Methods section auto-generated with correct software citations and version numbers.
