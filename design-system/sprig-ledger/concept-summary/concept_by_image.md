# Sprig Ledger — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

Sprig Ledger presents its subject through a design system that treats credibility and creative energy as simultaneous, not competing, goals. The site is structured as a narrative scroll — each section a discrete chapter that shifts register from expansive and typographic to dense and grid-based to intimate and conversion-focused. The underlying argument is that a full-service platform must demonstrate range, and the layout system makes that case section by section. Rather than relying on visual spectacle, the site earns attention through pacing: calm, spacious hero transitions into concentrated containers, which open back out into browsable product grids, creating a rhythm that feels authored rather than assembled.

## Color Architecture

The palette is built on a high-contrast foundation anchored by white and deep near-black, with three accent statements operating at distinct registers. A muted forest green functions as the primary brand color — present on CTA surfaces, selection highlights, and structural graphic elements, giving it full semantic ownership of "navigate forward." A warm ochre-amber appears as the secondary accent, soft and archival against the surrounding white. A third accent — a deep botanical dark — is reserved exclusively for the most emphatic typographic moments, making them the single most chromatic passages on the page. The discipline is precise: three chromatic statements, each with one job, none competing. The alternating backgrounds act as a structural rhythm device that maps white to open exploration and darker tones to focused attention.

## Typography System

The type system is built around a humanist display pairing: Plus Jakarta Sans for all structural heading functions — from massive hero headlines to section titles — and Inter for every body, label, and navigation element. The letter-spacing twist of 0.06em applied to all headings gives them a botanical herbarium quality: measured, precise, spaced like a specimen label rather than a marketing declaration. This is a calculated decision. A system built on a geometric-humanist sans-serif achieves maximum functional consistency; the isolated appearance of broadly tracked headings at hero scale reads as a brand signature of studiousness. The contrast between the tracked display and the tight, rational body text is the system's entire typographic personality.

## Spatial System

The spatial logic alternates between two distinct registers throughout the scroll. Container-based editorial spacing governs the hero and storytelling sections — generous margins, single large typographic statements, room to read. The global scale factor of 1.2× applied to all spacing values gives the system a quality like thick watercolor paper: substantial, with tooth. You feel the weight of the whitespace the way you feel the texture of a well-made publication between your fingers. The expanded grid gaps — 134px at tablet and 90px at desktop — amplify this quality, letting each content block sit as an object on the page ground rather than crowding against its neighbors. A strict card-grid system takes over for product and feature sections — two and three column layouts, uniform gutters, systematic card padding.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

Product and portfolio cards are the primary photography surface — high-quality case study imagery contained within rounded-rectangle frames, treated as equal inventory items regardless of medium. The editorial voice is fundamentally a pacing voice: photography appears when it serves a content function, not as atmospheric decoration. The lack of full-bleed hero photography is deliberate — the hero's credibility comes from typographic presence and the structural architecture of the page itself.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation system inherits its architectural character from the pale-engine luxury retail tradition: a centered logo position, full-width mega-menu panels that slide open with precise timing choreography, and active states revealed through an expanding underline indicator rather than background fills. The 0.875rem font size and 0.08em letter spacing give each nav link a pronounced spatial cadence — more legible and more authoritative than the 0.75rem baseline. The persistent scroll transition creates a clear separation from scrolling content without animating or changing color.

## Overall Design Character

Sprig Ledger is a precision instrument for communicating work that requires both rigor and warmth. Its intelligence lies in the botanical color palette that asserts brand identity without visual aggression, the letter-tracked headings that read like specimen labels from a scientific archive, and the unhurried motion system where transitions breathe rather than snap. The expanded spacing and gap values create a system that feels simultaneously spacious and structured — a design that communicates depth of thought without sacrificing approachability.
