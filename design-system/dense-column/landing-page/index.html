The Incident Record

7 Essays · 3 Post-Mortems · 2 RFC Commentaries · January 2026

When a system fails, the written record is the only artifact that survives. This issue collects seven first-person incident reports from engineers at scale: the cascade that erased three hours of Postgres WAL, the load-balancer configuration that took down a mobile payment network for 22 minutes, and the RFC that prevented a distributed deadlock pattern from reaching production. Each document is published as submitted — no editorial polish, no retrospective wisdom. The failure in its own words.
