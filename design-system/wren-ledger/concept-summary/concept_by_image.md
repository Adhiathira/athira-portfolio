# Wren Ledger — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

Wren Ledger presents itself as a precision tool for people who keep careful account of things — not in the bookkeeping sense, but in the deeper sense of knowing where everything stands. The palette, spatial system, and typographic register all enact the same argument: that good work is the product of sustained attention, and that the interface should honor that attention rather than compete with it. Every visual decision defers to the person doing the work.

## Color Architecture

The color system is built from a deliberately warm restraint. Parchment (#f5f2ee) functions as the dominant field — not white, not cream, but something closer to the surface of a well-made notebook, warm enough to feel organic, neutral enough to disappear behind content. Deep charcoal (#333333) carries body text and navigation with the density of ink on paper, keeping the reading experience grounded without the cold authority of true black. Near-black (#22211f) handles links and interactive elements with a slight warmth that prevents the interface from feeling antiseptic. The result is a palette that reads as considered rather than designed — a system that has already made its decisions so the user doesn't have to notice them.

## Typography System

The typeface pairing is the system's sharpest structural decision. Archivo, the grotesque display face, renders at an intentional 0.06em letter-spacing across all headings and display roles — each word opened up like characters given room to stand individually rather than press together into a mass. At 80px display size, this tracking reads as architectural: the headline becomes something to walk through rather than across. Inter carries body copy with a different disposition entirely — the honest precision of a typeface built for screens, carrying information without inflection. The combination creates a legible hierarchy: Archivo announces, Inter delivers. The 22px body size at 30.8px line height creates a reading column with enough air to sustain longer passages.

## Spatial System

The grid operates at a deliberately tightened editorial scale: a 1103px container maximum width (reduced 15% from the source's more expansive 1298px) creates a focused reading column that refuses to spread itself thin across the full viewport. Section gaps at 64px give each content unit significant autonomy — breathing room that reads as structural confidence rather than filler. The hero operates at its own generous register: 144px top padding, 192px side padding, creating the sense of arriving into a considered space rather than landing on a functional form. Button and input sizing is unambitious (5px/10px padding, 4px radius) — these elements do their jobs without announcing themselves.

## Visual Intelligence and Editorial Voice

The motion system's 1.5× duration scaling is where the aesthetic character becomes most audible. At 0.351s for fast transitions and 0.527s for fades, nothing moves quickly. The pace is that of someone who has decided how long each action should take and is not reconsidering. Hover interactions — opacity retreating to 0.75 across links, navigation, and cards — create a quiet acknowledgment of attention: interactive elements step back slightly when addressed, a gesture of recognition rather than excitement. The accumulated effect is a design that communicates self-possession.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation sits at 54px height — 10px slimmer than its source, a reduction that reads as deliberate economy. At this height, the nav recedes from structural prominence into functional transparency: it is present, it is usable, it does not perform. Left-anchored logo, generous item spacing, 16px font at −0.18px tracking. The typographic register in the nav inherits from the body system, creating continuity between navigation and content rather than treating the nav as a separate register.

## Overall Design Character

Wren Ledger is a warm-ground editorial system with charcoal as its single chromatic argument. Its design intelligence lies in what it withholds: no accent colors, no decorative motion, no typographic drama beyond the measured tracking of Archivo headings. The system communicates that it has been built by someone who has kept close account of what matters — and determined that the answer is the work itself, not the frame around it.
