How We Work

A methodology built
on verifiable evidence.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Initial consultation to establish what is known, what is needed, and what the documentation will ultimately serve. We map the existing record landscape before committing to a research direction.

  2. 02

    Research

    Direct access to primary sources: civil registries, church archives, notarial records, military rolls, land surveys, and emigration documentation. We work in original repositories wherever possible.

  3. 03

    Verification

    Each finding is corroborated across independent sources. We apply the Genealogical Proof Standard throughout — no conclusion is documented unless the evidence meets its requirements.

  4. 04

    Documentation

    Findings are compiled into a formal research report with complete source citations, annotated photocopies of key documents, indexed charts, and a written narrative tracing the lineage in full.
