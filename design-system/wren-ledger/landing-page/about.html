Who We Are

A practice built on precision and careful account.

Wren Ledger was established in 2003 by Eleanor Marsh, a Board Certified Genealogist with a background in historical archival science. The practice grew from a conviction that genealogical research is a form of historical scholarship — not a commercial service that delivers names and dates, but a rigorous investigative discipline with defined professional standards.

Over twenty-two years, we have documented more than 1,400 families across 38 countries. Our work spans the full range of genealogical engagements: private family research for individuals and estates, probate genealogy for legal proceedings, citizenship by descent documentation, and institutional archive compilations.

We are members of the Association of Professional Genealogists, the Association of Genealogists and Researchers in Archives, and the Board for Certification of Genealogists. Every member of our research team holds or is working toward professional accreditation.

How We Work

The principles that govern every engagement

Evidence First

Every conclusion rests on documented evidence. We never state as fact what the evidence does not establish. We are specific about what we found, what it shows, and what it cannot resolve.

Transparent Limits

When record survival makes a question unanswerable, we say so clearly. The limits of knowledge are as important to document as the knowledge itself.

Primary Access

We work from original sources wherever possible. Database indexes are starting points, not conclusions. We verify index entries against the original records before citing them.

Enduring Record

Every report we produce is built to outlast the engagement. Our documentation is formatted to institutional archive standards so it can be deposited, shared, and cited by future researchers.
