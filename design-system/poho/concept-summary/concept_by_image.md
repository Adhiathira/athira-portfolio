# Poho — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

Poho is a lifestyle e-commerce brand whose website makes a case for considered, curated commerce — the opposite of the overwhelming product-first grid that dominates online retail. The site is structured as an editorial journey that begins with brand atmosphere (full-bleed lifestyle hero), establishes orientation (value proposition bar, category navigation), shifts to browsable product inventory (carousel, product grid), and pauses for humanizing moments (testimonials, brand story). The design philosophy is warmth-as-credibility: the palette's warm cream offsets, the serif headline typeface, and the organic blob image frame in the storytelling section all argue that this is a brand with taste and intentionality, not a fulfillment warehouse with a homepage.

## Color Architecture

The palette is light, airy, and deliberately soft. White and very light neutral gray serve as the dominant background surface for most of the page. A warm off-white cream or pale yellow distinguishes the brand storytelling section near the end of the scroll, providing thematic warmth without introducing a harsh contrast. The footer is muted dusty blue-gray — a cool, grounding counterpoint to the warmth above it. Accent colors appear in two functional positions: a muted dusty blue for the primary CTA button in the hero and form sections, and a pale creamy yellow for the newsletter button in the footer. Neither accent is vibrant or demanding — both read as considered and understated, complementing rather than competing with the editorial photography. Dark gradient overlays on the category cards ensure white text legibility regardless of image content.

## Typography System

The site uses a two-typeface system with clear role separation. A large, elegant serif handles all major headline moments — the hero headline, the single oversized testimonial quote, and the brand story section — where it reads as classical authority. A clean, geometric sans-serif handles all UI text: subheadings, body copy, navigation links, card labels, button text, and form fields. The serif/sans-serif division maps precisely onto the editorial/functional split: when the site is telling a story or establishing identity, serif; when it is navigating or transacting, sans-serif. This is a disciplined and confident pairing that allows the site to be simultaneously sophisticated and easy to use.

## Spatial System

The site operates across several spatial registers, each mapped to a different mode of engagement. The hero is full-bleed and immersive — the entire viewport filled with lifestyle photography and centered text overlay. The value proposition bar below it is a flat, dense, four-column icon row — a functional transition that resets scale and announces the site's structural value. The category card grid introduces the site's most distinctive spatial element: four large rounded "squircle" cards with substantial corner radii, creating a friendly, tactile navigation surface. The horizontal product carousel introduces controlled overflow: four cards visible, a fifth partially bleeding off-screen as affordance. The centered testimonial and editorial sections return to single-column containment. All section boundaries use straight horizontal edges — no wave dividers, no diagonal cuts.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

Photography is both product and medium. The hero lifestyle photograph is art-directed with deliberate negative space to accommodate text overlay — the image and the UI coexist on the same surface rather than occupying separate zones. The category cards use the same compositional strategy: dark gradient overlay on each image ensures white label legibility while preserving the photography's full tonal range. Product photography in the carousel is clean, shot on neutral backgrounds, focused entirely on the object. The most distinctive editorial moment is the asymmetric split-screen storytelling section near the page's end, where custom organic blob image frames replace the grid's rectangular cards — a single section that allows the brand's more expressive visual vocabulary to emerge before the footer closes the journey.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar is sticky, solid light-colored from the first frame, and maintains a completely consistent appearance throughout the scroll. A 1px keyline at its base provides clean separation from content. Three zones: wordmark far left, primary links centered, utility icons (search, account, cart) far right. There is no primary CTA button in the navigation — the site does not urgently sell from the header; it guides. No transparency phase, no scroll-state change.

## Overall Design Character

Poho is an e-commerce site designed as an editorial catalog. Its intelligence lies in three layered decisions: the variable corner radius system (heavily rounded squircle cards for navigation, standard rectangles for product listings) that communicates function through shape; the serif/sans-serif typographic split that maps classical authority to headline moments and functional clarity to UI; and the single organic blob image section that introduces expressiveness as a deliberate exception before the site closes into the footer grid. The palette's warmth — the cream section, the muted blue-gray accents — positions the brand as considered and unhurried, a counterargument to the aggressive visual registers of mainstream e-commerce.
