# Poho — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads with the hero fully rendered — lifestyle photography at full bleed, headline and CTA visible at full opacity. There is no loading sequence, no entry animation on load. The value proposition bar below the hero is visible on first render. The site arrives complete rather than assembling itself.

## Hero Experience

The hero is a static lifestyle photograph — no ambient video, no parallax, no background motion. The composition is art-directed so that the centered text block falls in the image's quieter zone, allowing the photography to breathe without competing with the overlay. The call-to-action button appears as part of the static layout with no entrance animation. The hero is a reading experience rather than a viewing experience.

## Section Transitions

As the user scrolls, each section fades in as a unified block — not element by element, but the entire section content appearing together with a brief, gentle fade-in + slight upward slide. The transition is quick (roughly 300ms) and consistent across all sections. There is no stagger between individual elements within a section; each section has a single moment of entrance rather than a cascading sequence. The shift from white to the warm cream section background near the page's end signals a thematic change without any animated transition device.

## Content Rhythm

The page's content rhythm is established by the section structure itself rather than by element-level choreography. The four-column category grid arrives as a unit. The horizontal carousel's contents are present without animation — the scrollable track is ready immediately. The testimonial section presents a single oversized quote as a static block. The client logo ticker runs as a continuous, looping horizontal marquee independent of scroll position — it auto-scrolls and provides the page's only continuously running motion. The content rhythm is deliberate and unhurried.

## Footer Approach

The footer arrives as the page's final structural block — multi-column, dense, in the dusty blue-gray register that distinguishes it from the body. No animation, no special treatment. The footer is a functional close to the scroll journey, not a narrative one.

## Motion Principles

The motion philosophy is minimal and supportive: use animation only to signal the arrival of new content, not to characterize the brand. Section-level fade-in reveals are the entire vocabulary, applied uniformly and without exception. The one departure is the auto-scrolling logo ticker, which runs continuously but at a slow, unobtrusive pace. Duration is short (200–300ms), easing is ease-out, and there is no spring, bounce, or complex easing. The design trusts the photography and editorial composition to carry the experience without motion support.

## Video Placements

Two sections of this site would be meaningfully served by ambient looping video:

**1. Hero Background — Lifestyle ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `hero-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A slow, almost imperceptible dolly or drift across a styled interior scene — a coffee table with carefully arranged objects, morning light entering at an angle, 8–10s loop with minimal motion
- Duration: 10s loop
- Why video: The hero currently uses a single static lifestyle photograph. A slow, barely-moving video would preserve the editorial quality of the still image while adding a temporal warmth — the sense that this world is alive, not frozen. The motion would be subtle enough that on first glance the viewer might not realize it's video, which is the appropriate register for this brand.

**2. Brand Story / Storytelling Section — Material process ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `brand-story-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: Hands selecting or arranging objects in warm light — a close-up, material-focused loop emphasizing craft and selection; 8s loop, slow and deliberate
- Duration: 8s loop
- Why video: The asymmetric split storytelling section near the page's end uses organic blob image frames as a deliberate departure from the grid's shape language. A video in this slot — rather than the blob-framed still — would be the section's most expressive element, aligning with its function as the brand's identity statement. A process loop communicates intent and care in a way that a static portrait cannot.
