WRK
The Workshop Method

Form Before
Software

Every GRUNDFORM workshop begins on paper. Rules are drawn, proportions are calculated, type is set by hand. We believe that understanding the physical reality of a design decision — the weight of a line, the depth of a letterpress impression, the mass of a geometric form — cannot be shortcut by software.

Only once a student can demonstrate structural understanding with analogue tools do we introduce digital environments. This sequence is non-negotiable. It is the GRUNDFORM method.

Geometric
Typography
Foundry

12 Weeks · Mon & Thu 09:00–13:00

Instructor: Prof. Lena Hartmann

Apply for This Workshop G

Design and cut a complete uppercase Latin alphabet using only a ruler, compass, and 3mm pen. Work directly from a modular grid. No digital tools until Week 8.

Students construct each letterform from geometric primitives — the circle, the square, the diagonal. By Week 6, each student has a complete hand-drawn type specimen. By Week 10, a working set of vector drawdowns from the analogue originals. The final output is a 12-page typeface specimen booklet, offset-printed in the school's workshop.
