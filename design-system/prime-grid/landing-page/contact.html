Before You Apply

Common
Questions

Q1

Do I need design experience?

No. We accept students from all backgrounds. GRUNDFORM's method works precisely because it begins from zero — building structure before assuming any prior knowledge. A motivated beginner learns faster here than a designer who thinks they already know how grids work.

Q2

Can I enrol in more than one workshop?

Yes — students may apply for up to three workshops per semester. We will confirm schedule compatibility after selection. Most students begin with one and add a second in their second semester.

Q3

Are workshops taught in English?

Primary instruction is in German. Several workshops are also offered in English — the Typography Foundry, Architectural Model-Making, and Grid Construction are bilingual by default. Please indicate your language preference in your application.

Q4

What do workshops cost?

Workshop fees range from €480 (4-week intensive) to €1,440 (12-week programme), inclusive of all materials. Partial scholarships are available for students demonstrating financial need. Contact us for details.

Q5

Will I leave with a certificate?

You will leave with the work you made. GRUNDFORM does not issue certificates — our view is that a finished type specimen or a printed letterpress edition speaks more clearly than any formal document. Alumni are welcome to list GRUNDFORM in their professional biography.
