Space & Philosophy

Studio

Three floors of workshop space in a 1920s industrial building in Stuttgart-Mitte. Letterpress room, screenprint room, model-making workshop, and a Bauhaus archive collection of over 4,000 items.

STU
Our Manifesto

Design is
Structure

BHX

Design is not styling. Design is not taste. Design is the structural organisation of information in space. That structure can be taught, practiced, and mastered — and that is what GRUNDFORM does.

We were founded in 1969 by a group of Basel-trained designers who believed the Bauhaus method — making with materials, building with constraints, designing from function — was being lost to a generation of decorators. We have been teaching structure ever since.

Over 56 years, 1,840 students have passed through our workshops. Some became leading typographers, architects, and typefounders. Most went back to practice their craft with more intention than when they arrived. That is enough.

The Archive

4,000 Objects.
One Conviction.

The GRUNDFORM archive is the most complete collection of Bauhaus-era educational materials in private hands outside of the Bauhaus-Archiv Berlin. Assembled over 56 years by our faculty and founders, it is a working resource — not a display case.

Students are encouraged to work directly with archival materials under faculty supervision. A letterpress proof from Joost Schmidt's 1925 typography workshop sits beside a grid exercise completed by a GRUNDFORM student last Tuesday. The continuity is the point.

The archive is available to enrolled students by appointment. Researchers and institutions may request access through our Academic Liaison office.
