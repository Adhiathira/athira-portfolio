Core Principles

The Grid
is the Message

01

Visible Structure

The grid is not hidden beneath content. At GRUNDFORM, grid lines are content. We teach students to make the armature visible, deliberate, and expressive.

02

Primary Colour as Ideology

Red, blue, yellow are not decoration. They are vocabulary. Each carries structural weight and ideological intent. We teach colour as a system of meaning, not a palette of mood.

03

Typography as Architecture

Geometric sans-serif type at maximum weight, zero ornament, maximum contrast. Letters are built, not drawn. At GRUNDFORM, every letterform is a construction exercise.

04

Asymmetry as Intention

Symmetry is easy. Symmetry is institutional. Asymmetric composition requires decision — it forces the designer to commit to a hierarchy, a tension, a direction.

05

Form Before Style

Style is applied after structure is understood. We spend the first semester on pure form: proportion, axis, weight, density. No decoration until structure is mastered.

06

Making as Thinking

Every concept begins in the workshop, not at a screen. We letterpress. We silk-screen. We build models. The hand teaches what the eye only sees.

Our Grid System

The Asymmetric
5-Column Grid

Our curriculum is built around the 1fr 2fr 1fr 2fr 1fr asymmetric grid. Students learn to read it, break it, and justify every deviation. Visible 2px column gaps are never hidden — they are the structural notation of the page.
