Eight Practitioners & Scholars

Faculty

GRUNDFORM is taught by working designers, not academics. Each faculty member maintains an active practice alongside their teaching. Theory comes from doing.

FAC
Core Faculty · 2025

The People
Who Teach

01

Prof. Lena Hartmann

Typography & Letterpress

Former director of the Hochschule für Gestaltung, Offenbach. 22 years teaching structural typographic systems. Author of Buchstabe als Architektur (Birkhäuser, 2018). Her type workshop has been oversubscribed every semester since 2003.

Geometric Typography Foundry → Poster & Print Systems →
02

Markus Brandt

Grid Systems & Spatial Design

Trained under Wolfgang Weingart at the Basel School of Design. Specialises in modular grid derivation and proportional systems. Practicing architect, Stuttgart, with completed projects in Basel, Vienna, and Amsterdam. His grid course is the structural heart of the GRUNDFORM programme.

Grid Construction → Proportional Systems →
03

Yuki Nakamura

Colour Theory & Geometric Composition

PhD in colour science, Tokyo University of the Arts. Her research into simultaneous contrast and primary colour ideology is core to the GRUNDFORM first-year curriculum. Published in Color Research & Application and Eye magazine. Based in Stuttgart and Tokyo, alternating semesters.

Colour Systems → Silk-Screen Printing →
04

Henrik Solberg

Model-Making & Spatial Form

Architectural model-maker, Oslo and Stuttgart. Works exclusively with paper, card, and balsa. GRUNDFORM's most-booked workshop instructor for five consecutive years. His models are held in the collections of the Norsk Arkitekturmuseum and the Architekturzentrum Wien.

Architectural Model-Making →
05

Claudia Mertens

Design History & Criticism

Historian of European graphic design, with focus on the Basel-Ulm lineage and its afterlives. Her seminar The Grid as Ideology is a compulsory element of the GRUNDFORM curriculum. Former curator at the Museum für Gestaltung Zürich.

Design History Seminar →
06

Thomas Köhler

Typeface Design & Type History

Typeface designer, founder of Köhler-Schrift foundry, Stuttgart. Designer of Grundstein (2011) and Axial Grotesk (2019). Teaches typeface design, type history, and the material culture of the letterpress tradition. His studio has produced typefaces for Bosch, Daimler, and the city of Stuttgart.

Typography Foundry →
