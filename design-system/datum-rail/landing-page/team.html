Conduit was founded in 2023 by a team who spent years debugging latency regressions in distributed systems at Google, Stripe, and Datadog. We built the tool we wished existed.

We're a 12-person team based in San Francisco, New York, and Berlin. We're remote-first, and we operate on the same data discipline we ask of our customers: everything is measured, everything is traceable.

How we work

The operating principles we ship by

01
Data over opinion

Every product decision at Conduit is measured. If we can't instrument it, we don't build it. This applies to the product we ship and the company we run.

02
Speed as a feature

Latency is not a technical metric at Conduit — it's a product value. The trace explorer answers in milliseconds because we believe waiting for data is a design failure.

03
No vendor lock-in

We build on open standards — OpenTelemetry, OTLP, open data formats. You can export everything, migrate at any time, and run your own OTel collector. We earn your trust every month.

04
The engineer is the customer

We build for the person on call at 2am — not for the VP of Engineering in the QBR. If the interface doesn't serve that person, it doesn't ship.
