Built for the engineers who own the stack

Conduit is purpose-built for distributed systems — not retrofitted from log aggregators or APM tools designed for monoliths.

Ingestion

OTel-native. Zero-lock-in.

Conduit speaks OTLP natively over gRPC and HTTP. Any service already instrumented with OpenTelemetry SDK can start sending spans to Conduit with a single configuration change — no agents, no sidecars, no proprietary SDKs.

  • OTLP/gRPC and OTLP/HTTP endpoints
  • Tail-based and head-based sampling support
  • Batched ingestion up to 50,000 spans/s per tenant
  • Span enrichment with service catalog metadata

Alerting

Regression detection, not threshold alerts

Static thresholds miss regressions. Conduit uses a rolling statistical baseline per service and operation to detect when latency or error rates shift — even when they stay under your absolute threshold.

  • Baseline anomaly detection on p50/p95/p99 and error rates
  • Alert routing to Slack, PagerDuty, Opsgenie, or any webhook
  • Every alert ships with: trace ID, affected span, delta from baseline
  • Alert deduplication and correlation across related services
