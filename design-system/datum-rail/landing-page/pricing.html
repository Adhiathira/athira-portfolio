FAQ

Common questions

How do you define a "service"?
A service is any application that sends spans to Conduit with a unique service.name OTel resource attribute. Multiple instances of the same service count as one.
Is there a spans-per-day limit?
No. We do not cap or charge by span volume. Ingest as much as your services generate. Pricing is per instrumented service only.
Can I use my own sampling rate?
Yes. Conduit supports both head-based and tail-based sampling. Configure your OTel SDK sampler as you normally would — Conduit accepts whatever you send.
What happens when I exceed the service limit?
Conduit will alert you when you approach your service limit. Spans from services above the limit are still ingested but not displayed until you upgrade.
Can I export my data?
Yes. Full OTLP re-export is available on all plans. Forward spans to any downstream system — Datadog, Jaeger, a data lake — at any time.
Do you offer on-premise deployment?
Conduit is cloud-hosted only. For air-gapped environments, contact our enterprise team — we have a self-hosted roadmap for select customers.
