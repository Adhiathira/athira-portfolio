# Immersive Garden — Concept Summary

## Brand Overview
Immersive Garden is a creative studio specializing in high-end interactive digital experiences — likely WebGL, 3D, and experimental web environments. Their work targets luxury brands, cultural institutions, and forward-thinking companies seeking bespoke digital storytelling that transcends conventional web design.

## Visual Identity
The palette is a single restrained note: warm light gray (`#e8e8e8`) as the entire world, with near-black (`#1a1a1a`) for all mark-making — no accent colors, no gradients, no decoration. Typography splits between **PSTimes** (a custom-cut serif suggesting editorial refinement) for display and body, and **HelveticaNeueRegular** at 14px for navigation — a classic Swiss/editorial contrast that signals institutional confidence. Motion is patient and singular: a 1.9s `ease-in-out` opacity fade on media blocks, nothing more.

## Emotional Tone
Still. Unhurried. Like standing in a gallery before the doors open. The near-empty viewport, the solitary horizontal rule stretching to the edge, the centered logo mark at rest — it communicates certainty without performance. There is no urgency here, only quiet authority.

## Target Audience
Senior creative directors, heads of digital at luxury maisons, cultural institution curators, and independent art directors with substantial production budgets. People who read Dezeen and Wallpaper*, who already know what good looks like and are looking for a studio that doesn't explain itself.

## Design Principles
- **Restraint as statement** — the page is nearly empty; every element carries weight precisely because so few are present
- **Editorial typographic hierarchy** — PSTimes serif for narrative, Helvetica Neue for wayfinding; the same contrast found in Phaidon books or MoMA signage
- **Structural minimalism over decorative minimalism** — spacing is generous to the point of provocation (`355px` section margin-top, `384px` hero padding-bottom); the grid exists but is invisible
- **12-column precision** — a rigorous `12-col / 16px gap` grid at `2088px` max-width signals production discipline beneath the apparent emptiness
- **One motion gesture, used with conviction** — the single 1.9s opacity fade on media blocks is not a flourish but a breath; it refuses to compete with the work itself

## Distinctive Qualities
The loading state — a solitary dot in the bottom-right corner — is the entire interface while the site initializes. No skeleton screens, no progress bars, no spinners: just a single punctuation mark. This is the brand's philosophy compressed into a single pixel. Combined with the custom **PSTimes** font (a proprietary serif, not a system or Google font), the site signals that every decision was made from scratch, for a specific reason, by people who would notice if it were otherwise.
