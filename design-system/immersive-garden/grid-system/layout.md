## Notable Layout Patterns

### The Bisecting Rule
The horizontal hairline rule is the defining spatial move. It runs **full bleed from the logo lockup to the right edge of the viewport**, creating a horizon line that divides the composition into two equal halves. This is a **typographic/spatial device** — it gives the blank canvas a sense of ground plane.

### Off-Center Identity Placement
The logo sits at roughly **y=52%, x=6%** — not centered, not top-left conventional. This asymmetric anchoring in the lower-left of the upper half feels architectural, like a signature on a drawing.

### Extreme Negative Space as Content
The spatial strategy here mirrors luxury design codes: **restraint communicates quality**. The near-complete absence of visual content is the content. The grey field reads as off-white/warm light grey — not a default background but a considered chromatic choice.

### Implied Motion
The static nature likely obscures the true experience. The blank field almost certainly fills with a **3D scene, particle system, or generative canvas** — the logo and rule line serve as the fixed UI anchor while the background becomes an immersive environment consistent with the brand concept.
