# Brand Name — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately **8 major sections** in a clean, vertically stacked single-column document structure. The rhythm alternates between full-bleed image backgrounds, white content fields, and a single soft-tinted section. The overall feel is a straightforward informational site with minimal layout experimentation — content legibility and accessibility are clearly prioritized over visual sophistication.

---

## Header / Navigation

The header sits in a **fixed top bar** spanning the full viewport width. It uses a split layout: logo anchored left, primary navigation centered as a horizontal link list with dropdown indicators (`›`), and a high-contrast **Primary CTA button** isolated far right inside a bordered rectangle.

A **secondary navigation row** sits below the first — specifically housing an overflow item — creating a two-row nav structure that suggests the primary row ran out of horizontal space. This is a notable usability signal: the nav is close to overflowing at desktop widths.

The header background is semi-transparent, layering over the hero image beneath it.

---

## Hero Section

The hero is a **full-bleed, full-viewport-height image** with the photography spanning edge-to-edge. The image sits behind a dark overlay layer, creating contrast for white text.

Content is **center-aligned** in a single narrow column roughly 60% of the page width, positioned in the lower-center of the image area. Hierarchy descends from:
1. A large display headline — dominant, ~80–100px equivalent
2. A body paragraph at moderate width
3. Two horizontal CTAs side-by-side: Secondary CTA (lighter fill) and Primary CTA (darker fill)

The two CTAs introduce gentle visual weight differentiation without strong contrast — both are pill/rectangle shapes on the same baseline.

---

## Section 1 — Feature A (Asymmetric Photo + Text Banner)

Directly below the hero, a **full-bleed grayscale photograph** acts as a transitional panel. The image is roughly half-viewport-height. An editorial headline and body copy are **overlaid on the lower portion** of the image, left-aligned, creating a subtle overlay-on-photo pattern. No explicit card or box — the text floats directly on the image with no background container.

---

## Section 2 — Three Services (Equal 3-Column Card Grid)

A white background section hosting a **symmetric 3-column grid**. Each column contains:
- Section title centered at top
- Tall portrait photograph below the title (roughly 2:3 aspect ratio)
- Body copy centered beneath the image

The three columns carry equal visual weight — no featured/hero card. Spatial gutter between columns is moderate. The text sits beneath the image, not overlapping. This is a clean editorial card layout.

---

## Section 3 — Second 3-Column Grid (Feature B, Feature C, Feature D)

Same **3-column equal-weight grid** as the services section, with identical structure: title → photo → body text. One column adds an Action Button ("View Our Progress") underneath the body text, breaking the visual symmetry slightly — a single-column call-to-action that feels slightly isolated.

The two 3-column sections stack back-to-back, creating a unified "services showcase" block that reads as one large grid region.

---

## Section 4 — Resources (Asymmetric 2-Column, Text + Image)

A **left-image / right-text two-column layout** at roughly 50/50 proportion. The image is a wide landscape format on the left. The text block on the right includes a heading, body paragraph, and a standalone Secondary CTA button.

This horizontal split breaks the vertical card rhythm of the preceding sections and introduces left-right reading motion, adding spatial variety.

---

## Section 5 — Testimonials (3-Column Cards on Tinted Background)

Background shifts to a **soft tinted field** — the only section with a non-white, non-image background. This creates a visual pause and section boundary.

Layout is a **centered 3-column card grid**, but the card format differs from the service sections: photographs are **circular crops** (portrait-style headshots, heavily rounded), with name and caption text below. The circular images create warmth and human connection as opposed to the rectangular editorial photos above.

A centered Action Button appears below the cards.

---

## Section 6 — Team (Full-Bleed Image with Overlaid Text)

Another **full-bleed photography section**, similar in concept to the Feature A panel. A large group photo spans the full width. Text — heading and a long body paragraph — is **overlaid in the lower-left quadrant** of the image, white text on dark overlay. The text block feels narrow relative to the full image, leaving the right half as pure photography.

This creates an asymmetric, editorial quality — the composition is weighted left for text, right for imagery.

---

## Section 7 — Primary Conversion CTA (Centered, White, Narrow Column)

A clean **single-column centered content block** on white. The heading is large and wide; body copy sits in a narrow centered column (~50% viewport width); below that, a prominent wide rectangular Primary CTA button in the brand accent color.

This is the primary conversion section. The narrow text column and generous white space above and below give it strong visual breathing room — the highest spatial generosity on the page.

---

## Footer

A **two-zone footer**:

**Top bar:** Full-width accent-color band. Navigation links distributed left, copyright right — a simple two-column split at the top of the footer zone.

**Bottom band (same background):** Three distinct content columns:
- **Left:** Licensing and compliance text with regulatory logo
- **Center:** Policy disclaimer paragraph (small type)
- **Right:** Social media icon row

The footer communicates regulatory credibility alongside standard social/copyright information. The accent background carries from CTA zone to footer, creating a visual "closing cap" on the page.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Full-bleed photography as section dividers** — the site uses wide photographs not just as hero elements but as atmospheric transitions between content blocks. This creates vertical rhythm through alternating texture: image → white card grid → image → tinted cards → image → white CTA.

**Consistent 3-column grid language** — the page leans heavily on 3-equal-column grids for services and testimonials, establishing a reliable, predictable cadence.

**Overlay text on imagery** — multiple sections (hero, Feature A, Team) place text directly on top of photographs rather than adjacent to them, creating a layered depth effect that is the site's primary visual signature.

**No sticky elements** visible beyond the top navigation bar.

**Spatial hierarchy through background color** — white signals informational content, tinted signals human/community content (testimonials), accent-filled signals action/conversion (CTA + footer).
