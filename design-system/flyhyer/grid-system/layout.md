# Brand Name — Layout Brief

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately **8–9 major sections**, progressing from a dramatic hero through editorial content sections to a dark-footer resolution. The rhythm alternates between:

- **Full-bleed, immersive panels** (hero, split-panel services, dark app showcase)
- **Open-canvas white sections** with generous breathing room (value propositions, experience features)
- **Image-anchored content sections** (destinations, product interior)

The overall feel is **editorial and spacious** — very long vertical scroll with generous section padding, creating a premium, unhurried pace.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

The header is **transparent and minimal**, sitting directly over the hero image with no background bar — the nav floats above the content.

- **Left:** Nothing (no logo — the hero wordmark IS the logo, at massive scale)
- **Center:** Three horizontally-spaced text nav links (Feature A, About us, Contact) — relatively small, medium weight
- **Right:** A single circular hamburger/menu button — solid dark circle with light "≡" icon

This is a **split nav** without a left logo. The logo identity is handled entirely by the giant typographic hero, so the top bar stays pure and uncluttered. The circular menu button creates a deliberate visual anchor at the far right.

---

## 3. Hero Section

The hero is **full-viewport, full-bleed** with a photographic background (light tone fading to near-white at the bottom).

**Layering and overlap:**

- The wordmark **"Brand Name"** is enormous — it occupies roughly the left 65% of the viewport and spans from top to roughly 65% height. A registration or trademark mark sits at cap-height, top right of the letterforms, set separately in a lighter circle.
- A **3D rendered hero object** overlaps the wordmark — it sits compositionally *in front of* the type, creating a deliberate layering: type → behind object → object in front. This overlap is the visual hook of the entire page.
- **Bottom left:** A single bold tagline — "Primary value statement" — small relative to the wordmark, anchored to the viewport bottom edge
- **Bottom right:** Two CTA buttons — "Primary CTA" (dark/filled) and "Secondary CTA" (outline/ghost) — pill-shaped, also anchored near the bottom

The proportional relationship is key: the wordmark is *outrageously large*, the hero object floats diagonally across the center, and the CTAs and tagline hug the bottom corners — creating maximum negative space in the upper center-right where the subheading sits in a more restrained size.

The subheading appears **mid-right**, roughly center-screen vertically, at about H2 scale — a sharp contrast to the giant wordmark.

## 4. Content Sections

### Section 2 — Editorial Value Statement (White, Text-Dominant)

A **pure white, typography-first** section. A single massive paragraph-sized headline runs nearly full width, roughly 75–80% of the canvas, left-aligned and left-inset. The text is **very large** (display-scale) in heavy dark type.

Below, a **2×2 feature grid** divides into two columns, each with:
- A thin horizontal rule at the top
- Feature title in bold
- 2–3 lines of body copy in muted/grey

The grid is tight and organized, contrasting with the looseness of the headline above. Proportionally, the headline takes ~60% of the vertical space in this section; the grid takes ~40%.

---

### Section 3 — Services Overview (Three-Column Split Panel)

A **full-bleed, full-height horizontal three-panel layout** — the most structurally dramatic section on the page:

- **Left panel (~33%):** Dark background. Section headline top-left. Tagline and "Primary CTA" button at bottom-left. Lots of negative space in between — deliberate and confident.
- **Center panel (~34%):** A full-bleed photography panel. The image fills the column completely, edge-to-edge.
- **Right panel (~33%):** Accent-color background. A large curved dashed arc (abstract path graphic) fills most of the space with a small icon "moving" along it. Service category label at bottom: "Service A."

This is a **horizontally equal three-column layout** with no internal padding between panels — the bleeds create stark, clean joints. The left panel's label aligns with the center panel's service name and the right panel's label, creating an implicit horizontal baseline across all three.

---

### Section 4 — Featured Destinations / Items (Image + List, Asymmetric Two-Column)

**Left column (~50%):** A large rounded-corner photography card. The rounding is a distinctive design detail, used only here.

**Right column (~50%):** A **2×2 grid of list entries**, each consisting of:
- Item name (bold, large) + identifier code (parenthetical, lighter)
- A horizontal rule below
- Category label in small grey type below that

The two columns sit at roughly equal height to each other, with the photo aligned top to the list. The internal grid of entries has generous row spacing, creating an airy, data-elegant layout rather than a dense list.

---

### Section 5 — Product / App Showcase (Dark, Split)

A **full-bleed dark background**, split 50/50 horizontally:

- **Left half:** A device mockup centered in the frame — the device is large, practically portrait-filling the column, creating strong visual mass.
- **Right half:** Mostly empty dark space for most of the visible frame, with a **bottom-aligned text block** (short paragraph, light on dark). This creates intentional negative space tension — the text anchors the bottom-right while the device dominates the left.

The arrangement feels like a cinematic two-act composition: the product object on the left, the supporting narrative bottom-right.

---

### Section 6 — Key Experience Features (Asymmetric Three-Column)

White background. **Three-column layout** with very different column weights:

- **Left column (~25%):** Dense text — a short editorial paragraph plus a second paragraph with bold emphasis. Small relative to the right. Acts as the editorial anchor / "why."
- **Center column (~37%):** 2×2 feature icon grid — dashed-border square icon containers (small, line-drawn style icons) above bold feature titles and short descriptions.
- **Right column (~37%):** Same 2×2 feature icon grid mirroring the center.

The left column is visually lighter (text only, smaller type) while the right two-thirds carry the feature matrix. A **"Action Button" CTA** sits bottom-left beneath the editorial column — pill-shaped, dark/filled.

The icon containers use dashed borders — a technical, functional aesthetic that contrasts with the premium photography elsewhere.

### Section 7 — Full-Bleed Image Gallery (Full-Bleed with Counter)

A **full-width, edge-to-edge photograph** — no margins, no padding.

A slide counter sits **bottom-right within the image** ("← → 1/6"), suggesting a horizontal carousel. The counter uses directional arrows and is light-colored, overlaid on the photo.

Below the image, large white space before the next CTA — the emptiness is intentional, creating a pause in the rhythm.

---

### Section 8 — CTA Close (Typographic, Gradient)

A centered or left-leaning typographic CTA: small overline label ("Closing hook phrase") in grey above a **giant display headline** ("Primary CTA") in heavy dark type. The section sits on a subtle gradient background (same light tone from the hero), creating bookend symmetry with the opening.

**Two-tier dark footer** — near-black, very dark background.

**Upper footer:**
- **Far left:** Wordmark in light color, small

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

### Overlapping Layers / Z-Depth
The hero is the clearest example: wordmark behind, 3D object in front. Overlapping creates depth without any UI chrome. This layering technique appears only in the hero — the rest of the page is flat-plane, no overlap.

### Full-Bleed Horizontal Panels
The services section breaks the page into three full-height, full-width columns that feel like a triptych gallery — no gutters between panels. This is the boldest structural moment on the page.

### Extreme Typographic Scale Contrast
Throughout, there is aggressive contrast between type sizes: display text at near-viewport scale alongside small body copy. The hierarchy is communicated purely through scale, not decoration.

### Spatial Rhythm via Breathing Room
Almost every section uses **very generous top/bottom padding** — sometimes the content itself takes less vertical space than the whitespace around it. This is a premium signal: the brand can afford not to compress everything.

### Image Rounding
One destination/item photo uses visible rounded corners — the only instance on the page. It creates a card-like softness that distinguishes that section from the rest.

### Dashed Border Iconography
The experience feature icons use dashed-outline squares — a deliberate technical aesthetic that contrasts with the premium photography elsewhere.

### Color Panel as Content
In the services triptych, the accent-color panel carries no photography — just a large abstract graphic. Color itself becomes content, used at section scale rather than as a UI accent.
