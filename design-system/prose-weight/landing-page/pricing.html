Chapter II

Questions about the platform.

Question One

Does Folio take a percentage of my subscription revenue?

No. We charge a flat monthly fee. Every dollar your readers pay for your writing comes to you, minus Stripe's standard processing fee (2.9% + 30¢). We built a reading platform, not a revenue-share business. Your readers are yours.

Question Two

Can I import my existing writing from another platform?

Yes. Folio supports direct import from Substack, Ghost, Medium, and WordPress. Your existing stories, your subscriber list, and your formatting will transfer. Drop caps and pull quotes will be applied automatically to imported content based on your chosen style.

"We don't want to be your platform. We want to be your paper." — Folio founding principle
Question Three

What happens if I exceed my subscriber limit on the free plan?

Your existing subscribers keep reading — we never interrupt active readers. We notify you when you reach 400 (80% of your limit) and again at 500. You can upgrade at any time before you reach the limit. We've never cut off a writer's readers. We never will.
