# prose-weight — Concept by Image

## Design Concept

**Storytelling narrative editorial aesthetic.** Long-form reading experience as the design's primary purpose. The design does not announce itself — it disappears into the act of reading. Every decision serves sustained attention.

## Surface

Warm paper (#faf7f2) — not white, not cream, but the exact temperature of a quality newsprint or literary review. The surface is alive without being distracting. Text sits in it rather than on it.

## Typographic Furniture

**Drop caps** mark chapter openings in wine red Playfair Display at 5rem, floating left — the typographic tradition of manuscript illumination brought into the browser. The opening letter is the reader's invitation.

**Pull quotes** live in Playfair Display italic, anchored by a 3px bronze/copper left border (#c49b7c). They break the reading column not to interrupt but to crystallize — the sentence the reader will remember. Full-bleed within their section, centered, generous vertical breathing room.

**Chapter numbers** in bronze uppercase Source Sans Pro with wide letter-spacing (0.2em) appear above section headings. They say: you are here, in the sequence of a story.

**Marginal annotations** — short notes in Source Sans Pro 0.8125rem, warm slate (#4a5568), appear in the right margin of two-column layouts. Conversational asides. The author leaning in.

## Reading Progress

A wine-red 2px bar at the bottom of the fixed navigation header. It is the only interactive chrome on the page. As the reader scrolls, it fills. It is a measure of how much story remains, not a loading indicator.

## Color Logic

- **#faf7f2** — warm off-white paper, the base everything lives on
- **#1a1714** — warm near-black ink, not pure black, carries warmth
- **#8b1f2e** — deep wine red, the structural signal: chapter markers, links on hover, focus rings, CTA buttons
- **#c49b7c** — bronze/copper, secondary editorial signal: chapter numbers, pull quote border
- **#4a5568** — warm slate, the annotation voice
- **#6b6560** — muted ink for secondary text
- **#fff3cd** — soft yellow highlight, the literal highlighter pen, used for ::selection

## Typography

- **Lora** at 1.1rem / 1.85 line-height is the reading engine. Humanist warmth, generous lead.
- **Playfair Display** is the editorial authority: chapter titles, pull quotes, drop caps. High contrast strokes, classical proportions.
- **Source Sans Pro** is the functional voice: captions, annotations, navigation, reading progress label.

## What This Is Not

No shadows. No border-radius. No gradients. No hover scales. The editorial vocabulary is rectangular, flat, and typographic. The design disappears so the story can breathe.
