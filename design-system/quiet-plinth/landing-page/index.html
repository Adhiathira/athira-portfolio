How It's Made

Craft without shortcut

Each paper begins in a vat. Cotton fibres are beaten, suspended, and drawn onto a mould by hand. Drying takes 24 hours. Pressing takes five minutes. The process cannot be rushed — and the result cannot be replicated industrially.
