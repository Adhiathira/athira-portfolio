Bespoke Work

A paper made for you

How It Works

The commission process

01

Initial conversation

We begin with an email or a studio visit. You tell us what the paper is for, what you've tried before, what didn't work. We listen and ask questions. No design brief required — we translate intent into material decisions.

02

Sample press

We press two or three small samples in different weights and surfaces and post them to you. You hold them, write on them, fold them. If none is right, we adjust and send again. This stage is included in commission price.

03

Production batch

When you confirm a sample, we schedule your batch into the production calendar. Most commissions take 4–8 weeks from confirmation — longer if letterpress printing is included. We keep you posted at each stage.

04

Delivery and documentation

Papers are wrapped in archival tissue and shipped in a rigid mailer. Each commission comes with a batch card recording the date pressed, furnish composition, and weather conditions. This is your paper's provenance record.
