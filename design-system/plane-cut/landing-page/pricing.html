Questions

Before you decide

Everything you need to know about Stratum pricing.

Can I import files I already have?

Yes. Stratum imports DWG, DXF, PDF, SVG, and PNG. Files from AutoCAD, Rhino, Illustrator, and most BIM exports are supported.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

You choose a plan or your account reverts to a free tier with 2 projects and 5 layers max. No charge until you decide.

Is the Studio plan per-user or per-studio?

Per seat. Each team member has their own workspace. Projects can be shared within your studio without extra charges.

Can I export to formats my clients can read?

PDF and PNG exports work on all plans. Interactive web presentations (share a link, no software required) are available on Studio and above.
