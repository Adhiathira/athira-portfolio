Why Stratum

Depth that communicates
before words do

Every spatial decision deserves a presentation that matches its quality. Stratum layers your documentation the way a building layers its floors.

Layer Composer

Stack drawing layers independently. Each layer has its own z-elevation, opacity, and shadow — mirroring the way physical architectural models read depth.

Precision Cut-outs

Define geometric clip regions with sub-millimeter accuracy. Reveal the layer beneath through cut silhouettes — windows, voids, and openings as design elements.

Shadow Calibration

Fine-tune directional light angles and shadow intensity per layer. The result reads as a physical model under study — not a flat screen document.

Process

From drawing to
deliverable in three steps

Stratum's workflow mirrors your design process — from initial sketch layers through to client-ready output.

01

Import Your Drawings

Bring in DWG, PDF, or SVG files. Stratum automatically separates your drawing into logical layers — site plan, floor plan, sections, details.

02

Compose the Layers

Assign elevation, shadow depth, and cut-out geometry to each layer. Watch depth emerge as you work — the same logic as physical model-making.

03

Export with Precision

Output print-ready PDF, high-resolution PNG, or interactive web presentation. Every shadow and cut silhouette renders faithfully at any scale.
