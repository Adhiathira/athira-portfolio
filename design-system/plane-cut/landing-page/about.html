Our Story

Built by architects,
for architects

Stratum began in a competition studio at 2am, when our founders realized the gap between the quality of their physical models and the documents they were submitting. We set out to close it.

Mission

Documents that earn
the work they represent

Every drawing is a record of thinking. The best architectural work is buried in presentations that flatten, compress, and misrepresent the depth of what was designed.

Stratum gives drawings back their physical quality — the shadow between a ground plane and a section cut, the layered reading of a plan, the hierarchy that tells a client where to look first.

We build software that respects the intelligence embedded in architectural line work. The tool should be invisible. The drawing should be everything.

What we believe

Four principles

Precision is craft

Every pixel, every shadow depth, every layer offset is a decision. We treat our software with the same discipline we ask architects to bring to their drawings.

The tool disappears

A great tool is invisible. The drafter should be thinking about the drawing, not the software. We measure success by how rarely practitioners need to open documentation.

Depth is communication

Physical depth — shadow, layer, elevation — carries meaning that flat documents destroy. We preserve and amplify that signal in every output format.

Built from practice

Every feature started as a complaint from a practitioner under deadline. We do not build features we have not experienced the need for ourselves.
