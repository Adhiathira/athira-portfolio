The Studio

A workshop, not a factory.

Every piece leaves the workshop with the marks of the hands that made it — surface decisions, edge treatments, and mechanical tolerances managed by eye and touch, not machinery. We work with architects on projects where the hardware is part of the language of a building — not an afterthought ordered from a catalogue.

How It's Made

From brief to object,
each step attended to.

We document every decision. Casting records, surface notes, and mechanical specifications travel with the piece from first drawing to final delivery.

01

Brief & Drawing

We begin with a meeting. Dimensions, material preferences, and the building's context are gathered before a single drawing is made.

02

Pattern & Cast

Sand-cast brass or bronze from patterns made in-house. Each casting carries its own surface character — no two are identical.

03

Finish & Fit

Hand-filed, burnished, or patinated to specification. Mechanical components machined for tolerance. Delivered ready to install.
