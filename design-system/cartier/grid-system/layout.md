# Brand Name — Layout Brief

---

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page opens with a full-height viewport experience: a global navigation bar at the top, immediately followed by a full-bleed editorial hero image that dominates nearly the entire visible viewport. The screenshot captures the page in an interrupted state — a cookie consent modal has been overlaid at center-screen, so content sections below the hero are not yet visible. What we can analyze is the header system, the modal interrupt layer, and the hero image beneath.

The spatial hierarchy is immediately clear: **nav → hero** with no breathing room between them. The header sits flush above the image, implying a transparency or very minimal separation rather than a heavy dividing line.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation Layout

The navigation is a **two-tier system**:

**Top utility bar** (very slim, roughly 40px tall):
- Left cluster: region selector / contact / services — three utility links in small spaced caps, left-aligned
- Center: the brand wordmark logo — large, elegant, centered absolutely at the top of the page, acting as a visual anchor for the entire header
- Right cluster: icon-based utilities — wishlist, account, store locator, shopping bag — evenly spaced, right-aligned

**Primary navigation bar** (below, roughly 50px tall):
- Full-width horizontal nav with category labels in all-caps spaced lettering: Category A, Category B, Category C, Category D, Category E, Category F, Category G, Category H
- A thin vertical separator divides the main nav from a search icon on the far right
- Items are evenly distributed across the full width — not left-bunched

The two tiers feel unified as one horizontal band. The logo's size and center placement gives it commanding visual weight over both rows. Total header height is modest — roughly 90–100px — keeping the editorial image prominent.

---

## 3. Hero Section

The hero is a **full-bleed editorial photograph** — it spans edge-to-edge, top-to-bottom of the viewport below the header with no margins, padding, or framing. The image bleeds off the bottom of the viewport, implying a tall, scroll-activated section.

- The photograph is portrait-orientation content displayed in a landscape container — the subject is tightly cropped, product visible at focal point
- The image is treated as pure atmosphere and brand, not as a product callout — there is no headline, overlay text, CTA button, or any typographic layer on the image itself
- The color palette of the image is muted and editorial — consistent with a restrained brand voice over direct promotion

The hero communicates through proportion and restraint: the image occupies roughly 85% of the initial viewport height, creating an almost cinematic presence.

---

## 4. Modal Interrupt Layer

A **centered modal dialog** floats above the hero image:

- The modal is a clean white card, no rounded corners — sharp rectangular, ~880px wide and ~430px tall at displayed size
- It sits at visual center, slightly above the true vertical midpoint — approximately 60/40 split, upper section heavier
- The background (hero image) is not dimmed/overlaid — the modal sits atop it without a scrim, which is an unusual choice — it creates a layered but not blocked feeling

**Modal internal layout:**
- Header: section label in tight all-caps spaced tracking — same typographic register as the nav
- Body text: left-aligned body copy in a single column, generous line-height, ~60 character line length — comfortable readability
- Three CTAs sit at the bottom in a **horizontal three-button row**, evenly spaced: Primary CTA (filled dark), Secondary CTA (outlined/bordered), Tertiary CTA (filled dark, two-line label)

The CTA row uses a deliberate visual weighting — two dark fills bookending one outlined button — that subtly de-emphasizes the secondary option without hiding it.

## 7. Notable Layout Patterns

- **No-scrim modal**: The modal floats over the hero without darkening the background — preserving the brand atmosphere even during a compliance interrupt. This is atypical and deliberate.
- **Logo as crown**: The centered wordmark is not in a nav slot — it crowns the entire header system, positioned above both utility and primary nav rows, giving it near-ceremonial weight.
- **Typographic register consistency**: Every text element — nav, utility links, modal header, CTA labels — uses the same all-caps spaced-tracking register at varying weights. This creates a deeply unified visual language across very different UI elements.
- **Negative space as luxury signal**: The hero has zero text overlay. The navigation is minimal. The spatial restraint is itself the design statement — the proportions do the communicating, not copy.
- **Full-bleed with no safe zone**: The hero photo touches all four edges below the header. There are no margins, no padding gutters, no framing. The image and the viewport are treated as coextensive.
