The Pigments

Each ink in the collection originates from a named material source. Batches are numbered and produced in quantities of 50–120 units.

Spring 2026 botanical batches now available. Madder and Weld limited to 60 units each.

Mineral · Batch 24A · Featured

Burnt Sienna

Calcined iron-rich clay from coastal limestone deposits. Warm reddish brown with exceptional granulation on wet media. Highest lightfastness rating in the collection — suited to archival printmaking and permanent calligraphic work.

Mineral · Batch 24B

Raw Umber

Unheated manganese-iron deposit. Neutral brown with subtle green undertone.

Mineral · Batch 24C

Yellow Ochre

Classic iron oxide yellow from French deposits. Warm straw tone, earthy undertone.

Plant · Batch 23W · Seasonal

Madder Lake

Alizarin extracted from Rubia tinctorum root. Transparent warm red. Limited autumn batches.

Plant · Batch 26S · New

Weld Yellow

Luteolin-rich extract from Reseda luteola. Bright clear yellow. Spring 2026 batch now shipping.

Carbon · Batch 25V

Vine Black

Charred grapevine. Softer, warmer than lamp black. Ideal for brushwork and wash techniques.

Carbon · Batch 25L

Lamp Black

Pure carbon soot, intense near-neutral black. The oldest ink material in continuous use.
