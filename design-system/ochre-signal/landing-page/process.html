Our Commitments

The Principles

Principle 01

Named Materials Only

Every raw pigment is traceable to a named source — a specific deposit, a named grower, an identified region. We do not buy from distributors who cannot tell us where their material originates.

Principle 02

Small Batch, Full Documentation

No batch exceeds 120 units. Each batch is milled, tested, and released as a single lot with its own number, date, and material notes. When it sells, it's gone.

Principle 03

Tested Before Release

Every ink is tested for flow across five paper stocks — hot press, cold press, handmade, Japanese, and cotton — and assessed for 30-day UV fade resistance before a batch is offered.

Principle 04

No Synthetic Additives

Suspension mediums are limited to gum arabic, ox gall, and distilled water. No synthetic preservatives, no brighteners, no stabilisers derived from petrochemical processes.

Where It Comes From

Known Origins

Each material source in the Pellum supply chain is documented. The following are active suppliers for the current collection.

Roussillon, France

Ochre Quarries

Iron oxide ochre and sienna from natural clay deposits. Unprocessed raw pigment, purchased direct from the quarry.

Umbria, Italy

Terre Verte Deposit

Green earth (celadonite/glauconite), hand-quarried. Limited annual supply. Used in the Umbrian Green batch when available.

County Wexford, Ireland

Weld Grower

Reseda luteola harvested from a single 3-acre plot. Spring and autumn harvest. The only certified organic Weld source in our supply chain.

Punjab, India

Madder Cooperative

Rubia tinctorum root from a cooperative of 12 smallholders. Third-generation cultivation, traditional alizarin extraction method.

Burgundy, France

Vine Prunings

Charred Pinot Noir prunings for Vine Black. Sourced annually from a single domaine whose vines average 38 years old.

Luberon, France

Oak Gall

Quercus pubescens galls for iron gall variants. Foraged seasonally. Supply varies; batches produced when material quality is sufficient.
