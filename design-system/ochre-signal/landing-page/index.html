How It's Made

The Method

01

Forage & Source

Each raw material is collected or sourced from known origins — named deposits, specific regions, identified growers. Nothing anonymous.

02

Mill & Grade

Dry pigments are milled in small quantities, graded for particle size, and tested for consistency across the batch before suspension.

03

Suspend & Test

Final inks are mixed, tested for flow across five paper stocks, and assessed for fade resistance under UV exposure over 30 days.

04

Number & Ship

Each bottle carries a batch number, season, and material origin. You know exactly what you're working with before you uncap it.
