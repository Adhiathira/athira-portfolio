# Truly Financial — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads with the navigation bar already visible in its light gray state — there is no transparent-to-opaque transition on load. The hero enters immediately: the circular animated brand graphic is already in motion, running on its own autonomous clock rather than triggered by any user interaction. The opening state is therefore not a static frame waiting to animate — it is a live composition that the user arrives into. The two-column layout (text left, circular graphic right) is fully resolved at load.

## Hero Experience

The hero's motion energy lives entirely in the circular graphic: layered abstract gradients and a wordmark in continuous, subtle rotation or drift — slow enough to read as ambient, fast enough to confirm it is alive. This is the site's only autoplay ambient element. The surrounding copy and navigation are static. The circular frame itself is the visual anchor — sized to occupy a significant portion of the right column, it creates a weighted asymmetry against the left-column headline and CTA. On scroll, the hero exits cleanly; the circular graphic does not persist into subsequent sections.

## Section Transitions

Transitions are executed primarily through background color shifts with no animated crossfades — the boundary between sections is a clean horizontal edge. White gives way to light gray (`#f3f3f3`) for the feature grid, then white again for alternating media-text sections. The sinusoidal wave dividers are deployed as deliberate structural events rather than universal section separators: they bracket exactly two sections — the numbered process section (wave top and wave bottom) and the CTA section (wave top only). These are static wave shapes, not animated — they function as architectural ornament that frames the gradient-background sections and signals to the user that the content register has shifted from functional to persuasive.

## Content Rhythm

Content reveals follow a consistent stagger logic: section headline enters first (fade up), followed by supporting text, followed by the grid or media element. In the three-column feature card grid, cards enter with a left-to-right stagger — each animated UI mockup clip begins looping only after its card enters the viewport. The numbered process section on the gradient background staggers its numbered steps in sequence. The pricing comparison section on the dark background uses a high-contrast reveal — cards enter cleanly against the dark field. The horizontal carousel does not animate on entry; it is present in full and navigated by arrow controls with a card-peek affordance indicating off-screen content.

## Footer Approach

The dark footer arrives after the gradient CTA section as a hard color cut. No entrance animation — the section is static. The transition from gradient to dark functions as the page's final tonal descent, closing the pastel persuasion zone and shifting into institutional utility. The dark background echoes the pricing section's decision-zone register, ending the page on a note of gravity.

## Motion Principles

The motion vocabulary is disciplined and commercially purposeful. The circular brand graphic provides the page's only continuous ambient motion — everything else is scroll-triggered and single-firing. Reveals are functional rather than expressive: fade-and-slide patterns with modest stagger create a sense of the page populating as the user reads rather than performing for them. The sinusoidal wave forms are static rather than animated — they are geometric events, not motion events. The overall pacing is measured and professional, appropriate for a financial product where excessive motion would undermine trust.

## Video Placements

**1. Hero Section — Circular brand graphic context**
- Suggested file: `hero-graphic-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A slow, looping abstract video — layered soft gradients in warm pastels (pink, peach, soft blue, light amber) drifting and dissolving into one another, suggesting depth and organic motion; no recognizable objects, no faces, no products; the motion should feel like light through water or layers of translucent material; designed to be composited inside the circular frame of the hero brand graphic
- Duration: 12s loop
- Why video: The circular animated brand graphic is the site's defining visual element and the hero's only ambient motion source. A video background inside this frame — looping abstract gradients that align with the site's pastel accent palette — would give the element a richer, more dynamic quality than CSS animation alone, and reinforce the brand's warmth without adding competing visual content

**2. Split Media-Text Section — Professional context**
- Suggested file: `product-context-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A person in a modern, quietly styled workspace reviewing financial information on a laptop or tablet — desk surface, natural light from a window, warm neutral tones consistent with a contemporary professional environment; no logos, no text visible on screen; the subject should be in relaxed focus, communicating ease rather than urgency; 6–8s loop
- Duration: 6–8s loop
- Why video: The alternating split media-text sections present lifestyle photography alongside feature copy. One of these sections — ideally the one introducing the product's core value proposition — would benefit from a short looping clip rather than a still. Motion in a media container at this scale communicates that the product is active and present in the user's daily work life, not a static tool but an ongoing relationship
