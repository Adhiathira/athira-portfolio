Capabilities

Precision at every layer of the document

01

Structural Analysis

Recto maps the architecture of any document — headings, hierarchies, dependencies, flow. What editors know intuitively, Recto makes explicit and navigable.

02

Style Intelligence

House style is a living standard. Recto learns from the editorial record — not just from the style guide — and surfaces inconsistencies before they compound.

03

Proof-State Tracking

Every draft carries its history. Recto tracks the state of a document across revision cycles, preserving what was changed, why, and by whom.

04

Cross-Document Coherence

Series, volumes, editions — Recto holds the editorial thread across every piece, ensuring the vocabulary, register, and decisions travel with the work.

05

Annotation Intelligence

Margin notes are where the real editorial thinking lives. Recto reads annotations as structured data — queryable, resolvable, exportable.

06

Output Fidelity

From manuscript to print-ready PDF to digital edition — Recto ensures that what was written is what is published, without the last-mile errors that erode trust.

Where precision matters most

Academic Publishing

Citation integrity, cross-reference accuracy, edition tracking. Recto handles the structural weight so your editors focus on the intellectual work.

Literary Publishing

Voice consistency, style coherence, the invisible hand of the house. Recto holds the editorial line across revisions without losing what makes the work distinctive.

Legal & Policy

Where every word carries consequence. Recto flags structural ambiguity, surfaces version conflicts, and tracks definitional dependencies before they become problems.

Technical Documentation

Versioning is everything. Recto links documentation to the product lifecycle — ensuring no page exists in a state that doesn't match the software it describes.
