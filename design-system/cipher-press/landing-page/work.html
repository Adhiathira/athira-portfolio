How it works

The anatomy of a document, made navigable.

Recto ingests documents in any format and returns structured intelligence across six dimensions — available through API, integration, or the Recto editing environment.

01

Ingest

Recto accepts documents in Word, PDF, InDesign IDML, Markdown, plain text, and XML. Conversion is lossless at the structural level — headings, annotations, footnotes, and revision history travel intact.

File formats are normalized to Recto's internal document model — a graph structure that preserves all editorial relationships. The original file is never modified.

02

Analyse

Structure, style, terminology, and proof state are extracted in a single pass. Recto does not use generative language models for analysis — the intelligence is rule-based, deterministic, and auditable.

Analysis runs locally by default. Cloud processing is available for teams working with large document corpora. Both modes produce identical output — the difference is throughput and storage.

03

Surface

Structural issues, style deviations, and terminology conflicts are surfaced as actionable items — each linked to the exact location in the document and the rule that produced it.

Issues are classified by severity, type, and confidence. Teams can configure which rules apply to which document types — a house style rule for literary manuscripts may not apply to technical documentation.

04

Resolve

Each issue surfaces with a proposed resolution, the basis for that resolution, and the editorial precedent from your own document corpus. Accept, reject, or defer — every decision is logged.

Accepted resolutions update the document in place. Rejected resolutions teach the system — Recto learns your house standards from the decisions your team makes, not just the rules you write.
