# Kreor — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A German digital creative agency positioning itself through its work rather than its description. The site prioritizes cinematic immersion over rational explanation: hero sections open on 3D rotating objects against dark fields, a liquid wave divider marks the passage between the hero's atmosphere and the content that follows, and a persistent 3D scroll actor travels from the hero into subsequent sections before fading into the page. The design operates in two visual registers — dark, immersive narrative sections and white-background content sections — and the oscillation between them is managed through contrasting boundary geometry: animated organic waves for cinematic transitions, hard straight edges for structural ones. The orange accent (#ed7422) is the only warm element in an otherwise cool, high-contrast system.

## Color Architecture

The page background is white (#ffffff), but this surface is largely invisible in the hero and showcase sections, which use dark near-black fills to achieve cinematic contrast. Body text is neutral gray (#666666). Heading text is white (#ffffff) — confirming that display-scale headlines appear on dark section backgrounds where maximum contrast against illuminated 3D media is required. Navigation links and the primary CTA button carry a vibrant orange (#ed7422). The nav background is transparent, transitioning to a semi-transparent blurred dark surface on scroll. The color system supports a narrative of dark-dominant cinema opening into white-field content sections: the two registers alternate as the user moves down the page. Cookie consent UI tokens show the same orange repeatedly, confirming it as the single brand accent throughout all UI states.

## Typography System

Montserrat (Google Font, bold 600) is the display typeface — 72px at hero scale with +2px letter spacing, scaling to 38px for section headlines at -1px tracking. The uppercase spacing convention at display scale (+2px on h1, -1px on h2) creates a tension between openness and compression that varies by context. An h3/eyebrow label is set at 15px/500, uppercase with +1px tracking — functioning as a categorical prefix before section headlines. A notable data point: h4 is set at 180px/700 — this is a decorative typographic element, likely a large section number or a single oversized character used as a graphic backdrop. Questrial (Google Font, 400) appears in the font stack as a secondary body/utility face. Body and paragraph text is Montserrat 500 at 15–16px. Nav links are 13px/500.

## Spatial System

The page operates through four section types. The hero is a full-viewport composition: dark background, rotating 3D object as central focal point, centered typography cluster overlaid, animated liquid wave at the bottom boundary. Full-width media showcase sections are full-bleed image or video backgrounds with text placed asymmetrically in art-directed quiet zones — typically one vertical third of the screen. A card grid section uses a dark-background multi-column layout for project, service, or work examples — each card with slightly rounded corners, hover states signaled by subtle glow or scale. A terminal CTA/footer section closes the page with a single large headline and primary CTA on a dark field. Section boundaries shift between the animated organic wave (for cinematic content-to-content passages) and hard straight edges (for functional content-to-grid and grid-to-footer transitions).

## Photography and Editorial Voice

The primary media is 3D-rendered objects — metallic geometric forms that serve as the hero's visual protagonist and persist as scroll actors through the transition into subsequent sections. Full-width media showcase sections use high-resolution photography or looping video, always with art-directed text-safe zones that prevent type/image conflict. Card media in the grid uses static or auto-playing video thumbnails representing work examples. The visual voice is high-craft, technical, and immersive rather than warm or personal — the design performs capability rather than describing it.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar is sticky throughout: logo left, navigation links center, CTA button right. It opens fully transparent over the dark hero, then transitions to a glassmorphic semi-transparent dark blur as the user scrolls — maintaining legibility across both dark and bright subsequent sections. Nav links are orange (#ed7422) at all scroll states — the accent color functions as the wayfinding signal. The nav position token is "static" in the extracted data, but the analysis indicates the nav becomes sticky on scroll, consistent with standard Divi theme behavior.

## Overall Design Character

Kreor is an agency site that treats the hero as a theatrical opening act. The animated liquid wave section divider is its most distinctive structural decision — used specifically at the hero-to-content boundary to signal the shift from cinematic introduction to evidential content. The 3D scroll actor that originates in the hero and recedes through subsequent sections is the experiential thread that holds the scroll journey together: the user follows the object's disappearance as they descend into the work. The contrast between these organic cinematic elements and the hard straight edges of the card grid and footer boundaries is intentional — different visual syntax for different purposes. The orange accent is the site's single chromatic assertion, reserved for action and identity rather than decoration.
