Methodology

The calibration standard
behind every deployment.

72-hour thermal equilibration. Multi-point pressure calibration. NIST-traceable uncertainty budgets. Every sensor. Every time.

Process

47 checkpoints between
raw material and deployment.

  1. Raw material inspection

    Grade 5 titanium billets are individually tested for alloy composition and surface integrity before machining. Certificates of conformance accompany every lot. Rejected material is quarantined before it enters the production line.

  2. Sensor assembly in ISO 6 cleanroom

    Conductivity, temperature, and pressure sensing elements are assembled in a certified ISO 6 (Class 1000) cleanroom environment. Particulate contamination is the primary cause of conductivity cell drift; cleanroom assembly extends calibration stability to multi-year timescales.

  3. 72-hour thermal equilibration

    Every assembled sensor undergoes a 72-hour thermal soak cycle in our controlled-environment calibration bath. This allows component materials to fully settle before characterisation, eliminating the most significant source of post-shipment drift in similar instruments.

  4. Multi-point pressure calibration

    Pressure sensors are characterised at 12 reference points from 0 to rated full-scale using our primary pressure standard, cross-referenced to an NPL-traceable reference. The resulting calibration polynomial is stored on-device and used in real-time computation.

  5. Salinity cross-validation

    Computed salinity values are validated against OSEAN-SIO reference water samples at seven temperatures. Remaining offset is characterised and applied. Instruments that cannot meet ±0.003 PSU accuracy at this stage are disassembled rather than shipped with adjusted coefficients.

  6. Pressure test and depth certification

    Each instrument is cycled through a pressure vessel to 110% of its rated depth equivalent. Dimensional checks and electrical continuity testing confirm that housing and penetrator seals have not been compromised. Only then is the depth rating certified.

  7. Calibration certificate issue

    A complete calibration report is generated for each serial number, including all characterisation data, environmental conditions, reference standard traceability chain, and uncertainty budget. This document travels with the instrument and satisfies JCOMM documentation requirements without additional verification.

Reference Standards

Traceability that holds
in a Nature paper.

NIST — National Institute of Standards and Technology

Temperature and pressure standards directly traceable to NIST via calibrated reference instruments. Certificate numbers recorded per batch.

OSEAN-SIO Standard Seawater

Salinity reference water from the OSEAN-SIO batch program. Conductivity ratio certified to ±0.0001 S/m.

WOCE Hydrographic Cross-Validation

Batch-level cross-validation against published WOCE section data for regional consistency checks.

ISO/IEC 17025 — Laboratory Accreditation

Bergen calibration laboratory is accredited under ISO/IEC 17025 for temperature, pressure, and conductivity measurements.
