Data Quality

Calibration is
not a checkbox.

Tidemark sensors undergo a 72-hour thermal equilibration cycle followed by multi-point pressure calibration in our Bergen facility. Every unit ships with a full calibration certificate and traceable uncertainty budget.

Our primary standard references OSEAN-SIO and are cross-validated against WOCE hydrographic data. For users requiring in-situ recalibration, we offer the TideCal portable reference system.
