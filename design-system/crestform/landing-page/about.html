The company

About

Quire was built by type directors who spent decades solving the same compositional problems by hand.

Founded

2019

Team

12

Customers

3,400+

The tools available to type directors were built for graphic designers. Quire was built for us.

Quire began as an internal toolset at a small editorial design practice in Edinburgh. The founding team had spent two decades laying out books, journals, and annual reports in systems designed for advertising rather than publishing. Every project required the same workarounds: manual baseline grids, optical size adjustments done by eye, grid tokens that had to be rebuilt for every new tool in the chain.

In 2019, those workarounds became a product. The first version of Quire ran only on a single machine. By 2021, it was being used by twelve editorial studios across three continents. Today, Quire is used by type directors at major publishing houses, university presses, cultural institutions, and independent design studios.

The tool has changed. The principle has not. Quire is built to disappear — to measure, to reveal, to confirm. The design remains yours.

Principles

What we believe

I.

The grid precedes everything

Every typographic decision flows from a grid decision. The grid is not a delivery mechanism for content — it is the first and most fundamental act of editorial design.

II.

Precision is not perfectionism

Precision means knowing exactly what you are doing and why. A 4pt baseline grid is not pedantry — it is the difference between a page that reads effortlessly and one that accumulates invisible friction across 400 pages.

III.

Tools should disappear

The best compositional tool is one you stop thinking about. Quire is built to surface its intelligence and then get out of the way. The design remains yours — the decisions are never outsourced.
