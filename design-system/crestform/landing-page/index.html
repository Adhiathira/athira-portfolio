How it works

Precision built for precision work

Three interconnected systems — composition, type, and grid — designed to work together without compromise.

01

Compositional Intelligence

Quire reads your content and proposes grid structures that honor both the type hierarchy and the page architecture. Not automation — augmentation. The decisions remain yours.

Manifesto

III

Typography is not decoration. It is structure.

Every editorial decision is a spatial decision. Where a heading sits relative to a column edge, the measure of a paragraph line, the interval between a title and its subtitle — these are not aesthetic preferences. They are the architecture of comprehension.

Quire was built by type directors for type directors. Not as an automation layer, but as a precision instrument. The intelligence in the system is the intelligence of its makers — decades of editorial judgment encoded as tooling, then placed entirely in your hands.

We believe the best typographic tools disappear. They impose no style, suggest no aesthetic, prescribe no system. They measure, they reveal, they confirm. The design remains yours.
