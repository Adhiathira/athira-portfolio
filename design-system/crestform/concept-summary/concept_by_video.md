# Motion Brief: Crestform

---

## Scroll Opening

The page arrives already resolved — no loading state, no entrance fanfare. The navigation bar is present from the first frame, fixed and unhurried, its letter-spaced links legible against whatever is below. The hero photograph fills the viewport instantly, the modal card appearing with a single, clean 0.6s fade. There is no reveal sequence — the design earns its authority through restraint, not spectacle.

---

## Hero Experience

The hero is a full-bleed editorial photograph — warm-toned, figure-forward, tonal distribution intentionally designed to hold the white modal card in a quiet midrange zone. The photograph itself is the ambient video candidate: a slow, nearly imperceptible drift across the surface, as if the image is breathing. No panning, no zooming — only the slightest sensation of depth, as though light is shifting across fabric and skin.

**Designated video background:** hero photograph area, replaced with 10-second ambient loop. The subject is photographed in editorial stillness — fashion-adjacent, monochrome-dominant, slow light movement from frame left. The loop should be so subtle that a viewer cannot pinpoint the moment it restarts.

---

## Section Transitions

Section transitions are governed by opacity, not movement. As sections scroll into view, content elements fade in with a 0.6s ease curve — no slide, no bounce. The 0.1s stagger delay between sibling elements gives each transition a sense of sequence without being theatrical. The overall impression is of a document being read, not a presentation being delivered.

---

## Content Rhythm

Individual elements enter the viewport through the established fadeIn sequence. The stagger introduces a quiet cadence — first the heading, then the subheading, then the body text, then the CTA — each arriving 0.1s after the last. The typeface scale is extreme (206px h1, 384px h2 in source), meaning headings arrive as field events rather than typographic details. The animation budget must respect this: slow enough to register the letterforms, fast enough not to feel labored.

---

## Footer Approach

The footer is approached without ceremony. No reveal animation, no large-scale transition. The last section's content completes its stagger, and the footer resolves quietly below. The footer itself uses no animation — it is a functional zone, and the design treats it accordingly. The closing motion principle: the page ends the way it begins, with restraint.

---

## Motion Principles

The timing philosophy is **deliberate deceleration** — not slow for its own sake, but paced to match the visual weight of the content. At 0.6s for primary animations and 0.15s for micro-interactions, the system creates two distinct temporal registers: the slow time of content arrival and the fast time of interface response. These never bleed into each other. The stagger delay (0.1s per item) is the system's only rhythmic gesture — regular, metronome-like, never varying.

## Video Placements

### 1. Hero Section
- **Asset filename:** `hero-ambient.mp4`
- **Duration:** 10s loop
- **What it shows:** A fashion-editorial figure in near-stillness, warm neutral-greige environment, slow light drift from frame left — the kind of footage that lives between photograph and motion. Fabric texture, controlled depth of field, no discernible narrative movement.
- **Why video:** The hero photograph's entire compositional logic depends on tonal distribution — the greige mid-tone holds the modal card. A slow ambient video preserves this tonal relationship while adding the temporal quality of presence. A still image freezes time; a near-still video holds it.

### 2. Product Showcase / Brand Wordmark Section
- **Asset filename:** `brand-surface.mp4`
- **Duration:** 5s loop
- **What it shows:** An extreme close-up of a material surface — paper, marble, or coated stock — with slow lateral light rake. Texture becomes visible, then subsides. No legible branding in frame.
- **Why video:** The brand wordmark section is full-bleed display typography. A subtle looping material texture in the background gives the letterforms physical presence — the type gains weight and gravity against a surface that is perceivably real, not flat.

### 3. Editorial Content Band
- **Asset filename:** `editorial-still.mp4`
- **Duration:** 5s loop
- **What it shows:** A photographic moment held in near-stillness — a room corner, morning light through a window, shadow moving slowly across a white wall. The kind of image that earns attention through duration, not motion.
- **Why video:** This section is purely editorial — long-form text over a quiet background. A 5s ambient loop at very low motion level transforms what would be a static background into a living surface without competing with the typography. The video serves atmosphere, not information.
