Coating systems maintained on-site

Regalrez 1094 & 1126

Low-molecular-weight hydrocarbon resins with excellent optical clarity and full reversibility in mineral spirits. Regalrez 1094 (lower viscosity, gloss to satin) is our primary system for canvas. Regalrez 1126 is used where a slightly higher tack is needed for consolidation work on flaking paint layers.

All coating
systems

Our materials are sourced through Kremer Pigmente (Amsterdam) and Conservation Resources (Oxford). All are ISO or ASTM rated for archival permanence.

R

Regalrez Series

Regalrez 1094, 1126. Primary varnish and consolidant systems for oil and acrylic on canvas. UV-stable, mineral spirits reversible. ASTM D4303 rated for lightfastness.

G

Gamblin Conservation

Gamblin Conservation Varnish (matte and gloss), Gamsol for removal. Excellent for single-layer varnish application on oil paintings requiring long-term storage protection.

L

Lascaux Series

Lascaux 303 HV, 498HV for barrier coatings on acrylic and mixed-media. Water-reversible formulations available for works on paper with water-sensitive media.
