Methodology

Process

Every treatment follows a four-stage protocol. Nothing is applied before the work is fully understood.

The four stages

Precision at every step

01

Assessment

Raking-light examination, UV fluorescence, and fibre-optic analysis to map the condition of the work before any treatment begins. We document every area of instability, previous restoration, and surface contamination.

02

Selection

We test candidate coatings in micro-patches at the margin, matching refractive index, sheen level, and reversibility to the specific substrate. No system is applied site-wide until the test patch confirms compatibility after 48-hour cure.

03

Application

Brush, spray, or vacuum application in a climate-controlled environment at 50% RH and 20°C. Every pass is documented by weight and thickness measurement. For multi-layer systems, each layer cures fully before the next is applied.

04

Verification

Post-treatment spectrophotometry confirms colour integrity against the pre-treatment baseline. Full condition report, coating system record, and reversibility protocol supplied with every work returned to the client or institution.

Standards & ethics

Conservation
principles

Our work follows the professional ethics of the European Confederation of Conservator-Restorers' Organisations (ECCO). Every intervention is minimal, reversible, and documented.

I

Minimum Intervention

We do only what is necessary to stabilise and protect. No aesthetic re-interpretation, no visual enhancement unless explicitly requested and documented as such.

II

Reversibility

Every coating system we apply can be removed by a qualified conservator without damage to the original surface. We do not use irreversible treatments in standard practice.

III

Full Documentation

Condition before treatment, all products applied (by name, batch, and concentration), method, environmental conditions, and post-treatment condition — all recorded and delivered.
