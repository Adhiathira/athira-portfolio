Studio

A small practice dedicated entirely to archival surface treatments. We work with private collectors, commercial galleries, and public institutions.

About

Who we are

Velum was founded in 2009 by conservator Marte de Vries, following a decade of institutional practice at the Rijksmuseum and the Stedelijk. The studio occupies a purpose-built space on Prinsengracht with humidity and temperature control matched to conservation-grade standards.

I

Marte de Vries

Founder and lead conservator. Trained at the Stichting Restauratie Atelier Limburg (SRAL), specialising in works on paper and photographic materials. Member of the Netherlands Institute for Conservation + Art + Science (NICAS).

II

Pieter Salomons

Senior conservator, canvas and painted works. Trained at the Universiteit van Amsterdam, MA Conservation and Restoration. Previously head of paintings conservation at the Museum Boijmans Van Beuningen, Rotterdam.

III

The Studio Space

1,200 m² across two floors on Prinsengracht. Ground floor: climate-controlled treatment bays, spray booth, and vacuum tables. First floor: documentation suite with UV fluorescence, spectrophotometry, and raking-light recording.

What guides us

Principles of practice

I

The work is primary

Nothing we do overrides the integrity of the work. Treatment decisions are always secondary to understanding what the work is and what it requires.

II

Documentation is treatment

A treatment without a full written record is no treatment at all. Every action is dated, recorded by name of practitioner, and retained in perpetuity in the studio archive.

III

Reversibility is non-negotiable

If a future conservator cannot undo what we have done without harming the original, we should not do it. We refuse commissions where this principle cannot be upheld.

IV

Small by choice

We take on fewer than 250 works per year. This is not a limitation — it is how we maintain the standard of attention that each work deserves.
