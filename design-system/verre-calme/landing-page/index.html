How it works

Four steps to permanence

01

Assessment

Raking-light examination, UV fluorescence, and fibre-optic analysis to map the condition of the work before any treatment.

02

Selection

We test candidate coatings in micro-patches at the margin, matching refractive index, sheen level, and reversibility to the specific substrate.

03

Application

Brush, spray, or vacuum application in a climate-controlled environment at 50% RH. Every pass is documented by weight and thickness measurement.

04

Verification

Post-treatment spectrophotometry confirms colour integrity. Full condition report and reversibility protocol supplied for the conservation record.
