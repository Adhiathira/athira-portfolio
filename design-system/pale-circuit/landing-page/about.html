Our Story

Built by Engineers Who Know the Difference

Ossian grew from a simple frustration: every engineering team we knew was duct-taping their workflow together across five tools that didn't speak to each other. We built the integration they needed.

Origin

The Problem We Couldn't Ignore

Hardware engineering has a precision problem — not in the work, but in the tools. Schematics live in one place, calibration records in another, project milestones in a third. Each hand-off introduces a gap where decisions get lost, context gets stripped, and approvals happen without full information.

We started Ossian in 2022 after watching a calibration failure slip through a four-tool handoff at a medical device company. The failure wasn't in the engineering — the spec was right. The failure was in the system: a measurement record that never made it to the sign-off document. Ossian exists to close that gap.

Today, Ossian connects the complete engineering workflow — from schematic to calibration sign-off — in a single platform designed to the same tolerances as the hardware it supports.

What We Hold to Standard

01

Precision by Design

Approximation is a decision, not a default. Every feature we ship is designed to work within the tolerances of engineering work — not software project management stretched to fit.

02

Complete Traceability

Every measurement, every revision, every approval — logged with a timestamp and operator identity. Ossian produces the audit trail that compliance demands and good engineering deserves.

03

Zero-Gap Integration

The handoff is where failures happen. Ossian's architecture eliminates it — schematic, layout, BOM, calibration, and sign-off exist in the same data model, not in separate files.
