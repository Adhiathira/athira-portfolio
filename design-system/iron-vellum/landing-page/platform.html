The Platform

Everything the soil investigation requires.

Field capture, laboratory integration, and standards-compliant report generation — connected end-to-end, with a complete audit trail at every step.

→ assets/platform-overview.jpeg
Field Capture Lab Integration Reports Audit Chain
01 / Field Capture

Structured field data from the first horizon description.

Sampling forms are calibrated to Australian Soil Classification and FAO standards. GPS-tagged profiles, full horizon sequences, texture by feel, and Munsell colour — entered on mobile, available on desktop the moment you return to network.

  • Offline-first mobile capture — no data loss in remote paddocks
  • Horizon notation guided to AS 4419 and USDA terminology
  • GPS point snapping with elevation and datum recording
  • Photo attachment per horizon with depth annotation
  • Batch sync on return — all profiles matched to project automatically
→ assets/capture-mobile.jpeg
02 / Lab Integration

Results arrive already mapped to site and depth.

Direct connections to 38 NATA-accredited laboratories across Australia and New Zealand. Results import automatically into the correct sampling site and depth interval — no copy-paste, no transposition risk.

38

NATA-accredited laboratory connections pre-configured

4h

Median time from laboratory dispatch to results in your project

0

Manual data entry steps between laboratory results and your report

ISO

17025-accredited result validation on import — flagged before they reach your report

03 / Reports

From data to compliant document in minutes.

Reports generated to AS 4419, AS/NZS 2430, and relevant EPA method standards. Every report includes profile tables, horizon interpretations, contamination flags, and recommendations — structured for the regulator, readable for the client.

AS 4419

Agricultural and Land Management

Full soil description to Australian standards — profile morphology, classification, and land capability assessment in a single structured output.

AS/NZS 2430

Classification for Engineering Purposes

Unified Soil Classification System tables, Atterberg limits, bearing capacity estimates, and geotechnical risk flags — formatted for submission to engineering review.

EPA

Environmental Investigation Reports

Contamination screening against site-specific criteria, comparison tables with health investigation levels, and investigation strategy documentation to EPA guidelines.

Custom

Client and Internal Templates

Organisation templates for recurring clients — consistent structure, visual hierarchy, and section ordering applied to every project without manual formatting.

04 / Audit Chain

Nothing changes without a record.

Every field observation, laboratory result, and interpretive decision is timestamped, versioned, and attributed. Solum's audit trail satisfies the evidentiary standard for regulatory submissions and legal proceedings.

Version History

Every edit creates a version. Compare any two states of a project record side-by-side.

Attribution Log

Every entry records the operator, device, time, and GPS coordinates at point of capture.

Chain of Custody

Sample provenance traceable from field profile to laboratory container barcode to result table.

Regulatory Export

One-click export of the complete data chain as a structured PDF annex — accepted by state EPA, council, and federal agency review panels without additional documentation requests.
