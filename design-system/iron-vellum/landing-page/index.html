What Solum Does

Precision at every layer.

From sampling protocol to final report, Solum builds an unbroken chain of custody for your soil data — every measurement traceable, every result defensible.

  • Field Data Capture

    Structured sampling forms calibrated to soil taxonomy standards. GPS-tagged profiles, horizon descriptions, and texture classifications captured offline and synced on return.

  • Laboratory Integration

    Direct connections to NATA-accredited laboratories. Results import automatically, matched to the correct sampling site, depth interval, and project reference.

  • Structured Report Generation

    Reports generated to AS 4419, ASNZS 2430, and EPA method standards. Profile tables, interpretation text, and recommendation summaries in a single exportable document.

  • Audit-Ready Data Chain

    Every field observation, laboratory result, and interpretive decision is timestamped, versioned, and attributed. Nothing changes without a record.

The Workflow

From site to report in four steps.

  • 01

    Plan your investigation

    Define project scope, site extents, and sampling density. Solum generates a field schedule aligned to your soil classification objectives.

  • 02

    Record observations in the field

    Complete structured profile descriptions on mobile, with guided horizon and texture notation. No internet required.

  • 03

    Integrate laboratory results

    Upload or connect directly to your preferred laboratory. Results merge with field data automatically, mapped to site and depth.

  • 04

    Generate the report

    Select your output standard and export. Profile tables, interpretive text, site plans, and recommendations — complete and compliant.
