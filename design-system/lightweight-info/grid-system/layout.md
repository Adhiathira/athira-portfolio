# Brand Website - Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The site follows a **vertical storytelling layout** with discrete, full-viewport sections that transition as users scroll. Think of it as a digital lookbook or editorial magazine—each section is a self-contained "chapter" with its own visual rhythm.

The page has **approximately 8-10 major sections**, each occupying near-full or full viewport height. Sections alternate between dark and light backgrounds to create visual rhythm and guide the eye through the narrative. The overall flow is: Hero → Section A → Section B → Section C → Product Showcase → Video/Media.

---

## Header/Navigation Layout

**Fixed header, always visible:**
- **Left:** Brand wordmark in editorial italic serif
- **Center:** Horizontal navigation menu (5 items in clean sans-serif uppercase)
- **Right:** Search input field with icon
- **Background:** Semi-transparent or blurred backdrop that adapts to section background (dark on dark sections, light on light sections)

Header height is compact (~60-80px), maintaining breathing room without dominating vertical space.

---

## Hero Section

**Full-viewport, cinematic opening:**
- Large, centered product photograph serving as the hero visual—occupies ~70% of viewport
- Minimal text overlay centered above the product:
  - Small eyebrow text
  - Large display heading (broken into two lines)
  - CTA button below with pill-shaped outline styling
- **Background:** Deep black/charcoal with dramatic lighting on the product
- **Circular guide rings:** Subtle concentric circle line-art emanates from the product center, creating depth and focus
- **No explicit columns**—all content is center-aligned and layered over the hero image

---

## Section Navigation (Persistent Sidebar)

**Left-edge vertical index:**
- Thin vertical line running the full page height on the far left (~30-40px from edge)
- Three numbered anchor links stacked vertically:
  - "01 SECTION A"
  - "02 SECTION B"  
  - "03 SECTION C"
- Typography: Small caps, minimal weight, subtle color
- Acts as a visual anchor and progress indicator as users scroll
- **Plus signs (+)** at top-left and top-right corners (and repeated at section breaks)—decorative crosshair motifs that reinforce precision/engineering theme

---

## Content Sections (Repeating Patterns)

### Pattern A: Large Image Left, Text Right (Asymmetric Split)
**Used for Section A and Section B**

- **Image:** ~55-60% width, full-height bleed on the left
- **Text block:** ~35-40% width on the right, vertically centered
  - Small eyebrow label
  - Large heading (2-3 words, bold sans-serif or expanded font)
  - Body paragraph (2-4 sentences, generous line-height)
- **Background:** Light grey or off-white
- **Decorative circles:** Large circular guide/outline elements float behind content, partially cropped at edges—adds layering and depth
- **Negative space:** Generous padding around text block (~60-80px)

**Visual rhythm:** The large product photography dominates; text feels like a caption or annotation, not competing for attention.

---

### Pattern B: Text Left, Large Image Right (Reverse Asymmetric)
**Used for Section C**

- Same proportions as Pattern A, but **mirrored**: text on left (~40%), image on right (~60%)
- This alternating left/right pattern creates a dynamic "zigzag" reading flow down the page
- Same decorative circles, same generous white space

---

### Pattern C: Centered Product Showcase
**Used for featured product section**

- Product image centered, ~50% width, occupying left half of viewport
- Text block on right, similar to Pattern A
- **Full-viewport height**—this section feels like a "pause" or focal point in the scroll journey
- Background returns to light grey
- Primary CTA button below text

---

### Pattern D: Full-Width Media Section
**Video/Interactive section near bottom**

- **Full-width video embed** (~16:9 aspect ratio, or custom cinematic ratio)
- Dark background (black or very dark grey)
- **Play button** overlay (white pill-shaped button, top-right corner)
- Minimal chrome—no visible player controls until interaction
- This section feels like a "reveal" or climax after the product tour

## Notable Layout Patterns & Behaviors

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Full-bleed photography** throughout—images touch edges, creating immersion
- **Alternating dark/light sections** (dark hero → light content → light content → dark video) create visual "chapters"
- **Generous negative space**—text blocks never feel cramped; ~20-25% of each section is empty space

### Layering & Depth
- **Circular guide lines:** Repeated motif of thin concentric circles behind content—feels technical, precise, and adds subtle motion/parallax opportunity
- **Photography depth:** Shallow depth-of-field in many shots, with sharp product focus and blurred backgrounds—reinforces craftsmanship narrative
- **Overlapping elements:** Text sometimes overlaps decorative circles, creating z-axis hierarchy

### Proportional Relationships
- **60/40 split** is the dominant asymmetric ratio (image vs. text)
- **Typography scale:** Headings are 3-4x the size of body text, creating strong hierarchy
- **Vertical rhythm:** Each section is ~100vh (full viewport), encouraging scroll-triggered reveals or snapping

### Interaction Hints
- **Plus signs (+)** at corners suggest expandable content or section markers
- **Sidebar navigation numbers** imply click-to-jump or progress tracking
- **Pill-shaped CTAs** with outline styling (not solid fills) feel elegant and clickable
- Likely **scroll-triggered animations**: fade-ins, parallax on circular guides, image reveals

### Grid Philosophy
- Not a traditional 12-column grid—more like **two-column asymmetric splits** (major/minor columns)
- **Center axis alignment** in hero section
- **Edge-to-edge thinking**: Content pushes to edges; no centered container constraining width
- **Modular sections**: Each section is self-contained; you could reorder them without breaking layout logic

---

## Design Language Summary

**Cinematic minimalism with editorial pacing.** The layout feels like leafing through a high-end editorial magazine—each spread (section) has a single focus, and the rhythm alternates between product beauty shots and explanatory text. The circular guide elements and sidebar navigation reinforce the core product theme without being literal. The generous white space and asymmetric splits feel confident and luxurious, letting the product photography breathe. The dark hero and video sections act as bookends, creating a clear narrative arc from introduction to deeper engagement.
