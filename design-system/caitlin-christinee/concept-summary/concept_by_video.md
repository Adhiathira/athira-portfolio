# Caitlin Christinee — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads with the hero already fully rendered — no loading screen, no entry animation sequence. The subject's name and navigation appear at full opacity over the lifestyle background image from the first frame. The interface arrives complete rather than assembling itself.

## Hero Experience

The hero is a full-bleed lifestyle photograph with the page wordmark and navigation floating above it in transparent overlay mode. Text occupies the image's natural negative space rather than a dedicated text zone — the image and the label coexist on the same surface. A brief text fade-in executes on load: the name and navigation appear from opacity 0 to 1 over approximately 400ms. This is the site's single moment of intentional entrance animation. No background video plays in the hero; the lifestyle still image carries the full atmospheric weight.

## Section Transitions

Below the hero, the homepage transitions to the horizontal media gallery without any crossing animation or visual device. The content simply begins after the hero ends — a hard section boundary with no motion at the seam. The horizontal gallery does not animate its items into view; they are present and scrollable immediately. The links page and shop page load as fresh navigations rather than in-page scrolls, each arriving fully rendered.

## Content Rhythm

There is effectively no scroll-triggered content rhythm. Individual elements do not animate as they enter the viewport — no fade-ins on gallery thumbnails, no staggered card reveals, no counter animations. The page is a static document that responds to scroll by revealing pre-rendered content rather than choreographing its arrival. The one animated moment — the hero text fade-in — is a page-load event, not a scroll event.

## Footer Approach

The footer, if present, arrives as a continuation of the page's flat document scroll rather than a distinct cinematic arrival. No animation, no background shift, no special treatment at the page's end. The scroll journey ends quietly.

## Motion Principles

The motion philosophy is near-total restraint: use animation only at the page's single entry moment (hero fade-in), then let the content stand without motion support. Duration is short (300–400ms), easing is standard ease-out, and no spring or bounce is present. The system makes no distinction between sections — every part of the page after the hero receives identical non-animated treatment. This is not an oversight; it reflects a content hierarchy in which the photography is the experience and the interface exists purely to frame it.

## Video Placements

This portfolio does not call for ambient video. The design is built on the premise that a lifestyle still image at full bleed carries more editorial control than video — it fixes a moment rather than looping one. The interface's restraint extends to its media type: photography, not motion, is the primary expressive medium.

If video were incorporated in a future version, the only defensible placement would be a reel or compilation clip embedded within the horizontal content gallery — a documented work sample rather than an atmospheric backdrop. Even then, it would be muted, contained within the existing gallery frame, and treated as a content item rather than a background element.

**Recommended video placements: none.** The site earns its presence through the authority of a single well-chosen still image over the hero, not through motion.
