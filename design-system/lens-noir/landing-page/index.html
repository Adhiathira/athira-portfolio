The Workflow

Three Acts

Production has a natural dramatic structure. Montage is built around it.

Act I

Breakdown

Upload your script. Montage reads every scene, extracts cast, props, locations, VFX, wardrobe, and special requirements — in minutes, not days. Your first breakdown, ready before your first production meeting.

  • AI script analysis across all 12 breakdown categories
  • Scene strip generation with department flags
  • Budget estimate from page count and complexity
  • One-shot export to PDF or shared board
Act II

Schedule

Build your shooting schedule around real constraints: cast availability, location permits, department prep days, and weather windows. Montage optimizes for days, not drama.

  • Conflict-aware scheduling engine
  • Automated call sheet generation daily
  • Real-time day-out-of-days for every cast member
  • Location availability calendar integration
Act III

Production

On set, the plan meets reality. Montage keeps every department synced — changes propagate instantly. Shot logs, continuity notes, and budget actuals update in real time.

  • Live production dashboard for all departments
  • Change log with instant cascade notifications
  • Continuity and script supervisor tools
  • Budget actuals vs. estimates in real time
