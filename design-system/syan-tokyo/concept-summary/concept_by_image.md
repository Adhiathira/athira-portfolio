# Syan Tokyo — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A creative studio or agency site that treats the browser viewport as a print canvas. The design's primary architectural decision is to abandon the conventions of web layout — grid systems, section dividers, boxed components — in favor of composition-driven page construction drawn directly from print and editorial precedent. The interface uses one typeface, two colors (black and cyan), no borders, no backgrounds, and no decorative elements. Everything that appears on screen — text, image, space — is purposeful and precisely placed.

## Color Architecture

The palette is absolutely minimal: pure white background (`#ffffff`), black text and headings, and a single vivid cyan or turquoise accent as the sole chromatic presence. The accent color is used with extreme economy — reserved exclusively for active navigation link states and primary CTA buttons — making it a high-signal element: wherever cyan appears, action is possible. Because the background is pure white throughout every section of the site, the nav requires no scroll-adaptive behavior; black text on white is always legible. This is a design that earns its white space rather than filling it.

## Typography System

A single, lightweight geometric sans-serif typeface governs the entire interface — no secondary face, no serif contrast partner. The type system creates hierarchy through scale, case, and tracking rather than through family variation. Section titles and navigation links appear in small all-caps with generous letter-spacing, reading as categories or labels. Body text and personnel descriptions use sentence case at a legible body size. A distinctive framing element: vertically-oriented text labels are positioned on the far left and right edges of the viewport — secondary navigation or descriptive text rotated 90°, creating a spatial frame around the main content. The custom circular dot cursor is a typographic-scale element that completes the system's precision.

## Spatial System

Three distinct layout modes are used across the site. The primary showcase pages (collection, works) use asymmetric composition-driven layout: images and text blocks placed on the page canvas with intentional offset, stagger, and negative space — the layout is designed like a spread in a high-quality editorial magazine rather than a website. The staff page uses a strict two-column grid with uniform gutters and consistent cell proportions — a complete structural shift that communicates the change from editorial to informational content. All pages close with a minimal centered CTA section: a headline, supporting text, and a pill-shaped button in the center of the viewport. These three modes (editorial, grid, minimal CTA) cover the site's entire structural vocabulary.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

Photography is the primary content on the showcase pages — it is the reason for the layout, not decoration within it. Images are static, rectangular (sharp 90° corners throughout), and placed at varying scales within the composition. The placement strategy is compositional rather than systematic: no repeated formula for where the image sits relative to its text pair. Photography on the staff page is uniformly styled (consistent lighting, background, and cropping) to serve the structural regularity of the two-column grid. The contrast between the loose, curated placement of editorial photography and the precise uniformity of staff headshots communicates the difference between the site's creative and organizational registers.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation is a three-zone horizontal bar fixed at the top of the viewport: wordmark far left, five primary navigation links centered, a solid-fill CTA button far right. The entire nav sits on the same transparent background as the page — it has no container or fill of its own. Because the page background is consistently white, the nav requires no scroll-adaptive behavior; all elements remain in their initial state throughout. A deliberate shape distinction exists between navigation CTAs: the header CTA uses a sharp rectangle while the bottom-of-page terminal CTA uses a pill shape. This distinction is systematic — sharp for persistent global navigation, pill for terminal page conversion — a shape-language encoding of two different functional registers.

## Overall Design Character

The site operates at an extreme end of the visual restraint spectrum. Its choices are conspicuous by their absence: no colored backgrounds, no decorative dividers, no shadows or elevation, no rounded corners except the terminal CTA, no secondary typeface, no imagery-as-background. What remains is a system of pure relationship: the relationship between the image and its text, between occupied space and empty space, between the static content and the cyan accent that marks possibility. The peripheral vertical text labels are the site's most distinctive formal decision — they use the full height of the viewport as a typographic dimension, treating the browser as a three-dimensional space rather than a scroll container.
