# Brand Name Age Gate — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

This is a **single-screen verification gate** — a full-viewport interstitial that occupies the entire browser window. There are no scrollable sections, no navigation, no footer in the traditional sense. Everything is centered vertically and horizontally within one unified composition. The entire page is bathed in a saturated, high-chroma brand color that functions as both background color and brand statement.

The visual hierarchy is strictly vertical and centered: logo → headline → form → supporting controls → legal copy → tagline. Everything stacks on the vertical centerline with no horizontal asymmetry whatsoever.

---

## Header / Navigation

**There is no navigation.** This is an interstitial gate — the site's actual navigation is deliberately withheld until verification passes. The Brand Name logotype at the top of the composition serves a purely brand-identification role, not a navigational one.

---

## Hero / Primary Composition

Since this is a single-screen layout, the entire page is effectively the "hero." Key spatial relationships:

- The **Brand Name logo** sits in the upper-center of the composition, roughly at the top third of the visible viewport. It's modestly sized — not oversized — functioning as a reassuring brand anchor rather than a dominant statement.
- Below it, a significant amount of **breathing room** (generous vertical white space) separates the logo from the headline.
- The **primary headline** is set in a large, bold, all-caps display typeface — the single largest text element on the page. It sits roughly at the optical center of the viewport, creating clear visual dominance.
- The **form group** sits immediately below the headline with tight coupling — the small instructional label text flows directly into the input fields without excessive gap.

The composition has a **diamond-like spatial gravity**: wide empty space at top and bottom, dense interaction zone in the middle third.

---

## Form Section (Core Interaction Zone)

The entry form is the functional heart of the page. Its layout:

- Multiple **equal-width input boxes** sit in a tight horizontal row, divided by thin vertical borders. They share a single outlined rectangular container, making the fields read as one unified input group rather than separate elements.
- An **action button** is flush-right, directly attached to the input group — it shares the same height and border treatment, making it feel like an additional cell in the row rather than a separate CTA button. This is a deliberate spatial choice that keeps the interaction compact and legible.
- The whole input assembly is center-aligned and narrow — it spans roughly 40% of the viewport width, reinforcing the intimacy and focus of the form.

---

## Secondary Controls (Below Form)

Immediately below the form, three stacked elements continue the vertical rhythm:

1. **Checkbox row** — small, centered, with a checkbox icon left of the label text. Compact and unobtrusive.
2. **Micro-copy line** — slightly smaller than the checkbox label, center-aligned.
3. A **blank spacer gap** separates this from the legal/consent block below.

---

## Legal / Consent Copy

A small block of fine-print legal text sits in the lower-center of the composition:

- Three short lines, centered, set in a noticeably smaller type size than everything above.
- Policy links appear as inline links within the copy.
- This block maintains the page's strict center alignment but visually recedes — it's clearly secondary information.

---

## Tagline / Footer

The very bottom of the composition carries a single centered tagline set in a medium-weight serif or display italic. It acts as the page's closing note — a brand value statement rather than functional UI. There is no footer in the conventional sense (no links, no sitemap, no copyright row).

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

### Strict Vertical Centerline
Every element — logo, headline, form, checkbox, legal copy, tagline — is horizontally centered. There is zero left/right asymmetry. The page reads as a formal, ceremonial composition, reinforcing the legal/compliance gravity of the interaction.

### Full-Bleed Solid Color
The brand color fills 100% of the viewport with no borders, panels, cards, or containers breaking the surface. The form fields and logo exist directly on this field. This creates a brand-immersive, almost poster-like quality.

### Tight Form + Button Integration
The inputs and action button are visually merged into a single horizontal component. This is intentional UX compression — it reduces cognitive load and makes the primary action feel inevitable.

### Proportional Breathing Space
The composition uses generous top and bottom margins, but the interactive zone is compressed into a relatively tight vertical band in the center. This creates a clear **visual funnel** toward the form, even though the page is "empty" in the traditional sense.

### No Sticky, No Overlay, No Z-Axis Layering
Everything is flat — a single layer, no shadows, no modals, no floating elements. The restraint is deliberate: the legal context of a verification gate demands clarity over decoration.
