How We Work

The Protocol

We operate more like an analytical chemistry laboratory than a traditional darkroom. Every variable is measured. Every decision is documented. Reproducibility is the standard.

The Method

Step by
Step

01

Order Submission

Complete the online booking form specifying format, emulsion, EI rating, process type, desired push-pull compensation, and any special instructions. We respond within 2 hours on business days to confirm receipt and provide a shipping label if needed.

// Online form · Email confirmation within 2h
02

Intake & Inspection

Each submission is logged with a barcode on receipt. We inspect every roll or sheet before development: cassette condition, film leader, winding direction, any unusual exposure. If we find anything that warrants consultation, we contact you before proceeding — we never make unilateral decisions about your film.

// Barcode tracking from intake to delivery · 15min inspection per submission
03

Chemistry Preparation

We mix developer fresh for each order using the concentration and dilution specified by your emulsion and push-pull target. We do not use replenished or recycled developer. Temperature is brought to target and verified by NIST-traceable thermometer before film enters the tank.

// Fresh chemistry per order · 20.0°C ±0.1°C verified
04

Development

Development time is calculated from our calibration curves for the specific emulsion batch and chemistry combination. Agitation is timed with a digital interval timer. We log temperature at start and finish of development. Stop bath, fix, and wash times are protocol-specified and not abbreviated.

// Digital interval timing · Temperature logged at start and finish
05

Drying & Cutting

Film dries in a filtered cabinet for a minimum of 2 hours before handling. 35mm is cut in strips of 6. Medium format is cut per frame or left in full strips per your instruction. Large format sheets are individually sleeved. We do not cut under light — all handling is in darkroom conditions until the film is fully dry and confirmed clear.

// 2h minimum dry time · Filtered cabinet · Cut to order specification
06

Scanning (if ordered)

Scanning follows development on the same business day. We select scan resolution based on your order: standard (2000 dpi), high (4000 dpi), or archive (6400 dpi). Every scan is dust-spotted. Color work receives a calibrated baseline exposure correction — not a creative grade. Files are delivered as 16-bit TIFF.

// 2000 / 4000 / 6400 dpi · 16-bit TIFF · Dust-spotted as standard
07

Documentation & Delivery

Every order is accompanied by a PDF development card. Negatives are packaged in archival polypropylene sleeves and shipped via tracked courier. Digital scans are delivered via encrypted download link within 24h of order completion. Download links remain active for 30 days; we archive your files for 90 days after delivery.

// PDF development card · Archival sleeves · 30-day download · 90-day archive

Film Formats

What We
Accept

35mm 135 Format

All standard cassettes. Bulk-rolled cassettes accepted. 12, 24, 36, and 40-exposure rolls. DX-coded and manual ISO setting supported.

120 Medium Format

All 120 emulsions. 220 accepted with equipment note. 6×4.5, 6×6, 6×7, 6×9, and 6×12 formats. Panoramic accepted.

4×5 Large Format

Standard Grafmatic and film holder loading. Cut film in any emulsion. Fuji QuickLoad and Kodak ReadyLoad accepted.

5×7 Large Format

Cut film in archival sleeves. Standard tray development. B&W processes only for this format.

8×10 Large Format

Cut film in archival sleeves. Tray development in complete darkness. Contact printing available directly from 8×10 negatives.

Questions

Common
Questions

Can I specify a non-standard development time?

+

Yes. If you have your own development card with times, temperatures, and agitation intervals, send it with your film and we will follow it exactly. We document whether we followed your protocol or ours on the development card we return with your negatives.

What happens if you make an error in development?

+

We redevelop at no charge if the error is within our process and the negatives can be corrected. If the film is lost or destroyed by our error, we refund the full order including film stock at replacement cost based on your receipt. We do not charge for consultation time on error-related orders.

How do you handle film that has been fogged or damaged in transit?

+

We photograph the packaging on receipt if damage is visible. If we believe film has been fogged before processing, we contact you before any chemistry touches it. We will not process film that shows evidence of light leaks in the cassette without your explicit instruction to proceed.

Do you accept film from outside the country?

+

Yes. International orders are accepted. We recommend declaring film as "undeveloped photographic film" on customs forms and using a courier that does not X-ray small packages. We can provide a letter for customs describing the contents if needed.

Can I come in person to drop off and discuss my project?

+

Yes, by appointment. We are not a walk-in facility. Studio visits are available Tuesdays and Thursdays from 10am–4pm. Use the contact form to request a visit and describe your project. We'll confirm within 24 hours.
