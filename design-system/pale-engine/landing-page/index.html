What We Do

The Method

Precision is
a Protocol

From intake to delivery, every step is documented and traceable. We operate more like an analytical laboratory than a darkroom.

01

Intake & Assessment

We inspect every roll before development. Damaged leaders, unusual winding, or non-standard canister types are flagged and communicated before processing begins. Nothing proceeds without your confirmation.

// 15-minute inspection window per submission
02

Chemistry Selection

Developer, stop, fix, and wash protocols are selected per emulsion. We maintain 23 active chemistry configurations, documented by spectrographic analysis.

// Rodinal / D-76 / HC-110 / Pyrocat-HD / PMK Pyro — all fresh batches
03

Controlled Development

Temperature maintained to ±0.1°C. Agitation interval held to ±2 seconds. We use calibrated digital timers, not intuition.

// 20.0°C ± 0.1°C — verified by NIST-traceable thermometer
04

Documentation & Delivery

Every order ships with a development card: exact times, temperatures, chemistry batch ID, and agitation protocol. Your negatives are reproducible by this record.

// PDF development card included with every order
