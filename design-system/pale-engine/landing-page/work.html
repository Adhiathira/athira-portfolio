Featured Case

Owen St. Claire — British Columbia Survey

Owen St. Claire's landscape work requires extreme tonal control. The foggy conditions of the British Columbia coast produce naturally low-contrast negatives — he exposes to the high end of Zone VII for local detail, then pulls development to compress highlights without sacrificing midtone separation.

We developed this series in Pyrocat-HD at N-1, then printed platinum-palladium contact prints for his exhibition. The staining developer provided additional UV density for the contact printing process — the prints hold detail in zones that silver gelatin would block entirely.
