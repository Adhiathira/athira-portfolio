01 / Development

Push-Pull Development

From $18 / roll (35mm)

Push-pull development is the most variable process in film photography, and the most mishandled. Most labs push by adjusting time only. We adjust time, temperature, agitation interval, and sometimes developer dilution — because each emulsion responds differently to underexposure, and a single variable never tells the full story.

We work with Rodinal, D-76, HC-110, Pyrocat-HD, PMK Pyro, Xtol, and FX-39II. If you have a specific developer you want us to use, send it with your film and we'll use it. Every batch is mixed fresh. We do not use replenished developer.

Formats 35mm · 120 · 4×5 · 5×7 · 8×10
Push range N-3 to N+4
Temperature control ±0.1°C — NIST-traceable
Documentation PDF development card per order
Turnaround 24h standard · 4h rush
02 / Scanning

Large-Format Scanning

From $22 / sheet (4×5)

We scan on Imacon Flextight and Epson V850 depending on format and resolution requirement. All scans are delivered as 16-bit TIFF with embedded ICC profile. We do not deliver compressed JPEGs for archival work — if you need web-optimized files, we include them in addition to the archival master, not instead.

We dust-spot every scan before delivery. This is not a premium add-on. It is the standard. Delivering a scan with visible dust is delivering an incomplete product.

Formats 35mm · 120 · 4×5 · 5×7 · 8×10
Maximum resolution 6400 dpi (Flextight) · 2400 dpi (V850)
Bit depth 16-bit TIFF · ICC profile embedded
Delivery Encrypted download · 30-day cloud archive
Turnaround 48h standard · 12h rush
03 / Printing

Archival Contact Printing

From $140 / print

Contact printing from large-format negatives at 1:1 scale. We print silver gelatin and platinum-palladium on Arches Platine, Revere Platinum, and Canson Platine Fibre Rag. All platinum-palladium prints are coated in-house using Na2 and Ferric Oxalate sensitizers.

Minimum projected permanence for platinum-palladium on Arches Platine: 500 years under standard museum storage conditions. We provide a material certificate with each print documenting paper lot, sensitizer batch, and development chemistry.

Processes Platinum-palladium · Silver gelatin
Papers Arches Platine · Revere · Canson Platine
Maximum size 11×14" contact from 8×10 negative
Permanence 200–500 yr projected (process dependent)
Documentation Material certificate per print
