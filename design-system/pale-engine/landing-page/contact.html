Before You Ship

Good to
Know

→

Shipping

We accept film via any tracked courier. For international orders, declare film as "undeveloped photographic film — no commercial value" on customs forms. We can provide a supporting letter for customs if needed.

◎

Packaging

Film cassettes travel well in their original black film canisters or Jobo cassette holders. Flat rate priority boxes lined with bubble wrap work well for large-format film holders. Do not use envelopes for large-format sheets.

◈

Returns

Negatives are returned in archival polypropylene sleeves via tracked courier. We will use your carrier account if you provide a label. Otherwise, return shipping is billed at cost — we do not mark up shipping.
