How it works

From dimensions
to treatment plan.

  1. 01

    Enter your room geometry

    Input width, length, height, and surface materials. Lōmen accepts rectangular rooms and irregular shapes via simple coordinate input. No CAD software required.

  2. 02

    Choose your material library

    Select from 600+ validated material profiles — concrete, plywood, acoustic foam, glass, carpet, gypsum board — or upload custom absorption coefficient tables from your supplier's datasheets.

  3. 03

    Run the acoustic analysis

    Lōmen computes RT60, early decay time, modal frequency density, SBIR predictions, and flutter echo probability across all octave bands in under 8 seconds.

  4. 04

    Receive your treatment recommendations

    A prioritized list of acoustic treatment interventions — bass trap placement, diffuser positions, broadband absorber coverage — with projected results for each addition. Exportable as a PDF report for your contractor.
