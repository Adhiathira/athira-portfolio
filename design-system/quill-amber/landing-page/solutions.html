Recording Studios

Control room prediction before the first pour

Modal analysis and RT60 prediction let you validate your room geometry and treatment plan before concrete is poured or timber is fixed. Identify flutter echo corridors, bass mode clusters, and SBIR nulls at the mix position — with the option to iterate geometry until the numbers are right.

  • Sabine + Eyring RT60 at 7 octave bands
  • SBIR prediction for speaker placement
  • 3D modal node visualization
  • Treatment specification with projected improvement
  • ISO 3382 compliance report export

Home Theatre

Cinema-grade acoustics in domestic spaces

Residential rooms break every acoustic rule in the book — non-parallel walls, low ceilings, mixed surface materials. Lōmen models the exact geometry and flags listening position SPL variance before a single panel is ordered. Specify bass traps, diffusers, and absorption by location with budget-aware quantity estimates.

  • Irregular room geometry support
  • Listening position SPL uniformity analysis
  • Domestic material library (gypsum, carpet, glass)
  • Treatment placement map export
  • Before/after RT60 comparison view

Architectural Acoustics

Specification-grade prediction for design documentation

Architects and acoustic consultants use Lōmen to generate ISO 354-grounded specifications for concert halls, lecture theatres, office spaces, and hospitality interiors. Import room geometry from BIM references, run multi-zone analysis, and export PDF specifications that match contractor tender formats.

  • Multi-zone room analysis
  • 600+ ISO 354 material profiles
  • BIM-compatible geometry input (CSV/JSON)
  • Contractor-ready PDF specification export
  • Noise isolation (STC) overlay analysis

Broadcast & Podcast

Noise floors that pass technical delivery

Broadcast studios and podcast rooms live and die by their noise floor and room character. Lōmen models IIC impact isolation, airborne STC paths, and residual reverberation — then maps the treatment requirements to standard acoustic panel types available from the major manufacturers, with quantities and placement coordinates.

  • Airborne STC and impact IIC analysis
  • Residual RT60 at speech frequencies
  • Manufacturer panel quantity estimator
  • Background noise (NC curve) compliance check
  • On-axis microphone null analysis
