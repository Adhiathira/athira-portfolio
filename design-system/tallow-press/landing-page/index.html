The Method

Impression over reproduction

Every Guldmark edition begins with hand-set type — cast in lead, locked in the chase, inked by roller. The press exerts physical force. Paper receives an impression that no offset or digital process can replicate: a slight deboss, a density of ink that changes with the light.
