Chapter I

The Commission Process

1

Consultation

We begin with a conversation about your vision. Whether a single journal for daily reflection or a limited-edition artist's book, we listen to what the object should feel like in your hands.

2

Design

Paper weight, grain direction, binding structure, cover material. We present a selection of samples and sketch the architecture of your book, page by page.

3

Craft

Each sheet is formed by hand on a traditional mould. Signatures are folded, sewn, and bound in our English countryside studio. The process takes between three and eight weeks.

4

Delivery

Your finished book arrives wrapped in tissue, boxed in archival board. We include a certificate of craft describing the papers, materials, and binding method used.
