Tidal Ledger

Edition of 12 · Coptic stitch on indigo linen

A long-stitch codex inspired by the silt patterns of the Thames estuary. Each cover is individually dyed using botanical indigo harvested from Japanese polygonum and pressed between hand-formed cotton sheets. The text block holds 96 leaves of unbleached kozo, deckle-edged on all four sides.

Plate II

→ assets/plate-moth-atlas.jpeg

Moth Atlas

Edition of 8 · Accordion fold with letterpress plates

An accordion-bound atlas of nocturnal Lepidoptera, printed from polymer relief plates on a restored Vandercook press. Unfolds to reveal seven continuous spreads of specimen illustrations rendered in lamp-black ink on warm-toned gampi tissue.

Plate III

→ assets/plate-salt-margin.jpeg

Salt Margin

Edition of 15 · Exposed spine on stone-washed boards

An exposed-spine journal bound in naturally weathered millboard, with suminagashi-marbled endpapers floated on seawater collected from the Cornish coast. The binding reveals the linen thread structure as a deliberate decorative element.

Wren Quire

Quarter-bound · Tobacco calf · 180 leaves

Commissioned for a field ornithologist. Quarter-bound in vegetable-tanned calf with hand-marbled paste-paper sides, housing 180 leaves of acid-free Magnani laid paper chosen for its resistance to fountain pen feathering.

→ assets/journal-chalk-spine.jpeg

Chalk Spine

Full linen · Blind-tooled · 120 leaves

A writing journal for a calligrapher, bound in unbleached Belgian linen with the owner's cipher blind-tooled into the front board. The text block alternates between smooth wove and textured laid sheets for practice and finished work.

→ assets/journal-umber-folio.jpeg

Umber Folio

Oversized · Raw linen · 60 leaves heavyweight

An oversized landscape sketchbook for a botanical illustrator, sewn on raised cords with a flexible raw-linen spine. The 300gsm cotton paper accommodates wet media without cockling, and the binding opens flat to a full 180 degrees.

The Paper Collection

Flax Linen

160 gsm · Natural white

Formed from retted Belgian flax fibres on a laid mould, producing a subtle chain-line texture visible when held to the light. Ideal for letterpress and relief printing.

Cotton Rag

200 gsm · Warm ivory

Pure cotton linter pulp, vat-formed and air-dried between wool felts. A soft, absorbent sheet with exceptional permanence — archival without chemical buffering.

Mulberry Bark

45 gsm · Translucent cream

Kozo-family sheet beaten from inner mulberry bark. Translucent and remarkably strong for its weight, with long visible fibres that catch the light like threads of raw silk.

Kozo Washi

80 gsm · Off-white

Japanese-method kozo formed on a flexible bamboo su using nagashi-zuki technique. The rhythmic dipping produces a sheet with directional fibre alignment and gentle surface sheen.

Gampi Tissue

12 gsm · Gossamer white

The most refined sheet in the collection. Wild-harvested gampi bark produces a paper so thin it is nearly transparent, with a natural lustre that resembles parchment. Used for interleaving and conservation repairs.

Abaca Manila

120 gsm · Warm sand

Formed from Philippine abaca leaf fibres, producing a sheet with exceptional fold endurance and a warm, straw-toned colour. The surface accepts ink without bleeding, favoured for accordion-fold structures.
