Craft & Material

Paper that holds
the silver faithfully

Every Folia paper is coated on a baryta base — the same barium sulfate ground used in 20th-century darkrooms. The surface receives light with uniform grain structure, holding shadows in suspension without blocking highlight density. Printed images on Folia papers resist yellowing under museum glass for over a century under controlled conditions.

The Collection

Four grades.
One standard.

01

Velvet Matte

Our most requested surface. A fine-grained matte that renders shadow depth without the competing gloss of fibre gelatin. The velvet finish absorbs ambient light, eliminating specular reflection from the print surface. Ideal for portraits, architectural documentation, and any work where the image must be read without environmental interference.

Baryta Gloss

Maximum Dmax. For images that demand tonal range from pure white to absolute black. The gloss surface is not decorative — it is structural, enabling the silver layer to achieve maximum shadow depth.

Warm Satin

From slurry
to surface

Each sheet of Folia paper is produced in a controlled coating facility in Basel. The process has been refined over eighty years — automated where precision demands it, manual where judgement cannot be mechanised.

  1. 01

    Baryta Ground Preparation

    Barium sulfate is milled to a particle distribution of 0.8–1.2 μm and suspended in a gelatin matrix. The slurry is checked for viscosity and optical density before application. Any batch outside tolerance is returned for reprocessing.

  2. 02

    Base Coating

    The baryta layer is applied to cotton rag or resin-coated base stock at 34°C on a precision slot-die coater. Thickness is controlled to ±0.3 μm. The coated base is chilled and dried in a humidity-controlled chamber.

  3. 03

    Emulsion Application

    Silver halide emulsion is applied in low red-light conditions. The emulsion is formulated in-house for each paper grade — no licensed emulsion is used. Drying tension is monitored continuously to prevent surface curvature.

  4. 04

    Quality Inspection

    Each roll is step-wedge tested under standardised illuminant D50. Dmax, Dmin, and contrast curve are logged against the grade specification. Rolls outside specification are destroyed — not discounted, not sold as second-quality stock.

  5. 05

    Cut, Package, Ship

    Sheet sizes are cut to ±0.5 mm. Packaging is performed in a darkroom environment. Each box is dated, batch-coded, and vacuum-sealed. Cold-chain shipping is available for large studio orders.
