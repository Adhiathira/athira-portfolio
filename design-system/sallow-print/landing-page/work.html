FV-140 Velvet Matte

Mira Ortiz

Interior Light No. 12, 2023

Shot in a converted textile factory in Lodz during winter, the series uses available window light to explore reflective surfaces. Printed at 50 × 60 cm on FV-140 to retain the diffuse quality of the original light.

→ assets/work-jan-kowalski.jpeg

FG-220 Baryta Gloss

Jan Kowalski

Saltflat, Noon, 2022

Large-format landscape work from the Salar de Atacama. The Baryta Gloss was chosen for maximum Dmax — the shadow regions in the tire tracks needed to hold absolute black across a 70 × 90 cm print.

FR-300 Cotton Rag

Studio Halogen

Documentation Series, 2024

Museum-quality archival documentation of a private sculpture collection. The Cotton Rag substrate met the museum's conservation requirements for acid-free materials. Edition of 3, with storage specifications.
