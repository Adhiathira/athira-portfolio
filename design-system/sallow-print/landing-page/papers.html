01

Velvet Matte

A fine-grained matte surface that renders shadow depth without the competing gloss of fibre gelatin. The velvet finish absorbs ambient light, eliminating specular reflection. Ideal for portraits, architectural documentation, and any work where the image must be read without environmental interference. The most versatile surface in the Folia range — suited to both high-contrast and low-contrast subject matter.

Code FV-140
Weight 180 g/m²
Base Cotton Fibre
→ assets/grade-gloss.jpeg
02

Baryta Gloss

Maximum Dmax. The gloss surface enables the silver layer to achieve 3.85 Dmax — pure shadow density. For landscape, documentary, and any work where the full tonal range is the subject.

Code FG-220
Weight 220 g/m²
Dmax 3.85
→ assets/grade-satin.jpeg
03

Warm Satin

A slight warm cast in the base paper complements cold-toned chemistry and skin tone rendition. Between matte and gloss in surface sheen — the satin finish is preferred by portrait photographers working with split-tone developers. The warm base modulates shadow tone without requiring chemical toning. Pairs naturally with developer formulations that produce cooler shadows and warmer highlights.

Code FW-160
Weight 160 g/m²
Surface Semi-Gloss
→ assets/grade-cotton.jpeg
04

Cotton Rag

Acid-free cotton substrate for conservation-grade printing. Meets ISO 9706 requirements for permanent paper. For edition prints, museum acquisitions, and archival studio documentation.

Code FR-300
Weight 300 g/m²
Standard ISO 9706
