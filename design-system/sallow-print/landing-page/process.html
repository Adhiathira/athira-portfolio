3.85 Dmax — Baryta Gloss

The maximum optical density achievable on FG-220. A Dmax of 3.85 means the shadow regions absorb all but 0.014% of incident light — producing absolute black under any illumination condition. Measured at 23°C, relative humidity 50%, D50 illuminant.

Basel Coating Facility

The Folia production facility at Dornacherstrasse has operated continuously since 1938. The building has been extended three times — the coating hall, darkroom wing, and QA laboratory each occupy purpose-built structures added in 1962, 1981, and 2004 respectively.

The five stages

01

Baryta Ground Preparation

Barium sulfate is milled to a particle distribution of 0.8–1.2 μm and suspended in a gelatin matrix. The slurry is checked for viscosity and optical density before application. Any batch outside tolerance is returned for reprocessing. The milling stage takes four to six hours per batch.

→ assets/process-baryta.jpeg
02

Base Coating

The baryta layer is applied to cotton rag or resin-coated base stock at 34°C on a precision slot-die coater. Thickness is controlled to ±0.3 μm. The coated base is chilled and dried in a humidity-controlled chamber before the emulsion stage.

→ assets/process-coating.jpeg
03

Emulsion Application

Silver halide emulsion is applied in low red-light conditions. The emulsion is formulated in-house for each paper grade — no licensed emulsion is used. Drying tension is monitored continuously to prevent surface curvature.

→ assets/process-emulsion.jpeg
04

Quality Inspection

Each roll is step-wedge tested under standardised illuminant D50. Dmax, Dmin, and contrast curve are logged against the grade specification. Rolls outside specification are destroyed — not discounted, not sold as second-quality stock.

→ assets/process-qa.jpeg
05

Cut, Package, Ship

Sheet sizes are cut to ±0.5 mm. Packaging is performed in a darkroom environment. Each box is dated, batch-coded, and vacuum-sealed. Cold-chain shipping is available for large studio orders.
