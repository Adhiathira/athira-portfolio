# Brand Name — Layout Brief

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The site flows as a long-form vertical scroll composed of roughly **6–7 distinct content sections**. The rhythm alternates between full-bleed color-block sections and more structured grid sections displaying product imagery. The dominant visual language is **loud, editorial, and punchy** — reminiscent of a magazine spread brought to life. A persistent modal overlay appears on every screenshot, which is a significant UX pattern worth noting separately.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

The header is a **fixed, full-width strip** with a clean light or transparent background, sitting tight against the top of the viewport. It uses a **three-cluster layout**:

- **Left cluster:** Three text links — evenly spaced in lightweight uppercase tracking
- **Center:** The brand wordmark rendered in an accent color, slightly larger than the nav links — this is the typographic anchor of the page
- **Right cluster:** Two text links — mirroring the left cluster's weight and spacing, plus a social icon as the terminal element

The nav links are small and secondary; the wordmark holds visual dominance. Hierarchy is clear: brand identity over navigation utility.

---

## 3. Hero Section

The hero is a **typographic full-bleed statement** — the entire viewport is consumed by an oversized dark background with the brand name rendered at a massive scale in an accent color. The letterforms are cropped by the viewport edges, creating an immersive, poster-like quality. There's no image in the traditional sense — the typography *is* the hero image.

A centered light card (the modal) floats over this backdrop, which means the underlying hero communicates primarily through peripheral letterforms visible around the edges of the overlay.

---

## 4. Content Sections Below the Hero

### Section: Color-Block Feature (Full Bleed)

A **solid color full-bleed section** with large dark display text anchored to the left — category terms sit at wide horizontal margins, creating a strong typographic split. A large vertical photograph (portrait crop, roughly one-third of viewport width) is positioned center-left, partially overlapping into the section from above, reinforcing vertical continuity between sections. The layout is **asymmetric and kinetic** — text left, image center, text right, with generous negative space creating visual breathing room.

### Section: Products Grid — Category A

Layout shifts to a **three-column product grid** on a light background. Each column displays a product image (centered, floating on the field) with a short product name in uppercase below. The grid is uniform in height and spacing — clean, catalogue-like, rhythmic. A color-block label in the bottom-left names the product category in very large dark type, creating a **bold category header anchored to the bottom-left** while the products live in the upper-right visual zone. This is an intentional asymmetry that adds editorial energy.

### Section: Products Grid — Category B

Same three-column grid pattern repeats, this time on a **contrasting color block** at left serving as the section header. The consistency of this grid-plus-color-block pattern creates a strong **visual system** across product categories — learnable and brand-coherent. Product imagery is clean cutout-style, maintaining a light catalogue feel.

### Section: Products Grid — Categories C & D

Pattern continues: three-column grid, light background, product shots centered in each cell. Category names switch to alternating color blocks, maintaining the color-coded editorial identity. A lifestyle/editorial photograph (people interacting with products) appears in the right column of one row — breaking the product-only grid intentionally for visual variety and brand warmth.

### Section: "Where to Find Us" Feature

A **two-column asymmetric layout** emerges — left column contains a product image, right zone holds a large dark headline with a Primary CTA button (accent color, rounded rectangular). This section is sparse and high-contrast, using white space as a compositional tool. The CTA button is significantly smaller than the surrounding text, creating a clear visual hierarchy: awareness first, action second.

### Section: About / Brand Story

**Split layout** — left half holds a large photograph (portrait crop), right half contains editorial body copy in a smaller serif-adjacent typeface. The layout is 50/50 at a macro level but visually weighted toward the left image. The copy is dense and flush-left, creating a structured editorial block. This section serves as the human/personality anchor of the page.

### Section: Content Grid

**Two-column grid of media thumbnails** — each cell is a large image with headline text overlaid directly on the image (light type over photographic backgrounds). The thumbnails are full-bleed within their cells; the grid is edge-to-edge with no visible gutters. Content titles are positioned at the bottom of each thumbnail in bold uppercase. The grid is broken up by a **single full-width image row** (the featured piece), maintaining rhythm while introducing hierarchy.

## 5. Footer Layout

A **scrolling ticker/marquee strip** runs at the very bottom — a brand phrase repeated in uppercase, running horizontally across the full width. This replaces a traditional footer in the visible scroll and reinforces the brand's energy all the way to the page's bottom edge. (A more conventional footer with links may exist below, but wasn't captured in these screenshots.)

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

### Modal Overlay (Capture Popup)
The most structurally prominent pattern across all screenshots: a **centered light card modal** floats over every page state. It is mid-weight in size — not full-screen, not small corner widget — roughly **50% of viewport width and 60% of height**. It contains a bold accent-color heading, an email input field, a checkbox consent row, and a full-width accent CTA button. A close button (×) sits in the upper right corner. The modal has a subtle drop shadow separating it from the page behind. **Critically, it persists across scroll positions** — the background content visible behind it changes, but the modal stays fixed in the viewport center.

### Color Blocks as Structural Dividers
Full-bleed solid color sections serve as **visual chapter breaks** between product categories. They don't just separate content — they carry category identity, functioning as both navigation landmarks and brand expression.

### Oversized Typography as Layout Element
Type isn't just content — it's **structure and imagery**. The hero letterforms, the category names, and section headers all operate at sizes that make them feel like design elements first, text second. Typography and layout are inseparable here.

### Full-Bleed Photography
Lifestyle and portrait imagery extends completely edge-to-edge — no padding, no card containers, no borders. Images and color blocks share equal visual weight with text, creating a **flat, poster-like depth** rather than a layered card-based hierarchy.

### Sticky Navigation
The header remains fixed at the top across all scroll states, providing consistent wayfinding while the bold, immersive sections scroll behind it.
