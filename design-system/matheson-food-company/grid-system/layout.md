# Brand Name — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The site is a long-scroll, single-page experience that flows through five major zones: a full-bleed typographic hero → a split editorial hero → a scrolling product ticker → a dense mosaic product grid → an about section → an editorial grid. Throughout the entire scroll, a persistent newsletter popup floats centered over the content. The spatial rhythm alternates between explosive, full-viewport typographic moments and tight, grid-contained product cataloguing — creating a punchy push-pull between maximalist branding and utilitarian product display.

---

## Header / Navigation

A slim, fixed top bar spanning the full viewport width. Three-column structure:
- **Left cluster:** three text nav items in small caps weight, widely letter-spaced, left-aligned
- **Center:** wordmark in the brand accent color, slightly larger than the nav items — acts as the visual anchor
- **Right cluster:** two text items + a social icon, right-aligned

The bar sits on a light background with a subtle bottom border in the accent color. It is thin relative to the page — perhaps 50–60px tall — giving it a newspaper-masthead quality. No hamburger, no dropdowns visible.

---

## Hero Section

### Screen 1 — Typographic Splash
A pure full-bleed typographic hero. The brand name is set in massive, condensed letterforms in the brand accent color at near-viewport scale — the type bleeds off all four edges. Background is flat neutral. The letters are so large they function as abstract shapes rather than readable text. No imagery, no secondary content. The hierarchy is: type IS the design.

### Screen 2 — Split Editorial Hero
Immediately below, the viewport shifts to a three-panel horizontal composition on an accent-color background:
- **Left panel (~30% width):** Flat accent fill with a brand value word in large dark condensed type, vertically centered
- **Center panel (~40% width):** A full-bleed editorial photo (close-crop, intimate)
- **Right panel (~30% width):** Flat accent fill with a second brand value word in the same large dark type

Below this split, a section title appears in enormous dark condensed type on a neutral background — again cropped and bleeding, implying the text continues below the fold.

---

## Product Ticker / Marquee Band

A narrow horizontal band — roughly 40px tall — containing a looping text marquee of category names repeating. This acts as a category navigation strip and visual separator between the hero and product grid. The ticker is full-width, dark background, light text.

---

## Product Grid Sections

The bulk of the page is a continuous, borderless **three-column mosaic grid**. Each column is equal-width (~33%). The rows are dense — approximately square cells — and alternate between:

- **Color block cells:** Flat-fill panels in brand palette colors containing only a category label in oversized dark condensed type. These labels are flush bottom-left, with generous negative space above.
- **Product photo cells:** Neutral-background cells with a centered product shot and the product name below in small caps beneath the image.
- **Editorial photo cells:** Full-bleed photography (lifestyle, product-in-use) with no text overlay.

The grid has **no gutters** — cells butt directly against each other, creating a seamless mosaic effect. Color block cells create strong punctuation and category delineation within the flow. The alternation is irregular/asymmetric — never a predictable row pattern — which gives it an editorial magazine quality rather than a catalog feel.

A recurring horizontal callout cell appears as a full-width or two-thirds text block, breaking the three-column rhythm momentarily.

---

## About Section

Transitions into a **two-column layout** on an accent-color background:
- **Left column (~45%):** Full-height editorial portrait photograph, left-edge flush
- **Right column (~55%):** Dense body copy — a long founder quote in medium-weight type, right-aligned to the column

Below this, a full-width neutral section with a centered headline in large dark condensed type, functioning as a section break and brand manifesto moment.

---

## Editorial Grid

The editorial section uses a **mixed-format editorial grid**:
- A large, near-full-width feature card with a full-bleed lifestyle video thumbnail and a bold title below
- Below that, a **two-column equal grid** of cards — each with a full-bleed thumbnail and title beneath

The editorial grid uses thin borders between cells, a slight return to structure compared to the product mosaic above.

---

## Floating / Overlay Element — Newsletter Popup

A persistent, centered modal floats over **every section of the page** throughout the entire scroll. It sits at roughly viewport center, approximately 380px wide. Structure:
- Light background, thin dark border, no drop shadow
- Close button (×) top-right, small and unobtrusive
- Accent-color headline text, centered
- Email input field with an accent-color outline border, placeholder text in caps
- Small checkbox with consent copy beneath
- Full-width accent-color Primary CTA button in light caps

The modal does not dim or blur the background — the page content scrolls fully visible behind it. This creates a layered, almost confrontational spatial relationship: you see the full page but the modal demands engagement. The popup maintains the brand palette and typographic voice — it doesn't feel like a third-party widget.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

- **Borderless mosaic grid:** Zero-gutter three-column product grid creates visual density and makes color blocks and photos feel like a collage rather than a catalog
- **Type-as-full-bleed-image:** Several sections treat oversized type at near-viewport scale as the primary visual element — no photography needed
- **Alternating rhythm:** The page pulses between accent-color-saturated sections and neutral/product sections, creating a breathing cadence
- **Persistent floating modal:** The newsletter popup never dismisses across screenshots — it layers over all content without dimming, a bold UX choice
- **Category marquee ticker:** The scrolling text band acts as a wayfinding anchor between content zones
- **No footer visible in screenshots:** The page continues past the editorial grid; footer not captured
