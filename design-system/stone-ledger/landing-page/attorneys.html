The People

Those who appear

Three advocates in active practice, each with a distinct area of concentration. The chambers accepts instructions where the matter falls within the expertise of the advocate appearing.

T.R.A.
Founding Senior Advocate Bar Council of Kerala, 1984

T.R. Aravindan

T.R. Aravindan enrolled at the Bar Council of Kerala in 1984 and read under the late M.K. Nair at the Kerala High Court. He established Aravindan & Co. in 1987. He was designated Senior Advocate by the Chief Justice of the Kerala High Court in 2003.

His practice spans constitutional matters — fundamental rights, legislative competence, and the exercise of Article 226 jurisdiction — and civil appellate work before the Division Bench. He has appeared in matters before the Supreme Court on constitutional references.

Selected judgments include matters on the Kerala Education Act, the Kerala Public Service Commission Regulations, and the interpretation of Service Rules of the State of Kerala. His judgments appear regularly in the Kerala Law Times.

Constitutional Law Fundamental Rights Civil Appeals Supreme Court
Senior Advocate Bar Council of Kerala, 1998

Meera Krishnaswamy

Meera Krishnaswamy enrolled at the Bar Council of Kerala in 1998, having completed her LL.M. at the National Law School of India University, Bengaluru, where her dissertation concerned the law of adverse possession under the Limitation Act.

She was designated Senior Advocate by the High Court in 2018. Her practice concentrates in civil appellate matters — property law, succession disputes, and family partition proceedings before the Division Bench. She has reported judgments in the Kerala Law Times on the scope of the Hindu Succession Act and the application of the Kerala Land Reforms Act to specific categories of land.

Suresh Nambiar

Suresh Nambiar enrolled at the Bar Council of Kerala in 2010. He read as a junior in this chambers under T.R. Aravindan before joining as a regular advocate in 2014.

His practice is concentrated in writ jurisdiction — service matters, education law, and public law remedies against State instrumentalities. He appears regularly in matters before the Single Judge and has argued before the Division Bench in matters involving Kerala Public Service Commission proceedings and the Kerala University Service Rules.
