Founding

Established 1987

Aravindan & Co. was established at the Advocates' Chambers of the Kerala High Court in 1987 by T.R. Aravindan, then a junior practitioner who had read under the late M.K. Nair, one of the leading constitutional advocates of his generation.

The early practice was concentrated in service matters and writ jurisdiction — the bread-and-butter of a young chambers at the Kerala High Court. By the mid-1990s, T.R. Aravindan had begun to appear regularly in constitutional references, and the chambers gradually shifted its emphasis toward constitutional law and civil appellate work.

Today, the chambers has three advocates in active practice and a reading list of junior practitioners. The firm has appeared in matters before the Supreme Court, before the National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, and before the Central Administrative Tribunal.

Philosophy

How we practise

Volume is not virtue

We do not measure the quality of our practice by the number of matters we carry. A chambers that appears in fifty routine applications in a week serves neither the court nor its clients as well as one that prepares fully for the five matters that genuinely require it.

The record is everything

Every submission we make before this court is a matter of public record. We prepare submissions accordingly — with citation to authority, with acknowledgment of adverse precedent, and with clarity about the precise legal proposition we advance.

The Constitution is a living document

The constitutional provisions we argue today were drafted in a different world. The task of a constitutional advocate is to read those provisions in the light of the society they now govern — not to freeze them in 1950, but to apply their underlying values to contemporary facts.
