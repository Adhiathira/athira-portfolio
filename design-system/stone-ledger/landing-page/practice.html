Brief Us

Aravindan & Co.

Areas of Practice

We practice in four principal areas before the Kerala High Court and, on instruction from solicitors, before the Supreme Court of India.

01

Constitutional Law

The constitutional jurisdiction of the Kerala High Court is the heart of our practice. We appear in matters concerning fundamental rights under Part III — Article 14 equality and intelligible differentia, Article 19 freedoms, Article 21 due process and personal liberty. We argue the structural architecture of the Constitution: separation of powers, legislative competence, the limits of delegated legislation.

Our constitutional practice has produced reported judgments on the Kerala Education Act, the Kerala Land Reforms Act, and matters arising from the exercise of Article 226 jurisdiction. T.R. Aravindan has appeared in constitutional matters before the Supreme Court on constitutional references from the Kerala High Court.

02

Civil Appeals

Appellate advocacy before the Division Bench. Property, succession, contractual disputes, and judgments from the subordinate courts of Kerala and Lakshadweep that warrant appellate scrutiny.

I.

Property & Succession

Partition suits, testamentary matters, and the application of the Hindu Succession Act and Kerala Land Reforms Act before the Division Bench.

II.

Contractual Disputes

Specific performance, injunctions, and damages in commercial and civil contracts — from agreements for the sale of land to long-term service arrangements.

III.

Letters Patent Appeals

Appeals from Single Judge orders to the Division Bench under the Letters Patent jurisdiction — matters of procedural law and the interpretation of High Court practice.

IV.

Arbitral Award Enforcement

Enforcement and challenge of arbitral awards under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, before the original jurisdiction of the High Court.

03

Writ Petitions

The writ jurisdiction of the High Court — mandamus, certiorari, habeas corpus, prohibition, quo warranto — is the instrument through which the Constitution holds public authority to account. We represent petitioners in matters where a statutory authority, tribunal, or instrumentality of the State has acted beyond its mandate, in violation of a statutory duty, or in breach of the principles of natural justice.

Our writ practice spans service matters (government service, University service), education law (University Act, technical education), and matters arising under the Kerala Public Services Commission and Kerala State Electricity Board.

"Where the law grants a right, it grants a remedy."

Foundational principle of writ jurisdiction
04

Commercial Disputes

Injunctions, specific performance, and enforcement of arbitral awards in commercial matters before the original jurisdiction. We appear for corporates, partnerships, and individual proprietors in disputes that have reached the threshold of High Court litigation — where interim relief, declaratory orders, or the enforcement of awards is in issue.

  • Interim and permanent injunctions
  • Specific performance of contracts
  • Enforcement of foreign arbitral awards (New York Convention)
  • Insolvency and winding-up (companies jurisdiction)
  • Intellectual property disputes (infringement, passing off)
