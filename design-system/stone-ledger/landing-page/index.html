Chambers

Four decades
of practice

Founded in 1987 at the Advocates' Chambers of the Kerala High Court, Aravindan & Co. has built its practice through the steady accumulation of constitutional precedent. Our work begins at the foundation of the constitutional text and proceeds outward — to the legislature that enacted the impugned provision, to the authority that applied it, to the court that must now review it.

We do not practice volume litigation. We accept matters where the legal question is non-trivial, where the stakes justify the counsel, and where the accumulated case law of this court — four decades of it, much of which we have ourselves argued — is directly in issue.
