## Prompt

Extreme close-up drift across the spines of law reports — All India Reporter, Kerala Law Times, Supreme Court Cases — shelf after shelf in a very slow rightward drift, warm tungsten light from below the frame. The spines are cloth-bound in various shades of burgundy, dark green, and navy, with gilt lettering. The camera moves at a pace that suggests inexhaustible depth — as if the shelf continues beyond any frame. No hands, no faces, no motion other than the slow camera drift across surfaces that accumulate decades of precedent. The light is low and warm, the shadows deep, the overall mood one of weight, accumulation, and domain authority. Dust particles visible in the light shaft.
