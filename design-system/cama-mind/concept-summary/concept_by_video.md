# Cama Mind — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads with the hero fully rendered — the full-bleed yoga lifestyle photograph at full opacity, the wordmark and typographic cluster in the right half of the frame already placed, and the semi-transparent navigation bar above. No entrance animation on load. The composition is complete from the first frame. Below the fold, the three-card navigation section is present but not yet visible.

## Hero Experience

The hero is static. The full-bleed lifestyle photograph does not scale, parallax, or drift. The typographic cluster — wordmark with its horizontal rule separator, heading, supporting text — is present without a fade-in sequence. The semi-transparent nav bar over the hero is present from the first frame and does not animate. There is no CTA button hover animation on load. The hero's single designed moment is compositional: the photograph and the typography are pre-arranged so that the text occupies a naturally quiet zone in the image, creating harmony rather than conflict between the two layers.

## Section Transitions

As the user scrolls, each section fades in and slides up gently as a complete unit — not as individual elements. The animation is subtle and slow (the recording notes "calm and graceful"), contributing to the contemplative register of the content. No section stagger, no cascading reveals within a section. The entire block arrives together. The three navigational cards appear as a group. The split-media sections appear as complete two-column units. Transitions from section to section rely on whitespace rather than shape, color change, or geometric divider — the background remains consistently white throughout, so the only rhythm device is the generous vertical gaps between blocks.

## Content Rhythm

The full-width four-image media strip is the most visually active moment in the scroll journey — not through animation but through composition. Four images presented in a horizontal band, each showing the same subject in a different yoga pose, read as a sequence of movement across the width of the viewport. This is the design's most kinetic statement, achieved entirely through still photography in a panoramic arrangement rather than through actual motion.

The audio content listing section introduces the site's one interactive animation: hover states on the rounded-corner audio cards (background color change, ~0.2s). This is the only UI element that uses a distinct visual state on hover beyond a standard cursor change. The logo carousel scrolls horizontally at a slow, controlled pace — the one persistent ambient motion in the experience.

## Footer Approach

The footer section appears as a straight cut to a light gray background — no transition device, just a background color change at the section boundary. The four-column link columns are static. Below the main footer, the dark sub-footer arrives as a full-width dark field containing the centered wordmark. The dark sub-footer creates the closing visual statement — the brand name, centered, held in dark space — before the copyright line ends the page.

## Motion Principles

The motion philosophy is gentle, uniform, and content-serving. Each section fades and slides up as a single unit — no element-level choreography, no stagger. The duration is slow and graceful, reinforcing the contemplative register. There are no keyframe animations on content elements, no parallax, no scroll hijacking. Hover states on links and interactive elements use standard 0.2s transitions. The logo carousel is the only persistent ambient motion — a slow, horizontal drift that runs independently of scroll position. The design does not use motion to demonstrate capability or signal energy; it uses motion to introduce content softly, matching the tone of the audio and movement content it hosts.

## Video Placements

Two sections of this site would be meaningfully served by ambient video:

**1. Hero Background — Contemplative yoga practice ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `hero-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: An extreme slow-motion, close-range study of a yoga pose or meditative gesture — hands resting in a specific position, a slow shoulder rotation, a deliberate arm extension — shot in warm natural light with a soft, consistent background; 10s loop; the movement should be so gradual that the viewer must look for it; no fast cuts, no wide angles; shot to feel like a still photograph that breathes
- Duration: 10s loop
- Why video: The hero's full-bleed lifestyle photograph is the site's primary brand statement. A near-still ambient video in the same register — the same warm light, the same human-body subject, the same compositional quiet — would add the temporal quality of embodied practice without disrupting the composition. Mindfulness content is about time and attention; the hero should reflect that. The video loop must be slow enough to feel like presence rather than performance.

**2. Full-Width Media Strip — Movement sequence ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `sequence-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A slow, horizontal drift through a single yoga practice sequence — four to six poses shown in wide-angle panoramic framing, each held for 1–2 seconds before the camera drifts to the next; the background should be consistent across all poses; 12s loop; the motion is in the camera, not in the subject; the subject holds each position with stillness
- Duration: 12s loop
- Why video: The current four-image panoramic strip communicates sequence through still photography. A video that slowly drifts across the same sequence — showing each pose held in stillness, then moving to the next — would preserve the panoramic, contained quality of the current design while adding the temporal experience of watching a practice unfold. This is the site's most cinematic position: a full-viewport-width section with no text, designed purely for visual rhythm.
