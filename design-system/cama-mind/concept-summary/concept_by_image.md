# Cama Mind — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A Danish mindfulness and yoga audio content platform. The site's product is sound and attention — guided sessions, meditation audio, movement content — and the design reflects the register of its offering: calm, uncluttered, and anchored in the body rather than in data or features. The brand communicates premium wellness without resorting to the standard wellness visual clichés (rose gold, soft pastels, mandala patterns). Instead, the design borrows from high-end editorial and cultural institutions: a single rounded sans-serif family, a deep navy that grounds the heading hierarchy, a warm terracotta link accent, and lifestyle photography shot with genuine compositional intention. The result is a platform that feels crafted and specific rather than templated.

## Color Architecture

White (#ffffff) is the dominant surface — hero-to-footer consistent, never broken by dark sections. Body text runs in medium gray (#646464), creating a deliberately soft reading weight that suits the contemplative register of the content. Headings use a deep navy (#102137) — not near-black, but a specific dark teal-navy that grounds the typographic hierarchy with color rather than pure contrast. The link and accent color is a soft terracotta/salmon (#de8b7a) — warm, earthen, and non-aggressive; it signals interactivity without demanding attention. The three navigational cards below the hero introduce muted solid field colors (earthy rose, slate blue, and a third neutral tone) as visual waypoints — the only use of saturated color as section backgrounds. The color system avoids bright commodity wellness colors; the terracotta and navy register as considered material choices rather than category defaults.

## Typography System

A single rounded humanist family in two variants. Nunito (light 300 weight) handles all headings: h1 at 36px, h2 at 23px, h3 and h4 at 20px. Nunito Sans (medium 500 weight) handles body copy, paragraphs, links, and navigation text at 16px. The split between Nunito and Nunito Sans is subtle — both are rounded humanists from the same design family — and the weight inversion (thin for headings, medium for body) is a considered typographic decision that keeps the display scale airy while making the body text more readable at paragraph length. The heading scale is modest (no large display sizes), reflecting the content register: this is a reading and listening experience, not a visual spectacle. The wordmark uses the same typeface at display scale with a horizontal rule separating the two words, treating it as a graphic element while remaining typographically consistent.

## Spatial System

The page operates across five distinct spatial modes. The hero is a full-viewport composition-driven layout: a full-bleed lifestyle photograph fills the entire background, with a typographic cluster (wordmark, heading, supporting copy) positioned in the right half of the frame — placed where the photography has been art-directed to provide a soft, out-of-focus text-safe zone. Immediately below, three equal-width navigational cards sit within a contained container as a horizontal row — solid color fields, purely typographic, no imagery. Content sections use a two-column container-based split (photo left or right, content the other side), consistent with the container grid. A full-width four-image media strip breaks the container for one immersive horizontal moment in the scroll journey. An audio content listing section uses a two-column card grid with subtle rounded corners on the interactive cards — the only point in the system where a corner radius appears. The footer is dark: a four-column light gray footer above a full-width dark sub-footer carrying the centered wordmark.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

Photography is editorial and intentionally composed. The hero lifestyle photograph is the most significant: a single subject (a person in a yoga pose) occupies the left compositional zone, while the right zone is intentionally softened — shallow depth of field and a quiet background — providing a natural and harmonious text-safe area. This is not a photograph used as a generic lifestyle backdrop; it is a photograph composed with the text overlay in mind. The full-width media strip presents four images in sequence — the same subject in different yoga poses, shot against a consistent neutral background — creating a panoramic impression of movement and sequence. Elsewhere, photography is contained in grid columns (not full-bleed), supporting editorial text rather than replacing it. All photography reads as commissioned and art-directed rather than stock.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation is a single sticky bar: wordmark left, three to four primary links centered, language switcher and accessibility icon right. The background is semi-transparent at load (floating over the hero image) and maintains this appearance throughout the scroll — it does not transition to a solid background as most sticky navs do. The nav links use the deep navy color (#102137), creating high contrast against the translucent background. The navigation is minimal and does not compete with the content. Primary user journeys are handled through the three-card navigation hub below the hero — the cards function as the site's primary wayfinding device.

## Overall Design Character

Cama Mind is a premium wellness content platform with a Danish design sensibility: restrained, considered, and confident in whitespace. Three decisions define the system. The typographic discipline — using a single rounded sans-serif family in two weights, inverting the conventional weight hierarchy — creates a quietly sophisticated voice without relying on serif authority. The art-directed hero photograph, where the UI and the photography are designed as a unified composition rather than as a photo with text placed on top of it, establishes the brand's visual commitment upfront. The navigation card color fields — muted earth tones, not wellness pastels — signal the content categories through material color rather than decorative imagery, trusting color alone to differentiate three user journeys. The result is a site that does not look like the wellness category it serves.
