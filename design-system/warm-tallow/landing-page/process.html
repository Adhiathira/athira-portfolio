How We Work

The
Process

A commission takes as long as it takes. There is no standard lead time — only the time the work needs. What follows is an honest account of how a piece comes to exist.

The Workshop

Glasgow,
Southside

Seven people work in our Southside workshop. There are no assistants or apprentices — everyone who works here is a maker. The person you speak with is the person who builds your piece.

We have been in this building since 2014. The space itself has shaped how we work — small enough for conversation, large enough for a long timber plank and the room to move around it.

Step by Step

What
Happens

First Contact

Use the enquiry form or email us directly. Tell us what you are looking for — even a rough description is enough to start. We will suggest a time to speak, and that conversation is free, without obligation, and usually thirty minutes.

Site Visit

For fitted joinery and any furniture that must work within a specific room, we visit. We measure, photograph the space, and talk about how the piece will be used. This visit costs nothing and binds you to nothing.

Proposal & Costing

Within a week of the site visit, we send a written proposal: a clear description of what will be made, the timber to be used, the finish, dimensions, and a fixed price. We do not quote ranges. You know what you are agreeing to.

Timber Sourcing

Once the commission is agreed, we go to the yard. Boards are selected by hand — we are looking for the grain direction and figure that suits your piece specifically. This is not sourcing from stock; it is choosing for you.

Making

Work begins. We send photographs at key stages — after cutting, after assembly, before finishing. You are not kept in the dark. If something needs to be reconsidered, we discuss it then, not after delivery.

Finishing

We use oil, wax, or lacquer depending on the timber and the use. We do not apply a finish because it is the quickest. A dining table takes different treatment from a cabinet door. Both are finished until they are right.

Delivery

We deliver and install every piece ourselves, within Scotland. For commissions further afield, we arrange specialist couriers and attend the installation. The final balance is due when the piece is in place and you are satisfied with it.
