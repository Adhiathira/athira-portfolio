Commission Enquiry

Common Questions

Before
You Ask

Do you have a minimum spend?

No. The simplest piece is costed the same way as the most complex — by the time and material it needs. We do not take commissions we cannot make properly, and price is not the filter we use.

How long will it take?

Current lead time is 14–18 weeks from agreement to delivery. This includes time at the timber yard, in the workshop, and for finishing. We will give you a date — not a range — once we have discussed the brief.

Can I choose the timber?

Yes. We welcome clients to the yard if you want to choose your own boards. We can also send photographs of candidate boards before cutting — for most commissions, this is how we work.

Do you work outside Scotland?

We have delivered commissions in London, the Lake District, and Northern Ireland. Ask — we will tell you honestly whether the logistics work for the piece you have in mind.
