How We Work

Four Acts,
One Object

Conversation

Every commission begins with the room it will enter. We visit, measure, and listen — to what you need and what the space already knows. No brief is fixed until both parties are satisfied.

Timber Selection

We source from British and European yards with long relationships and long memories. The board you receive was chosen for your piece specifically — its figure, weight, and warmth considered together.

Making

Work happens in our Glasgow workshop. Joints are cut by hand. Surfaces are finished by touch. Time is not rationed — a piece is done when it is done, not when the schedule says so.

Delivery & Fitting

We deliver and install every piece ourselves. Furniture lives in a room — fitting it is not separate from making it. We leave when it is right.

The Materials

What We
Work With

Primary Timber

European Ash

Pale, open-grained, and resilient. Ash accepts stain and oil with equal generosity. It is our most-used timber for tables and cabinetry because it ages without drama — just slowly better.

Accent Timber

British Walnut

Richer in figure and darker in tone. Reserved for pieces that need weight — a writing desk, a headboard, a door frame that reads as architecture.

Joinery Grade

Scots Oak

The structural timber of Britain for a thousand years. We use it in joinery work where strength and stability must outlast fashion. Often fumed or limed to bring out the medullary ray.
