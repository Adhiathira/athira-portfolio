The Press

Handset type,
hand-pulled.
No exceptions.

Every print begins with a botanical specimen collected on Mainland, Orkney, during the same week as the pressing. The specimen is prepared and dried before being incorporated into the composition alongside handset lead type. Each forme is unique to its edition.

“The impression is not a reproduction. It is the thing itself, pressed into the paper.”
