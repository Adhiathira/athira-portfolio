# Teenage Engineering — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads instantly — no entrance animation, no loading state, no staggered reveal. The hero section with its centered product photograph and multi-column navigation is fully rendered at the first frame. The visitor arrives to a complete, already-stable image. The absence of any introductory motion is the first statement: this site does not need to announce itself.

## Hero Experience

The hero is a static composition. A single product photograph, lit with precision against a neutral gray gradient, occupies the center of the viewport. No ambient video plays. No parallax shift occurs on scroll. The product is still. The text in the lower-left corner does not animate. The experience is closer to opening a product photography book than loading a website. The hero makes no demands on the visitor's attention through motion — it trusts the image to hold.

## Section Transitions

As the user scrolls through the homepage, sections arrive as hard cuts. There are no animated transitions, no fade-in reveals, no slide-up entrance animations for content. A full-bleed dark media section follows the light hero with no gradation. A structured product grid follows the cinematic showcase with no interpolation. The rhythm is deliberately abrupt — each section is a visual reset rather than a continuation. This is the site's most distinctive motion choice: the decision to use *no* motion as the transition mechanism, letting the contrast between sections carry all the pacing energy.

## Content Rhythm

There is no scroll-triggered content rhythm. Individual elements within sections do not animate into view. Product grid cards do not stagger. Headlines do not slide up. The page is a sequence of static compositions that the user scrolls through — each a complete, considered image in its own right. The only continuous motion is looping video in the full-bleed media showcase sections, which plays automatically and silently when the section is in the viewport.

## Footer Approach

The footer, like everything else, arrives as a hard cut. A dense, multi-column informational zone in a neutral background. No closing animation, no fade-in, no special treatment. The scroll journey ends the same way it began: quietly, completely, without ceremony.

## Motion Principles

The motion philosophy is near-total stillness. The only motion in the entire experience comes from looping video in the cinematic full-bleed showcase sections. No scroll animations, no hover transitions beyond simple color shifts on links, no entrance choreography. The design's confidence in stillness is its most radical statement: a product site that trusts its photography to hold attention without animation.

## Video Placements

Two specific sections of this site are pre-designated for ambient looping video and one additional placement would amplify the design's strongest moment:

**1. Full-Width Media Showcase — Product cinematic loop**
- Suggested file: `hero-showcase.mp4`
- Subject: A single product — a synthesizer or device — shot from an extreme close-up angle in dramatic, high-contrast light; slow rotation, 8–10 seconds, seamlessly looping; emphasis on material texture, machined surfaces, the play of directional light on plastic and metal
- Duration: 8s loop
- Why video: The full-bleed media showcase sections are the site's cinematic moments. Video in this position makes the product feel like a physical object with presence and dimension rather than a photographed artifact. The slow rotation communicates precision engineering in a way no still image can.

**2. "In-Situ" Collage Section — Workshop ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `insitu-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: Hands manipulating hardware on a workbench — close-up, tactile, tool-focused; components being connected or adjusted in soft, warm studio light; 10s loop with minimal camera movement
- Duration: 10s loop
- Why video: The in-situ collage sections already use a layered, mood-board aesthetic that communicates the creative and technical context surrounding the product. Video of the act of making — or using — would animate the collage's implied narrative and turn a static composition into a living one.

**3. Hero Section — Subtle animated product introduction (optional)**
- Suggested file: `hero-product-reveal.mp4`
- Subject: The product slowly coming into focus from soft blur to sharp clarity, held at full sharp for 4 seconds, then cycling; or a very slow 1–2° rotation settling to a final presentation position; 6–8s total
- Duration: 6–8s loop
- Why video: The hero currently uses a static product photograph. A near-still video — imperceptibly moving, resolving from blur to sharpness — would preserve the page's stillness while adding the sense that the product is being revealed rather than simply displayed. The visitor may not consciously notice it's video, which is precisely the right register for this brand.
