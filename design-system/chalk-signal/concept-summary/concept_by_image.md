# Chalk Signal — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A data intelligence platform operating in a register of measured authority rather than technical complexity. The site's formal argument rests on a single unifying choice: the squircle — a rectangle with a continuously high corner radius — applied at every scale from button to media container to footer capsule. This geometric consistency functions as a brand identity rather than a decorative preference. Against an off-white field (`#f2f2f2`), the squircle system reads as a deliberate encoding of precision and accessibility: not the sharp-cornered rigidity of enterprise dashboards, nor the soft amorphousness of consumer apps, but a middle register that holds both at once. Condensed uppercase display type, generous white-field spacing, and a single reserved accent work together to position the product as serious without being austere, and modern without being trendy.

## Color Architecture

The palette is built on restraint. An off-white (`#f2f2f2`) serves as the universal surface color — not pure white, which would feel clinical, but a barely-warm neutral that reads as chalk, matte paper, or a clean linen ground. Near-black (`#202020`) handles all display headings; pure black (`#000000`) carries body text and link text for maximum legibility. Warm greige (`#b1ada7`) provides a secondary text tier for labels, metadata, and muted UI states — its warmth preventing the palette from reading as cold. A single dark high-contrast section mid-page (near-charcoal `#1a1a18` to `#3d3a34`) functions as the site's primary dramatic beat, the one moment where the light-field logic is suspended and a cinematic register takes over. The accent — reserved exclusively for primary CTAs and active states — is the only chromatic value in the interface; its rarity is the source of its authority.

## Typography System

Barlow Condensed (weights 400–700) performs all display work: h1 at 40px with 3px letter-spacing and uppercase transform, delivered at weight 500 — a deliberate step down from the source system's 700, which softens the compressed character from architectural slab to composed announcement. H2 and H3 continue the condensed-uppercase register at 14px and 16px respectively. EB Garamond (weight 500) lives in the CSS variable system as the serif voice — available for editorial interludes, pullquotes, or moments where the condensed grid needs the warmth of a classical serif. Caveat (weight 400) is the handwritten voice: a system-level affordance for annotation, emphasis, or brand personality that never appears in the primary content hierarchy. The type system is a three-voice instrument — condensed/industrial, classical/warm, personal/handwritten — with Barlow Condensed holding virtually all structural content.

## Spatial System

Six zone types structure the page. A full-viewport typographic hero section occupies the opening frame: the headline is the visual event, set in Barlow Condensed at display scale with only a small decorative accent as companion. Alternating split media-text feature sections present at two-column (50/50) balance, with media contained inside squircle frames — the same shape as the buttons and footer, maintaining visual continuity across scales. A pricing or comparison section uses squircle-framed cards at high contrast (dark primary card against light background) to drive hierarchical clarity. An FAQ section adopts a single-column accordion format separated by thin horizontal lines. A full-width conversion section provides a cinematic pause before the footer. The footer itself is a large squircle card — a geometrically contained light capsule floating on the dark terminal section above it, closing the page on a self-bounded gesture rather than a raw horizontal edge. Spacing throughout reflects a ×1.35 scale factor: nav padding at 32px, main content at 43px side margins, CTA padding at 22/32px — spacious without becoming airy.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

The squircle frame is the site's only photographic treatment. All media — screen recordings, interface demonstrations, abstract animations — enters through the same heavily rounded rectangular container, making the frame shape a brand marker as consistent as the wordmark. No rectangular crops, no custom masking, no organic shapes. The squircle's continuous curvature is the only geometric ornament. Product content is always foreground: media containers are primary visual objects, not backgrounds. Section backgrounds remain solid and color-specific; photography does not occupy background layers. The visual editorial logic prioritizes the integrity of the container over the specifics of what it holds — a single container type applied with discipline across every media instance.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar adopts a Stripe-derived geometry: 64px height, 16px inline padding, wordmark on the far left, primary links and CTA cluster arrayed with 440px of item spacing. The distinctive addition: `0.08em` letter-spacing applied globally to all navigation typography, creating a slightly open, composed rhythm that distances the nav from default browser rendering. On the dark-themed homepage, the nav is transparent; on light-themed inner pages, it carries a solid white background with a bottom border for separation. This page-type-conditional behavior (transparent on dark, solid on light) is the nav's only adaptive logic — it does not perform a scrolled transition independently of page theme.

## Overall Design Character

The site's defining quality is the completeness of its commitment to a single container vocabulary: the squircle applied without exception from the smallest button to the largest section boundary. This is not the organic language of curves and blobs, nor the strict orthogonality of enterprise design — it is a third path, where geometric precision meets continuous curvature. The Barlow Condensed display type at reduced weight (500 vs. the source's 700) introduces a composed quality that the full-weight compressed version would not — condensed but not forceful, tracked but not distant. The combination of a languid motion system (×1.5 timing across all transitions), generous spacing (×1.35), and hover states that soften to 0.75 opacity rather than amplifying creates a site that responds to the user's attention without rushing toward it.

## Distinctive Qualities

The most singular choice is the **intra-sentence luminance split in headlines**: primary words rendered at full foreground weight, continuation phrases at muted gray, all within a single heading element. This is not a heading hierarchy — it is weight modulation within a single statement, exerting what might be called typographic pressure: the primary words carry mass, the secondary words recede, and the eye registers the difference as a gravitational field rather than a visual list. The **squircle footer capsule floating on the dark terminal section** has the quality of a die-cut object: not a section that transitions to light, but a bounded form that arrives complete, its edges defining a finished perimeter against the dark ground that surrounds it — as if lifted from the page and set down. The `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` easing on the primary navigation transition carries the haptic signature of a spring-loaded mechanism: fast departure, unhurried settle, the feeling of a surface that knows where it belongs.
