Arca's entire pipeline — sensor hardware, on-device inference, data transmission, and dashboard delivery — was designed to remove the gap between a field ecologist placing a sensor and an analyst reading meaningful biodiversity data. No specialist acoustic knowledge is required at any stage.

Hardware

The Arca Capsule

The capsule is a 140mm squircle-form unit — a continuously curved rectangle that attaches flush to natural surfaces without requiring drilling or permanent fastening. A silicone mounting pad and two stainless banding points secure the unit to trunks up to 600mm diameter, fence posts, or stake mounts. The form has been field-tested at temperatures from −20°C to +55°C.

140mm Form factor
IP68 Water rating

02
Recording

Continuous Capture

Two microphones record in parallel: an audible-range channel (20 Hz–20 kHz) at 48 kHz sample rate, and an ultrasonic channel (15 kHz–100 kHz) at 192 kHz. Adaptive noise gating suppresses continuous background (wind, rain) without discarding the acoustic events embedded within it. Approximately 2.3 TB of compressed audio is stored and transmitted per sensor per year.

48 kHz Audible channel
192 kHz Ultrasonic channel
03
On-Device AI

Edge Inference

Species identification runs entirely on the Arca module, using a compressed transformer model occupying 340 MB of onboard flash. The model was trained on 3.8 million confirmed recordings across 14,200 species, with a secondary calibration layer per geographic region to account for subspecies dialect variation. Cloud round-trips are not required for any identification result — the module pushes confirmed detections, not raw audio.

99.2% Species accuracy
14,200 Species in model
3.8M Training recordings
04
Transmission

Low-Power Sync

Detections are batched and transmitted via LTE-M (Cat-M1) during the daily synchronisation window, typically at local predawn when network load is minimal. Where LTE-M coverage is unavailable, LoRaWAN gateways can receive transmissions at up to 15 km line-of-sight range. A local SD card backup ensures no detection data is lost during connectivity gaps lasting up to 60 days.

LTE-M Primary protocol
60 days Offline buffer
05
Dashboard

Indices & Alerts

Incoming detections are assembled into biodiversity indices — Shannon-Wiener, Simpson's D, Acoustic Complexity Index — computed per site per day. Threshold alerts fire when novel species are detected, established species disappear for >3 days, or ACI drops below a configured baseline. All data is available via REST API and GIS-compatible webhook.

Shannon-H Primary index
REST + GIS Export formats
