How Arca Works

Four steps
from field to insight

Each Arca capsule is weatherproof, solar-assisted, and requires no maintenance after placement. Data flows continuously to your dashboard.

01 — Deploy

Place & Forget

Mount an Arca capsule to any surface — tree trunk, fence post, wetland stake. Solar-assisted power and onboard storage means zero maintenance for 18 months. The squircle form integrates unobtrusively into the habitat rather than announcing itself as infrastructure.

02 — Record

Continuous Capture

48 kHz passive recording, 24 hours a day. Onboard compression preserves acoustic fidelity while managing storage. Ultrasonic channels capture bat echolocation beyond the audible range.

03 — Identify

On-Device AI

Species identification runs on the Arca module itself — no cloud round-trip required. A 14,200-species model trained on 3.8 million confirmed recordings returns results at 99.2% accuracy.

04 — Deliver

Structured Indices

Biodiversity indices — Shannon, Simpson, acoustic complexity — sync nightly to your dashboard. Export raw detections as CSV or push to GIS platforms via webhook.

05 — Alert

Real-Time Signals

Anomaly detection flags unusual species arrivals, sudden silences, or invasive species calls. Receive alerts before you visit the site.

What Arca Detects

Detection groups

Birds — Passerines & Raptors

Dawn chorus identification to species and individual in optimal recording conditions. Migratory passage events flagged automatically by date and call pattern deviation.

Bats — Echolocation Signatures

Ultrasonic channel records in full bandwidth up to 192 kHz. Seventeen European bat species differentiated by call peak frequency, pulse interval, and characteristic shape.
