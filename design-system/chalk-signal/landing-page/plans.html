FAQ

Yes. Every subscription tier includes the capsule hardware, mounting kit, and pre-loaded species model. There are no separate hardware purchase requirements. Hardware is replaced at no charge if a unit fails within 24 months of deployment.
The Arca capsule includes a dual-radio module supporting LTE-M (Cat-M1) and LoRaWAN. In most UK and European landscapes, LTE-M coverage is sufficient. For remote sites, a LoRaWAN gateway kit can be added to any plan. The on-board buffer stores up to 60 days of detections during extended connectivity gaps.
Top-1 accuracy across the full 14,200-species model is 99.2%, measured against held-out test sets from the BirdCLEF and BatDetective benchmarks. Accuracy varies by species group: common passerines and horseshoe bats perform at the high end; experimental insect detection ranges from 85–92% depending on vegetation density. All accuracy figures are published in our quarterly model report.
Landscape and Network plans include GIS webhook export (GeoJSON format, compatible with QGIS, ArcGIS, and ESRI Living Atlas). All plans support CSV export of raw detections. The Network plan additionally exposes a full REST API with authentication, allowing detections to be pulled into custom pipelines at configurable intervals.
Field and Landscape plans are available month-to-month or on 12-month annual billing (20% discount). Network plans are custom-term — typically 24 or 36 months for programs requiring custom model calibration. There are no cancellation penalties on monthly plans; you retain access until the end of the paid period.
