# Notion — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A large-scale productivity SaaS platform whose homepage uses a two-act structure: a dark, cinematic hero designed to establish aspiration and brand personality before handing off to a light, utilitarian body built entirely for rational information gathering. The hero is the product's opening argument; everything after it is organized evidence. The design's central structural achievement is the card component — a single, highly reusable surface that serves as features grid, testimonial showcase, pricing tier, and entry point alike. The system is mature, disciplined, and self-consistent.

## Color Architecture

The color system operates in two modes that correspond to two emotional registers. Dark mode (hero): a deep near-black (`#191918`) background with white text and vibrant blue CTAs — cinematic, premium, tech-forward. Light mode (body): white and light gray backgrounds with black text — clean, airy, focused on information clarity. The vibrant blue (`#0057ff`-range) primary CTA color carries through both modes as the system's single chromatic assertion, ensuring the conversion thread is visible regardless of section theme. Card borders are thin and light-neutral; elevation is conveyed through soft drop shadows. There are no gradient fills, no multi-color decorative elements, no warm tones.

## Typography System

NotionInter (a proprietary variant of Inter) operates as the sole typeface across all weights and contexts. The display scale: 64px/600 with −2.125px tight tracking — a compressed, dense headline that reads as deliberate and modern at scale. The eyebrow label: 12px/500/+0.125px tracking — a very small all-caps or tight upper scale used as section categorization prefix. Body: 16px/400. The entire type system communicates through the contrast between the oversized tight-tracked hero headline and the small, legible functional text everywhere else. No serif counterpart, no editorial split — one face, maximum range.

## Spatial System

The page flows through a clear progression of section types. Hero: full-viewport dark background, centered headline + subhead + dual CTAs above a large product UI frame, floating illustrated characters in continuous parallax background loops. Logo ticker: auto-scrolling horizontal strip of brand logos, white background. Feature card grids: 2–3 column grids with white or light gray backgrounds, card-based; sections alternate background shade for implicit visual separation. Alternating split-screen sections: two-column left/right pairing of product media and text, alternating alignment. Pricing table: four-column comparison with tier cards, segmented billing toggle. Footer: standard multi-column utility layout on white. All section boundaries are straight horizontal edges — no divider shapes anywhere on the site.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

Media falls into two categories. Animated product UI screenshots: the hero features a large, animated software window frame demonstrating the product in action — a rounded rectangle with medium corner radius. Card-embedded screenshots: product UI screenshots contained within card bounds, always with consistent corner radii matching the card. The hero background layer includes floating illustrated character graphics (not photography) in slow, looping parallax motion — the site's most distinctive non-structural visual decision, adding personality and implied playfulness to an otherwise clean system. No full-bleed photography, no lifestyle imagery.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The sticky nav has a standard three-zone layout: logo left, primary links center (small number, short labels), dual CTA cluster right (ghost + solid). The nav is not sticky initially, allowing the dark hero to breathe at full height. On scroll past the hero threshold, it fixes to the viewport top, simultaneously transitioning from transparent/white-text to solid-white/black-text with a soft drop shadow. The transition from the nav "floating over dark media" to the nav "as a solid surface elevated over light content" is contextually precise — the element reads as part of the hero in its first state and as an independent control surface in its second.

## Overall Design Character

The site's most telling design decision is what it withholds. There are no decorative section dividers, no scroll-actor narratives, no custom frame shapes, no hero video backgrounds. The animatic hero background characters are the single exception — deployed specifically to humanize a product whose own aesthetic vocabulary is technical and minimal. The card system earns its consistency: the same component, governed by the same rules of padding, corner radius, elevation, and typography, is used to answer every question a prospective user might have. The bimodal dark/light structure does the emotional work in the hero; the card grid does the evidential work in the body. Together they form a rational conversion architecture with a single cinematic opening statement.
