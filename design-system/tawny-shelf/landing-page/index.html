The Method

From site survey to specification sheet in three stages

01

Field Assessment

Our material surveyors document samples in situ: dimensional analysis, petrographic testing, mortar analysis, dendrochronological sampling where applicable. Every material enters the archive with full site context.

02

Provenance Chain

Each material record traces its origin — quarry, kiln, sawmill, demolition site — through documented ownership and movement. Conservation architects receive a complete paper trail acceptable under ICOMOS guidelines.

03

Specification Export

Archive entries export directly to NBS, RIBA, or bespoke format specification sheets. Structural data, composition analysis, and sourcing records transfer to your project documentation without transcription error.

The archive spans forty years of documentation. The work continues.
