Featured · Stone Restoration

Bradford Wool Exchange — Victorian Gothic Facade

Bradford, West Yorkshire · 2021–2023

A Grade I listed Victorian Gothic trading hall requiring like-for-like replacement of failed sandstone dressings. Foliant sourced 340 m² of matching Morley Moor sandstone with petrographic confirmation and provided full chain-of-custody documentation acceptable to Historic England and Bradford Council's conservation officer.

Clerkenwell Warehouse Conversion

London, EC1M · 2022

Selective repointing and brick replacement for a Grade II* warehouse conversion. 4,200 hand-matched London Stock Brick units sourced from a demolished Kent brewery of the same firing period.

Framlingham Malt House Roof

Framlingham, Suffolk · 2020

Structural oak frame repair to a 17th-century malt house roof. Dendrochronologically dated English Oak sourced and matched to felling period 1680–1710, accepted under SPAB repair philosophy.
