The Process

Methods

Three stages from field survey to conservation-grade specification.

How the archive is built and how you use it

Every Foliant material record begins in the field and ends as a specification document. The process is repeatable, documented at every stage, and structured to meet the evidentiary requirements of listed building consent applications.

01

Field Assessment

Material surveyors document samples on-site before any remediation begins. Dimensional survey, petrographic thin-section testing, mortar analysis by XRF, and dendrochronological sampling where timber is involved. Nothing enters the archive without site documentation.

  • Dimensional analysis and photographic record
  • Petrographic thin-section microscopy (stone)
  • XRF mortar composition analysis
  • Dendrochronological dating (structural timber)
02

Provenance Chain

Every material record traces origin through documented ownership. Quarry to demolition site to Foliant to your project. Conservation architects receive a complete paper trail acceptable under ICOMOS guidelines and Historic England requirements for listed building work.

  • Source origin documentation (quarry, kiln, sawmill)
  • Chain-of-custody transfer certificates
  • ICOMOS-compliant provenance report format
  • Digital audit trail, exportable on request
03

Specification Export

Archive entries export directly to NBS, RIBA, or bespoke format specification sheets. Structural data, composition analysis, and sourcing records transfer to your project documentation without transcription error. Formatted for consent applications, contractor packages, and conservation management plans.

  • NBS clause-ready specification exports
  • RIBA job book integration (on request)
  • Bespoke format for conservation management plans
  • PDF and structured XML deliverables

The frameworks we work within

Foliant's documentation standards are aligned to the principal frameworks governing listed building work in the United Kingdom and Europe. Every archive record is structured to produce evidence acceptable under these frameworks.

  • ICOMOS International conservation guidelines for cultural heritage fabric
  • Historic England Guidance on building conservation and listed building consent
  • NBS Specification National Building Specification clause format for tender packages
  • SPAB Society for Protection of Ancient Buildings repair philosophy
  • BS 8221 British Standard for stone masonry cleaning and repair
