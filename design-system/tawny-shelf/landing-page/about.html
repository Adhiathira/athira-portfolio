About Foliant

Forty years documenting the built world's rarest materials.

The archive that builds.

Foliant was founded in 1981 by a conservation architect who could not reliably source like-for-like materials for a Grade I listed manor house in Wiltshire. The problem was not availability — the problem was documentation. Reclaimed materials existed in quantity. What was missing was the provenance chain that made them acceptable to conservation bodies.

The answer was a systematic archive: materials catalogued at point of demolition, with petrographic testing and written provenance before storage, not after. Forty years later that founding logic has not changed. Every entry in the Foliant archive is documented to the standard of evidence required by a listed building consent application.

We work with conservation architects, heritage contractors, planning authorities, and building owners. Our archive does not replace site visits or specialist surveys — it provides the material evidence that makes those decisions faster and more defensible.
