TheBasqueCoast

45°N — Cold current, tidal estuary, ancient water.

Origin

Where everything begins.

The Bay of Biscay narrows here. The cold Labrador current pushes south along the Basque coast, dropping the water temperature to 12–15°C year-round — precisely the conditions Acipenser gueldenstaedtii requires for the slowest possible development.

Maison Cendré maintains a single facility on the Nivelle estuary. No expansion. No second site. Everything grown in this water, from egg to harvest, takes seven years at minimum.

The word 'cendré' means ash. The colour of the water at dusk.
