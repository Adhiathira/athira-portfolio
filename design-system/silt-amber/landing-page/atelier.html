The Facility

Built for cold.

The atelier maintains 4°C throughout. Every surface is marine-grade stainless steel. The processing room was designed by the same architect who designed La Maison de Bricourt — precision cuisine infrastructure applied to fish cultivation.

How It’s Made
01

Cold Selection

Eggs are selected by hand, graded by diameter and tone.

02

Salt Calibration

Malossol: minimum salt for maximum life. 3.5% by mass, sea-sourced.

03

Conditioning

Forty-eight hours at 2°C before tin sealing.

04

Allocation

Production is limited to 600 tins per year. Sold by allocation only.
