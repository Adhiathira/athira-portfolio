# Framer — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

Framer is a no-code website builder and design tool whose site performs the argument that the tool makes possible: a dense, high-craft web experience that is simultaneously sophisticated and approachable. The site is structured as a progressive case — immersive hero demonstrating output breadth, editorial feature walkthrough demonstrating depth, social proof, pricing — but what elevates it beyond the standard SaaS template is the quality of execution at each layer. This is a design-tool company advertising its product by being the best example of what its product can produce. The dark-mode aesthetic is not merely fashionable — it is a canvas choice that makes the product's output (the colorful website thumbnails) stand out with maximum luminance.

## Color Architecture

The palette is dark-mode and disciplined: a consistent near-black serves as every section background, dark gray appears as the card surface level above the background, and white functions as the primary text color. This three-level monochromatic hierarchy — black ground, dark gray surface, white foreground — is sufficient for the entire interface without requiring any additional structural color. The single accent is a bright blue, deployed exclusively at the pricing table to highlight the recommended subscription plan. The restraint is the strategy: by holding every structural color constant and reserving the accent for exactly one semantic purpose, the system ensures it reads as a recommendation signal rather than decoration. All other chromatic energy in the interface comes from the product media — the colorful website thumbnails in the hero grid and the UI demonstrations in the feature section.

## Typography System

A modern, geometric sans-serif operates across the full typographic range: from hero-scale display headlines to navigation labels and small caption text. The typographic scale is wide — the difference between the largest and smallest text scales is substantial, creating an unambiguous hierarchy without requiring a second typeface. A clear five-level hierarchy (display hero, section headline, subheading, body, label) is consistently applied across all section patterns. The system is clean and utilitarian: typographic decisions serve navigation and comprehension, not expression. The one moment of typographic expressiveness is the hero headline, which operates at a scale large enough to feel like a statement of confidence.

## Spatial System

The spatial logic uses four distinct modes across the page. The hero section is media-dense and full-width: a centered text block over a grid of animated website thumbnails that fills the horizontal viewport. The sticky-scroll feature explainer is a two-column split — left column sticky with progressive text, right column scrolling with product demonstrations — an editorial layout that controls pacing by constraining horizontal space while allowing vertical expansion. The carousel sections introduce controlled horizontal overflow: cards extend beyond the viewport edge, with partial card bleed acting as an affordance for horizontal navigation. All remaining sections (pricing, FAQ, footer) operate within a centered maximum-width container with consistent horizontal margins. Section boundaries are straight horizontal edges with generous whitespace; there are no wave dividers or shaped section boundaries.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

Media is product-demonstrative and diverse. In the hero, an animated mosaic of website thumbnails — drawn from the design tool's output — functions simultaneously as marketing proof and visual spectacle: these are the kinds of sites you can build. In the sticky feature explainer, screen recordings of the product's UI in action serve as the primary visual content, paired directly with explanatory text. The visual argument throughout is show-don't-tell: rather than describing what the tool does, the site shows the tool operating and the output it produces. There is no lifestyle photography, no team photography in the primary sections, and no abstract illustration. The editorial register is confident and direct — a tool this capable needs to demonstrate, not persuade.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar is sticky, solid near-black from the moment the page loads, and maintains a consistent appearance throughout the scroll without transparency shifts or size changes. A thin bottom border separates it from the content below. The logo anchors far left, navigation links cluster center, and login/signup CTAs sit far right. A thin horizontal rule separates the nav from content below. No accent colors appear in the navigation — it operates entirely in the black/white register. The approach is invisible architecture: always present, never demanding attention.

## Overall Design Character

Framer is a design-tool platform site that earns its authority through example rather than assertion. Its intelligence lies in three decisions executed with exceptional consistency: the disciplined dark-mode palette that makes every piece of product media read at maximum luminance; the sticky-scroll feature explainer that gives the user editorial control over a product narrative rather than presenting it as a one-way sequence; and the structural variation between section patterns — from media-dense hero to controlled editorial split to horizontal carousel to dense grid — that creates pacing and prevents the cognitive fatigue that afflicts most long SaaS pages. The system holds its discipline across every scale.
