# Griting-Ellipsus — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads with the hero fully rendered and stable — the deep forest green field fills the viewport, the headline is visible at the bottom-left at full opacity. No assembly animation, no staggered element arrivals. The one distinction from the primary griting system: the hero's green field carries a barely-perceptible grain or atmospheric texture — not animated, simply a material quality to the stillness that signals this is a designed environment, not a flat fill. The visitor arrives to a complete, already-weighted image.

## Hero Experience

The hero text sequence mirrors the primary system: headline fades up from slight offset over 600ms, subheadline at 150ms delay, CTA buttons at 250ms. What the editorial influence adds is a slower breath after the sequence resolves — the hero holds for a perceptible moment before the page becomes fully interactive. The effect is subtle: a pause that belongs to editorial print design, not digital product. The hero is not eager; it assumes you will take the time to read before you scroll.

## Section Transitions

The section alternation between deep green and light off-white is the editorial influence's primary contribution to motion: each dark-to-light or light-to-dark transition is understood as a narrative beat, not merely a layout change. The transitions themselves are straight hard cuts with no blending — the color shift is abrupt. But the internal spacing at each section boundary is generous, creating natural pause between beats. The rhythm is chapter-based: you know when one thing has ended and another has begun.

## Content Rhythm

Individual elements use the same stagger-fade-up pattern (opacity 0→1, y 24→0, 400ms ease-out, 80ms stagger) as the primary system. The distinctive editorial addition is a horizontal ambient video scroll track: a six-panel row of muted, looping videos — talent network animations, workflow assembly loops, data-flow visualizations — that scrolls as a continuous strip in one section of the page. These videos play automatically, have no controls, and carry no captions. They exist as environmental media, not content to be watched: atmosphere in motion. The track loops. The user passes through it. This is the editorial contribution to content rhythm: media as wallpaper, not media as statement.

## Footer Approach

The footer arrives as a hard cut to deep forest green — the same environmental color as the hero, closing the visual bracket. No entrance animation. The editorial influence on the footer is atmospheric: the closing state of the page feels designed as a scene, not a utility. The wordmark is prominent, navigation links are minimal, and the section has the tonal authority of a closing page in a printed document.

## Motion Principles

The motion philosophy is deliberate, evidence-oriented, and editorially paced. Durations: 400–600ms. Easing: deceleration-based. Stagger: 80–100ms. Data element count-ups: 800ms. Nothing bounces or springs. The editorial influence deepens the principle of treating stillness as an active state: the green field in the hero is not empty — it contains texture, intention, and the sense of having been placed. Motion does not enliven a dead field; it arrives into a field that was already alive and holds that atmosphere after it subsides.

## Video Placements

Three sections of this site would be meaningfully served by ambient looping video:

**1. Hero Background — Deep environment with atmospheric texture**
- Suggested file: `hero-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A slow drift across a deep forest surface — moss, earth, or dense foliage — in low directional light; barely-perceptible camera movement, 10s loop; dark enough to preserve text legibility; carries a faint grain that echoes the editorial texture layer added to the primary green field
- Duration: 10s loop
- Why video: The editorial influence introduces a subtle textured quality to the hero green field. A near-still nature video with fine organic grain would make that texture temporal — the field breathes, the viewer senses depth and permanence rather than flatness. The visitor may not know it is video. That is the correct register.

**2. Ambient Media Scroll Track — Six-panel looping reel**
- Suggested file: `media-track-01.mp4` through `media-track-06.mp4`
- Subject: Six short looping clips (6–8s each) — talent network node graphs forming, skill graph connections drawing in, workflow roadmap steps assembling, abstract data-flow signals moving across a dark green background; all muted, no captions, all in the deep forest green palette with white/cream content
- Duration: 6–8s loop each
- Why video: The editorial aesthetic contributes the principle of passive, looping media as atmosphere rather than content. A six-video continuous scroll track — all looping silently in the deep green palette — performs the platform's core claim (a talent network operating at scale) without stating it. The viewer passes through a living system rather than a pitch.

**3. Product Feature Section — Evidence-in-motion ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `product-eval-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: The evaluation dashboard animating — skill percentage bars filling, evidence sources populating, graph connections appearing — shot at a slight angle in soft light; 8s loop, slow and deliberate, suggesting a live system rather than a demo recording
- Duration: 8s loop
- Why video: The product feature sections carry the "proof over promise" principle into static UI mockups. A slow-motion dashboard animation would make that proof kinetic — the data arrives, the evaluation completes, the report assembles. This communicates the system's verifiability through motion.
