Every idea begins with a spark, a question that refuses to fade. For us, it began when we stepped away from our comfort zones and realized how hard it is to find real guidance.

The AI era is reshaping how we live, work, and grow. Skills expire faster, careers shift overnight, and for many, confidence has become fragile.

But one truth remains, we all need human wisdom to guide us forward in our careers. We all crave guidance and clarity rooted in experience, empathy, and purpose.

That's why we built Griting, the first Agentic Career Mentorship Platform born in Silicon Valley, powered by high-density human mentors in the AI era. A place where mentorship evolves into something more structured: a path, a graph, a network, a living ecosystem of growth that belongs to you.
