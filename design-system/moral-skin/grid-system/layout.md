# Brand Name — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page comprises roughly **9–10 major sections** flowing vertically from a minimal announcement bar at the very top down to a dark footer. The rhythm alternates between full-bleed cinematic imagery and text-heavy content zones, with a persistent modal overlay (newsletter capture) that floats above all sections throughout the scroll. The palette shifts progressively: warm light tone → cool grey → warm mid tone → dark accent, giving each section its own tonal identity without breaking the linear flow.

---

## Header / Navigation

A **fixed, minimal top bar** that persists across all scroll positions. Three distinct zones:

- **Left:** Two text links (Primary Nav, Secondary Nav) — tight horizontal spacing, uppercase tracking
- **Center:** Brand wordmark — centered precisely, absent on the hero where the oversized logo takes over, then appears in mid-scroll and stays for the rest of the page
- **Right:** Two utility links (Account, Cart) — mirroring the left's weight and case

The nav sits on a transparent or white background and collapses back to the wordmark-centered version after the hero. There's also a very thin **announcement bar** above the nav — light background, single centered line of small caps text (promotional offer copy). This adds a third micro-tier above the standard two-row header structure.

---

## Hero Section

This is the most complex layout on the page — a **full-bleed, edge-to-edge split composition** with multiple overlapping layers:

- **Left half:** Full-height photographic panel — editorial photography, warm palette, raw and editorial
- **Right half:** White/off-white abstract product photography panel — calm, minimal
- **Overlapping type:** The brand wordmark is rendered in an enormous serif display typeface that **bleeds across both halves** — sitting on top of the image at roughly the vertical midpoint, letterforms partially obscured by the photography beneath (z-index layering effect)
- **No CTA buttons visible** — the hero is purely immersive; conversion happens below
- The nav announcement bar sits above this, meaning the hero starts just below that thin strip

The split is a hard 50/50 vertical divide. The oversized type creates the illusion of depth — letters appear embedded in the scene rather than placed above it.

## Content Sections (Below Hero)

### Section 2 — Full-Bleed Editorial Text with Floating Image Inserts
A dark background fills the full viewport width. A massive display headline describing the brand's core value proposition is set in a large editorial serif that spans almost the full content width. **Inline image inserts** — small circular crops of product and campaign photography — are embedded directly within the text runs, floating inline like editorial magazine design. The text alternates between roman and italic weight within the same sentence.

### Section 3 — Two-Column Product Detail (Split Layout)
A **50/50 horizontal split**:
- **Left column:** Product name in large serif (stacked across two lines with italic variation), followed by key differentiator descriptor, price, size, star rating, and a long-form product description paragraph. Primary CTA button — full-width within the column, solid dark pill
- **Right column:** Product packaging photography on a neutral background — centered, prominent, takes up most of the column height

This section has generous top and bottom padding; feels unhurried.

### Section 4 — Feature Labels Row (Horizontal Ribbon)
A **full-width, single horizontal row** of four small label pairs (Feature A / Feature B / Feature C / Feature D) — evenly distributed across the column width. No icons, just small text badges. Very low visual weight; acts as a separator rhythm beat.

### Section 5 — Results/Claims List
Left-aligned text block, two-thirds width maximum, with a structured list format:
- Bold header labels (time-based or usage-based milestones)
- Checkmark bullets followed by benefit or result lines
Austere and clinical — the layout leans into a document/report aesthetic intentionally.

### Section 6 — Feature Text + Circular Portrait Grid
Two-column asymmetric layout:
- **Left:** Large editorial headline with italic emphasis on key words
- **Right:** Stacked vertical sequence of circular image crops, each paired with a bold uppercase category label and a short descriptor paragraph. Pill-shaped outline Secondary CTA button at the bottom of this column

The circular crops create a distinctive rhythm — they feel like a repeating modular card unit stacked vertically, but the organic photo crops soften any rigidity.

### Section 7 — Brand Philosophy Marquee + Split Editorial Panel
Two distinct sub-sections stacked:

1. **Full-bleed mid-tone background** with a centered display headline in large serif (brand philosophy or proprietary differentiator), body copy paragraph, and italic link below
2. Immediately below: a **horizontal scrolling marquee ticker** — repeating pill-separated phrases (brand values or product attributes) — full viewport width, continuous scroll motion implied
3. Then: a **two-column split** — left is the mid-tone editorial panel, right is a light panel with product imagery — the two panels are separated by a clean hard vertical line

### Section 8 — Full-Bleed Photography + Large Italic Headline
Full viewport width image taking roughly 40% of the viewport height. Immediately below on a dark accent background: an oversized italic display headline with mixed weight/style in a single line. Huge type, minimal surrounding space.

### Section 9 — Horizontal Photo Gallery Strip
A **4-column equal-width grid of editorial photography** — full bleed, no gaps, images running edge to edge across the full viewport. Each column is a tight vertical crop of campaign imagery. Below this strip, the dark footer zone begins.

## Footer

**Three-column layout** on a dark accent background:
- **Column 1 (INFO):** Vertical list of policy links — Returns, Terms, Privacy, Shipping, Account, FAQ
- **Column 2 (COMMUNITY):** Short vertical list of social/contact links — Social Platform 1, Social Platform 2, Contact link
- **Column 3 (NEWSLETTER):** Promotional offer, single-line email input with Action Button (inline, no separate submit button — the arrow is embedded right-aligned inside the input field)

**Center of the footer:** The brand wordmark rendered large in a stylized stacked lockup — purely decorative, treated as a graphic element rather than navigation

**Sub-footer (below the main footer grid):**
- Centered currency selector dropdown
- Single line: copyright text and hyperlinked credits
- Full row of payment method icons — centered, equal weight

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Persistent modal overlay:** A newsletter capture modal floats centered above every section throughout the scroll journey. It does not dismiss on scroll — it requires active interaction (close or sign up). The modal is light, center-aligned, with a large italic script headline, minimal body copy, underline-only email input, and a solid dark pill button. This creates a persistent secondary layer above the entire page z-stack.

**Overlapping typography:** The hero wordmark bleeds across image layers — type sits behind/in-front of photography simultaneously through careful z-index staging.

**Color-coded section rhythm:** The page uses background color as a section separator rather than dividers or whitespace — each section has its own distinct background tone that bleeds full-width, creating a patchwork of tonal panels when viewed at full scroll height.

**Inline editorial image inserts:** Text with circular image fragments embedded mid-sentence — a high-craft magazine layout pattern rarely seen in standard e-commerce layouts.

**Marquee strip:** A continuous horizontal ticker acts as a visual breathing beat between heavier content sections.

**Circular crop motif:** Circular masks on portrait photography repeat consistently in the benefit cards section and the footer area — a deliberate shape language that softens the otherwise geometric grid structure.
