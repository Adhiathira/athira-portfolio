On Calibration

The Certificate Travels with the Instrument

Every instrument we deliver includes a calibration certificate traceable to national standards. The certificate names the instrument-maker, the reference standards used, the five test points, and the date of calibration.

Recalibration is available. We ask that instruments be returned to Dresden. We do not offer remote calibration services.
