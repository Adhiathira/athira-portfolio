On Measurement
A number is only as honest as the instrument that produced it. We build instruments that do not flatter.

Digital readouts interpolate. They present a resolved answer where only a distribution exists. Our analog scales force the reader to reckon with the actual position of a needle — to hold the ambiguity, to read the meniscus, to commit to a judgement. This is not nostalgia. It is epistemology.

The Work

Made by Hand, in Dresden

Our workshop on Nikolaistraße has operated without interruption since 1931. The lathes are manual. The dial printing is letterpress. Each instrument passes through nine pairs of hands before it leaves the building.

We employ six full-time instrument-makers and two apprentices. There is a waiting list. We do not rush it.
