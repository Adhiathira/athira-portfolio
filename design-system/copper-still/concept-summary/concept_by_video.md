# Motion Brief: Copper Still — Creative Director's Guide

## Scroll Opening

The page opens into a white field. The navigation is already present — a left-anchored logo beside tracked nav links set in uppercase Open Sans at 0.08em spacing, not animated into existence but discovered. Below it, the hero content arrives as a single synchronous event: no cascade, no stagger, no progressive disclosure. Content materializes at full opacity with a 20px upward drift over 1.17s, using the fade-up pattern that governs this system's scroll vocabulary. The easing is `ease-in-out` — a deliberate decelerate that arrives and settles without apology. The opening is not a performance; it is a fact.

## Hero Experience

The hero occupies the full viewport as a white, open zone with significant vertical breathing room. The headline at Cormorant Garamond 600 weight with 0.06em tracking resolves from opacity 0 with a subdued upward drift. The warm-grey text (#4e4946) on the white field creates a tonal warmth rather than a hard chromatic event — the palette asks for a corresponding softness in any video material placed here. This section is a **video background candidate**: the editorial container (807px) and achromatic discipline suit a slow macro drift across a material surface — hammered copper, rough linen ground, beaten tin — lit from a single raking source that reveals surface topography without color drama. The material carries the temperature that the type system evokes.

**Video candidate — hero-surface.mp4:** A slow lateral drift across a copper surface — hammered, matte-finished, showing the tool marks of manual work — lit from a single low-angle source that creates a relief shadow map of every indentation. No color grading, no saturation boost: copper in its natural warm-grey-orange, desaturated toward the warm-stone register of the text system. Motion quality: imperceptible lateral translation, no rotation, no subject change. Duration: 10s loop. Mood: material intelligence, restraint, latent craft.

## Section Transitions

Transitions between sections operate through content arrival, not background color change — the white palette holds throughout. Each section's content block enters the viewport and resolves from opacity 0 and translateY(20px) over 1.17s at ease-in-out. Content lands with finality. Cards and grid items enter with the same duration but with a 150ms stagger per item — below the threshold of choreography, above the threshold of simultaneity. The grid arrives as a composition, not as a sequence.

Heading and body text pairs enter as a single unit — no headline-first then body. The composition is the unit of animation. Elements that belong together arrive together. The 1.3× duration scaling that governs this system means every motion event is slightly longer than expected — never slow enough to feel labored, always measured enough to feel considered.

## Content Rhythm

Individual elements within sections are treated as coequal units except where hierarchy is deliberately imposed. The primary rhythm: full-section fade on scroll enter, with a mild stagger only for grid and card groupings. The stagger increment is 150ms — consistent across all breakpoints. The system never narrates its own assembly.

At the masonry grid sections, images arrive in column order rather than row order — a reading sequence that follows the visual logic of how the eye travels a grid column before crossing to the next. The compressed gutter (5px in the spacing scale after the 1.35× expansion) means items land in close proximity; the animation reinforces this tightness rather than spreading them open.

## Footer Approach

The footer resolves from opacity 0 over 1.17s as it crosses the viewport threshold — the same fade-up gesture that governs the rest of the page. No special closing choreography. If the brand wordmark appears in the footer, a hover opacity shift (0.75, 0.3s transition) is the only kinetic acknowledgment — consistent with the interaction system's hover opacity rule. The page ends the same way it began: by being present, not by performing.

## Motion Principles

The timing philosophy is **measured and resolved** — deliberately unhurried. The 1.3× duration scaling over the source system produces transitions that breathe: 0.65s for medium interactions, 1.17s for content reveals, 0.26s for fast micro-interactions. The primary easing is `ease-in-out` — a symmetric arc that communicates evenness and restraint. The warm-grey text field never changes color. The white field is a fixed condition, not a state. The system has time.

## Video Placements

**1. hero-surface.mp4**
- Section role: Full-viewport hero background, furthest depth layer beneath headline content
- Subject: Slow lateral drift across a hand-hammered copper surface — warm, matte-finished, with the shallow relief of tool marks visible under a single raking light source at 15 degrees from horizontal. No human presence, no branded content. Color temperature: tungsten, slightly warm, not corrected to daylight.
- Motion quality: Imperceptible lateral translation, no camera rotation, 0.4× playback speed from original capture, no visible loop point
- Duration: 10s loop
- Why video: The hero's white field reads as clinical when static; a living copper surface gives the palette its material anchor — the warm-stone tonality of #4e4946 becomes a visual echo of the copper oxidation, not a design decision but a physical truth. The slow breathing quality of the surface under raking light cannot be replicated by a still image.

**2. process-ambient.mp4**
- Section role: Portfolio / work showcase section — the full-bleed background of a featured work section, or the right-hand media zone in an asymmetric informational split
- Subject: A slow macro drift across a work surface — an unrolled length of copper sheet, a freshly tooled wooden block, or a compositor's type tray with metal sorts — shot at the angle of natural north-facing studio light. The surface should be in active use, not arranged: residual tool marks, slight oxidation at edges, the evidence of a working process rather than a display.
- Motion quality: Very slow horizontal drift, 20–30 second movement across frame compressed to 5s loop, slightly cooler exposure at the leading edge to preserve the white-field continuity of the surrounding layout
- Duration: 5s loop
- Why video: The work showcase zone is where the brand's output is placed before the audience. A living work surface adds material weight at the moment of highest stakes — and grounds the system's warm-grey typographic logic in physical production, where letters have weight and copper has grain.

**3. editorial-detail.mp4**
- Section role: Split editorial section — the right-hand image zone in the personal or about narrative section
- Subject: An extreme close-up of a typeset page or printed proof — Cormorant Garamond at large scale, freshly letterpress-printed on matte cartridge stock — with a single point light source creating a slow focus rack from the paper texture in the foreground to the sharp letter forms behind (5s for a full cycle). The fine ink impression of the letterpress sits slightly proud of the paper surface; the raking light reveals this relief.
- Motion quality: Static camera, slow focus rack, the paper grain occupying the first 2s in soft focus before the letterforms resolve with precision; the copper-warm color of the light source appears at the paper's edge as a warm fringe, then exits
- Duration: 5s loop
- Why video: The editorial section is the system's most direct human moment — the place where the brand names itself and its intent. A focus rack on a letterpress page provides temporal depth without horizontal motion that would disrupt the flat, rectilinear layout language. The shift from surface texture to letter form is an enacted metaphor for this design system's logic: from material warmth to structured meaning.
