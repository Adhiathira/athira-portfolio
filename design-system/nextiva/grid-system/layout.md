# Layout Brief: Brand Name Homepage

## 1. Overall Page Structure

This is a long-scroll marketing page built as an alternation between two worlds: immersive full-bleed atmospheric imagery (dark, moody, expansive) and clean, editorial white/off-white content sections. That alternation is the page's defining rhythm — dark, atmospheric, emotional moments punctuated by bright, information-dense, rational ones.

The major sections in order:

1. Announcement bar + navigation
2. Hero (full-bleed dark atmospheric imagery with floating product UI)
3. Logo/social-proof strip
4. Platform overview with tabbed capability explorer
5. Capability deep-dive (sidebar list + immersive product collage)
6. Value-proposition card pair (two dark image cards)
7. Split editorial section
8. Three-card feature row
9. Feature banner (full-bleed illustrated card) + three supporting cards
10. Customer stories (testimonial card grid)
11. Results carousel
12. Pricing with three plan cards
13. Founder/thought-leadership section

A floating pill CTA bar and a chat bubble persist over everything as the user scrolls. A promotional modal interrupts mid-journey.

## 2. Header / Navigation

Three stacked layers at the top:

- **Announcement bar**: a full-width rounded pill floating just inside the top edge, centered message with an action button and a dismiss X on the far right. It reads as a capsule sitting on the dark hero, not a hard-edged bar.
- **Primary nav**: classic horizontal split. Logo far left, four nav items (three with dropdown carets) grouped left-of-center, then a right-aligned utility cluster: contact link with icon, support dropdown, a ghost secondary button, and a solid white primary CTA button as the visual endpoint. The strongest-contrast element is deliberately last in reading order.
- The nav sits transparently on the hero's dark background, so it feels part of the scene rather than a separate chrome bar.

## 3. Hero Section

Full-bleed, full-viewport dark atmospheric photograph with everything center-aligned in a single column:

- Rating row (stars + rating badge + review count) as a small eyebrow
- A very large two-line serif headline — the serif is the emotional register; everything functional on the page is sans-serif
- A short supporting paragraph, max-width constrained to roughly a third of the viewport
- A CTA pair: solid primary CTA + outlined ghost secondary CTA, side by side

Below the text, a large **product UI mockup emerges from the bottom of the viewport**, cut off by the fold — a three-panel app window (list panel / detail panel / info panel) rendered in frosted glass over the background imagery. Two smaller floating cards overlap it asymmetrically on the left: a notification card breaking out of the app window's bottom-left corner, and a stats card layered above. The overlapping, off-grid float of these cards is a key signature — the product literally floats in the scene.

The hero background transitions from a dark tone at top to a warm tone at the bottom as you scroll, so the app window appears suspended between two atmospheres.

## 4. Content Sections Below the Hero

### Logo strip
A quiet full-width band on white. Left-anchored trust statement with a small icon, then six or seven grayscale logos of recognizable brands distributed evenly across the remaining width. Single row, generous vertical padding, clear palate-cleanser after the immersive hero.

### Platform overview
Asymmetric two-part header: on the left, an eyebrow chip above a large left-aligned serif headline taking roughly half the width; on the right, a **three-column stat row** ([Stat] / [Stat] / [Stat]), each stat with an icon, bold figure, and small caption, separated by thin vertical rules. Headline left, proof right — a pattern the page repeats.

Below it, a **full-width segmented tab bar** with four equal-width cells with icons, the active tab inverted to solid black. This controls the section beneath.

### Capability explorer (tabbed content)
A large rounded container split roughly 1/3 – 2/3:

- **Left column**: a vertical stack of seven capability cards (Capability A, Capability B, Capability C, etc.), each a slim outlined row with a black icon tile, title, one-line description, and a trailing arrow. Uniform height, tight vertical rhythm — it reads as a menu.
- **Right column**: an immersive atmospheric photograph with layered frosted-glass product UI floating over it — notification toasts stacked on the left, a glass phone mockup showing an active state in the center, and a translucent panel bleeding off the right edge. Again: overlapping glass layers over full-bleed photography.

### Value-prop card pair
Two large rounded image cards side by side (roughly 60/40), each a dark photo with white text overlaid in the lower area. One light-context, one nearly black — they bookend the row tonally.

### Split editorial section
A big rounded container split 50/50: left half is white editorial space (eyebrow chip, an enormous serif headline stacked over two lines, a bold sans-serif subhead, body copy, primary CTA); right half is a full-bleed atmospheric photograph with a single delicate line-art illustration floating in the empty space. Lots of negative space on both sides — this is the page's biggest breathing moment.

### Feature cards (three-up)
Three equal-width cards, each topped by a full-width atmospheric image and followed by a bold statement headline and body copy on white. Below the copy, small outlined "tag" chips act as scannable proof points. Same card anatomy repeated three times — icon tile, headline, paragraph, tags.

### Feature banner
A full-width rounded card with an illustrated dark atmospheric scene. Text is left-aligned in the left third — badge, large white serif headline, paragraph, primary CTA — leaving the right two-thirds as pure illustration. Followed by the three-card row described above.

### Customer stories
Header row: left-aligned serif headline with a right-aligned trio of rating platform logos separated by vertical rules. Below, a **3×2 grid of testimonial cards**, each with identical internal anatomy: brand logo top-left, a metric pill top-right ([Metric]), quote, thin divider, star rating, and attribution block. The consistent logo-left / metric-right tension gives every card a balanced header.

### Results carousel
Left-aligned serif heading with paired circular prev/next arrows on the far right. Then a horizontally scrolling row of five compact stat cards: category chip, oversized stat ([Stat]), thin rule, company name, description, and a text link. The fifth card is cropped by the viewport edge, signaling scrollability; one card carries a darker border as the active/hover state.

### Pricing
Eyebrow chip + huge left-aligned serif headline + short paragraph, all left-anchored with the right half of the row left empty. A **dual segmented toggle row** (audience segment toggle, then billing period toggle with a savings note) sits between header and cards.

Three plan cards: Plan 1 and Plan 2 on white, and **Plan 3 inverted to solid black** — the dark card is the visual anchor and featured tier. Each card centers its label chip, serif plan name, giant price with strikethrough anchor and savings badge, then left-aligns the description, feature checklist, add-on row, and a full-width bottom CTA. A centered fine-print line closes the section.

### Founder section
Eyebrow chip + large serif headline, then a two-column split (~60/40): a large rounded photo/video card with the founder's avatar and name chip in the top-left corner, beside a solid-black card quoting a social media post (platform icon + label at top). Light media left, dark text right — the same tonal pairing used throughout.

## 5. Footer

The footer itself isn't captured — the page continues past the founder section. Based on the system, expect it to follow the established container width and likely sit on a dark ground to close the light/dark alternation.

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

- **Persistent floating pill CTA**: a horizontally centered, pill-shaped promo bar (offer message + secondary CTA + primary CTA + dismiss X) floats over the bottom of the viewport on every scroll position. It's the page's always-available conversion path.
- **Sticky chat bubble**: a circular chat button pinned bottom-right with a notification dot, present in every frame.
- **Interrupting modal**: a centered promo modal over a dimmed page — split internally with offer copy left and a phone mockup with three floating feature chips right, plus an offset close button breaking the modal's top-right corner.
- **Frosted-glass UI over full-bleed photography**: the core signature. Product mockups are never on flat backgrounds; they're translucent layers floating over atmospheric imagery, often bleeding off container edges or overlapping each other.
- **Rounded-container architecture**: nearly every major block is a large radius-cornered card inset from the viewport edges, rather than true edge-to-edge bands. Full-bleed imagery lives *inside* these rounded frames.
- **Serif/sans hierarchy split**: serif for every major emotional headline and plan name; sans-serif for all UI, body copy, stats, and labels. The typeface itself signals "story" vs. "system."
- **Eyebrow-chip convention**: every section opens with a small gray pill label in caps — a consistent wayfinding device that lets headlines stay purely expressive.
- **Left-anchored headers with right-side proof**: section headers repeatedly pair a left-aligned headline with right-aligned supporting evidence (stats, rating platforms, carousel controls), creating a consistent asymmetric tension across the page.
- **Light/dark cadence**: dark immersive band → light informational band, repeated top to bottom, with the inverted black pricing card and black quote card echoing the rhythm at the component level.
