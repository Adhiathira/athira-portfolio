Frequently asked questions.

What counts as a "seat"?

A seat is one authenticated user who has access to at least one team vault. Read-only service accounts that only use `ghost run` in CI are not counted as seats.

Is there a limit on number of secrets?

No. All plans, including Solo, have unlimited secrets. We will never charge per-secret or per-rotation.

How does on-premise deployment work?

Enterprise customers receive a container image of Ghost Server that can be deployed in your own infrastructure. The CLI connects to your self-hosted instance instead of our cloud. Full air-gap support available.

Can I export my secrets if I leave?

Yes. `ghost export --format env` decrypts and writes all your secrets to a local file. You own your data. We make leaving easy because we want you to stay for the right reasons.

What encryption does Ghost use?

X25519 key exchange, AES-256-GCM for symmetric encryption, Ed25519 for audit log signing. All cryptography uses libsodium. Source available for audit on request.
