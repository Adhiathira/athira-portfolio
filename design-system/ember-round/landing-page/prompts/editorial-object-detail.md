## Prompt

A macro camera track — motorized slider at 1 to 2 millimeters per second — traveling slowly across the surface of a single dried seed head or beeswax-sealed seed envelope. The focus plane moves as the camera moves: first the surface texture of the beeswax seal reveals in detail — the slight drag marks from a thumb, the translucency of the wax catching light. Then the edge of dried plant material: a stem, a husk, a seed casing. The background is flat warm terracotta, slightly out of focus. No movement except the camera. Single overhead directional light source from above-right, casting a long deliberate shadow to the lower left. The palette is amber, beeswax yellow, deep warm near-black, with one accent of dried-pod rust orange. This is a still life that moves — methodical, attentive, like inspection.
