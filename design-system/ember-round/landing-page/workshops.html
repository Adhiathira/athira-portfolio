How we teach

Learning happens in the field, not the lecture hall.

Every workshop is led by the farmer whose land we are on. We translate and facilitate, but the knowledge comes from the people who have grown these varieties for decades. Our role is connection, not instruction.

Groups are small. We cap every workshop at twelve participants — fewer for multi-day formats. This is deliberate. Farming knowledge transfers at the pace of a slow conversation, not a seminar.
