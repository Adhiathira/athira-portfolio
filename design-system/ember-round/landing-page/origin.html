Provenance & Practice

Where seeds come from.

We work with growers who have tended the same varieties for three generations. Every seed in our library has a name, a place, and a story we can trace.

Our approach

Selection before catalogue

We do not list seeds speculatively. Every cultivar goes through a minimum three-year observation period across multiple climate zones before it enters the catalogue. We grow each variety ourselves, then verify it with partner farms before offering it to our gardeners.

The result is a smaller catalogue than most seed companies. That is intentional.

Our growers

The farm network

We source from eight farms across five countries. None are commercial seed producers — all are working kitchen or market gardens whose owners have maintained their own seed lines for decades.

Mas de la Roque, Catalonia

Method

From field to envelope

01

Field visit & variety assessment

We visit the source farm during the harvest window. We document growing conditions, soil type, irrigation method, and seed-saving practice. We take notes on flavour and texture at the moment of peak ripeness.

02

Isolation and seed extraction

Seeds are extracted from selected parent fruits, fermented where appropriate (tomatoes, cucumbers), washed, and dried at controlled ambient temperature — never in a dehydrator. This preserves embryo viability.

03

Germination testing

Each batch is tested at two temperatures: 15°C (cool-season crops) and 20°C (warm-season). We record germination percentage at day 7 and day 14. We only ship seed with ≥85% germination rate at the relevant temperature for that crop.

04

Cold storage and packaging

Seeds are stored in sealed tins at 6°C and 35% relative humidity. Packaged in beeswax-sealed glassine envelopes. Date of harvest, germination rate, and recommended sowing window are printed on every packet.
