Provenance

Every seed
has a story.

We work directly with family farms, botanical preserves, and seed-saving networks across Southern Europe and the Americas. Every cultivar in our library is sourced from growers who have tended the same varieties for at least three generations.

01

Source selection

We visit farms personally to evaluate growing conditions and genetic integrity before listing any cultivar.

02

Germination testing

Every batch is tested at 15°C and 20°C. We only ship seed with a minimum 85% germination rate.

03

Cold storage & packaging

Stored at controlled humidity in beeswax-sealed envelopes. Designed for long-term viability, not fast retail.
