The seed catalogue

Over 140 cultivars this season. Each variety selected for flavour complexity, culinary interest, and documented provenance.

Tomato — Heirloom

Brandywine

Pennsylvania Dutch heirloom. Beefsteak type, rich umami depth, pink-red flesh. Season: July–September.

Tomato — Languedoc

Corne de Boeuf

Horn-shaped French beefsteak. Low seed count, dense flesh. Rare outside the Languedoc.

Tomato — Crimean

Black Krim

From the Black Sea coast. Dark-shouldered, complex saline note from saline growing conditions.

Herb — Liguria

Genovese Basil

DOP-designation Ligurian strain. Large leaf, clove-anise aroma. Only correct basil for pesto Genovese.

Herb — Provençal

Summer Savory

Sarriette — essential to the Provençal herbs de Provence. Pepper-thyme flavour, vigorous in poor soil.

Herb — Italian

Gigante d'Italia Parsley

Flat-leaf giant. Robust flavour, excellent for stock, chimichurri, and gremolata.

Brassica — Florentine

Nero di Toscana Kale

Cavolo nero. Frost-sweetened, dark savoyed leaves. The kale that belongs in ribollita.

Brassica — Roman

Romanesco Broccoli

Spiral Fibonacci heads. Nutty flavour, firmer than standard broccoli. Sow August for winter heads.

Legume — Italian

Borlotti Climbing Bean

Spectacular pink-speckled pods. Creamy texture when dried. Climbing habit, needs support.
