# Brand Name — Layout Brief

---

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page is a single long-scroll marketing site with roughly **6–7 major content sections** below the header. The visual rhythm alternates between **full-bleed dark hero moments** and **two-column editorial sections**, creating a cinematic pacing: breathe wide, then anchor with content. The entire canvas is a deep near-black, which makes white type feel almost luminous. Sections don't use traditional dividers — they bleed into each other through spacing alone, with thin 1px horizontal rules occasionally marking transitions.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

The navigation is a **fixed top bar**, spanning the full viewport width with a transparent or very dark background that sits just above the content. It uses a classic **left logo / right links** structure:

- **Left:** Small logomark + wordmark, sitting roughly 24px from the left edge
- **Center-right:** Horizontal nav links in a tightly spaced row — rendered in a small, regular-weight sans-serif, subdued gray
- **Far right:** A vertical separator pipe, then a ghost text link, and a **filled white pill button** — the only high-contrast interactive element in the header

The nav has very low visual weight; it recedes behind the hero content hierarchy.

---

## 3. Hero Section

The hero is **full-bleed, full-viewport height**, with a stark asymmetric composition:

- **Top-left quadrant:** The headline occupies roughly the left 60% of the viewport. It's set in a massive weight, tightly tracked sans-serif. Two lines, very large — sized to feel like environmental type, not web copy. Below it sits a two-line subtitle in a much smaller, lighter weight.
- **Top-right:** A small announcement strip sits isolated in the upper-right corner, acting as a floating label
- **Bottom half:** A large **mock UI panel** bleeds upward from below the fold — a dark-on-dark product screenshot rendered at near-actual-size scale. It's not contained in a frame or device mockup; it floats as a cropped panel, implying continuity with the actual product

The hero has **no background imagery**, relying entirely on typographic scale and the product UI as its visual anchor. Spatial rhythm is generous — lots of breathing room between the headline and the UI mock.

---

## 4. Content Sections (Below Hero)

### Section 1 — Social Proof / Logo Bar
A **single horizontal strip**, vertically centered, displaying 7–8 customer logos in a uniform light-gray, equally spaced across the full width. No heading. Pure whitespace above and below. Functions as a visual pause — low visual weight, high credibility signal.

### Section 2 — Full-Width Statement
A **centered editorial block** with a large multi-line headline where a key phrase is rendered in an accent color — the only non-white text highlight on the page. This creates a typographic focal point mid-scroll. No imagery, no columns — pure type on dark background.

### Section 3 — Feature Pillars (Three-Column Card Grid)
**Three equal-width columns**, each containing:
- An **abstract 3D isometric illustration** (wireframe-style, monochromatic) occupying the top ~60% of the card
- A short bold heading below
- A 1–2 sentence descriptor in a smaller weight

Cards are borderless and set directly on the dark background with no container styling — they exist through spatial separation alone. The grid fills the full content width.

### Section 4 — Asymmetric Two-Column Feature Sections
A **split layout**, roughly 40/60 in proportion:
- **Left column (40%):** Large headline, stacked over two lines, heavy weight — anchored to the left
- **Right column (60%):** A short paragraph of body copy, left-aligned within its column, sitting at a slightly lower vertical position than the headline — creating a subtle diagonal visual flow

This pattern repeats **3 times** as the page scrolls, alternating which column holds the headline vs. the content. Each instance introduces a new feature area with a section number acting as a subtle chapter marker.

### Section 5 — Feature Detail (Inline Product Screenshot + Sub-nav)
Within each "chapter" section, a **large cropped product UI screenshot** fills the content area below the two-column text. It bleeds edge-to-edge within the content column. Overlaid on the lower portion of the screenshot is a **numbered sub-navigation grid** (2×2 or 2×3 layout) listing feature names with their section numbers — creating a clickable chapter index that sits on top of the UI mock.

### Section 6 — Two-Column with Split Data Panels
Another two-column editorial split, but this time the right side contains **two side-by-side data panels**. The panels have a slightly elevated dark surface, distinguishing them from the background. This is the most data-dense section — content-heavy right, text-sparse left.

### Section 7 — Content List Transition
A **single large heading** set bottom-left, implying the section continues below the fold. Sparse, editorial — functioning as a transition into a content-list section.

---

## 5. Footer Layout

The footer follows a typical **multi-column link grid** pattern — muted type, clean column structure, low visual presence.

---

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

**Cropped UI panels as content:** Product screenshots are never shown in device frames or browser chrome — they're cropped panels that suggest infinite interfaces. This makes the product feel large-scale and real, not "mocked up."

**Chapter numbering system:** Section numbers are used consistently as a spatial wayfinding system, rendered at small scale in a muted tone. This creates a document-like hierarchy overlaid on the marketing page.

**Type-as-layout:** Several sections use no imagery at all — hierarchy is communicated entirely through weight contrast between large display heading and body copy. The scale difference is extreme (likely 5:1 or greater ratio), which creates strong spatial rhythm without needing visual assets.

**Persistent vertical breathing room:** Sections have very generous top/bottom padding — likely 120–160px between major sections. The page never feels compressed. Whitespace is the primary layout element.

**Accent color used surgically:** A single accent color highlight appears exactly once in the hero headline area and as status indicators within UI mocks. Its scarcity makes it feel intentional — a signal, not decoration.

**Floating agent/activity panels:** A floating side panel rendered at near full fidelity within the marketing page reinforces the "real product, not a mockup" visual strategy.

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
The most structurally important things preserved here are the **proportional values** (40/60 split, 60% card height, 120–160px padding) and the **pattern names** (cropped UI panels, chapter numbering, type-as-layout) — these are the reusable design vocabulary that transfers across any SaaS site brief. The genericized version loses zero layout information.
