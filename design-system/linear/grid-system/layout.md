# Brand Name — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page is organized into approximately **10–12 major sections**, flowing vertically with a clear rhythmic alternation between full-bleed narrative sections and tighter content zones. The visual pacing is deliberate: wide open breathing room above each headline, followed by a compact interactive demo panel, then more white space before the next statement. The overall feel is **sparse, editorial, high-confidence** — Brand Name never crowds information.

The scroll journey reads as: Hero → Social proof logos → Manifesto statement → Feature pillars (repeating: left headline + right body copy + numbered sub-items + UI demo) → Testimonials → CTA → Footer.

---

## Header / Navigation

The header is a **fixed, full-width horizontal bar** that sits at the top of every scroll position. It is extremely minimal:
- **Left:** Logo mark + wordmark in white
- **Center:** Flat text navigation — evenly spaced, no dropdowns visible on default state
- **Right:** A thin vertical divider, then "Log in" (ghost text) and "Sign up" (white-outlined button, tight pill shape)

The nav bar has no background fill — it sits transparently over the dark page. Very low visual weight. The divider between Log in and Sign up is a subtle spatial affordance that groups the auth actions together.

---

## Hero Section

The hero is **full-width, asymmetrically weighted**:

- **Top half:** Pure typographic headline — massive display heading spanning roughly 60% of the viewport width. Two-line break is intentional, creating a staircase rhythm. Left-aligned, generous left margin (~10% from edge).
- **Below the headline:** A subdued one-line descriptor sits left-aligned. To the far right of this same row — almost at the opposite edge — a small badge + secondary link floats. This creates a **horizontal tension** across the full width, anchoring both edges without a rigid column system.
- **Lower 55% of the viewport:** An **app interface mockup** — a large, dark rounded-corner panel positioned centrally, slightly inset from the edges. This panel itself has an internal three-column layout: left sidebar nav, center detail view, right metadata + assistant overlay. The UI mockup **bleeds just slightly below the fold**, implying there's more to scroll.

The hero has no background image — pure dark background. All visual interest comes from the UI mockup panel and type.

## Content Sections

### Social Proof / Logo Strip
A **single horizontal row** of partner/customer logos centered in the viewport with generous vertical padding above and below. Logos are monochrome white, evenly distributed at roughly equal spacing. No cards, no labels — just the marks. Extremely minimal.

### Manifesto / Brand Statement
A full-width typographic section: one large statement in **two tones** — the first clause in bright white, the continuation in muted gray. This tonal shift within a single sentence is a deliberate hierarchy trick, guiding the eye and creating a fade-out effect. No imagery. Pure type, left-aligned, very large scale.

### Feature Pillar Sections (repeating pattern, 4–5 instances)

Each feature pillar follows an **asymmetric two-column structure**:

- **Left column (~35–40% width):** Tall, bold headline — 3–4 words max, massive type. Anchored top-left of the column.
- **Right column (~45–50% width):** Body copy paragraph (2–3 sentences), then a numbered section reference in smaller weight. The right column starts mid-height relative to the left, creating a **visual descent** — not a flush top alignment.

Below this text pairing, each pillar has **a sub-navigation grid** — typically 2×2 or 2×3 arrangement of numbered sub-feature labels in a quiet, monospaced-style typographic list.

Then each pillar transitions into a **full-width UI demo panel** — a floating, rounded dark card showing real product screens. These panels are wide but not edge-to-edge; they have visible left/right margins. Some panels show side-by-side split screens. The panels have a subtle depth — they feel like they're resting on the page rather than embedded in it.

### Comparison / Diff Section
One pillar specifically showcases a **split-screen side-by-side comparison view**: two content blocks at equal width within the floating panel. Removed items highlighted on the left, added items on the right. This is the most information-dense moment in the page.

### Analytics / Data Visualization Section
A floating panel containing a **scatter plot + bar chart**, each occupying roughly half the panel width. Left half is a dense data visualization with colored data points. Right half shows status cards with progress indicators. Very dashboard-like, breaking from the narrative flow into hard data.

### Testimonials / Quotes Section
Transition to a **two-column card layout**: two large cards side by side filling most of the viewport width with tight gutters. One card is **very light panel**, the other is **high-contrast accent** — a high-contrast pairing. Each card contains a large pull-quote in dark text. The accent card is a strong visual interrupt — the only chromatic color on an otherwise monochrome page. This is clearly intentional as a scroll anchor moment.

### Content / Release Cards
A **4-column card grid** — compact cards with thumbnail images at top, title, abbreviated description, and date stamp. Cards have a dark card surface, subtle border, and no shadows. Equal-width columns, consistent height.

---

## CTA Section (Pre-Footer)

A **full-width, centered typographic close**: two-line headline at large scale, center-aligned. Below, two side-by-side buttons — Primary CTA (filled, white) and Secondary CTA (outlined, ghost). Buttons are horizontally centered as a paired group. Massive vertical padding above and below — this section breathes heavily, treating the CTA as a ceremonial moment.

---

## Footer

A **multi-column horizontal layout** with significant structure:

- **Far left:** Logo mark only (no wordmark), small scale
- **5 columns of links:** each with a category header and 5–7 text links below. Links are small, low-weight, tight line-height. Columns are evenly distributed across the remaining ~85% of the footer width.

Footer has generous top padding. No background color shift — it blends with the dark page background. No dividing line at the top edge of the footer.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Sticky navigation:** The header remains fixed through all scroll positions, maintaining wayfinding without visual intrusion.

**Floating card system:** Nearly every UI mockup is presented as a slightly inset, rounded-corner panel that feels elevated off the page surface — not edge-to-edge bleed, not flush inline. This consistent "floating panel" motif creates a sense that the product lives in its own plane.

**Controlled asymmetry:** The recurring two-column feature sections never feel like a rigid grid — the right column deliberately starts lower than the left, creating a cascading descent rather than flat alignment. This asymmetry signals craft.

**Color as punctuation:** The entire page is monochrome (dark, light, gray) except for two moments — the accent testimonial card and small status-color indicators within the UI. Color is withheld until it matters, making those moments land hard.

**Spatial rhythm via generous padding:** Every section has what feels like 15–25% of its height in vertical breathing room. The page never feels rushed. Negative space is the primary design element.

**Sub-navigation grids as section maps:** The numbered feature sub-lists function as a persistent wayfinding system — they orient the viewer within the product architecture while remaining visually quiet.
