About

Built by engineers who've lost signal in production and refused to accept it.

2019 Founded
840+ Active Installs
34 Countries

Lacuna Grade was founded after a live broadcast interruption that cost our founding engineer's team an entire night of production. The root cause — a 4ms latency drift in a signal path no one was watching — was entirely preventable. We spent the next year building the instrument we needed.

Today Lacuna Grade instruments are deployed in broadcast facilities, recording studios, and live event productions across 34 countries. Our singular focus remains unchanged: give signal engineers the clarity they need to prevent problems before they reach air.

We make instruments, not software that pretends to be one.

01

Precision first

Every specification is a real number from a real measurement. We publish our noise floor, our latency, and our resolution — because signal engineers deserve instruments they can rely on to the decimal.

02

Transparent signal path

Grade sits in your signal chain without altering it. Observation without interference is the core architectural principle — and it extends to how we write documentation, too.

03

Zero-install operations

A tool that requires a long setup process fails in live production. Grade deploys in minutes, monitors via browser, and alerts via webhook. It should be the easiest thing in your rack.

04

Built for the engineer, not the procurement manager

We write documentation that assumes expertise. We answer technical questions directly. We do not sell through a channel that adds distance between our team and the person using the instrument.
