# Heron Code — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

Heron Code positions itself as a creative development studio where craft and culture intersect — the homepage hero's use of a classical engraved bust, orbital animation paths, and architectural line drawings signals an aesthetic rooted in the history of making as much as in the technology of building. The site is a demonstration of the studio's full-stack capability: the interaction design, the scroll choreography, and the thematic depth of the visual system are simultaneously the portfolio and the argument. This is a studio that has no need to show screenshots of past work when the current work is this.

## Color Architecture

The palette is an off-white/near-black binary with a single acidic yellow accent. The off-white background functions as a light-table surface — neutral, bright enough to read as active rather than passive, the kind of ground on which line drawings and architectural diagrams belong. Near-black appears in all structural elements: the decorative line art, the typographic system, the footer zone. The yellow accent is the palette's only chromatic statement: a hard-edged rectangular block in the hero composition that partially obscures the central illustration, functioning simultaneously as a design element, a compositional weight, and a brand color signal. Its geometric precision in a sea of organic curves and orbital paths is deliberate — a marker of the studio's ability to work in both registers.

## Typography System

The type system is minimal and measured. A clean geometric sans-serif handles all hierarchical levels — from the large-scale typographic interstitial sections (where a single statement fills the viewport at an extreme display scale) down to body text and UI labels in the navigation. The system does not rely on serif contrast or script variation; its differentiation comes entirely from scale, weight, and the relationship between type and animated composition. In the hero, text blocks occupy carefully defined safe zones within the collage, positioned to the right of the central orbital composition where negative space is deliberate. The typographic confidence comes from restraint — the system has fewer decisions to make because the animated visual system carries the expressive weight.

## Spatial System

The spatial logic is radial rather than grid-based for the primary experiential sections. The hero organizes elements compositionally around a central orbital point — a circular reveal mask, surrounding animation paths, layered illustration and line art — with text positioned in the negative space zones that the circular composition creates. The grid returns for functional sections: the Mission & Values section deploys a clean two-column layout with illustrative icons paired with text blocks, providing rest after the complexity of the hero and carousel sequences. The horizontal carousel sections operate in an expanded spatial register — scroll-hijacked, full-viewport — treating each service or bio entry as its own spatial world rather than a grid cell.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

The imagery is a deliberate hybrid of historical and contemporary registers. The classical engraved bust — a scholarly, archival image type — is the hero's central visual, surrounded by modern 3D rendering, orbital line art, and abstract symbol systems. This juxtaposition is the studio's editorial thesis: that building for the web is a craft with historical depth, not just a technology with a current specification. The dark narrative chapter section introduces monochrome photography with accent-colored lighting effects, creating a cinematic register that shifts from the hero's light expansiveness to something more immersive and archival. Team bio portraits appear in the horizontal carousel with the same composition-forward treatment as the service illustrations.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar is sticky and transparent — it hovers over the animated hero without a background, allowing the complex visual composition below it to extend to the top edge of the viewport. The nav's transparent state is both a practical choice (the hero is too layered and beautiful to interrupt with a colored bar) and a conceptual one (the studio's navigation does not assert itself over the work). A single CTA button in the nav carries a thin, slightly rounded border — restrained against the bold compositions below. As sections transition through light and dark backgrounds, the nav adapts its text color accordingly.

## Overall Design Character

Heron Code is a creative development studio site built around the circular/orbital design language as a system metaphor: paths that connect points, revolutions that produce results, elements that orbit a central idea. The off-white and near-black palette with a single yellow accent, the scroll-hijacked horizontal carousels, the classical+contemporary visual hybrid, and the composition-driven anti-grid hero all serve a site that positions its work as both technically sophisticated and culturally aware. The design system is simultaneously the portfolio and the proof of concept.
