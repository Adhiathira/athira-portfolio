Six

The Collection

Six classes.
847 instruments.

Every object has a service history. None have been deaccessioned. Each is documented in the archive ledger from the day it arrived.

I

Class I — Angular Measurement

Sextants & Octants

The collection holds 94 angular measurement instruments spanning 1788 to 1961. The Kelvin series (accession nos. HG-0012 through HG-0018) represents the most complete single-maker run in private hands. All Vernier scales are original; no replacements.

94 Objects — 67 Available

II

Class II — Pressure

Barometers & Barographs

Sixty-three barometric instruments. The two mercurial station barometers (HG-0089, HG-0090) remain calibrated to their 1938 factory certificates and have not been adjusted. Three Fuess barographs retain their original roll-chart mechanisms.

63 Objects — 41 Available

III

Class III — Thermal

Reversing Thermometers

Twelve Negretti & Zambra reversing thermometers from 1951, the full original issue set, in the original carrying case. Six protected-type Richter & Wiese instruments from the 1930s. Used in the Palacios-Vane surveys of the Galician shelf 1934–1952.

48 Objects — 37 Available

IV

Class IV — Depth

Sounding Leads & Echo Sounders

Sounding leads in fourteen calibrated weights from 7 to 56 pounds, spanning four national specifications. Two early transistor echo sounders (Kelvin Hughes, 1961) retain working transducers. Provenance includes the Spanish Hydrographic Institute survey programme.

126 Objects — 98 Available
