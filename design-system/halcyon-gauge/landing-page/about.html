Our History

The archive, the surveyor, the institution.

1894 — 1959

Edith Palacios-Vane

Born in Cádiz in 1894, Edith Palacios-Vane spent thirty-four years as a senior hydrographic surveyor with the Spanish Hydrographic Institute. She was among the first women appointed to the Institute's survey corps and conducted systematic depth surveys of the Galician continental shelf from 1920 to 1954.

Over the course of her career, Palacios-Vane accumulated 847 instruments of working survey provenance. She did not collect for collection's sake. Each object was acquired because she needed it, repaired it, recalibrated it, or had it fabricated to specification. Many instruments in the archive bear her annotations in pencil on the original carrying cases.

"I have never been interested in instruments as objects. I have been interested in what they tell you, when they are properly used."

Edith Palacios-Vane, letter to the Instituto Hidrográfico de la Marina, 1956

Upon her death in 1959, Palacios-Vane's estate fulfilled her standing instruction: the instruments were to be governed by working oceanographers and made available to those who still needed them. The Halcyon Gauge archive was formally established in 1962 at the converted lighthouse keeper's station at Punta Carreiro. Nothing has been sold or deaccessioned.

Archive history.

  1. 1894

    Edith Palacios-Vane born in Cádiz

    Daughter of a lighthouse keeper and a schoolteacher. First accessed a Kelvin sextant at age eleven at the Cádiz maritime station.

  2. 1920

    Appointed to Spanish Hydrographic Institute survey corps

    One of the first women in the corps. Assigned to systematic depth surveys of the Galician shelf and the Ría de Arousa estuary.

  3. 1938

    Acquisition of the Negretti & Zambra barometers

    Two mercurial station barometers acquired from the decommissioned Gibraltar naval meteorological station. Both calibrated to their original 1938 factory certificates. Both still in the archive.

  4. 1951

    Reversing thermometer set commissioned

    Twelve Negretti & Zambra reversing deep-sea thermometers commissioned to Palacios-Vane's specification for the Bay of Biscay deep-water temperature survey. The original carrying case is still present.

  5. 1959

    Death of Edith Palacios-Vane

    Her estate implemented the standing instruction. 847 instruments inventoried. Lighthouse keeper's station at Punta Carreiro conveyed to a trust governing body of active oceanographers.

  6. 1962

    Halcyon Gauge archive formally opens

    First fellowship awarded to Dr. Manuel Ferreira, Universidade de Vigo. The archive ledger opens with his loan of the Kelvin sextant series.

  7. 2026

    847 objects. 14 active fellowships. Nothing deaccessioned.

    The archive continues its original purpose. Every instrument received is still present. Every loan is documented.
