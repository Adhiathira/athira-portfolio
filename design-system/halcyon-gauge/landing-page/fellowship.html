Annual Programme

The Fellowship

Access to the full Halcyon Gauge collection for working oceanographers. Fourteen fellowships active at any given time.

What a fellowship provides.

3

Maximum Concurrent Loans

Three instruments

Fellows may borrow up to three instruments simultaneously from the archive collection.

18

Loan Duration

Eighteen months

Each loan period runs for up to eighteen months, renewable once with current-fellow endorsement.

847

Objects Available

Full collection

All instruments catalogued are eligible for loan. Condition grade is disclosed at the time of request.

Eligibility criteria.

  • i.

    Active affiliation with an oceanographic research programme at the time of application and throughout the fellowship period.

  • ii.

    Nomination by a current Halcyon Gauge fellow or by a department head at an affiliated institution. Self-nomination is not accepted.

  • iii.

    Demonstrated need for analogue measurement instrumentation in active fieldwork. The fellowship is for researchers who will use the instruments, not study them.

  • iv.

    Willingness to return all instruments in the condition received, and to document their use in the archive ledger upon return.

Application for nomination.

This form reaches the archive committee. Applications are reviewed quarterly. You will receive a written response by post.
