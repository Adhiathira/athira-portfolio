Founding Document — 1962

On the purpose of instruments that remain useful.

The instruments in this collection were not collected. They accumulated. Over thirty-four years of active survey work along the Galician coast, Iberian shelf, and the Bay of Biscay, Edith Palacios-Vane acquired, repaired, recalibrated, and in some cases had fabricated to order the objects now catalogued in these pages.

"A sextant does not become imprecise with age. It becomes imprecise through neglect. These instruments are not obsolete. They are simply unused."

Upon her death in 1959, Palacios-Vane left instructions for the establishment of a lending institution to be governed by working oceanographers. The Halcyon Gauge archive opened in 1962 in the converted lighthouse keeper's station at Punta Carreiro. The collection has not been deaccessioned. Every object received is still present.

Access is by annual fellowship nomination. Nominators must be affiliated with an active oceanographic research programme. Fellows may borrow up to three instruments simultaneously for periods not exceeding eighteen months. All loans are documented in the archive ledger and returned to it.

Annual Fellowship Programme

For those who still need to measure.

Fourteen fellowships are active at any given moment. Each grants access to the full collection for eighteen months, renewable once. Nomination requires a letter from a current fellow or department head.
