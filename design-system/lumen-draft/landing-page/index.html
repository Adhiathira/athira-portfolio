How we make

Four stages,
one intention

  • Earth & Form

    We begin with stoneware clay sourced from the Alentejo plateau — a body with high iron content that responds to heat with a depth of warm umber and slate. Each form is thrown or hand-built to specification, never moulded.

  • Glaze Development

    Our glazes are compounded in-studio from raw minerals — feldspar, silica, iron oxides, wood ash. Each commission receives a bespoke palette developed from the architecture's material language: stone, textile, light quality.

  • The Firing

    Work is fired in an electric kiln to 1280°C. Reduction-influenced iron glazes bloom during the cooling cycle — this phase of the process is collaborative between the maker's intention and the material's will. No two firings are identical.

  • Installation & Curation

    For architectural commissions, we work directly with the project architect through installation, providing technical specifications, grout recommendations, and on-site consultation to ensure the work integrates as designed.

"The kiln is the first architect — it decides what the form
becomes, not what it was meant to be."

We work at the intersection of craft and construction. Every piece made in our Alfama studio carries the record of its making — the pull of the hand, the chemistry of the glaze, the temperature of the firing. This is memory in material form.

The workshop

600 square metres
of intention

Our studio occupies a converted warehouse in the Alfama quarter of Lisbon. We keep a small team of four makers, one glaze chemist, and one installation coordinator — large enough to take on complex architectural briefs, small enough for every decision to pass through a single pair of hands.

  • Founded 2018, Lisbon
  • Speciality Architectural stoneware — floor tile, wall cladding, bespoke tableware
  • Commissions Private residences, design hotels, restaurant interiors
  • Lead time 8–16 weeks depending on scale and complexity
