About

We make things that outlast the people who made them

Foramen Studio was founded in Lisbon in 2018 by two ceramicists, Catarina Esteves and Rafael Nunes, with the intention of making work at the intersection of craft and construction — ceramics that function as architecture rather than decoration.

The name Foramen is taken from the Latin term for an aperture — specifically the opening in a kiln through which light and heat pass during firing. We use it as a metaphor for the studio's position: a conduit between raw material and built environment.

The workshop

A working studio, not a showroom

Our workshop in the Alfama quarter was purpose-fitted for production ceramics. We have two electric kilns (120 and 80 litres), four wheel stations, a slab roller, and a dedicated glaze room. The space was designed for efficiency and for focus — no retail floor, no walk-in showroom. Visits are by appointment only, for clients with active commissions.

  • 600 Square metres of workshop and storage space
  • 2 Electric kilns — 120 L and 80 L production capacity
  • 60+ Architectural commissions completed since 2018
  • 8–16 Weeks typical lead time from commission to delivery
