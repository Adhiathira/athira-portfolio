# Brand Name Homepage — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page flows through six major sections, each with a distinct spatial personality: a pinned utility bar, a transparent nav overlay, a full-bleed editorial hero carousel, a category grid, a cinematic campaign banner, a product grid, a services strip, and a full-width footer. The rhythm alternates between immersive full-bleed moments and structured grid sequences, creating a luxury editorial cadence.

A persistent modal overlay (email signup flyout) appears anchored to the lower-right quadrant across all scroll states — a floating layer that sits above every section without affecting page layout.

---

## 1. Utility Bar (Top Edge)

A slim, dark strip pinned to the very top of the viewport — roughly 30px tall. Three zones of equal horizontal weight: store locator (left-aligned with pin icon), a centered promotional ticker ("Primary CTA" with a chevron arrow), and a locale/currency selector (right-aligned). This strip remains visible above the main nav throughout.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

A full-width transparent overlay nav sits directly atop the hero imagery, allowing the photograph beneath to show through. The nav is organized in three balanced columns:

- **Left:** Hamburger + "Menu" label, then Search + "Search" label — icon-text pairs with generous breathing room
- **Center:** The Brand Name wordmark in an elegant serif, approximately 2× larger than surrounding text — clear typographic hierarchy focal point
- **Right:** Wishlist (heart icon + label), Sign In (person icon + label), Bag (tote icon + label)

The nav has no background fill, relying entirely on the contrast between the light type and the dark editorial imagery below. This creates a seamless photo-into-navigation blur.

## 3. Hero Section

**Full-bleed, edge-to-edge, viewport-height imagery.** No margins, no padding — the photograph occupies 100% of the visible area from below the utility bar to the bottom of the viewport.

The current view shows an editorial photograph as primary content. Atop it, two elements float in the lower-left corner:
- A light bold editorial caption — left-anchored, approximately 1/4 from the bottom
- Two category navigation pills in uppercase tracking, rendered as small capsule labels — horizontally stacked below the caption

The second screenshot reveals the hero operates as a **split-panel carousel**: the viewport divides into two equal columns mid-scroll, each showing a full-height product photograph with a single category label in the lower-left of each panel ("Category A" / "Category B"). This is a 50/50 asymmetric editorial split that breaks from the single-hero moment.

**Floating overlay (persistent across scroll):** A modal card anchored to the lower-right — approximately 35% width, roughly 35% height. It is a two-part composition: a square editorial photograph (portrait, roughly 1:1) on the left half of the card, and a light content panel on the right with headline, body copy, email input field, and legal microcopy. This layer floats consistently across all scroll positions.

## 4. Product Grid Section

Below the hero: a **4-column equal-width grid** with very tight hairline vertical dividers between cells. Each cell contains:
- A product photograph on a white or near-white background, centered and floating with generous padding
- No captions visible at this scroll depth

The grid spans full viewport width with no outer margins — the dividers give it a structured editorial grid feel rather than a soft card layout. Proportions suggest approximately square cells.

---

## 5. Campaign Banner (Full-Bleed Cinematic Strip)

Immediately below the product grid: a **full-bleed horizontal image/video banner** at roughly 40–50% viewport height. It behaves as a cinematic interlude — a close-up macro shot with extreme shallow depth of field, creating an immersive texture moment.

Two overlapping text elements float in the lower-left:
- An editorial headline in light bold — "Primary CTA" with an underline treatment
- A secondary label in uppercase

No border, no card container — the text sits directly on the imagery.

## 6. Services Strip

A full-width horizontal strip with light background (near white). Two tiers:
- **First line:** Section heading — left-aligned, bold, slightly larger than the service labels
- **Second line:** 8 service links in a single horizontal row, evenly distributed across the full width with generous spacing between: Service A, Service B, Service C, Service D, Service E, Service F, Service G, Service H

The strip has comfortable vertical padding, creating a clean white-space buffer between the campaign imagery above and the footer below.

## 7. Footer

A two-zone horizontal layout across full width:

**Left zone (~40% width):**
- Bold uppercase brand statement heading
- Short body paragraph
- Full-width email input field with arrow submit button — spans the full width of this zone
- Legal fine print
- Social media icon row — small, evenly spaced
- Locale selector with chevron

**Right zone (~60% width):**
- Four equal-width columns: SUPPORT / SERVICES / COMPANY / LEGAL
- Each column has a bold uppercase label with 2–4 link items below in regular weight
- Consistent vertical rhythm, links aligned to the top of each column

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Full-bleed imagery dominance:** Nearly all primary content sections use edge-to-edge photography with zero outer margin. White-space moments (product grid, services strip, footer) serve as intentional breathing pauses between immersive photo sections.

**Transparent nav overlay:** The navigation has no background, creating the illusion that the brand name and icons float directly in front of the photograph. This is a luxury editorial signature — the site starts as a photograph with navigation on top, not a nav bar above a page.

**Floating persistent modal:** The email signup card uses a fixed-position anchoring strategy — it appears over every section as the user scrolls, never disrupting the underlying layout flow. Its two-part composition (editorial image + functional form) is itself a miniature luxury editorial layout.

**Typographic placement over imagery:** CTAs and section labels appear in the lower-left corner of full-bleed images consistently — this is a deliberate spatial pattern that keeps text readable without obscuring the focal point of the photographs (which tend to be upper-center or upper-right).

**Hairline grid dividers:** The product grid uses 1px dividers rather than card shadows or gap spacing, producing a structured editorial grid feel aligned with luxury print design conventions.
