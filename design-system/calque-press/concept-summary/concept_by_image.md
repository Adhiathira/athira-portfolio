# Calque Press — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A creative studio or agency site that treats the browser viewport as a print canvas. The design's primary architectural decision is to abandon the conventions of web layout — grid systems, section dividers, boxed components — in favor of composition-driven page construction drawn directly from print and editorial precedent. The interface uses one typeface, two colors (black and near-white), no borders, no backgrounds, and no decorative elements. Everything that appears on screen — text, image, space — is purposeful and precisely placed.

## Color Architecture

The palette is absolutely minimal: a cool off-white ground (#fcfcfc), black text and headings, and navigation rendered in near-black ink at consistent weight. The palette earns its restraint: there is no accent, no secondary tone, no gradient. Color here is a decision against color — a deliberate choice to make form and space do all the work. Because the background is consistently off-white throughout every section of the site, the nav requires no scroll-adaptive behavior; black text on near-white is always legible. This is a design that earns its white space rather than filling it.

## Typography System

A single geometric grotesque typeface — Space Grotesk — governs the entire interface, no secondary face, no serif contrast partner. The type system creates hierarchy through scale, case, and tracking rather than through family variation. At the heading level, a deliberate 0.06em letter-spacing opens the geometry of each letterform, giving the large-scale display text a material presence you can sense even before reading: the space between letters feels considered rather than default, like the deliberate kerning decisions a print compositor makes by hand when setting a title page. Section labels and navigation links appear in small all-caps with generous letter-spacing, reading as categories or taxonomies. Body text uses sentence case at a legible body size. The type system's single-family discipline is itself the formal statement.

## Spatial System

Three distinct layout modes operate across the site. The primary showcase pages use asymmetric composition-driven layout: images and text blocks placed on the page canvas with intentional offset, stagger, and negative space — the layout is designed like a spread in a high-quality editorial magazine rather than a website. A tightened container (1220px) increases the sense of editorial focus without sacrificing room for the compositions to breathe. All pages close with a minimal centered CTA section: a headline, supporting text, and a pill-shaped button at the center of the viewport. These modes — editorial, grid, minimal CTA — cover the site's entire structural vocabulary.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

Photography is the primary content on the showcase pages — it is the reason for the layout, not decoration within it. Images are static, rectangular (sharp 90° corners throughout), and placed at varying scales within the composition. The placement strategy is compositional rather than systematic: no repeated formula for where the image sits relative to its text pair. The contrast between loose, curated editorial placement and the structural regularity of supporting grids communicates the difference between the site's creative and organizational registers.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation is a three-zone horizontal bar: wordmark far left, primary navigation links centered, a CTA on the right. The entire nav sits on the same transparent ground as the page. Letter-spacing of 0.08em on nav links gives the wayfinding system a typographic openness that reads as considered rather than default. A deliberate shape distinction exists between navigation CTAs: the header CTA uses a sharp rectangle while the bottom-of-page terminal CTA uses a pill shape — shape-language encoding of two different functional registers.

## Overall Design Character

The site operates at an extreme end of the visual restraint spectrum. Its choices are conspicuous by their absence: no colored backgrounds, no decorative dividers, no shadows or elevation, no rounded corners except the terminal CTA, no secondary typeface, no imagery-as-background. What remains is a system of pure relationship: the relationship between the image and its text, between occupied space and empty space, between the static content and the typography that marks possibility. The 0.06em letter-spacing applied to headings is the system's most distinctive formal decision — it uses the space between letterforms as a dimension, treating the browser as a spatial instrument rather than a document container.
