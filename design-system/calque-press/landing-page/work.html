Pilots & case studies

Pellucid is in limited deployment with institutional research and credit teams. Three representative pilots are described below.

01

Credit Research

European asset manager — high-yield credit review

A European asset management firm piloted Pellucid across a portfolio of 140 high-yield issuers. Analysts used Pellucid to compare covenant language across successive annual filings, surface material changes in issuer risk disclosures, and generate structured credit summaries tied to specific document passages. Time from document receipt to initial credit assessment was reduced by 64%.

Documents processed 1,840
Time reduction 64%
Analysts 12

02

Investment Banking

Global investment bank — M&A due diligence

An investment banking team used Pellucid during a cross-border acquisition process to structure a 2,000-page virtual data room into a queryable corpus. Pellucid identified 23 material disclosure variations between target and acquirer filings that had been missed in a prior manual review pass. The audit trail exported directly into the deal team's existing workflow.

Data room pages 2,000+
Material variations found 23
Review cycle reduction 3 weeks → 4 days
→ assets/work-case-manda.jpeg

03

Regulatory Research

Regulatory intelligence team — Basel IV implementation tracking

A regulatory intelligence function used Pellucid to monitor and compare Basel IV implementation language across 14 jurisdictions. As consultation papers and final rules were published, Pellucid surfaced divergences in disclosure requirements and capital calculation methodologies at the paragraph level, enabling the team to produce jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction gap analysis in a fraction of the time previously required.

Jurisdictions monitored 14
Regulatory documents 340+
Gap analysis time reduction 78%
