Capabilities

01

Structural parsing at institutional scale

Pellucid ingests multi-hundred-page documents — 10-Ks, offering memoranda, Basel disclosures — and maps every section, table, footnote, and cross-reference into a queryable layer graph. The document is no longer a PDF. It is a navigable object.

How it works →

02

Cross-document comparison

Surface material changes between filing vintages. Pellucid aligns document versions at the paragraph level, highlighting additions, deletions, and reframings.

03

Footnote and exhibit resolution

Every footnote, exhibit reference, and cross-reference is resolved in-context. Nothing requires a second tab. The document's full argumentative structure is always visible.

04

Institutional audit trail

All queries, summaries, and annotations are logged with provenance. Every insight traces back to a specific line in a specific filing. Reproducibility is built in.

05

Private deployment

Pellucid runs inside your infrastructure. Documents never leave your environment. The intelligence layer is yours; the processing stays private.

The problem

Institutional documents are designed to be complete,
not legible.

A modern 10-K runs to 200 pages. A leveraged buyout credit agreement to 600. Analysts spend the majority of their time locating information, not using it. Pellucid inverts this ratio: the document becomes a surface to query, not a text to read end-to-end.
