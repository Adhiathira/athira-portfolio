# Brand Name — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately 8 major sections, all unified by a persistent dark background that transitions across the full scroll. The aesthetic is cinematic and immersive — a continuous dark environment (deep dark-to-charcoal gradient with animated wireframe terrain) rather than discrete white-box sections. Sections feel like acts in a presentation, not separate pages.

---

## Header / Navigation

A fixed, full-width navigation bar floats above everything. It is three-zone: logo anchored far left, a centered announcement banner (thin pill/bar), and login + CTA button anchored far right. The CTA ("Primary CTA") is a warm accent filled button — the only strongly colored element in the nav. A dismissible thin top bar sits above even the nav, acting as a system-level announcement layer. The nav itself has no background fill — it's transparent, reading directly over the hero.

---

## Hero Section

Full-viewport-height, full-bleed. The hero uses heavy layering:

- **Background:** An animated 3D wireframe terrain (tinted grid mesh) fills the entire viewport. Large ghost-lettering ("Brand Name") is rendered in the background at massive scale — barely visible, pure atmospheric texture.
- **Content column:** Centered, roughly 50% of viewport width. Stacked vertically: eyebrow label → headline (large serif weight) → overlapping italic/script display text (a different font, rotated or offset, creating a typographic collision effect) → body paragraph → two CTA buttons side-by-side.
- **Spatial rhythm:** The typographic stack has generous vertical breathing room. The script overlay crosses over the headline — deliberate collision, not alignment.
- **Buttons:** Two pill-shaped buttons sit on the same row, centered. One outline/ghost, one filled.

---

## Section 2 — Pain Point

A centered content column, roughly 600–700px wide, against the full-bleed background. This is a pure text section — headline, body paragraph — with no cards or columns. It transitions immediately into the value proposition sub-heading, also centered. Minimal spatial footprint; functions as a conceptual bridge.

## Section 3 — Value Proposition (Three Feature Cards)

A **three-column card grid**, roughly equal-width columns with moderate gutters. Cards have:
- Frosted/semi-transparent dark backgrounds (subtle glass effect)
- Large headline inside each card, body copy below
- No icons — pure type hierarchy
- Cards sit on the full-bleed terrain background — they float visually, not contained in a section box

Vertical rhythm: the section heading is centered above the grid with comfortable top margin. Cards are equal height, aligned at top.

---

## Section 4 — Positioning Statement

A centered, wide headline — typographically large, italic/serif. Subtitle directly below, also centered. Very compressed vertically. No images, no cards. Pure declarative statement section. Transitions into the next section.

---

## Section 5 — Audience Segments

**Asymmetric two-column layout.** Left column holds a large display headline that spans roughly 35% of the width. Right column holds a stacked list of audience segments — each with an icon, label, problem statement, and key differentiator paragraph. The right column occupies ~55% of width. Segments are separated by generous vertical spacing — not card-boxed, but list-structured with clear visual breaks between items. Small geometric icons prefix each segment label.

---

## Section 6 — Product Demo Preview

Full-bleed background section. Centered headline and subtitle. Below: a **product screenshot mockup** displayed as a centered, slightly elevated "floating screen" — browser chrome visible, light background inside the UI. The mockup appears to break slightly out of its section boundary, overlapping into adjacent sections. This creates a layered, depth-suggesting effect — the product floats *in front of* the environment.

---

## Section 7 — Pricing

Centered headline, subtitle. A **toggle control** (Option A / Option B) sits centered, styled as a pill segmented control. Below: **two pricing cards** side-by-side, roughly equal width, moderate gap. One card is visually elevated/highlighted (featured badge, filled border or stronger treatment). Cards contain: plan name, price, description, CTA button. Layout is symmetric — classic two-column pricing split.

---

## Section 8 — FAQ

Centered header. Below: a **stacked accordion list** — full-width (roughly 700px max-width, centered). Each FAQ item is a flat card/row with question text on left and a chevron on right. Items are separated by thin dividers or spacing. Transparent/frosted background on each item. Standard disclosure pattern.

---

## Footer

Three-column layout with generous padding:
- **Left:** Logo mark + copyright text
- **Center:** "Legal" label + two links (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service)
- **Right:** "Support" label + two links (Sign In, Contact)

Clean, minimal. No decorative elements. Footer sits on a dark background that fades from the terrain to flat near-black.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Full-bleed continuous environment:** The wireframe terrain background runs the entire page height — sections do not have individual backgrounds. The "sections" are purely content regions floating within one continuous environment.

**Typographic layering in hero:** Two different type treatments (clean sans headline + oblique/script display type) deliberately collide and overlap — not a mistake, a compositional choice creating visual tension and energy.

**Floating product screenshot:** The demo mockup appears to break its section boundary, creating overlap with adjacent content — a depth/parallax suggestion without actual parallax.

**Transparent/glass cards:** Feature cards and FAQ items use frosted translucency rather than opaque fills, preserving visual continuity with the terrain background behind them.

**Centered spine:** Almost every section uses a centered content column (varying widths: 40% for text, 60–80% for cards/pricing). The page has a strong vertical centerline with no persistent sidebar or off-center anchoring.

**Top announcement bar + nav layering:** Two persistent top elements — announcement strip + navigation — create a layered header zone. The nav is transparent, pulling the hero visually upward to fill the full viewport.
