# Brand Name — Layout Brief

---

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page is organized into roughly **8–10 distinct full-width sections**, flowing vertically with clear thematic breaks. The rhythm alternates between **dark full-bleed hero sections** and **light content sections**, creating a strong visual pulse. There's a consistent horizontal max-width container centered on the page, but several sections intentionally break out to full-bleed color. The overall proportion favors generous whitespace in content sections contrasted against dense, bold typographic moments in transitional sections.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

- **Full-width sticky bar**, dark background, moderate height (~60–70px)
- **Three-column navigation structure**: left cluster of 3 links, centered logo (Brand Name in bold all-caps), right cluster of 3 links
- Navigation text is light, lightweight, evenly spaced
- The logo sits exactly on the vertical centerline of the bar
- As the user scrolls, the nav remains fixed — visible in every subsequent screenshot with content scrolling beneath it
- The bar has a subtle **border-radius on the bottom corners**, giving it a pill-like "floating tray" feel rather than a sharp edge

---

## 3. Hero Section

- **Full-bleed dark background**, full viewport height
- Layout is **center-aligned typographically**, but the composition is punctuated by two **large floating illustrated figures** — one on the left and one on the right — that extend beyond the text column and nearly reach the viewport edges
- The figures are **oversized relative to the text block**, creating a playful, off-balance tension
- Central text stack (top to bottom):
  - Small overline label (centered, light, small caps)
  - Massive display headline in light color, bold, condensed, all-caps — 3 lines
  - Last two lines break into an **accent highlight block** — a full-width accent-color rectangle sits behind those words, creating a stacked color-block effect within the headline itself
  - Short body paragraph in light color, centered, ~50–60% page width
  - Two pill-shaped CTA buttons side by side: filled (Primary CTA) + ghost (Secondary CTA), centered
- The floating illustrated figures are **not contained in a box** — they float freely in the dark field, sitting slightly lower than center, creating a compositional asymmetry

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

**Floating illustrated figures** — The illustrated characters are the most distinctive spatial pattern. They are oversized, uncontained by any grid column, and appear between content zones or overlapping section boundaries. They create a sense of playful chaos within an otherwise orderly grid.

**Accent highlight blocks inside headlines** — Rather than inline color, the accent is applied as a full-width background rectangle behind selected lines of display text. This makes the highlight feel architectural rather than typographic.

**Sticky left panel + scrolling right column** — Section 3 uses a split-pane model where one side anchors while the other scrolls, creating a storytelling rhythm without a modal or slide pattern.

**Alternating card offset** — Section 2 staggers cards left-right rather than centering them, creating diagonal eye movement down the page.

**Pill/chip tags** — Used consistently across search filters, article categories, and resource topics. Always light on dark, or dark on accent — never generic square checkboxes.

**Two-color section rhythm** — The page pulses between dark (hero, CTAs, emphasis) and light (content, reading zones), with an accent color that only appears on highlights, buttons, and card fills. This three-color system is applied with architectural discipline throughout.
