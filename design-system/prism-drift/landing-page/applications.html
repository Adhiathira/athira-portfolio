03 / Optics in Motion

Light as Material

The film doesn't reflect a color — it constructs one. Alternating layers of high- and low-refractive-index dielectrics, deposited at atomic precision, determine which wavelengths undergo constructive interference. The result is a surface that responds dynamically to its environment: every beam of light is separated, redistributed, made visible.

Fashion

High Fashion

Couture houses specify IRID's textile coatings to achieve the effect of shot silk without weave complexity. A single base fabric — silk, technical satin, or bonded nylon — becomes a shifting chromatic surface that changes as the wearer moves. No two angles of view produce the same color.

02

Performance Wear

IRID's moisture-resistant thin-film process bonds directly to engineered stretch fabrics without affecting hand or breathability. Athleisure and cycling apparel gain dramatic visual identity — without dye that fades after twenty washes. UV-stable for 5+ years under direct exposure.
