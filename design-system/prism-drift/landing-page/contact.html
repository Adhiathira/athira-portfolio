FAQ

IRID structural color coatings are compatible with metals, glass, high-performance polymers, and select composite substrates. We evaluate each surface for adhesion and optical compatibility before confirming suitability. Contact us with your material specification for a preliminary assessment.

Structural color does not fade because it derives from light interference rather than dye chemistry. Our coatings are rated for 15+ year UV stability. Hardness and scratch resistance vary by substrate and application environment — we provide application-specific test data on request.

Yes. The angle at which peak color is expressed — the flop angle — is a specifiable parameter in IRID's interference stack design. We work with clients to define both primary hue and the angular range over which the color shifts. Custom color-angle specifications require a full technical brief and lab validation phase.

For standard catalogue coatings, our minimum order is 10 m² of substrate area. Custom interference stacks require a minimum prototype run of 2 m² for validation, followed by a production run. Pricing scales with surface area, stack complexity, and substrate type.

Yes. IRID ships coated substrates globally and supports remote project management for international clients. For large architectural or automotive programs, we can coordinate with local applicators. Initial project consultations are conducted remotely via video call with sample kits shipped to your location.
