Our
Story

IRID was founded in 2019 by a group of materials scientists and optical engineers who refused to accept that color had to come from pigment. For centuries, color has been locked into molecules — dye, ink, paint — all systems that fade, wear, and pollute. We believed there was another way.

Inspired by the wing scales of the Morpho butterfly and the surface structure of peacock feathers, we began building a new science: structural color through biomimetic thin-film deposition. Light doesn't pass through a dye. It interferes with itself across nano-scale film layers, and the result is spectral color of extraordinary purity — color that cannot wash out because it has no chemistry.

Today, IRID operates the world's only commercial-scale structural color deposition facility. Our coatings have been applied to aerospace components, haute couture textiles, and architectural surfaces — anywhere the demand for permanent, vivid, sustainable color is non-negotiable.

Philosophy

What We
Believe

01 —

Nature Already Solved Color

Every iridescent surface in the natural world — beetle carapace, soap film, dragonfly wing — demonstrates that the most vivid color requires no pigment at all. The engineering challenge is not inventing a new chemistry but decoding and replicating an architecture. We are translators, not inventors.

02 —

Precision Is the Product

Film thickness is measured in nanometers. A deviation of 20nm shifts the color by tens of degrees on the color wheel. This is not a manufacturing tolerance — it is the product itself. Every deposition run is an act of precision engineering, and we hold that standard in every client engagement, at every scale.

03 —

Permanence Is the Promise

Structural color does not fade because it has no absorbing molecule to degrade. It does not leach because it contains no soluble dye. We design for a 200-year surface lifetime. This is not a warranty claim — it is the physics of light interference, and physics does not expire.

How We Work

The Five-Step
Deposition Process

01
Brief

We define exact target color coordinates (CIE L*a*b*), substrate material, and performance specification — durability, angle-dependence, thermal range.

02
Substrate Analysis

Surface chemistry, roughness, and thermal coefficient are measured. Adhesion interlayer selection follows from material compatibility modeling.

03
Film Design

Transfer-matrix simulation models the full optical stack. Layer count, material selection, and thickness targets are optimized to achieve the specified spectral output.

04
Deposition Run

Physical vapor deposition in a controlled vacuum environment. Each layer is deposited and measured in situ — real-time feedback adjusts deposition rate to nanometer precision.

05
QA Spectroscopy

Full spectral analysis confirms reflectance peak against simulation. Environmental stress testing validates performance. Certified spectral data sheet delivered with every batch.
