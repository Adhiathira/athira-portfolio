Frequently asked

Can I change plans?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. No penalties, no lock-in.

Is there a free trial?

Pivot offers a 14-day free trial on the Pro plan. No credit card required. You'll get full access to every Pro feature from day one.

How does billing work?

Plans are billed monthly or annually. Annual billing saves 20%. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.

What analytics does Pivot track?

Pivot captures custom events, page views, user flows, retention cohorts, funnel drop-offs, and A/B test results. You define what matters.

Do you offer annual discounts?

Annual plans are discounted 20% versus monthly billing. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom annual contracts with added terms.

What support is included?

Starter plans include email support with a 48-hour response time. Pro includes priority email and live chat. Enterprise gets a dedicated success manager.
