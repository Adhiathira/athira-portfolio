# Brand Name — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page reads as a long-form scroll experience with approximately **10–12 distinct sections**, alternating between light/off-white backgrounds and deep dark sections. This alternation creates a strong rhythmic pulse — dark sections feel like punctuation marks that reset the reader's eye before returning to content-dense light zones. The overall flow is: announcement bar → nav → hero → trust strip → dark problem section → process section → value proposition → portfolio grid → testimonials → audience accordion → FAQ → lead magnet dark section → blog preview → product shop → footer.

---

## Header / Navigation

The header is **fixed/sticky** with a clean light background and tight vertical padding — roughly 60–70px tall. It uses a classic three-zone split: logo lockup (icon + wordmark) hard-left, centered horizontal nav links with generous letter-spacing, and a single CTA button hard-right. The CTA button (accent color, all-caps, wide letter-spacing) carries significant visual weight relative to the text links, creating a clear call-to-action hierarchy. A thin full-width **announcement bar** sits above the nav — dark background, light centered text with a `→` arrow — adding urgency without competing with the nav's whitespace.

---

## Hero Section

The hero is a **split two-column layout** with a heavily layered right side. Left column (roughly 40% width) contains a stacked hierarchy: small overline text, large serif headline (two lines, very heavy weight), a body paragraph in a secondary typeface, and a large solid CTA button. The right side (60%) is an elaborate **collaged composition** — a full-bleed atmospheric photograph bleeds to the edges, with a device mockup floating over it and a branded product overlapping into the foreground. These elements are layered with generous depth: the product appears in front of the device which appears in front of the background. The overall hero has a **warm atmospheric gradient wash** behind the background image. The bottom edge transitions to a dark section via a **concave wave/scallop shape**, not a hard line — this wave shape recurs throughout the page as a major layout motif.

## Content Sections

### Problem Section (Dark, Full-Bleed)
A **two-column asymmetric layout** on a dark background. Left: a large illustrative scene (journey/process metaphor). Right: a bulleted pain-point list with `►` markers. The illustration takes about 45% width, occupying the full section height. The section uses the wave top-edge transition from the hero.

### Process / "Quickest Path" Section (Light)
Starts with a **centered heading block** (large bold serif headline with an accent-color word), followed below by an **icon-trio row** — three equally spaced icons with labels, suggesting a horizontal 3-column process flow. Generous vertical padding gives this section room to breathe.

### Value Proposition Section (Light, Asymmetric Repeating Rows)
A series of **alternating two-column rows** — each pairing a photography card (left or right, ~55% width, with an overlapping circular icon/badge at the corner) against a text block (headline + body paragraph). The photography card has an accent-color caption bar at the bottom as a CTA strip. Rows alternate left-image / right-image, creating a classic zigzag reading pattern. Decorative line-art watermarks appear in the background at large scale, adding texture without competing with content.

### Portfolio / Work Section (Light)
Opens with a **centered headline** + subtext + underlined text link. Below: a **4-column card grid** of work screenshots — equal-width columns, uniform card heights, appearing as a tightly packed portfolio mosaic.

### Category Ticker Strip
A **full-width horizontal scrolling ticker** (or static overflow row) of category/niche labels — small caps, widely spaced — on a light background. Acts as a spatial divider between sections.

### Testimonials Section (Dark)
Centered heading hierarchy followed by a **4-column testimonial card carousel**. Cards have a slightly darker background than the section, creating subtle depth. Each card: star rating, truncated review text, "Read more" link in accent color, source logo badge, avatar + reviewer name + time. Carousel navigation arrows float on the left and right edges. Pagination dots below.

### Audience / "Who We Help" Section (Light, Accordion)
**Two-column layout**: left column has a stacked headline + subtitle + CTA button. Right side contains a **stacked accordion list** of audience segments in spaced-caps typography, each with a chevron icon. When an accordion item is expanded, a dark content card appears to the right of the accordion, paired with a photography image below it. A circular badge ornament floats at the junction between the two columns, overlapping both zones.

### FAQ Section (Light, Two-Column)
Left: a compact block with a decorative ornament, section title, and body text + CTA button. Right: a **vertical accordion list** of FAQ items — all in spaced-caps formatting, each with a chevron. The section sits above a silhouette SVG shape that forms the transition into the next dark section.

### Lead Magnet Section (Dark)
A **centered two-column form block** on a near-black background. Left: headline + body copy. Right: email input field + a prominent high-contrast CTA button ("Primary CTA"). Decorative contour lines form a large background element in the bottom-right corner.

### Blog / Insights Section (Light)
Opens with centered label + large serif headline (accent-color words). Below: a **3-column equal-width card grid** — each card has a full-width image thumbnail, article title text below. Tight vertical rhythm.

### Product Shop Section (Dark)
Centered headline followed by a **4-column product card row** — each card shows a device mockup with the product rendered on screen. Cards have equal proportions and generous internal padding.

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Wave / organic section dividers** — Instead of flat horizontal rules, sections transition via CSS-generated wave or silhouette shapes. This is the single most distinctive layout signature of the page.

**Overlapping decorative elements** — Circular badge/icon ornaments float precisely at column junctions, partially overlapping both the image and text zones. This is used at least 3–4 times across the page and creates a layered, hand-crafted feel.

**Decorative line-art watermarks** — Full-bleed decorative background graphics (contour lines, silhouette outlines) are used at very low opacity or as stroke-only art, giving depth to sections without adding visual noise.

**Dark/light alternation as rhythm** — The page never stays in one palette register for more than 2 sections before switching. This creates strong visual pacing and prevents scroll fatigue.

**Sticky navigation** — The nav bar remains visible throughout the full scroll, always providing the CTA button in the upper-right corner.

**Photography cards with branded CTA footers** — In the value proposition zigzag section, image cards have an accent-color strip across the bottom containing a CTA link — turning the image itself into an interactive element with a clear affordance.
