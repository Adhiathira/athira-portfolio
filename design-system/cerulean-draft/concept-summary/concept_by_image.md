# Cerulean Draft — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A design system built around the precise discipline of organized clarity — category structures, legible information hierarchies, and conversion patterns elevated by a distinctive typographic identity and deliberate color-blocking strategy. The system's primary sophistication is structural: background color alternation as the sole section separator, and a shift in component shape language (sharp throughout browsing, pill-shaped at conversion points) as a psychological signal that changes register from browse-mode to commit-mode. The wordmark deploys a geometric precision face, establishing an instruments-grade technical tone rather than a commodity register.

## Color Architecture

The color system divides into two conceptual layers. Backgrounds operate through a cool neutral-to-accent palette: pure white for content grids, a light muted blue-grey (`#f0f0f4`) for structural interstices, accented moments of deep midnight (`#1b1b37`) for emphasis containers. These background alternations are the page's primary organizational mechanism — no decorative dividers or borders between sections. Accents are purposeful and precisely weighted: a clear teal (`#63cabe`) for interactive affordances, a dusty violet (`#b79cff`) for secondary signals, and a mid-blue (`#477cbe`) for tertiary emphasis. The border color (`#cccce1`) is a blued grey that reads as structural precision rather than decorative line-work — an instrument calibration line.

## Typography System

A single typeface family (Syne) serves all roles. Its geometric structure and distinctive character in wider-set display sizes — especially at the 0.06em letter-spacing applied to all headings — produces an architectural, almost technical quality. Display headings at 72px/300 weight read as quiet structural statements rather than announcements. Utility text at uppercase 14px with the same 0.06em tracking reads as system labels. Body text runs at 18px/300 with 0.02em tracking — restrained but not tightened. The uniformity of the type family across all roles creates a system that feels internally coherent — like all text belongs to the same instrument — rather than a layered editorial composition that mixes personality types.

## Spatial System

The material texture of this system is expressed through negative space. Section padding of 152px top and bottom — generous for an editorial register — creates a breathing rhythm between content zones that is felt as much as seen: each section arrives with the weight of room around it, like pages in a well-bound book where the margins are part of the reading experience. The container's tightened 1296px maximum width (slightly narrower than a full 1440px grid) means that on wide screens, the content sits within implied margins — not because the edges are decorative, but because the reading measure is deliberately kept from sprawling. Column gaps at 17.778px are tight, emphasizing the grid's regularity.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

The visual system is built for high precision over atmospheric warmth. Product contexts should deploy imagery that implies measurement, refinement, or testing — not craft or handwork. Where photography appears, it functions as documentation rather than aspiration: clinical studio setups with controlled light, instruments laid against neutral surfaces, or architectural details photographed with the flatness of technical illustration. The teal and violet accent palette operates as a labeling system — colors that identify rather than ornament.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation system is structured as an overlay that dissolves into content — positioned absolutely, with a 96px height, 40px inline padding, and Syne at 14px with 0.08em letter spacing. This letter spacing gives nav labels an architectural quality — each link reads as a precisely spaced system label rather than a casual word. Hover states fade to 0.75 opacity rather than using underlines or background highlights, creating a deferential interaction vocabulary that keeps the nav visually subordinate to content. The nav disappears on scroll via opacity/visibility toggling, allowing page content to occupy full viewport without a persistent interface layer competing for attention.

## Overall Design Character

The sophistication of this system lives in its calibration. The 0.06em letter spacing on headings is not decoration — it is the typographic equivalent of a measurement tolerance, a deliberate opening of the letter-forms that makes each heading feel precisely set rather than casually placed. The choice of a single typeface family across all roles removes the hierarchy-by-personality approach and replaces it with hierarchy-by-scale-and-weight — a more disciplined register, closer to system design than editorial design. The cool blue-white palette and teal/violet accents read as instrument panels rather than brand colors. The result is a system that communicates through precision: not warm, not cold, but exact.
