Subsurface Modeling

The ground, rendered
at instrument precision.

01

3D Grid Construction

Variogram-based kriging interpolation builds a three-dimensional soil classification grid at 0.5m resolution from sparse borehole data. The interpolation is calibrated to site-specific soil classification schemes — not generic textbook parameters.

02

Formation Boundary Detection

Geological formation boundaries — the interfaces between soil and rock layers — are extracted using CPT friction ratio transitions and cross-validated against borehole classification logs, producing a formation model with uncertainty bounds at each boundary surface.

03

Water Table Mapping

Piezometer readings and seasonal high-water observations are interpolated across the site extent to produce a dynamic water table surface — with confidence intervals that reflect the density of measurement points.

04

Load-Ground Interaction

Import a structural foundation layout and apply load scenarios to the 3D model. Bearing capacity, settlement, and lateral earth pressure are computed at each foundation node using the Eurocode 7 partial factor framework.
