The studio

We started with one question.

What happens if you take the most active possible form of a botanical ingredient and design everything around the moment it enters water?

How we began

Solubl was founded in Copenhagen in 2021.

Our founders — a cosmetic chemist and a product designer — had been working independently for a decade. One building formulations for pharmaceutical clients. The other designing packaging that would eventually be thrown away. They met at a seminar on effervescent drug delivery systems. The conversation that followed lasted eight hours.

The question they kept returning to was this: pharmaceutical-grade effervescent technology is used to deliver active compounds into the body with precision and speed. Why had nobody applied the same rigour to bath and skincare? The industry used fizzing as a novelty. They saw it as a delivery mechanism.

"We weren't trying to make a nice bath product. We were trying to find the most technically honest way to get an ingredient into contact with skin."
Solubl founders, 2021

Three years of formulation work followed. The first stable cold-process effervescent base was achieved in 2022. The first commercial batch — Abyssal Magnesium, now part of the Mineral Drift collection — shipped in January 2023.

Today Solubl operates from a 280m² lab and studio space in Nørrebro, Copenhagen. We produce 18 SKUs in batches of 500–2,000 units. Zero synthetic ingredients. Zero heat in production. Everything shipped in zero-waste packaging.

What we believe

Four positions we will not negotiate on.

Ingredient before formula

We start with the ingredient and ask what it needs. We don't start with a marketing brief and find ingredients to fill it.

Process before convenience

Cold-process production is harder, slower, and more expensive than conventional approaches. It is also the only way to preserve what we care about in the ingredient.

Traceability before scale

Every ingredient we use has a named source, a traceable lot, and a direct supplier relationship. We will not scale faster than we can maintain this.

Less, better

18 SKUs is a deliberate constraint. We would rather make 18 things that are genuinely excellent than 80 things that are adequate.

The space

280m² in Nørrebro.

The Solubl lab is in a former tile factory on Blågårds Plads in Nørrebro, Copenhagen. The concrete floor is original. The roof is glass. Formulation and packaging happen in the same space, under the same light.
