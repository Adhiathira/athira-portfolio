The dissolution

Three moments. One transformation.

01

Contact

The concentrate meets water. The effervescent matrix activates — releasing a controlled burst of carbon dioxide that begins breaking the tablet's crystalline structure from the outside in, while pulling active ingredients toward the surface.

02

Diffusion

Active compounds enter the medium. They don't mix — they diffuse, following concentration gradients through the water in organic bloom patterns. This is the moment the formula becomes visible: colour, scent, mineral haze radiating outward from the dissolution point.

03

Equilibrium

The formula reaches full dissolution. Every molecule is now evenly distributed through the bath water — pH balanced, osmotically calibrated, isotonically matched to skin. What was solid is now everywhere, invisible, and working.

Inside the formula

We begin with the ingredient, not the brief.

Every source material enters our Copenhagen lab as a botanical extract, mineral concentrate, or cold-pressed oil. We test its behaviour in water — solubility, diffusion velocity, pH impact — before designing the carrier matrix that will hold it in suspension until the moment of use.

  • Nordvest Atlantic kelp Wild-harvested from the Faroe plateau, dried at 28°C, cold-extracted in reverse osmosis water
  • Deep-sea magnesium complex Crystallised from 800m Atlantic brine — no heat, no chemical precipitation
  • Rosa damascena extract Steam-distilled in Isparta, Turkey — each gram requires 3,000 petals
  • Cold-pressed bergamot peel oil Calabrian harvest, unrefined — the phototoxic furanocoumarin fraction retained intact for bath use
