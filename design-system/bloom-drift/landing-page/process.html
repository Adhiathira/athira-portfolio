How we make it

Cold-process formulation — start to finish.

No heat. No shortcuts. Every step in the Solubl process is designed to preserve the active character of the ingredient — until the moment it meets water.

→ assets/process-sourcing.jpeg
01

Sourcing

Every ingredient enters our Copenhagen lab with a complete provenance record. We source directly from the primary producer — no distributors, no blended lots. Atlantic kelp from the Faroe plateau, rose from Isparta, bergamot from a single Calabrian grove. The source determines the character; the character determines the formula.

→ assets/process-extraction.jpeg
02

Cold Extraction

We extract at or below 28°C. Heat destroys volatile aromatics, degrades polyphenols, and alters mineral ratios. Our extraction uses reverse osmosis water, slow agitation, and controlled time rather than temperature. The resulting extracts are denser, more complex, and structurally closer to the source material than conventionally extracted alternatives.

→ assets/process-matrix.jpeg
03

Matrix Formulation

The effervescent carrier matrix is where chemistry becomes visible experience. We calibrate the ratio of citric acid to sodium bicarbonate to control dissolution velocity — faster matrices release colour and fragrance immediately; slower matrices diffuse over 3–5 minutes. Each formula has a target dissolution profile that is tested against the sensory character of the extract it carries.

→ assets/process-compression.jpeg
04

Compression

Tablets are compressed at pharmaceutical grade. No binding agents, no fillers, no synthetic flow aids. The only materials present in a Solubl tablet are the active ingredients and the effervescent base. Compression pressure is calibrated per formula — too light and the tablet crumbles; too heavy and dissolution slows below the target profile.

→ assets/process-qa.jpeg
05

Dissolution QA

Every batch undergoes a 38°C dissolution test in a controlled water column before release. We measure dissolution time (±8 seconds of target), dispersion colour against the formula's reference palette, pH (calibrated to within ±0.2 of spec), and residue. A batch that fails any single parameter is reformulated, not released.

Our commitments

What doesn't change, batch to batch.

◎

No heat, ever

The maximum temperature reached at any point in the Solubl process is 28°C — room temperature in our Copenhagen lab in summer. Heat is not a tool here; it is a constraint.

◎

Single-origin ingredients

Every raw material is traceable to a single producer, a single harvest, a single geography. Blended or averaged supply chains produce averaged results. We want the specific one.

◎

Zero-waste production

All extraction byproducts are composted, dried, or returned to ingredient suppliers for secondary use. The packaging is water-soluble film inside recycled paper. Nothing goes to landfill.
