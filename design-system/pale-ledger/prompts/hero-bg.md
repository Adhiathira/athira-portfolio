## Prompt

The palette is restricted to deep navy (#0e1726), warm off-white cream (#fcfbf9), and muted rose-gold accent tones. The image has the quality of a luxury financial publication: no people, only the architecture of precision — curved monitor arrays, printed ledger sheets fanned across a surface, soft bokeh in the background. The mood is quiet authority before markets open.
