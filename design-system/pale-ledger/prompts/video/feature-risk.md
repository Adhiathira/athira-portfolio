## Prompt

Editorial still-life video on a dark navy (#0e1726) surface. A translucent acrylic card showing a risk heatmap in cream-to-blue gradient tones rests in the center of the frame. A compass tool is open to a precise angle. A printed stress-test scenario sheet with fine typography completes the composition. The camera makes an imperceptibly slow lateral drift — less than two centimeters of movement over the entire clip — causing the dramatic upper-left key light to shift its shadows with glacial precision, the long clean shadows rotating slightly across the dark surface. The heatmap on the acrylic card catches the light differently as the angle changes, its gradient shifting from warm cream to cold #0067b8. The palette is strictly limited: deep navy background, cream (#fcfbf9) paper elements, Microsoft blue (#0067b8) accent details only. No decorative motion — only the geometry of instruments of measurement revealing themselves through light. The mood is surgical, authoritative, controlled risk.
