## Prompt

Editorial top-down video of a cream-colored desk surface with financial newspapers from multiple countries arranged in precise overlapping layers. Morning light streams in from the left at a 30-degree angle — and over the course of the shot, it slowly advances across the newspapers as if minutes are passing at dawn, the shadows shortening by a fraction of a degree. A single fine-tipped pen rests on a headline and remains perfectly still. The newspapers do not move — they are anchored, deliberate, structured to an invisible grid. The only motion is light itself traveling across paper, gradually illuminating the headline text and the single blue-highlighted passage (#0067b8). Color palette: warm off-white paper (#fcfbf9), deep navy ink text (#0e1726). The impression: global market intelligence curated with calm editorial precision, a mind absorbing everything and filtering to only what matters. Cinematic, unhurried.
