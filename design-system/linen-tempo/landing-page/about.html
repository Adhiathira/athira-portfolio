Our Story

We outfit the long way in

Ardenne Supply Co. was founded in Montréal in 2019 by two people who kept coming home from long routes disappointed — not by the land, but by the kit. We decided to fix that. What started as a product blog became a buying operation, then a small warehouse, then a crew of seven who collectively spend more time in backcountry terrain than behind desks.

🏔️

Where it began

A workshop in Mile End, a question about weight

Founders Sébastien Ruel and Marie-Claude Brodeur met on a Gaspésie traverse in 2016. Three days in, they were both carrying too much. The conversation that started on that ridge — about what gear was earning its place and what was dead weight — didn't stop when they got back to the city.

Three years later, they opened a small shop on Boulevard Saint-Laurent to test a different model: every item reviewed in terrain before it ships. No dropshippers, no brand deals, no commission bias. Just kit that has been carried and tested by the people recommending it.

The workshop in the back — where we repair packs, re-waterproof shells, and replace buckles for customers who've been using Ardenne gear for years — was there from day one. It remains the clearest signal of what we believe: equipment that lasts isn't a bonus feature. It's the whole point.

What We Stand For

The principles we curate by

01

Terrain is the only test that counts

We carry every piece in our collection on multi-day routes before it ships. Lab ratings are a starting point for conversation, not a finish line. If something fails in the field — even once, in conditions a buyer is likely to encounter — it comes out of the collection.

02

Weight is a decision, not a compromise

Every gram you carry has a cost paid in calories, miles, and recovery time. We apply a single question to every item: does the benefit justify the weight at full load, over five or more consecutive days? If the answer is uncertain, it doesn't ship.

03

Repair is not optional

Every Ardenne purchase comes with a lifetime repair commitment. We maintain a full workshop in Montréal staffed by two repair technicians. If your pack, shell, or shelter develops a fault — from wear, abuse, or manufacturer error — we fix it. No expiry date on that commitment.

04

Honest writing, no commission bias

Our journal and buying guides are written by people who carry the gear. We have no brand sponsorships that influence which products we stock. When something in our collection stops performing, we say so in print — and then remove it from the shelf.
