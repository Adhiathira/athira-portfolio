# Brand Name — Layout Brief

---

## Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately **10–12 major sections**, organized as a long-form marketing scroll. The overall rhythm alternates between light (off-white/near-white) and dark (near-black) backgrounds, creating strong visual punctuation between content blocks. The page is center-anchored with a fixed-width nav floated above the content. Full-viewport-width sections are used liberally, with the most impactful moments using edge-to-edge dark panels or oversized type that bleeds beyond comfortable margins.

---

## Header / Navigation

The nav is a **floating pill/bar** — not edge-attached to the page top, but inset and centered horizontally, appearing to hover above content with slight padding on all sides. It's a compact, dark horizontal bar with three zones:

- **Left:** Hamburger "Menu" label — a word+icon pairing, not just an icon
- **Center:** Brand logomark
- **Right:** Two CTAs — Secondary CTA (ghost/outline pill) and Primary CTA (filled accent-color pill)

The bar maintains a **sticky floating presence** throughout scroll, always visible but never touching the edges of the viewport. It reads as a design object sitting *on top of* the page rather than being part of it.

---

## Hero Section

**Full-width, light background.** Two structural layers:

1. **The announcement ticker** — immediately below the nav, a full-bleed horizontal marquee strip with monospaced announcement text scrolling horizontally. This acts as a loud visual interrupt between nav and hero content.

2. **The main hero content** — center-column, vertically stacked:
   - Massive display headline, two lines, taking up roughly 40% of the viewport height
   - A mixed-media subtitle line where inline keywords are styled as **pill-shaped tag chips** (light gray rounded rectangles wrapping individual words), creating a typographic collage feel
   - Below the subtitle, a **floating card gallery** of approximately 5–6 angled/rotated product preview cards arranged in a loose fan or carousel arc. Cards are 3D-rotated (slight perspective tilt), overlapping at the edges, and extend beyond the visible viewport width — intentionally cropped on both sides. This creates a sense of depth and motion, like reaching into a deck.

---

## Section 2 — Platform Statement + Rotating Cards

Dark background section. The layout is the same floating-card arc pattern, but inverted (dark surface). Cards fan outward from a central perspective vanishing point. On the left and right edges, cards from adjacent sections in the sequence bleed in from outside the viewport — reinforcing the idea of infinite horizontal scroll content.

Below the cards, a single centered paragraph in large text — a mission/value statement. This is a **typographic anchor** amid the visual complexity.

---

## Section 3 — Profile + Updates Panel

A **two-column asymmetric split** on a light background:

- **Left column (~35–40% width):** Dark accent card featuring a portrait photo bleeding out of the card frame at the top, a small "Created by" handwritten label, and name + Action Button at bottom
- **Right column (~55–60% width):** Large dark circular shape functioning as a visual container, housing a featured content card in the center — an accent-on-dark resource card with title, meta tags, and thumbnail preview

The circle shape on the right is notably **larger than the column** — it clips at the edges, creating an organic, non-rectangular visual boundary. The two columns sit side by side with no traditional gutter, the contrast between the rectangular accent card and the circular dark element creating a strong visual tension.

---

## Section 4 — The Platform (Product Preview)

Light background, center-column. Sequence:

1. A small label badge at top
2. Large display headline spanning full column width
3. A supporting body paragraph, narrow-set (~50% of content width, centered)
4. A **framed product screenshot** — a dark dashboard UI mockup displayed at approximately 60–65% of viewport width, centered, with rounded corners. The screenshot sits elevated off the page via subtle shadow/containment, suggesting an actual product interface rather than a decorative illustration.

This section is intentionally restrained — the product screenshot does the heavy lifting.

---

## Section 5 — Features Grid (Tiers)

Dark background. Three large **tilt-rotated feature cards** arranged in a loose horizontal row, slightly overlapping, with the center card displayed frontally and the left/right cards angled outward (3D tilt, same treatment as the hero). Each card fills roughly 30–35% of viewport width.

Cards have distinct background colors:
- Left: Dark accent
- Center: Near-black with product UI screenshot
- Right: Light accent

Each card contains an icon, title, subtitle, and an Action Button. The cards bleed off screen edges — again communicating infinite selectable content.

---

## Section 6 — Value Propositions (Feature List)

Light background transitioning from the dark section above, creating a striking contrast cut. Layout is **two-column, left-right split**:

- **Left (~40%):** Massive display headline, left-aligned, running across multiple lines
- **Right (~60%):** Stacked list of 3 feature rows, each separated by a thin horizontal rule. Each row is itself a two-column layout:
  - **Bold feature title** (left ~40%)
  - **Body copy description** (right ~60%)

This is a **definition list** style — clean, editorial, like a magazine feature spec sheet. Generous vertical spacing between rows creates breathing room and gives each feature equal visual weight.

---

## Section 7 — Testimonials + Regional Map

Dark background. A **two-column layout with a strong left-right contrast**:

- **Left (~35%):** A dark oval/ellipse shape containing a simplified map illustration with one region highlighted in an accent color. Section label at top, handwritten label at bottom. The oval clips to the column bounds.
- **Right (~65%):** A large accent testimonial card — full height of the section, containing a quote headline, user avatar (circular photo), name in handwritten script, role badge, and body quote text.

On the far right edge, a **vertical navigation indicator** — 4–5 small dashes stacked vertically, suggesting a slide/carousel position indicator.

---

## Section 8 — Pricing

Dark background, full section. Structure:

1. **Toggle control** at top center — a pill-shaped toggle with "For Individuals" / "For Teams" options, styled as a segmented button
2. Large centered headline: "Pricing for Individuals"
3. Short centered descriptor body copy
4. **Two pricing cards** side by side, roughly 45% width each:
   - **Left card:** Light/white background — the lower-tier plan
   - **Right card:** Accent background — the premium plan, visually dominant

Each card contains: user type badge, plan title in display type, price + billing period, an interval selector toggle (Quarterly / Annually), a Primary CTA (full-width, inside the card), and a benefits list. The accent card includes a bonus offer badge in a contrasting highlight color.

The two-card layout uses **equal widths with strong color contrast** to visually separate tiers without a hierarchy — both feel premium.

---

## Section 9 — Social Proof ("Made with Brand Name")

Dark background. Three structural layers:

1. A **three-word oversized typographic lockup** spanning the full viewport width in display type. Each word sits in its own column zone, left / center / right, creating a tripartite horizontal rhythm. A handwritten annotation sits beneath the center word with a curved arrow.

2. A **center-focused featured project card** — a large dark rectangle with rounded corners, outlined in an accent color, containing a video/screen preview. This card is positioned centrally and sits at roughly 40–45% of viewport width.

3. On left and right, **additional project cards** peek in from off-screen, showing partial edges — indicating a horizontal scroll gallery of user-made projects.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Floating nav** — The navigation bar is a self-contained pill that hovers above the page as a distinct object, maintaining separation from both the header background and the content below.

**Rotated card fans** — Multiple sections use 3D-perspective-tilted cards arranged in a radial fan, deliberately cropped at viewport edges. This recurs as a signature layout motif throughout the page, suggesting unlimited content depth.

**Circle/oval containers** — Two sections use circular or elliptical shapes as content boundaries, breaking the rectilinear grid. These shapes are oversized relative to their columns, creating clipping tension.

**Ticker/marquee strip** — A full-bleed scrolling ticker sits just below the hero nav as a persistent energy injection. This functions as both announcement and decorative rhythm break.

**Alternating light/dark rhythm** — Sections strictly alternate between the off-white and near-black backgrounds, creating a predictable-yet-punchy page scroll rhythm where every section flip feels like turning a page.

**Handwritten annotation layer** — Throughout the page, small handwritten-style notes in contrasting colors appear as annotations layered over structured content. These float freely, outside the grid, functioning as a personality/voice layer above the layout system.

**Radial tick marks** — Circular dashed/tick-mark rings appear as background decorative elements in multiple sections (around circular shapes, around video containers), adding a watchface or compass-like spatial vocabulary to the dark sections.
