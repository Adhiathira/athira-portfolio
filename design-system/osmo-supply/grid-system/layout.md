# Brand Name — Layout Brief

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page is a single long-scroll marketing landing page with approximately **10–12 distinct sections**. The vertical rhythm alternates between **light (off-white) and dark (near-black)** backgrounds, creating a cinematic push-pull contrast as the user scrolls. This color alternation isn't arbitrary — it acts as a visual pacing device, signaling "new chapter" without hard horizontal rules. The overall flow moves from hero → value proposition → social proof → product showcase → features list → trust logos → testimonials → pricing → portfolio → footer.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

The navigation is a **floating pill-shaped bar** horizontally centered in the viewport. It does NOT span full width — it hovers as a compact, rounded-rectangle element roughly 530px wide, centered both horizontally and vertically near the top of the screen. It contains three zones:
- **Left:** Hamburger menu icon + "Menu" label
- **Center:** Brand logo mark
- **Right:** "Login" ghost button + Primary CTA button

This is a **sticky element** that persists across all scroll positions — it floats above all sections regardless of background color. The pill maintains its dark background even over light-background sections, making it always legible. The spatial effect is of a toolbar floating above the page, not embedded in it.

---

## 3. Hero Section

The hero uses a **full-bleed light background** with an extra-large typographic headline centered horizontally. The headline spans two phrases separated by a typographic accent glyph that sits inline at ~3× the cap height of the surrounding words. Font weight is heavy, tracking is tight, and the size is enormous (roughly 15–18vw equivalent), filling nearly the full viewport width.

Below the headline sits a centered, narrower descriptor text block with **inline text-pill highlights** — key product categories and feature names appear wrapped in light rounded pill badges, creating a tag-cloud-like inline rhythm within running text.

The hero lower half shows a **scattered card layout** — product preview cards (screenshots of individual resources) are positioned at **rotated angles (±10–15°)**, overlapping each other and bleeding off the left/right edges of the viewport. This creates depth and motion-energy without any actual animation needing to fire first. The cards are not in a grid — they cascade diagonally, suggesting a physical spread of materials on a desk.

---

## 4. Content Sections

### Value Proposition (Section 2)
Full-bleed light background. **Single-column centered layout.** One massive typographic statement set at display scale (~5–6vw), center-aligned, taking up roughly 60–70% of viewport width. Below it sits a centered video thumbnail, smaller in proportion (roughly 400px wide), flanked by words rendered at display scale but in a ghosted/light tone — creating a **split-text framing device** where those words function as one typographic composition around the media element. A social proof pill (with avatar stack) anchors the bottom center.

### Founder / About Section (Section 3)
**Asymmetric two-column layout** — left column holds a large circular image cropped to a circle, with a dotted arc border. Right column holds a dark card containing product preview screenshots. The columns are roughly 50/50 but the circular photo breaks into the dark-background right panel, creating an **overlapping bleed** between the two panels. The section background transitions from light on the left to dark on the right, forming a hard diagonal split between panels.

### Platform Explanation (Section 4)
Single-column centered on a light background. A large display-scale headline fades in from the bottom. A secondary label appears as a handwritten annotation with a curved arrow — a **marginal note / editorial overlay** pattern that recurs throughout the site.

### Features Grid / Product Dashboard (Section 5)
A **dark background section** with the product UI shown as a browser mockup, centered and large (taking ~55% viewport width). This is framed as a hero image within the section — above it sits a centered headline, and the layout feels like a feature showcase tile. The interface detail is legible at this scale.

### Feature List (Section 6)
Returns to a **light background**, two-column horizontal layout:
- Left column: feature name (bold, larger text)
- Right column: feature description (regular weight, smaller)
- Separated by thin horizontal rules above each row

This is a **table-style list**, not a card grid. Three rows. Proportions feel roughly 40% / 60% left-to-right. The left labels are styled as headers, right as body copy. Generous vertical padding between each row creates breathing room.

### Trust Logos (Section 7)
A horizontal marquee/scroll strip showing partner or client logos. This is a **single-row scrolling band** on a light background, preceded by a small label set in small caps/uppercase tracking.

### Testimonials (Section 8)
**Two-panel layout** — left half shows a dark globe/map visualization (circular, large, taking ~40% viewport width). Right half shows a testimonial card on an **accent-color background** with a large quote headline, avatar, name, title badge, and body copy. The quote is enormous at display scale. This section background is split: dark for the visualization, accent color for the testimony. The testimonial card has slightly rounded corners and feels like a floating panel.

### Pricing (Section 9)
Dark background with a **two-card side-by-side layout**. Cards are large (each ~45% of viewport), vertically centered. Left card has a light/white background (the base plan), right card has the accent-color background (the premium plan). Each card contains: plan label badge, plan title, price, billing toggle, description, Primary CTA button, and benefit list items with numbered badges. The visual hierarchy is clear: the premium plan gets the brand's accent color, signaling premium status.

### Portfolio Showcase (Section 10)
**Dark background**, full-bleed section. The headline is split across three positions — first word far left, middle word centered, last word far right — all at display scale (~15vw). This creates a **kinetic, word-spread headline** that spans the full width like a horizontal banner. Below it, a centered large-format project preview card (with accent-color border on the featured card) is flanked by blurred/partially-visible cards on left and right, creating a **carousel-like depth stack**. Attribution handles appear below in small text.

---

## 5. Footer

The footer is **minimal and centered** on a dark background. It contains a compact block of metadata text: plan info, community note, technical details — all in a condensed, mono-spaced or uppercase small-text treatment. Below that, a credit/attribution line. This is not a traditional multi-column footer with links — it reads more like a colophon or stamp.

---

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

**Sticky floating nav pill:** The navigation never embeds into the page — it maintains z-layer separation at all times. This creates a persistent UI layer that feels detached from scroll content.

**Alternating light/dark section rhythm:** The page breathes with roughly equal amounts of each tone. Sections are not random — dark sections tend to contain immersive content (video, showcase, testimonials, pricing), while light sections carry informational or editorial content.

**Oversized typographic fills:** Multiple sections use typography at display scale (100px–250px equivalent) that fills or overflows the viewport. This is a deliberate visual strategy: type becomes texture and structure simultaneously.

**Handwritten annotation overlays:** Recurring pattern of italic/script notes with curved arrows — these float above structured content as editorial annotations, adding personality and directing attention to specific elements.

**Bleed cards and rotated objects:** The hero's scattered product cards and the portfolio section's stacked cards both bleed off screen edges, implying a larger, richer content universe beyond the frame.

**Full-bleed color panels used as section backgrounds:** Rather than containing content in boxes, entire sections are drenched in a solid color (accent, black, off-white), making the background itself a design element that shapes the emotional register of each section.

**Inline text badges:** Throughout body copy, individual words are wrapped in rounded pill badges (gray-bordered or filled), creating a visual taxonomy within prose — distinguishing product names, categories, or CTAs without breaking reading flow.
