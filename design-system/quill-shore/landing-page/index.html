Folio & Thread was founded in 2009 by conservator and bookbinder Helena Voss, who trained under master binders in Florence and London before establishing her independent studio. Every binding is conceived as a permanent object — made to be read, marked, passed on.

The studio works with private collectors commissioning journals and presentation volumes, publishers seeking limited-edition treatment for significant titles, and archival institutions requiring repair and housing for damaged or fragile documents. We work with vellum, full and half leather, cloth, and hand-marbled papers, sourced from mills and suppliers we have used for over a decade.

What we make

Each commission begins with a detailed conversation about purpose, materials, and lifespan. We do not accept more commissions than we can execute with full attention.

01

Fine Binding

Full leather, vellum, or cloth bindings for journals, presentation volumes, and literary editions. Sewn on tapes or cords, headbands hand-worked, covers tooled or blocked to commission.

How we work

A commission in four stages

01

Enquiry & Brief

We discuss your object — its purpose, the conditions in which it will be used and stored, materials preferences, and timeline. No deposit is taken until the brief is agreed in writing.

02

Material Selection

We prepare material samples — leathers, cloths, papers, thread colours — and send them by post for your consideration. Most commissions require one round of revision at this stage.

03

Binding & Conservation

The work is done by hand in our London studio. We photograph progress for institutional clients and complex conservation projects. Timelines range from four to sixteen weeks depending on complexity.

04

Delivery & Documentation

All work is delivered with a condition report and care notes. For collections requiring ongoing attention, we offer annual inspection and retreatment at a fixed annual fee.
