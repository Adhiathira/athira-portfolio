History

Founded on a long tradition

Helena Voss founded Folio & Thread in 2009 after seven years of training — four at the Opificio delle Pietre Dure in Florence, where she specialised in conservation binding for illuminated manuscripts, and three at the Designer Bookbinders programme in London. She established the studio in Borough Market, working from a ground-floor workshop on a narrow street that had previously housed a bookbinder in the 1870s.

The studio's first commissions came from a small circle of London antiquarian booksellers and one institutional client: the archive of a private estate in Suffolk that required ongoing conservation attention for a collection of 18th- and 19th-century account books. The relationship with that archive continues.

Over fifteen years, the client list has grown carefully: private collectors who commission journals and presentation volumes as personal artefacts, publishers seeking distinctive binding treatment for limited editions of significance, and institutional archives requiring professional-level repair and housing. The studio does not seek volume. It seeks work that merits the full attention it will receive.

Materials

What we work with

Leather

We source full Morocco goatskin, Niger goatskin, and alum-tawed pigskin from J. Hewit & Sons in Edinburgh, whom we have used since 2010. Goatskin is our primary binding leather for commissioned work: its tight grain accepts tooling cleanly and its surface ages well under handling.

Cloth & Vellum

Bookcloths from Winter & Company and Brillianta, in a range of colours maintained in the studio stock. Vellum sourced from William Cowley Parchment Works in Newport Pagnell — the oldest parchment maker still working in the UK. We use vellum for long-stitch and certain conservation bindings.

Paper

Archival tissue from Hahnemühle and Zanders for conservation work. Hand-marbled papers from Payhembury Marble Papers, Dover Marbling, and occasional import from Cockerell Marbling — all alkaline-sized and archival. Japanese tissues for guarding and repair from Washi Arts.

Thread & Adhesives

Linen thread for sewing, waxed in the studio. Wheat starch paste and methylcellulose for conservation work; PVA and PVA/paste mixes for structural elements in fine binding. We do not use hot melt adhesives in any commission work.

Helena Voss

Founder & Lead Bookbinder

Helena trained at the Opificio delle Pietre Dure in Florence (2001–2005), where she worked under Istituto Centrale's exchange conservation programme on 15th and 16th century Florentine bindings. She then completed the Designer Bookbinders programme in London (2005–2008) before establishing the studio in 2009.

She is a Fellow of the Designer Bookbinders and a member of the Institute of Conservation. She teaches occasional workshops in conservation binding at the London Centre for Book Arts.
