# Appleyard Flowers — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads complete. The three-tier sticky navigation assembly is present from the first frame — solid white, opaque, containing all three bars. The full-bleed hero photography is rendered at full opacity immediately below it. No entrance animation, no loading choreography, no fade-in sequence. The above-fold composition is fully resolved on arrival: the lifestyle photography background, the centered headline (uppercase, with yellow accent), supporting copy, and two CTA buttons are all present.

## Hero Experience

The hero is a static composition. The background lifestyle photograph does not animate, scale, or parallax. The overlaid text block does not entrance-animate. The two CTA buttons are present without hover feedback on load. The social proof band below the hero — a thick full-width dark bar containing review ratings and counts — is also static. The hero makes its statement completely in the first frame and asks nothing more of the visitor than to scroll.

## Section Transitions

Sections arrive as the user scrolls without any designed transition layer. Background color shifts — white to light gray, gray back to white — are the transition device. These are hard cuts at horizontal boundaries, not gradients or blended zones. No section slides in, fades up, or wipes across. The alternating gray/white rhythm down the page creates visual pacing without motion.

## Content Rhythm

The primary motion event in the page experience is image lazy-loading. As the user scrolls into the product grid sections, placeholder areas briefly appear before product photographs resolve into view — a functional performance optimization, not a designed motion. There is no stagger, no reveal sequence, no scroll-triggered choreography. The accordion section expands its content on click with a standard browser disclosure animation. No other designed motion occurs during the scroll journey.

## Footer Approach

The combined newsletter and footer section sits on the same light gray background that the accordion section uses above it — the gray field continues uninterrupted from one section to the other, signaling the closing zone through color continuity rather than a visual statement. The newsletter form and footer link columns appear without animation. The page ends with a copyright strip — a typographic terminus.

## Motion Principles

Motion is absent as a design choice. The only transitions in the system are standard browser behaviors: cursor-to-pointer on hover, lazy-load image resolution, accordion disclosure on click. No JavaScript animation library is present. No CSS keyframes animate any content element. The 0.2s ease transition on interactive elements provides state confirmation without aesthetic expression. This is a site that trusts its photography, typography, and grid structure to hold attention without motion support.

## Video Placements

One section of this site would be meaningfully served by ambient video:

**1. Hero Background — Seasonal floral lifestyle ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `hero-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A slow, extremely gentle tracking shot through a bouquet arrangement at close range — individual stems, petals, and soft out-of-focus flower forms in warm natural light; shot from above or at petal level; 8s loop; color temperature should match the editorial register of the existing hero photography (natural, not artificially brightened)
- Duration: 8s loop
- Why video: The hero's current full-bleed lifestyle photograph is the highest-impact creative position on the page. A near-still ambient video of the actual product — flowers at close range, barely moving — would add the temporal quality the static image cannot: the sense of living, breathing material. The yellow promotional headline could remain overlaid as it does over the photograph. The loop should be slow enough that visitors may not immediately register it as video.

**2. Category Page Header — Product category establishing ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `category-header-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A slow, contemplative close-up of the specific flower type associated with the category — petals unfurling slightly, a leaf edge with morning moisture, or the top of a bouquet from a gentle overhead angle; 6s loop; the left text panel's solid dark gray-blue overlay would remain in place, the video playing only in the right photographic panel
- Duration: 6s loop
- Why video: The category page headers use a split layout where the right ~65% is a photographic panel distinct from the white product grid below. This is the only position in the category template where an atmospheric brand statement can be made before the transactional grid begins. A brief ambient video of the relevant floral product — positioned just in the photographic right panel — would add tactile warmth and a sense of craft without competing with the product grid.
