# Heights Agency — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

This is a local insurance agency website serving a specific geographic community. The site communicates trustworthiness and service accessibility through established corporate conventions rather than aesthetic distinction. The brand name contains a place name, signaling local identity and community rootedness. Product category navigation is the primary organizational principle — the site exists to route visitors to the correct service type and then prompt contact. There is no aspirational positioning, no narrative build, and no attempt to differentiate through visual identity. The design priority is clarity, legibility, and direct conversion.

## Color Architecture

Deep navy (#032f60) anchors all heading text and the topbar band across the top of every page, establishing the primary brand signal. Medium blue (#0070A8) handles link text, button accents, and interactive elements — a lighter, more accessible variant of the same corporate blue register. Body copy runs in medium gray (#666666) throughout content sections. White surfaces serve as the background for all primary content zones. The topbar uses deep navy as a filled background band with white text, creating the only saturated color statement on the page. The hero's left panel shifts to a muted blue-gray field distinct from the white sections but tonally related to the navy — creating a subtle gradient impression across the above-fold composition. No other chromatic elements enter the system. The palette is the generic corporate blue register: institutional, legible, and without expressive intent.

## Typography System

A single-family approach with no editorial contrast. Lato handles all headline-weight elements (h2: 38px, weight 400, line-height 49px) — a contemporary geometric humanist with comfortable readability at display scale. Body copy, paragraph text, navigation links, and button labels fall to Helvetica Neue (with Arial and generic sans-serif as fallbacks) — a system font stack with no custom loading. There is no serif voice, no weight contrast, and no typographic hierarchy beyond scale. The uniform sans-serif treatment reflects the site's priority: information delivery and direct navigation, not editorial expression.

## Spatial System

The layout is conventional symmetrical corporate. The hero is a horizontal 50/50 split: the left panel contains the primary heading, a paragraph of body copy, and a CTA button on a muted blue-gray background field; the right panel fills with a lifestyle photograph edge-to-edge. Below the hero, three equal-width cards occupy a column grid, each with a heading, body copy, and a "learn more" action link. A full-bleed photographic band follows, with a semi-opaque overlay card anchored to the left side containing a secondary headline and CTA — carousel navigation arrows indicate additional slides. The footer begins with a contact form section (input fields + submit button) over a photographic background, followed by a dark-blue utility footer with column-based link groups and a promotional card. All section boundaries are hard horizontal cuts — no curves, no diagonal geometry, no shape-based transitions.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

Photography serves a functional role. The hero lifestyle photograph occupies the right half of the split — a standard family grouping, professional but not art-directed to a specific brand identity. The mid-page full-bleed photo provides a content background for an overlay card, reduced to an atmospheric surface rather than a photographic statement. No circular crops, no macro detail shots, no editorial framing that would signal design ambition. The imagery is drawn from the standard stock library register — warm, approachable, demographically broad — calibrated for a local community services audience rather than for visual distinction.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation system has two zones: a topbar and a main navigation bar below it. The topbar sits at the very top of the viewport and contains the agency's address, phone number, and email address alongside social media icons — all in white text on the deep navy background — and a yellow CTA button that serves as the persistent above-fold conversion anchor. Below the topbar, the main navigation is a static (non-sticky) bar 46px tall containing the wordmark and a horizontal list of direct product category links alongside utility links. The navigation does not follow the user as they scroll. Item spacing is 30px. There are no dropdown menus visible — product category access is handled through dedicated sub-pages rather than nested navigation. The nav is straightforward and static: it marks the top of the page, not a persistent wayfinding tool.

## Overall Design Character

This is a template-derived local business site with no custom design character. The navy-and-blue palette, the Lato/Helvetica type stack, and the hero-split + card-grid + form layout are the direct output of a standard insurance agency CMS template. There is no typographic contrast system, no shape language, no motion vocabulary beyond 0.2s link hover transitions, and no conceptual unification. The design is not a statement — it is a container for information. Its value is functional: a local community member can identify the agency's service areas, find contact information without searching, and reach a specific product page in one click. Those goals are met straightforwardly. Nothing aspires beyond them.
