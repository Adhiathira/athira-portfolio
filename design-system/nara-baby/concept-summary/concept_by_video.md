# Nara Baby — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads into a dark navy hero with the 3D product object already in continuous slow rotation at the center of the viewport, and headline text visible above it at full opacity. The nav is transparent with white link text. There is no page-load entrance animation — the scene is already established on arrival. The opening is cinematic and immediate: the product is the first thing the user sees, rendered with studio-quality lighting against the dark field.

## Hero Experience

The hero's defining element is the 3D object — a continuously rotating product representation rendered with realistic lighting and materiality. The object rotates on its own clock, independent of scroll, creating a sense of life before the user interacts. As the user begins scrolling, the large symmetrical concave arc at the hero's bottom boundary is revealed — the "bowl" of the divider scooping down into the section, creating a smooth visual passage from dark to light. The 3D object begins its descent as a scroll actor: it scales down and moves into the background layer of the subsequent section, visible behind the content as a persistent depth element.

## Section Transitions

The hero-to-content transition is the site's architecturally defining moment: a single large symmetrical concave-down arc (approximately 10–15% of viewport height amplitude) sweeps smoothly between the dark navy hero and the warm cream content section. This arc is static — it does not animate on its own — but its scale and symmetry make it the most visually impactful boundary on the page. The nav simultaneously transitions from transparent/white-link to solid-light/dark-link, signaling the chapter change in the wayfinding layer simultaneously with the layout layer. All other section boundaries use straight horizontal edges. Dark-to-light and light-to-dark background shifts serve as the primary section pacing mechanism throughout the remaining scroll.

## Content Rhythm

Scroll-triggered reveals use a progressive fade+slide pattern: text headlines animate first (establishing the section topic), then body copy, then media — a cascading hierarchy that directs focus from the general to the specific. Media assets in split-layout sections have a subtle scale-up or fade-in on reveal. Staggered animation applies when multiple elements appear in sequence (headline then paragraph then list items). The horizontal product card grid uses user-driven drag/swipe; vertical scroll may be temporarily held while the user interacts with the horizontal track. The 3D scroll actor moves through the page behind content layers at a different rate than the foreground, providing continuous depth throughout.

## Footer Approach

The footer returns to the dark navy palette that opened the page — a tonal bookend that closes the color narrative. Content reveals in the footer use the standard fade-up pattern. The transition from the final light section to the dark footer is a straight horizontal edge. The page ends as it began: dark, considered, premium.

## Motion Principles

Three motion registers operate at different tempos. The 3D object rotation: continuous, self-operating, not scroll-responsive. The scroll actor travel: scroll-scrubbed, tied directly to scroll position, always present in the background. Content reveals: scroll-triggered, short-duration, staggered by element hierarchy. The result is a page that never feels still: the 3D object always moves, the scroll actor always travels, and content always reveals fresh material on descent. No spring physics, no scroll hijacking (except the horizontal card track), no complex per-element orchestration beyond basic stagger.

## Video Placements

**1. Feature Showcase Section — Product preparation or feeding walkthrough**
- Suggested file: `feature-showcase-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A close-up, warm-toned clip showing the product being prepared or a product demonstration moment — gentle hands measuring, a container being opened in natural kitchen light, soft steam or mixing motion; 8–10s loop; shot from slightly above at a 30° angle; warm-white soft lighting, cream/neutral surfaces; no faces; the motion is slow and deliberate; the clip communicates care and ritual rather than speed or convenience
- Duration: 8–10s loop
- Why video: The split media-text feature sections use media as direct illustration of product claims. A warm ambient video of product preparation in this position demonstrates trust through process — showing what the brand does rather than describing it. Still photography cannot convey the gentleness of the ritual that this product category requires.

**2. Horizontal Card Grid — Product or lifestyle ambient loop(s)**
- Suggested file: `card-ambient-01.mp4`
- Subject: A short (4–6s) close-up ambient clip used within one or more of the horizontal product cards — gentle product detail, soft light on a container surface, or a quiet lifestyle moment (morning light, a calm table scene); extremely low motion, essentially a living still photograph; looping seamlessly; meant to distinguish one card in the horizontal track from its neighbors without competing with the product image cards around it
- Duration: 4–6s per card, looping
- Why video: The horizontal card grid uses a dark background that makes autoplay video within cards highly visible. A single ambient motion card within an otherwise static horizontal track creates a focal anchor — it catches the eye and signals that this card represents the brand's featured or flagship offering.
