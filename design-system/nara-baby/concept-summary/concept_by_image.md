# Nara Baby — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A premium infant nutrition brand that opens on a dark, cinematic hero before opening into the warm, light-toned palette that defines its content. The cinematic opening establishes aspiration and product quality before the site shifts into its natural register: warm cream, soft sky blue, and deep navy — a palette that reads as safe, premium, and nurturing simultaneously. The site treats its hero product as the central protagonist, rendered as a 3D scroll actor that accompanies the user from the hero into subsequent sections, functioning as a visual thread through an otherwise gentle, editorial experience.

## Color Architecture

The page operates across three color registers. Hero: deep navy (`#25446b`) provides the dark background against which the 3D product object is rendered with maximum luminance — the section reads as cinematic. Content sections: warm cream (`#fff7e9`) is the predominant surface for informational and feature sections — warm, airy, and soft. Accent surfaces: soft sky blue (`#BBE2EE`) appears in the hero band and as structural section differentiation alongside a lighter variant (`#EFF8FC`). Deep navy (`#25446b`) reappears in the footer, framing the page with matching dark bookends. The orange CTA (`#f16224`) is the single warm accent in the chromatic system — used for primary action only. Body and heading text are navy throughout: dark blue text on light backgrounds creates high contrast without the harshness of pure black.

## Typography System

A duotype system pairs two proprietary typefaces with distinct personalities. Self Modern (serif, 400/600) serves the display tier: 56px/600 at h1, 30px/600 at h4, and as a body serif for editorial copy at 24px — functioning as the brand's voice for narrative, claims, and emotional statements. Sharp Grotesk (sans, 400/600/700) handles the functional tier: h2 at 20px/700 (section labels), h3 at 18px/600, and body/nav at 16px/400 — structured, legible, and efficiently informational. The serif-for-voice, sans-for-function split is the site's primary typographic design decision: warmth in the editorial layer, clarity in the structural one.

## Spatial System

The page flows through five section types. Hero: full-bleed dark navy with 3D product object centered, headline above and supporting text below, large symmetrical concave arc at the bottom boundary. Feature split sections: two-column grid — media left, text right — on warm cream background; media containers have subtle rounded corners (8–12px) and a soft drop shadow. Horizontal card grid: a draggable horizontal track of product/content cards on dark background, cards partially bleed off-screen right to signal scrollability. Testimonial/quote section: minimal, centered, light background — a reading pause. Footer: dark navy, multi-column utility layout. Vertical rhythm alternates dark and light backgrounds, creating four distinct chapter breaks.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

All media containers use rounded rectangles with small consistent corner radii (8–24px) — a soft, modern shape language that applies uniformly to video frames, feature illustrations, and card media. No organic shapes, no custom frame geometries. The 3D scroll actor — a product-representative object rendered with realistic lighting — is the hero's central visual element and the one instance where media exits its container, freely floating in the composition. Its material treatment (luminous, wireframe-adjacent, or glossy product rendering) communicates technical craft against the dark hero background. Photography in content sections is product-focused and warm-toned, reflecting the cream/navy palette.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

A sticky three-zone nav: wordmark left, primary links center, pill-shaped CTA button right. Opens transparent over the dark navy hero with white nav link text and dark wordmark; transitions to a solid light background with a subtle drop shadow and bottom keyline border on scroll, with text inverting to navy throughout. The transition is timed to scroll position rather than threshold-based. The pill-shaped CTA button is the nav's primary differentiator — it persists at high visual salience across both nav states.

## Overall Design Character

Nara Baby uses a structural argument that is unusual in the infant nutrition category: open cinematic (dark, 3D, dramatic), then open into warmth. This sequence positions the brand as technically premium before it presents as emotionally warm — the opposite of the gentle-pastel-only default for baby brands. The large concave arc section divider at the hero boundary is the site's single most distinctive design gesture: a smooth transition between emotional registers, from cinematic aspiration to nurturing content. The duotype serif/sans system reinforces this dual character throughout the scroll.
