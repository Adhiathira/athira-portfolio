How it works

From acquisition to edition

Compendium is built around the actual workflow of a working archivist — not a software abstraction of it. Each step follows the material, not the other way around.

  1. I

    Ingest the document

    Upload high-resolution scans, TIFF sequences, or direct camera captures. Compendium normalizes lighting, corrects folio skew, and registers verso-recto pairs automatically.

  2. II

    Identify the hand

    Select from 400+ scribal hand profiles, or train a custom profile from a reference folio set. The model adapts to the document's specific character forms within 30 minutes of calibration.

  3. III

    Transcribe and annotate

    Review the machine-generated transcription in the dual-pane editor — facsimile on the left, editable transcription on the right. Add annotations at any granularity. Confidence flags guide your review to the points of uncertainty.

  4. IV

    Publish or export

    Produce a TEI-encoded XML edition, a print-ready typeset PDF, or a web-accessible public-facing edition. Your annotation layer travels with the text into every export format.
