# Stripe Press — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A premium book publishing imprint site whose primary interface is a scroll-driven 3D carousel of photorealistic book models. The site inverts the conventional relationship between navigation and content: there is no traditional navigation bar, no grid, no filters — the scroll input itself is the browsing mechanism, and the books are both the content and the UI. The design's core insight is that books are physical objects with material presence, and that presence should be felt in the browsing experience rather than abstracted into a card grid.

## Color Architecture

The palette is built on a near-black ground (`#201819` — a very dark, slightly warm charcoal with a faint burgundy undertone, not pure black) with white text and headings throughout. The sole chromatic accent is a warm gold or amber (`#dfc78e`) applied to links — a refined, literary color that reads simultaneously as a classical publishing accent (gold-leaf binding) and a warm highlight in low light. The palette inverts at one strategic moment: the informational text section at the bottom of the page shifts to stark white, creating the most dramatic tonal contrast on the page. Product detail pages extend the system by sampling a dominant color from each book's cover art as the full-page background, creating a unique "micro-brand" environment for each individual title.

## Typography System

Two typefaces, both proprietary, both from the Ivar type system (Dutch Type Library or equivalent premium foundry). Ivar Headline (600 weight) handles all display, headline, button, and link text — rendered at a notably modest scale (h1 at 17.6px, h2 at 16px italic) because this is not a visual-display site but an editorial reading site. The type scale is sized for comfortable reading at close range rather than for large-format viewport announcement. Ivar Text (500 weight) handles all body copy and paragraph text at 17px with a 25.5px line height — generous leading that supports long-form editorial reading. h2 is explicitly set to italic, making the italic serif a systematic typographic role rather than an incidental variation. The entire system communicates through typographic refinement rather than scale: both faces are distinguished by their letterform quality, not by size or weight contrast.

## Spatial System

The main landing page organizes into four zones. The full-viewport 3D carousel section occupies the opening experience: photorealistic book models stacked in z-space, the central book lit and enlarged as the focused item, books above and below receding with depth-of-field blur. A custom vertical scrollbar on the left viewport edge serves as the only explicit navigation indicator. A static media showcase section follows — a single centered poster image on the dark background, a moment of visual rest. A video section uses a full-bleed dark atmospheric video background with centered headline text overlay and an animated circular graphic element. A white informational and newsletter section provides the rational, text-focused close. Product detail pages exist as distinct full-page experiences with thematic backgrounds.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

The site's primary visual element is not photography but 3D rendering: each book is modeled as a photorealistic physical object with accurate material simulation — matte covers, glossy finishes, textured spines, accurate page-edge geometry. This choice communicates material authenticity without requiring physical product photography. Within product detail pages, the only media is a static 3D render of the selected book at an angled perspective, presented without a frame or container — the book floats against the thematically colored page background. All image containers use sharp 90° rectangular corners exclusively; one small pill-shaped metadata tag on detail pages is the sole rounded element and serves as a metadata-type distinction.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The interface eliminates traditional navigation entirely. Persistent UI elements are reduced to a minimal HUD-style overlay: a wordmark and tagline anchored in the top-left corner of the viewport, and a help icon in the bottom-left. These elements remain fixed throughout all scroll interactions and sit directly over content without a background container. On product detail pages, a back arrow replaces the wordmark. This approach maximizes content surface area and maintains the cinematic, immersive quality of the dark interface — any traditional nav bar with a background fill would break the illusion of depth created by the 3D carousel.

## Overall Design Character

The site's defining quality is its commitment to a single formal premise: that the book as object should be experienced, not catalogued. Every design decision follows from this — the 3D rendering over product photography, the elimination of navigation chrome, the dark atmospheric environment, the editorial typography. The warm gold accent, the Ivar type system, the subtle particle texture overlay on dark sections, and the thematically colored detail pages are all expressions of a publishing aesthetic that takes craft seriously. The one white section is not a concession but a deliberate contrast — it functions as a reading environment precisely because everything before it was an immersive browsing environment.
