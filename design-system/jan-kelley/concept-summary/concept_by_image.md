# Jan Kelley — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

Jan Kelley presents a marketing and communications agency through a design system that treats credibility and creative energy as simultaneous, not competing, goals. The site is structured as a narrative scroll — each section a discrete chapter that shifts register from expansive and typographic to dense and grid-based to intimate and conversion-focused. The underlying argument is that a full-service agency must demonstrate range, and the layout system makes that case section by section. Rather than relying on visual spectacle, the site earns attention through pacing: calm, spacious hero transitions into concentrated dark containers, which open back out into browsable portfolio grids, creating a rhythm that feels authored rather than assembled.

## Color Architecture

The palette is built on a high-contrast black-and-white foundation with three accent statements operating at distinct registers. A bright acidic yellow-green functions as the primary action color — present on CTAs, top-edge accent lines in the navigation, and occasional graphic highlights, giving it full semantic ownership of "do something." A secondary teal/cyan appears in decorative circular graphics, soft and approachable against the surrounding white. A third accent — a brushed-texture pink/red — is reserved exclusively for the animated hero keyword, making it the single most chromatic moment on the page. The discipline is precise: three accent colors, each with one job, none competing. The light-to-dark alternating backgrounds (white sections for browsing, black sections for impact and conversion) act as a structural rhythm device that maps white to open exploration and black to focused attention.

## Typography System

The type system is built around a single clean geometric sans-serif that handles all structural functions — from massive hero headlines to small navigation labels — supplemented by a single expressive script or brush-stroke font deployed on exactly one element: the animated cycling keyword in the hero. This is a calculated decision. A system built entirely on geometric sans-serif achieves maximum functional consistency; the isolated appearance of an expressive hand-drawn letterform at hero scale reads as a brand signature rather than a decorative indulgence. The contrast between the two is the system's entire personality: rational structure punctuated by one controlled moment of warmth. Button labels are consistently pill-shaped, reinforcing the rounded edge vocabulary without introducing serif complexity.

## Spatial System

The spatial logic alternates between two distinct registers throughout the scroll. Container-based editorial spacing governs the hero and storytelling sections — generous margins, single large typographic statements, room to read. A strict card-grid system takes over for portfolio and feature sections — two and three column layouts, uniform gutters, systematic card padding. Between these registers, the site inserts shaped containers: a large dark blob-like module with heavily rounded corners that sits as a self-contained compositional object on the light page ground. This shaped container strategy — where the module is not a section background but a sculptural object — is the spatial system's most distinctive feature. It creates depth through figure-ground contrast without requiring photography. The wavy multi-arc section dividers that appear at specific transitions add organic movement to the page's otherwise rectilinear structure.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

The portfolio cards are the primary photography surface — high-quality case study imagery and video contained within rounded-rectangle frames, treated as equal inventory items regardless of medium. The editorial voice is fundamentally a pacing voice: photography appears when it serves a content function (portfolio demonstration, team introduction), not as atmospheric decoration. The team carousel introduces circular-framed headshots, the only photograph format that departs from the dominant rounded-rectangle language. The lack of full-bleed hero photography is deliberate — the hero's credibility comes from typographic animation and the structural architecture of the page itself, not from a lifestyle image.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar is sticky and solid from page load — white background, thin yellow-green top-edge accent line, no transparency phase. This signals that the site's navigation is a working tool, not a design statement that yields to the content below it. The bar contains five primary links, a search affordance, and a high-contrast pill-shaped CTA on the right — a complete wayfinding kit. Its drop shadow on scroll creates a clear separation from scrolling content without animating or changing color. The persistent CTA ensures that no matter how deep into the scroll journey the user travels, the primary conversion action is always one click away.

## Overall Design Character

Jan Kelley is a marketing agency portfolio designed around the argument that range and confidence are the same thing. Its intelligence lies in the organic shaped containers that interrupt the grid rhythm without abandoning it, the three-accent color system where each chromatic statement has a single semantic role, and the hero's animated keyword that makes the case for creative versatility without adding visual noise. The wavy dividers, alternating light/dark chaptering, and pill-shaped interactive elements create a system that feels simultaneously polished and energetic — a design that communicates the agency's ability to work across registers, from austere brand strategy to playful campaign concepting.
