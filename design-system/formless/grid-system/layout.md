# Brand Name — Layout Brief

---

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page flows through **7 major sections** with a consistent full-bleed dark canvas (near-black throughout):

1. **Hero** — Immersive split-layout with atmospheric gradient orb
2. **Product Scroll Series** — Three numbered, full-screen sections (01, 02, 03) using a sticky-style left image + right content split
3. **Manifesto / Collage Section** — Typographic declaration with overlapping image collage
4. **Mission Statement** — Asymmetric two-column with quote
5. **CTA / Join Section** — Full-bleed gradient with large centered type
6. **Footer Pre-section** — Social links row
7. **Footer** — Logo lockup + legal bar

The spatial rhythm is deliberate and slow — generous vertical breathing room between elements, no tight stacking.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

A **minimal fixed navbar** spanning the full width:

- **Left:** Small geometric logo mark sitting alone in the far left — roughly 40px from edge. In the hero it appears solo; on subsequent sections it expands into a full wordmark with the logo prepended.
- **Right:** Four nav links — `Primary Link`, `Secondary Link`, `About`, `Contact` — spaced evenly with generous letter spacing. Right-aligned cluster, sitting close to the right edge.
- **No visible background/border** — the nav floats transparently over whatever section is beneath it.
- The nav transition from icon-only (hero) to full wordmark (interior) signals a scroll-triggered logo reveal — a classic luxury/tech pattern.

---

## 3. Hero Section

**Full-viewport, two-column asymmetric split:**

- **Left column (~40% width):** Headline copy — large display heading, left-aligned, roughly mid-page vertically. Below the headline, two CTAs side-by-side: one ghost/outline button (Primary CTA) and one filled pill (Secondary CTA).
- **Right column (~60% width):** Atmospheric glowing orb — a radial gradient bloom that bleeds into pure black. No hard edges, purely additive light painting. The orb feels like it emanates from behind, giving depth without imagery.
- **Bottom of the viewport:** The full brand wordmark appears oversized — approximately 1/3 of the screen height — with the logo mark to its left. This creates a dramatic reveal: the brand name is both a decorative element and a typographic statement. It sits at the very bottom, partially cropped, implying it continues below the fold.
- The overall spatial hierarchy reads: *medium headline → action → vast negative space → massive brand.*

---

## 4. Content Sections

### Sections 01–03: Numbered Product Scroll (Three Screens)

These three sections follow an **identical compositional template**, suggesting a scroll-locked or scroll-synced panel system:

- **Left (~55%):** A full-height product render — a sculptural 3D form. The object is large, centered in its column, with generous dark padding on all sides. The object subtly shifts form across the three slides, animating the brand identity.
- **Right (~45%):** Content column, top-aligned, consisting of:
  - A massive numeral (`01`, `02`, `03`) — approximately 200px+, ultra-light weight, sitting near the top of the column. This is a decorative typographic anchor.
  - A thin vertical rule/divider runs the full right edge, separating the content pane from the page edge.
  - Below a significant vertical gap: a section heading (medium-large, regular weight)
  - Two short body paragraphs beneath, with noticeable paragraph spacing.
- **Rhythm:** The number acts as a skyline anchor; the text descends from roughly mid-page. The vertical rule creates a subtle column-within-a-column framing device.
- The objects feel **full-bleed** within their left column — no card, no border, just the 3D form floating on a dark background.

### Section 04: Collage / Feature Overview

**Dramatic, high-energy layout break** — the most complex section:

- **Top:** A massive typographic declaration spans the full width across 3 lines — very large, tracking-tight, light on dark. The text is interrupted: a portrait photograph is placed *inline within the text*, cutting between words on the second line. This creates an intentional visual clash — text wraps around the image implicitly.
- **Below:** A three-column card grid, each column containing:
  - **Column 1 (left):** A dark card with a Feature A screenshot or visual. Below it, a section title and body copy.
  - **Column 2 (center):** The portrait image continues as a standalone card, with a label and Action Button below.
  - **Column 3 (right):** A secondary visual card, with a section title and body copy below.
- The three columns are approximately equal width with consistent gutters.
- **Layering:** The large type and imagery overlap at the top — the image appears to float *over* the letterforms, creating a collage aesthetic. This is the page's visual climax.

### Section 05: Mission / Manifesto

**Two-column, horizontal text-image split:**

- **Left (~35%):** A small decorative logo or bullet mark precedes a large, multi-line headline — left-aligned, comfortable line spacing.
- **Right (~65%):** Empty at the top (vast negative space used intentionally), then a quote block — large italic or serif-weight quote text, inset from both sides.
- **Below (same section, continued scroll):** A portrait photograph in a roughly 2:3 portrait ratio floats on the left side (~30% width). On the right, the quote repeats or continues. The image is not full-bleed — it has visible crop margins within the section.

---

## 5. CTA / Join Section

**Full-bleed, centered, atmospheric:**

- Background is the same radial gradient orb treatment as the hero — glowing from center.
- Centered typographically: one enormous headline rendered at display scale (approx. 120px+), centered horizontally, low on the vertical axis.
- Directly below: a minimal text link/Primary CTA — small, understated, arrow-linked.
- Below that: four social icons in a horizontal row, left-aligned, spaced apart. The icons feel like they're in a footer zone even though this is still CTA territory.
- The section feels like it bleeds directly into the footer rather than having a hard demarcation.

---

## 6. Footer

**Two-tier layout:**

- **Upper tier:** The full brand wordmark with the logo mark — rendered at full-page scale (similar to the hero's bottom treatment). This is purely a brand statement, no utility. The wordmark sits centered or left-aligned with generous top/bottom margin.
- **Lower tier (legal bar):** Three items in a horizontal row — `© Company`, `Privacy Policy`, `Terms of Service` — small, low contrast, left-aligned and spaced to thirds. Minimal, utilitarian.

---

## 7. Notable Layout Patterns

**Atmospheric gradient orbs** — Used at the hero and CTA section as full-bleed background art. They anchor emotional transitions (introduction and call to action) and create bookend symmetry.

**Typographic scale as visual architecture** — Enormous numerals (section counters) and display-scale headlines serve as structural dividers, not just content. The brand wordmark appears at extreme scale both at the bottom of the hero and in the footer, making it a recurring spatial landmark.

**Image-in-type interruption** — In the collage section, a portrait photograph is placed physically within a line of large display text. This layering implies z-axis depth and feels editorial/magazine-like — a hallmark of luxury brand web design.

**Consistent 55/45 split for product sections** — The three product scroll panels use an identical compositional template. This implies either scroll-jacking (sections snap into place) or a repeated component. The consistency creates rhythmic predictability that makes the form animation across panels feel intentional.

**Vertical rule as framing device** — A thin 1px line runs the full right edge of the product section's content column. It reinforces the split without heavy visual weight.

**Near-zero decorative elements** — Beyond the gradient orbs and the 3D object, there is almost no decoration. All visual interest comes from scale, negative space, and typographic hierarchy. This is an extremely disciplined, confidence-first layout system.
