# Brand Name Website — Layout Brief

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page is a single, continuous scroll composed of **roughly 8–9 major sections**. The visual rhythm alternates between full-bleed atmospheric moments and structured content panels. The overall flow is:

1. Hero (full-viewport, split layout)
2. Transition / Wordmark reveal (full-bleed, oversized type)
3. Product section 01 — Product 1
4. Product section 02 — Product 2
5. Product section 03 — Service A
6. Manifesto / Statement section (full-bleed, centered copy)
7. Founder / Quote section
8. CTA / "Primary CTA" section
9. Footer

The page uses **generous vertical spacing** as a design element — sections feel like separate cinematic frames rather than a tight continuous document. Negative space is load-bearing; it gives content room to breathe and creates hierarchy through emptiness.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

The header is a **fixed, full-width navigation bar** that persists across all scroll positions (sticky). It sits at the very top edge with no visible background fill — fully transparent, floating over whatever section is beneath it.

Layout is a classic two-pole split:
- **Left pole:** Logo mark (small geometric icon) + wordmark ("Brand Name™") — appears in compact lockup
- **Right pole:** Four nav links in a horizontal row — "Link 1", "Link 2", "Link 3", "Link 4" — evenly spaced with generous gaps between items

The nav sits very high in the viewport with minimal vertical padding, creating a thin, almost invisible presence. Typography is light-weight, white, small — it doesn't compete with the content beneath.

**Notable:** On the hero section, only the logo icon (not the wordmark) appears in the top-left, suggesting the wordmark is hidden or fades in on scroll — creating a reveal moment as you scroll past the hero.

---

## 3. Hero Section

The hero occupies the **full viewport height** and uses a **split two-column layout** at roughly 55/45 proportions (left/right).

- **Left column:** Holds the primary headline in large, heavy-weight display type. The text is left-aligned, sitting in the upper-left quadrant of the viewport.
- **Right column:** Contains body copy and serves as the secondary information zone.
- **Below the text columns:** Two CTA buttons sit side-by-side — a dark bordered/outlined button ("Primary CTA") and a solid light-filled button ("Secondary CTA"). These buttons have fully rounded pill shapes and sit at the lower portion of the left side.

**Background treatment:** A large, soft radial glow blooms from the center-right of the viewport. It's atmospheric and painterly, not geometric. The rest is near-pure dark background. This creates a focal depth that draws the eye rightward and downward.

**Spatial hierarchy:** Headline dominates by size and weight. The description copy is smaller and sits in counterbalance. The buttons anchor the bottom of the composition.

---

## 4. Content Sections

### Transition — Oversized Wordmark Reveal
A **full-bleed, full-viewport moment** where the brand wordmark appears in massive display type — the letters extend beyond the viewport width, cropping at both edges. This creates a typographic identity stamp, a pause beat between the hero and the product content. No grid, no columns — pure theatrical scale.

### Product Sections (01, 02, 03)

These three sections share a **consistent two-column template** that becomes the page's primary structural rhythm:

- **Left column (~45% width):** A large 3D rendered object — sculptural form — positioned left-aligned, vertically centered in the frame. The object is photographically treated (not flat illustration) and takes up significant vertical real estate.
- **Right column (~55% width):** Contains the section number in massive display numerals (01, 02, 03) at the top, then a section headline in medium display type, followed by two to three body paragraphs.

The **number is typographically enormous** — taking up the full right-column width — creating an anchoring counter-element that floats above the actual content. It establishes vertical rhythm within each section panel.

**Asymmetric vertical pacing:** The number sits near the top of the right column. Below it is a significant gap, then the headline, then more space, then the body copy. This creates a deliberate, unhurried reading pace.

**3D objects:** Each section features a different sculptural form. They have iridescent chromatic aberration — rainbow light dispersion — which is the site's primary visual motif.

### Manifesto / Statement Section
A **centered, single-column layout** with very large display text spanning ~80% of the viewport width. The text is centered horizontally and sits low in the frame. Below the headline, a collage-style arrangement of three images breaks out — one code snippet panel (dark), one editorial portrait (color), one architectural photograph — creating an irregular horizontal strip. This is the most compositionally complex section: it feels layered rather than gridded.

### Founder / Quote Section
A **two-column asymmetric layout**:
- **Left column (narrow, ~25% width):** A black-and-white portrait photograph, square-cropped. Below it: the subject's name in large display type and title in small caption type.
- **Right column (~75% width):** A large pull-quote in display size, positioned high in the column with significant leading.

The left/right weight imbalance is intentional — the quote dominates spatially, the portrait is intimate and small by comparison.

### CTA — "Primary CTA"
A **full-viewport, centered single-column** layout. The headline is massive — taking up ~50% of the viewport width — centered both horizontally and vertically. Below it, a single understated text link ("Action Button →") in small, light type. The background returns to the radial glow treatment from the hero. Social icons sit in a horizontal row in the lower-left, very small and widely spaced.

---

## 5. Footer

The footer has **two distinct layers**:

**Upper layer:** Social icons row on the far left — these may be shared with the CTA section above, creating a visual merge between the CTA and footer.

**Lower layer (true footer bar):** A very minimal three-item horizontal row:
- Far left: "© Brand Name"
- Center: "Privacy Policy"
- Right: "Terms of Service"

Small type, maximum restraint. The footer is visually swallowed by the oversized brand wordmark above it — the wordmark bleeds upward from the footer into the CTA section, creating a **final dramatic typographic moment** that closes the page.

---

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

**Sticky transparent navigation:** The header floats over all content without a background — it reads as part of the composition, not a separate UI shell.

**Full-bleed atmospheric backgrounds:** The hero and CTA sections use radial gradient glows that extend to all four edges. These sections feel like environmental spaces rather than content containers.

**Typographic scale as spatial anchoring:** Section numbers (01, 02, 03) and the closing wordmark are sized so large they function architecturally — they occupy space the way structural elements do, not decorative ones.

**Consistent left/right column pairing:** The three product sections establish a strong rhythmic template — object left, content right — that creates expectation and payoff as you scroll. The visual weight of the 3D objects balances the informational weight of the text.

**3D objects as section identity:** Each section's sculptural form acts as a "hero image" for that content unit, replacing the more conventional icon or illustration approach. Their iridescent treatment ties them together as a family while their distinct shapes differentiate the sections.

**Generous vertical whitespace as pacing:** Large empty zones between content blocks slow the reader down deliberately. The page doesn't rush. White space is the scroll speed governor.
