# Flux Finance — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads fully rendered with no entrance animation sequence. The hero content appears at full opacity from the first frame. Within a beat of page load, the hero headline, body copy, and CTA button execute a staggered fade-in-and-slide-up — a brief, sequenced entrance that signals the site is animated without making the visitor wait. The mobile product mockups appear simultaneously with their container, already positioned within their circular background anchor.

## Hero Experience

The hero is static media: two overlapping product screenshots in device mockups, placed within a vibrant solid-color circle. There is no ambient video, no parallax background, no scroll-triggered hero transformation. The hero's energy comes from the staggered text entrance animation and the presence of the product imagery. Once the entrance completes, the hero is fully stable — the visitor reads rather than watches.

## Section Transitions

As the user scrolls, each section's content executes a staggered reveal: headline appears first (fade-in + slight upward slide), followed by body text, then the CTA button. The timing is quick — approximately 300–400ms per element with 100–150ms stagger — giving the effect of a graceful, controlled deployment rather than a mechanical pop. In the alternating feature sections, this stagger is mirrored on both the text and media columns, though the media column typically animates slightly after the text to reinforce reading direction. The sinusoidal wave dividers between sections are static — they do not animate during scroll.

## Content Rhythm

The most sophisticated motion on the site occurs in the editorial storytelling sections, likely on the About or brand story page. Here, entire phrases reveal sequentially — one line or cluster fading and sliding into view before the next begins — creating a paced, cinematic reading experience that controls the flow of information rather than presenting it all at once. This sequential text reveal is the page's most expressive use of scroll timing, and it is reserved for the brand narrative sections where prose rhythm matters most. The testimonial card grid and the article grid sections animate as groups — cards appearing together with a brief collective stagger — rather than cascading individually.

## Footer Approach

The footer is a multi-column informational zone that arrives without special animation. The scroll journey ends in a flat, organized informational surface: link columns, social icons, legal text. No closing flourish, no special treatment at the page's end. The final CTA section before the footer — with its floating currency illustrations and rounded content container — is the page's true emotional conclusion.

## Motion Principles

The motion philosophy is animated-but-functional: animation is used consistently to introduce all content on scroll, but it is always brief, never theatrical. Duration is short (300–500ms), easing is ease-out or smooth, and there is no spring or bounce. The key expressive choice is the stagger — by sequencing elements within sections, the system creates the impression of a designed reveal even though each individual animation is simple. The floating currency illustrations in the final CTA section are the site's one indulgent motion element: they drift and float continuously, creating depth through layered z-index movement. All other animation is enter-only — nothing continuously moves once content has revealed.

## Video Placements

Two sections of this site would be meaningfully served by ambient looping video:

**1. Hero Background — Product-in-use ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `hero-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A human hand navigating a smartphone displaying a financial dashboard — soft focus, warm natural light, 8s loop with slow handheld drift
- Duration: 8s loop
- Why video: The hero currently uses a static product mockup against a colored circle. A looped video of the product being used would add temporal warmth and human presence without disrupting the text-forward composition. The existing circular background element suggests a container that could hold video rather than a still.

**2. CTA Section — Abstract finance ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `cta-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: Stylized currency forms dissolving and reforming in soft light — macro-scale, the motion language of the existing floating illustration elements translated to video; 10s loop
- Duration: 10s loop
- Why video: The final CTA section already has multiple animated layers (floating currency illustrations at different z-indexes). A looping background video with a very light overlay would deepen the dimensional quality of this section and reinforce its role as the site's emotional climax — the moment where the design allows itself to be most expressive.
