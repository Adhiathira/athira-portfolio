The catalogue

Materials

340 materials,
four families

The catalogue is arranged by material family. Within each family, entries are ordered by origin country, then by producer. Every entry includes the edition in which the material first appeared.

Not all catalogued materials have been included in a public edition. Some appear only in private commissions or institutional archives. The public entries below are those available through open commission intake.

Handmade gampi fibre, 35 g/m², translucent, pH 6.8. Extremely high fold endurance (1,200+ folds). Edition IV.

Pur Coton 300

100% cotton rag, 300 g/m², hot-pressed, pH 8.5 (alkaline reserve). Cold-press surface available. Edition I.

Clay

Stoneware & ceramic bodies

→ assets/material-clay-1.jpeg
Burgundy, France

Grès de Bourgogne

Stoneware body, cone 10 reduction (1,280°C). Shrinkage 11.4%. Water absorption <0.5%. Warm grey, light speckle. Forthcoming.

Limoges, France

Porcelaine Translucide

Translucent porcelain, cone 12 oxidation (1,310°C). Shrinkage 14.2%. Pure white, no iron speckling. Private commission.

Shigaraki Stoneware

Traditional Shigaraki clay, wood-fired. Heavy iron flecking, coarse feldspar inclusions. Variable shrinkage 10–13%. Documented at Tsujimura Studio.
