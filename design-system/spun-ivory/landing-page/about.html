The studio

About

Founded 2019

A studio for
material intelligence

Ivoire Bureau occupies the space between scientific measurement and craft knowledge — a space that has no standard format, no established archive discipline, and no agreed vocabulary. We are building that vocabulary, edition by edition.

Our method

"We are not a publisher, not an archive, not a material library. We are something that does not have a name yet — a studio that makes instruments for knowing materials. The instruments happen to be beautiful, but that is a consequence of precision, not an ambition in itself."

Margot Fleury, 2023

What we believe

Three positions

I

The hand precedes the record

All knowledge of material starts with touch. Documentation that is not grounded in direct handling produces descriptions that cannot be trusted. The hand must be involved before the archive opens.

II

Measurement and interpretation are different things

A weight is not an opinion. A shrinkage rate is not an aesthetic judgment. Ivoire Bureau keeps these categories strictly separate. Measured data is permanent; interpretation is attributed and dated.

III

The record should outlast the tools

A digital file has an expected lifespan of 20–30 years before format obsolescence makes it unreadable. A well-made physical archive has an expected lifespan of 200 years. We build for 200 years.
