# Brand Name — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

Six major sections flow vertically in a clean editorial sequence:
1. Fixed header / global navigation
2. Full-viewport hero with video background
3. Editorial feature split — "Primary Feature"
4. Editorial grid — "Content Collection" (blog/press)
5. Product grid — "Shop"
6. Footer

The page uses a strict vertical stack with no diagonal cuts or overlapping sections between modules. Rhythm alternates between full-bleed immersive moments and white-ground editorial grids — tension between cinematic and catalog.

---

## Header / Navigation

Thin, horizontal bar at the very top. Pure white background, no shadow or border — it floats visually. Three zones:

- **Left:** A single search icon, left-aligned with generous padding
- **Center:** Brand wordmark in tall, spaced serif caps — dominant and centered, clearly the visual anchor
- **Right:** Two utility icons (account, cart) symmetrically mirroring the left search icon

Below the wordmark, a secondary nav row spans the full width: navigation labels in widely letter-spaced small caps, evenly distributed across the horizontal center. The double-row header creates a ceremonial quality — the brand name sits above the navigation like a masthead, not beside it.

---

## Hero Section

Absolutely full-bleed, full-viewport-height video background. No margins, no padding, no container — the video touches all four edges of the browser window.

Content is centered both horizontally and vertically within the frame:
- A large all-caps display headline in heavy white type
- A subtitle line in tracked small caps beneath it
- Two ghost/outline CTA buttons side by side, horizontally centered

The buttons use white borders on dark backgrounds — they float against the dark cinematic imagery without competing. No box, no card, no overlay panel — text and buttons are composited directly onto the video. Spatial hierarchy is purely typographic scale.

---

## Section 2 — Editorial Feature

White ground section. Centered section title in letter-spaced small caps sits above two featured image panels arranged in a **50/50 horizontal split**. The two panels are equal width, full-bleed within their column, and tall — roughly square proportions.

Each panel is a full-bleed photograph with text composited directly over the image:
- A large editorial title in white caps
- A smaller Secondary CTA button beneath it (white outline style, matching the hero)

The effect is two editorial "covers" sitting side by side — immersive but contained within a defined content zone. The white margin above the title creates breathing room before the images begin.

---

## Section 3 — Editorial / Press Grid

Three-column editorial card grid. Section title centered above, with a "Primary CTA" link directly below it in small caps — understated hierarchy.

Each card is a **portrait-format photograph** with no surrounding border or card shadow. Below each image:
- A date in small regular weight type
- A multi-line title in centered, tracked caps

Cards are equal width, equal gutter. No hover states visible. The type sits below the image with a generous gap — the text zone is open, not cramped. This section feels like a magazine archive rather than a product listing.

---

## Section 4 — Product Grid

Section title centered, in the same letter-spaced caps treatment used throughout. Below it: a **3-column product grid** with consistent gutters.

Each product cell:
- Portrait photograph with neutral light studio background
- A dark "Status Badge" overlaid in the top-right corner of the image
- Product name in tracked small caps below the image
- Price in regular weight below the name

Grid is uniform and systematic — no asymmetry, no featured/hero product. Two rows visible before the Primary CTA button. The filled Primary CTA button at the bottom of this section is the only filled/solid CTA on the page — everything else uses ghost/outline style — giving it visual authority as the primary commerce call to action.

---

## Footer

Two-column asymmetric layout on a white ground, separated from the product section by a thin horizontal rule:

- **Left column (narrow):** Stacked text links in tracked small caps — utility and policy navigation. Generous vertical rhythm between each link.
- **Right column (wide):** Newsletter signup block. Heading in tracked small caps, a short descriptor sentence, an underlined email input field with a submit arrow icon, then a row of social media icons.

Below both columns: a centered row of payment method icons, then the copyright line centered at the very bottom.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Full-bleed immersion alternating with contained grids.** The hero and the 50/50 feature panels are edge-to-edge; the editorial card grid and product grid sit in a centered max-width container. The page breathes through this alternation.

**Text composited over photography.** Both the hero and the editorial feature panels use text overlaid directly on images rather than text+image side-by-side. Image and copy are unified, not separated.

**Ghost CTA buttons throughout.** Nearly every call-to-action uses white outline / transparent fill — except the final Primary CTA in the product section, which uses a solid dark fill. This creates a deliberate hierarchy: browse is light, buy is solid.

**Consistent typographic language as the layout system.** Rather than relying on geometric shapes or decorative elements to create structure, the page uses one repeated typographic treatment — tracked all-caps, minimal weight, consistent small size — as the connective tissue between all sections. The layout is restrained because the type system does the organizational work.

**No sticky elements beyond the header.** Navigation is fixed/sticky at top. No floating CTAs, no chat widgets, no back-to-top buttons.
