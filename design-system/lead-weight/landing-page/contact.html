Commissions Open

Begin a Commission

We accept a limited number of commissions each year. Every enquiry is read personally by a senior editor within five working days, and we respond to each one in full — even if the project is not something we can take on. Please tell us as much as you can about what you have in mind.

VERDURE does not use an enquiry management system. Your message reaches us directly. We do not retain enquiry data beyond the life of the correspondence. If you do not hear from us within seven working days, we would ask you to write again — occasionally mail is lost, and we would not want a commission to go unmade because of a technical failure.
