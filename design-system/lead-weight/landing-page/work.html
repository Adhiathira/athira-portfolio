A record of typographic arguments made in print. Each project is a position taken — on weight, on proportion, on silence.

Featured 2024 Specimen Book

Didot Observed

A comprehensive examination of Didot across weight registers, tracking the typeface from its origins in Parisian rationalism to its contemporary reinterpretation in luxury brand identities. The specimen isolates each weight against silence — no color, no image, no texture beyond the pressure of ink on cotton rag. What remains is argument: that Didot at 900 weight reads as power, at 300 as restraint, and at anything between as a considered negotiation between the two.
