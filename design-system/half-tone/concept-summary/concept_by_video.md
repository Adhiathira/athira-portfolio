# Verso — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening
Right panel content arrives first as the page loads. Left color panel sweeps in from the left edge with 200ms delay — the book opening. The two panels meet at center with the drama of two pages settling flat.

## Hero
Full-viewport split. Left panel: solid deep plum (#4a3f5c) with white serif title "VERSO" reversed at 11rem, weight 300 — ultra-light, monumental. The letter-spacing begins wide (0.1em) and compresses to -0.03em over 1.2 seconds as the panels arrive, the title becoming denser, more present. Right panel: warm vellum with body copy, CTA pair, edition details in caption tracking.

## Video Placements
1. **hero-left-panel.mp4** — Slow camera pan across a letterpress printing press, muted footage, dark-tone color grade. 8-second loop. The moving image within the solid-color panel creates depth without disturbing the color field.
2. **process-spread.mp4** — Hands turning the pages of an art book, macro lens, warm tone. 6-second loop. Placed in the right vellum panel of the Process section.

## Section Transitions
Panels "close" like pages when scrolling between sections: a clipPath wipe from center outward reveals the next split pair. The sensation is physical — you are turning a page, not scrolling a website.

## Motion Principles
- Deliberate and unhurried. The split reveal is the signature motion — never instant, always 600ms minimum.
- Hero panels slide from opposite edges (left from -100%, right from +100%) and meet flush at center.
- Content within panels staggers in at 100ms intervals after the panel has settled.
- Service bands reveal via clip-path expanding from center — a book opening mid-page.
- Quote section enters with y:30→0 and opacity resolve — text rising from the surface of the page.
- The overall sensation: you are reading something printed, not browsing something digital.
