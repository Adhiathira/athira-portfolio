About FORMFACTOR

Precision testing instruments made in Sheffield since 1971.

Company

Built on the
premise that measurement is not an approximation.

FORMFACTOR was founded in Sheffield in 1971 by structural engineer Margaret Holroyd and metrologist David Rawcliffe. Their argument was simple: laboratories were spending precision budgets on specimens and zero on the machines measuring them. The result was measurement error compounding through every result. The FF-1 Static Load Frame was built to end that.

The original workshop was a converted steel-processing shed in Attercliffe. The first FF-1 took fourteen months to build and was sold to the University of Sheffield. It is still in service.

Today FORMFACTOR employs 142 people across manufacturing, metrology, software, and service. The FF-9 Series — the current production instrument — is manufactured on the same site, two streets from the original shed. The same founding constraint applies: measurement accuracy is a first-order requirement, not a specification checkbox.

Every FF-9 is calibrated on a UKAS-accredited reference load cell before leaving the factory. The calibration certificate ships with the instrument. No exceptions.

Heritage

The FF-1 is still in service.

The first instrument produced by FORMFACTOR — a 500 kN static load frame commissioned by the University of Sheffield Department of Civil Engineering — has been in continuous service since 1974. Over fifty years of compressive testing, calibrated every three years, never replaced.

We consider this a performance requirement, not a marketing story. An instrument that lasts fifty years is an instrument that was made correctly. We have not changed our view on what correctly means.

What we are built on

Four principles.
No exceptions.

01

Measurement accuracy is not negotiable

Every FF-9 leaves the factory certified to ISO 7500-1 Class 0.5. If a machine fails the calibration, it is reworked, not passed. There is no tolerance on tolerance.

02

Instruments outlast their operators

We manufacture to a 30-year service life minimum. Parts are stocked for 20 years after production ends. We have never EOL'd a product mid-life.

03

Every result must be traceable

FORMFACTOR DAQ generates complete test records — operator, protocol, calibration chain, environmental conditions. No result should exist that cannot be reconstructed in five years.

04

Made in Sheffield

Manufacturing, machining, assembly, and calibration all happen on-site in Attercliffe. Not for heritage reasons. Because it is how quality control works.
