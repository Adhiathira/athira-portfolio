FF-9 Deployment

Applications

Industries and test standards served by the FF-9 Series Static Load Frame.

Every material fails.
The question is how.

The FF-9 answers that question with measurement precision that leaves no ambiguity for the standards body, the regulator, or the record. Certified at Class 0.5 — the tolerance class that can be quoted in a court of law.

Industries served
▶

Structural Civil Engineering

Reinforced concrete, prestressed beam testing, masonry core compression, aggregate CBR. Paired with ISO 7500-1 and ASTM C39 protocols.

▶

Aerospace and Defence

CFRP and GFRP panel testing to ASTM D3039, D6641. Sandwich composite core shear. Environmental conditioning to 180 degrees C integrated.

▶

Medical Device Regulation

Spinal implant fatigue per ASTM F1717, hip stem push-out per ISO 7206-8. 21 CFR Part 11 audit trail built into FORMFACTOR DAQ software.

▶

Geotechnical and Mining

UCS, point load, and Brazilian disc testing on rock core from 30 mm to 150 mm diameter. Acoustic emission monitoring optional for fracture event detection.

▶

Automotive Components

Seat structure, seatbelt anchor pull-out, door module crush to FMVSS and ECE standards. High-rate displacement capture at 10 kHz resolves quasi-static to sub-dynamic events.

▶

Advanced Materials Research

Novel alloys, ceramics, additive manufactured samples, bio-inspired composites. Open API enables custom control loops for research-defined protocols not covered by standards.

Test standards

Tension testing of metallic materials. Full extensometry compliance.

Tensile testing of metallic materials at room temperature.

Flexural properties of plastics. Three-point and four-point configurable.
