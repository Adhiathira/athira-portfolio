FF-9 Series

Technical Specifications

Complete mechanical, electrical, and software specifications for the FF-9 Static Load Frame. All values independently verified.

Mechanical

Maximum load capacity 2500 kN
Minimum load 50 N
Load accuracy ISO 7500-1 Class 0.5
Crosshead travel 1200 mm
Crosshead velocity 0.001 to 500 mm/min
Velocity accuracy plus or minus 0.1%
Column spacing 680 mm clear
Test space height (max) 1100 mm
Machine weight 4200 kg

Measurement and Control

Displacement resolution 0.1 micrometer
Data acquisition rate 10 kHz
Load cell type Strain-gauge, hermetically sealed
Extensometry inputs 4 differential analogue, 2 digital
Control modes Load, displacement, strain
Feedback bandwidth DC to 100 Hz
Position feedback Optical linear encoder

Electrical and Environment

Supply voltage 380 to 480 V, 3-phase, 50/60 Hz
Power consumption (peak) 22 kVA
Operating temperature 10 to 40 degrees C
Humidity (non-condensing) 20 to 80 percent RH
Communications Gigabit Ethernet, USB 3.2, RS-422
Safety standard EN ISO 7500-1, EN 61010-1

Software

Operating system Windows 11 Pro (supplied)
DAQ software FORMFACTOR DAQ 4.2
Report export PDF, CSV, XLSX, HDF5
API access REST over Ethernet, open spec
Audit trail 21 CFR Part 11 compliant

Accessories

Compression platens 100, 200, 300 mm diameter
Tensile grips Hydraulic, mechanical, pneumatic series
3-point flexure rig 100 to 1000 mm span
Environmental chamber Minus 40 to plus 300 degrees C
Clip gauge extensometers 10 to 100 mm gauge length
