Instrument capabilities

What the
FF-9 measures.

  • 01

    Static Compressive Strength

    Monotonic and cyclic loading protocols from 50 N to 2500 kN. Crosshead velocity 0.001 to 500 mm/min, servo-controlled to plus or minus 0.1 percent full scale.

  • 02

    Tensile Pull-Out Testing

    ASTM E8/E8M and EN ISO 6892-1 compliant. Extensometry via dual-clip gauges, digital image correlation, or LVDT chain — operator selectable.

  • 03

    Three-Point and Four-Point Flexure

    Configurable span from 100 mm to 1000 mm. Roller assembly hardened to 58 HRC. Deflection resolution 0.1 micrometers. ISO 178, ASTM D790.

  • 04

    Fracture Toughness K_Ic

    Pre-cracked compact tension and single-edge notched beam specimens. Real-time load-displacement acquisition at 10 kHz. ASTM E399.

  • 05

    Creep and Relaxation

    Temperature-controlled test chamber to 300 degrees C optional. Sustained load hold to plus or minus 0.05 percent over 1000-hour protocols. ISO 899-1.

Applications

Structural Concrete Research

Aerospace Composite Qualification

Interlaminar shear, open-hole compression, filled-hole tension per ASTM D3846, D6484, D6742. Compatible with temperature chambers to 180 degrees C for cure simulation.

Medical Implant Compliance Testing
