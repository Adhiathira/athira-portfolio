Forecast Methodology

How Spectra Predicts

01

NOAA ACE Satellite

1.5M km from Earth

Real-time solar wind monitoring at the L1 Lagrange point. ACE measures magnetic field strength, particle density, and velocity — providing a 30–60 minute warning before geomagnetic impact.

02

48-Station Network

Global ground array

Ground-based magnetometers distributed across auroral zones measuring geomagnetic field disturbances in real time. Cross-station correlation improves Kp derivation accuracy to ±0.1 units.

03

ML Forecast Engine

87% accuracy at 1hr lead

Trained on 25 years of solar cycle data spanning cycles 22 through 25. Ensemble model combining LSTM sequence prediction with gradient-boosted short-range estimators for robust storm forecasting.

Data latency under 4 minutes from NOAA ingestion to display
