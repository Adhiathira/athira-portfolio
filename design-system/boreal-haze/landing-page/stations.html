Instrumentation

What Each Station Measures

01

Fluxgate
Magnetometer

0.1 nT resolution / 1s cadence

Measures three-axis magnetic field variations with 0.1 nT resolution at 1-second cadence. Primary Kp input sensor. The fluxgate core saturates and desaturates with the ambient field, producing a voltage signal proportional to field strength in all three spatial components.

X / Y / Z axes Primary Kp sensor 1s sampling
02

Search-coil
Magnetometer

0.001 – 10 Hz band

High-sensitivity pulsation detector capturing ULF wave activity 0.001–10 Hz. Captures Pc1–Pc5 pulsation bands. The induction coil detects rapid field changes invisble to fluxgate sensors, providing critical early warning signatures during substorm onset and solar wind pressure pulses.

Pc1 – Pc5 bands Substorm onset ULF waves
03

All-sky
Camera

4K / 30s exposure cycle

Full 180° fisheye lens capturing the aurora oval every 30 seconds. 4K resolution, monochrome + RGB filter channels. Each frame covers the entire sky dome overhead, enabling automated morphology classification — arcs, bands, coronae, diffuse glow — feeding directly into aurora shape prediction models.

180° fisheye Mono + RGB 30s cycle
