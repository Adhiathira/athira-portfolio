# Moral Skin — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

Moral Skin is a premium skincare brand whose website positions itself at the intersection of luxury editorial design and clinical credibility. The site does not follow conventional e-commerce structure — it builds a brand narrative before presenting product, using composition-driven hero design, poetic editorial typography, and a scroll-driven product storytelling mechanism that keeps the physical object at the center of the experience. The design philosophy is intelligent luxury: a considered aesthetic that signals premium quality through negative space, precise typography, and a strict system-level rule that separates content from interaction through shape.

## Color Architecture

The palette is minimal and nature-referencing. Off-white and a warm light beige form the dominant background surfaces across most of the page, creating a gallery-like neutrality that allows photography and typography to carry the visual weight. A muted soft sage/mint green serves as the sole accent color, appearing in the announcement bar at the top, the social proof image gallery section, and the footer — using color to demarcate these zones without introducing a vivid or aggressive hue. Near-black anchors all text and primary CTA buttons. No other chromatic elements enter the system. The palette communicates warmth, naturalism, and restraint simultaneously.

## Typography System

A two-font system creates the site's editorial character. A high-contrast, classical serif typeface handles all display moments — the full-bleed wordmark in the hero, section headlines, and product names — at large scales where its distinctive contrast (thick and thin strokes) reads as luxury authority. A clean, geometric sans-serif handles everything else: body copy, navigation links, button labels, ingredient lists, and product detail text. This serif/sans-serif division maps precisely onto the brand/function split: when the site is expressing identity or making a statement, serif; when it is transacting or informing, sans-serif. The size contrast between the two is significant — the display serif appears at scales that dwarf the functional sans-serif — creating an immediate and unambiguous visual hierarchy.

## Spatial System

The site operates across five distinct spatial modes. The hero is a full-bleed split-screen composition: left half is a lifestyle photograph, right half is a soft abstract 3D-rendered sphere background, with a massive serif wordmark spanning both halves in the foreground. A single gentle concave arc at the bottom of the hero section creates an organic opening into the body content below — the site's only curved section boundary. The editorial headline sections are minimal and centered, using generous horizontal margins and expansive vertical breathing room to create focused typographic statements. Product sections are relaxed asymmetric two-column layouts with product photography left and detail copy right. The floating benefits section is scroll-driven: a product object is pinned to the viewport center while text content scrolls behind it. The social proof section uses a horizontal auto-scrolling image gallery.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

Photography is the primary content medium and occupies the site's most important compositional positions. The hero lifestyle photograph fills half the viewport — a professional, art-directed image with its subject angled toward the center of the split, creating compositional balance with the abstract spheres on the right. Product photography is clean and contained within sharp-cornered rectangular frames. Circular image masks appear as decorative elements in the editorial headline sections — small, cropped macro shots of skin or product texture that function like inline illustration rather than primary photography. The social proof section's auto-scrolling gallery uses user-generated content at a consistent scale, presented without captions as a continuous material evidence stream.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar has three zones: links left, centered wordmark, utility links right (Account, Cart). It is sticky from the first frame — white background throughout, no transparency phase. The only adaptive change on scroll is the appearance of a subtle 1px separator line or soft drop shadow at the navigation's bottom edge, providing visual lift above scrolling content. An announcement bar above the navigation uses the sage/mint green accent for its background — a deliberate top-of-page signal in the only saturated color in the system. The navigation contains no dropdown menus; the product offering appears through page structure rather than nested navigation.

## Overall Design Character

Moral Skin is a luxury product site designed with three sustained decisions: the strict shape dichotomy — rectangular containers for all media and content, pill-shaped forms for all interactive controls — that makes the distinction between "observe" and "act" a system-level geometric principle; the editorial typography split — serif for brand expression, sans-serif for function — applied without exception across every text element; and the floating product scroll-actor, a technique borrowed from cinematic design where the physical object remains fixed in the center of attention while the narrative scrolls around it. The palette's deliberate restraint (off-white + beige + sage green + near-black) positions the brand in the territory of natural luxury: sophisticated enough to feel premium, warm enough to feel human.
