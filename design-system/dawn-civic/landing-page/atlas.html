The Atlas

Three bodies of work. One cartographic standard.

01

City Survey Maps

Block-level surveys of historic urban centres, drawn at 1:2,000 scale on archival linen-finish stock. Our city series spans seventeen European capitals, each produced from primary survey data validated against municipal cadastral records. A Ordnance Press city map is a legal-grade document with an aesthetic argument.

Scale
1:2,000
Stock
Archival linen-finish, 140gsm

200 copies per city, numbered

02

Topographic Atlases

Elevation studies and contour atlases for mountain regions, river watersheds, and coastal territories. A hydrological argument bound in cloth.

→ assets/topo-atlas-mountain.jpeg

Alpine Series

Eight volumes covering the central Alpine arc from Savoy to the Dolomites. Contour intervals at 10m. Each volume bound in natural buckram with gilt spine lettering.

Watershed Studies

River basin surveys with drainage network overlays. Rhine, Danube, Rhône, Po — each watershed mapped from headwater to tidal limit.

Coastal Surveys

Intertidal zone mapping with bathymetric overlay. Produced in partnership with the Institut Géographique National for the Atlantic and Mediterranean coastlines.
