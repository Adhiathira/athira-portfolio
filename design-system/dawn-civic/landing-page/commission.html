Bespoke cartography

Begin a commission

Every commission begins with a conversation. Tell us what you need to map.

What we commission

Individual

A single map, produced to your geographic brief. Named locations, family estates, historical surveys of a place important to you. Printed in a single edition of 1.

Typical delivery 14–18 weeks

Institutional

Municipal planning documents, archive editions, and research cartography. Produced under institutional brief with full provenance documentation and statutory compliance where required.

Typical delivery 20–32 weeks

Library Edition

Numbered editions for library and collector acquisition. We accept a small number of proposals annually for maps we believe should exist and don't yet. These are co-produced with the commissioning party.

Typical delivery 24–40 weeks

What to expect

Response within 5 working days

Every enquiry is reviewed by a cartographer, not an account manager. We will tell you whether we can do what you need, and if not, why not.

No deposit until brief is signed

The survey brief is a contractual document. We will not ask for a financial commitment until both parties have agreed on the scope, the standard, and the timeline.

A single point of contact

You will work with one cartographer from brief to delivery. That person's name will appear on your map.

Send an enquiry

Use this form to describe your commission. Be as specific or as vague as you like — we will ask clarifying questions. There is no wrong way to start a conversation about a map.
