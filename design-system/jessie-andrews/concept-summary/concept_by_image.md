# Jessie Andrews — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A model and creative's portfolio site built on the Format.com platform. The site positions its subject through work rather than biography — the primary navigation leads directly to curated media archives, and the design philosophy is one of deliberate exclusion. Every potential embellishment has been removed: no accent colors, no decorative shapes, no gradient overlays, no animated entrances. The UI is a neutral armature whose sole purpose is to hold images and video. The work is the content; the interface asks nothing of the viewer except to look.

## Color Architecture

Pure binary — white (#ffffff) and black (#000000) are the only colors present in the UI layer. No tints, no neutrals, no accent. All color in the design exists exclusively within photographic and video content. This creates a total chromatic separation between interface and media: the UI is achromatic, the content is chromatic. The nav background is solid white at all scroll states — no transparency, no blur, no scroll-triggered color change. Link color is black throughout. The chromatic boundary is absolute and consistent at every scroll position and viewport state.

## Typography System

Open Sans (Google Font, 400 regular weight) is the sole typeface — no display scale, no serif editorial counterpart, no weight variation for hierarchy. The nav wordmark (multi-line stacked identifier) is set in all-caps or small caps at a restrained point size on the left. Section labels, nav links, and navigational text use all-caps treatment throughout. There is no conventional h1–h6 typographic hierarchy — the type scale exists to label and navigate, not to narrate. Typography functions as a wayfinding instrument, not a design element.

## Spatial System

The page oscillates between two compositional states. Full-bleed horizontal media scrollers — the primary content surface — extend edge to edge at a fixed track height, with media items butted together at minimal gaps, extending rightward out of the viewport. Between scrollers, text sections (section title in all-caps, optional caption) occupy a narrow centered column with generous vertical whitespace above and below. The contrast between the full-bleed scroller track and the narrow text field creates a rhythm of immersion and pause. No decorative dividers — all section boundaries are pure whitespace with straight horizontal edges.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

All media is presented in sharp rectangular frames at a uniform track height. No cropping effects, no rounded corners, no drop shadows, no color overlays, no vignettes. Auto-playing video and still photography coexist within the same horizontal track without visual differentiation between media types — the container is neutral. The editorial voice is that of a photographer's working archive: systematic, comprehensive, and unsentimental. Each scroller is a filmstrip. The presentation method is the content's primary editorial statement.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

A sticky white nav bar holds the stacked multi-line wordmark on the left and navigation links on the right — all-caps, small point size, black text. The nav maintains a solid white background at all scroll states without responding to context changes: no transparency at the top, no color shift over dark media. A small pill-shaped "info" button appears on hover over individual media items — this is the only rounded UI element on the site. Its departure from the otherwise strictly rectilinear system is justified by utility: it is transient (visible only on hover), functional (reveals metadata), and small (a point of interaction, not a design gesture).

## Overall Design Character

The site makes its statement through subtraction. Every design element that competes with the photography and video for visual attention has been removed. What remains is a precise spatial lattice — white field, black type, rectangular media, horizontal motion. The horizontal scroller is the site's defining structural innovation: it turns portfolio browsing into a spatial act, a drag through a filmstrip rather than a scroll through a grid. The strictly monochrome UI boundary ensures the viewer's eye is never redirected away from the image content by an interface gesture. This is not minimalism as aesthetic trend — it is a deliberate service relationship between the interface and the work it carries.
