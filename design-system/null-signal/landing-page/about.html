About PHANTOM

We tracked them before we built this.

PHANTOM is built by former intelligence analysts, offensive security researchers, and network engineers who have spent careers tracking the same adversaries they now help defend against.

Mission

THE INTELLIGENCE
GAP

Most security products aggregate data and alert on thresholds. What they cannot do is tell you who is attacking you, why, and what they are likely to do next.

The intelligence gap is the distance between a network alert and adversary attribution. PHANTOM closes that gap. Every detection is cross-referenced against our adversary dossier database, built from 12 years of continuous tracking.

We built PHANTOM because we got tired of watching defenders react. The information to anticipate existed — it just wasn't accessible at the speed and scale a modern SOC demands. Now it is.
