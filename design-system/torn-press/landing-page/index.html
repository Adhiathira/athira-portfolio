We Press. We Book. We Print.

Torn Press is a DIY pressing collective operating from a converted bus depot on Salford Quays. We press your vinyl, dub your cassettes, book your shows, and publish a hand-screened zine for every release. No outside investors. No aesthetic consultants. Just ink and machinery.

How It Works

From
Tape To
Wax

Send us your audio. We master it, cut it, press it, and sleeve it — start to finish in the same building. No middlemen. No disappearing acts.

01

Send Your Audio

WAV, 24-bit/96kHz. We accept tape transfers. No MP3s.

02

Mastering + Lacquer Cut

Our in-house cutting lathe. Flat-response mastering chain. You approve before we press.

03

Press + Package

50–500 copies. Vinyl or cassette. Sleeves printed on-site via screen or risograph.

04

Zine + Distribution

We print a hand-folded zine for every release. Optional distro through our network.
