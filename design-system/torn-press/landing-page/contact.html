FAQ

Common
Questions

What's the minimum run?

50 copies for cassette. 100 copies for 7". 150 copies for 12". No exceptions — below these numbers the unit cost doesn't make sense for either of us.

How long does it take?

6–10 weeks from approved lacquer to finished copies in your hands. Cassettes are faster: 3–4 weeks. Rush is available if you're buying us coffee as well.

Do I have to record with you?

No. We'll press anything you bring us as long as it's your music and the audio is at spec. We don't take exclusivity, we don't take points.

Can I get a coloured wax pressing?

Yes. Black, white, clear, bone, and splatter are all available. Custom colours are possible at 300+ copies with 8 weeks lead time.

Do you distribute?

We have a network of 40 independent record shops across the North of England who take Torn Press releases on consignment. It's not formal distribution — it's personal relationships. If your record is on our label, we'll include you. If you're pressing independently, ask us and we'll see what we can do.
