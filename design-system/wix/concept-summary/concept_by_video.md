# Wix — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads into a live state: the hero gradient is already in slow motion, and the floating blob shapes are already drifting in the foreground layer before any interaction. There is no loading animation, no reveal sequence, no text that animates in on load — the page arrives already breathing. The opening frame is an ambient composition in motion, not a static frame waiting to begin.

## Hero Experience

The hero is organized around three layers with distinct motion behavior. The background gradient — blending warm peach, soft lavender, and gentle blue-purple tones — drifts and evolves slowly and continuously on its own autonomous clock. The floating organic blob shapes in the middle layer move slightly faster, with subtle morphing at their edges and a mild response to cursor position or scroll depth. The foreground content layer (headline, paragraph, CTA buttons) is static. When the user begins to scroll, the floating shapes begin their longer journey: they start to transition from foreground accent elements to background decoration, crossing the concave arc section divider and continuing downward as persistent scroll actors behind subsequent sections. The hero is the launch point for the entire page's motion vocabulary — everything that moves downstream was introduced here.

## Section Transitions

Three types of transition are employed, each for a specific structural purpose. The concave arc at the hero base — symmetrical, approximately 15–20% of viewport height — curves deeply upward into the hero's space, creating a visual scoop that connects the animated hero register to the structured feature grid below it. An organic "drip" shape at the bottom of one split media-text section creates an irregular melting boundary — five or six downward-hanging teardrop forms of varying lengths and asymmetric spacing — that transitions from the informational zone into the showcase section below. Background color inversions between light sections and the single dark mid-page section are hard straight cuts by contrast, relying on the tonal shift itself rather than geometric ornament.

## Content Rhythm

Scroll-triggered reveals follow a consistent hierarchy: section headline enters first with a fade and upward slide, followed by the sub-headline, followed by the card grid or body content in a staggered sequence from top to bottom or left to right. This cascading pattern creates a sense of the section being populated as the user reads it rather than arriving all at once. In the horizontal scroll section, scroll hijacking takes over — vertical scroll input is rerouted to advance the horizontal card track, with a peek affordance at the right edge signaling the presence of additional content. The hijack releases cleanly once the final card is reached, returning to vertical navigation without a jarring transition.

## Footer Approach

The footer arrives as a neutral closure after the logo wall. No entrance animation, no tonal shift beyond the structural transition from the body's final section. The blob vocabulary that defines the page's motion identity does not carry into the footer — the footer is informational territory, and the organic language yields to function.

## Motion Principles

The motion vocabulary has two registers: ambient and responsive. The hero's gradient and blob elements run continuously on their own clock, independent of user input — they establish the page's atmospheric baseline. All content reveals are scroll-responsive: triggered by viewport entry, not by time. The scroll actors are a bridge between these two registers — initially ambient (hero foreground), becoming environmental (background decoration) as the user descends. The horizontal scroll section introduces a third dynamic — input substitution, where scroll velocity maps directly to horizontal track position. The overall pacing is unhurried: the ambient elements move slowly, the reveals are gentle, and the hijacked scroll section presents one focused card at a time.

## Video Placements

**1. Hero Background — Animated gradient field**
- Suggested file: `hero-gradient-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A slowly morphing, abstract soft-gradient field — warm peach, muted lavender, and cool blue-purple tones blending and shifting at the edges, no sharp boundaries, no recognizable objects; the motion should be almost imperceptible second-to-second but clearly evolving over the full loop; light-mode luminosity throughout (the field should feel bright and airy, not dark or cinematic); 12s loop
- Duration: 12s loop
- Why video: The hero's background is the page's atmospheric foundation — a static gradient cannot replicate the slow-drift, color-evolution quality that establishes the site's sense of life at load. A looping ambient video occupying the full hero background layer would provide the gentle, continuous motion that the blob shapes react against, giving the entire hero composition a more deeply textured sense of dimension and warmth.

**2. Full-Width Dark Media Section — Atmospheric showcase**
- Suggested file: `dark-section-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: An abstracted, slow-moving atmospheric composition on a dark field — light catching a translucent surface, a subtle illuminated lens flare drifting across a dark space, or soft volumetric light ray in a dim architectural void; no text, no product, no faces; the palette should be dark with occasional warm highlights; 10–12s loop
- Duration: 10–12s loop
- Why video: The single dark section mid-page is the site's primary high-contrast dramatic beat. A looping ambient video in this section — providing atmospheric depth without distraction — would amplify the sense that this section is a different register entirely, a cinematic pause between the structured light-mode zones that surround it.

**3. Split Media-Text Section — Blob-masked product clip**
- Suggested file: `feature-product-in-use.mp4`
- Subject: A screen recording or product walkthrough of an interface being assembled or customized — a smooth, intentional cursor movement building or editing something on a clean surface; the clip should have clear text-safe zones (the lower half should be low-movement or low-contrast) to remain legible when masked inside the organic blob frame; neutral light mode background; 6–8s loop
- Duration: 6–8s loop
- Why video: One of the alternating split media-text sections — ideally the one most focused on the act of creation rather than the result — would benefit from a short looping product interaction clip instead of a static screen. Masked through the organic blob frame, the moving interface would communicate the product's real-time, dynamic quality in a way that a still screenshot cannot, while maintaining the section's balanced composition through the blob mask's containment.
