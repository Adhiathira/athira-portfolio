# Wix — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A SaaS platform presenting itself as approachable and creatively empowering rather than technical or enterprise-grade. The site's formal argument is that a single organic shape language — the asymmetric, soft-lobed "blob" — can function as a complete visual system when applied consistently across every scale of the interface: as decorative floating elements in the hero, as image-masking frames for photographic content, as persistent scroll actors that travel across section boundaries, and as architectural section dividers. This blob vocabulary is not an aesthetic affectation but a systematic identity choice, encoding a brand personality that is modern, friendly, and compositionally alive. The structured grid and the organic shape system coexist throughout without conflict: the grid handles information architecture, the organic language handles atmosphere and brand presence.

## Color Architecture

The palette is predominantly light-mode and professionally restrained. White (`#ffffff`) and a very light gray (`#f6f6f6`) serve as the primary surface colors for most sections, providing a bright, high-readability foundation. Near-black (`#1c1d21`) is the heading text — a value with barely perceptible warmth relative to pure black, softening the reading experience at display scales. Body text and link text are pure black (`#000000`) for maximum legibility. A single vibrant accent — a saturated blue-purple — is reserved exclusively for primary CTA buttons, interactive highlights, and key brand moments; it does not appear in decorative backgrounds or section fills. One dark section appears strategically mid-page as a full-width media showcase: this background inversion creates a high-contrast dramatic beat and provides visual pacing relief from the sustained light register. The abstract blob shapes in the hero and section backgrounds use softer gradient tones — peach, lavender, warm sand — that harmonize with the overall palette without competing with the primary accent.

## Typography System

The Wix Madefor family (proprietary, entirely internal to the platform) performs all typographic work across the site. Two sub-families handle distinct registers. Madefor Display (400 weight at display scale) carries the hero headline at 99px with tight negative tracking (`-2.97px`) that makes the large-scale text feel dense and purposeful rather than airy — a design decision calibrated for authority at extreme size. A second display cutpoint at 57.6px with minimal tracking handles section-level headlines. A medium-weight variant of Madefor Display (`wix-madefor-display-v2`, 500 weight) at 25.2px handles sub-section titles and navigational typography. Madefor Text (400, 16px/24px line-height) handles all body paragraphs; a 14px variant serves button labels. The type system is a single-family instrument without serif contrast or editorial voice variation — its range is expressed entirely through weight, size, and tracking adjustments within the same geometric-humanist sans-serif. Pill-shaped buttons are standard throughout: primary CTA with solid accent fill, secondary with ghost outline.

## Spatial System

Seven zone types structure the page. A full-bleed hero section occupies the opening viewport: animated gradient background, floating organic blob shapes in the foreground layer, centered content column with headline hierarchy and CTA pair, and a large concave arc divider (symmetrical, single wide valley, ~15–20% viewport height deep) at its base. A feature card grid follows: 2×2 or 3×2 card arrangement within a centered container, cards with slightly rounded corners (~8–12px radius), circular icon frames, light background with subtle card differentiation (thin border or tonal background shift). Alternating split media-text sections present at two-column (50/60–50/40) balanced layouts, with media frames using organic blob masks rather than rectangular crops. A scroll-hijacked horizontal showcase section locks vertical scroll and advances a card track laterally, with a peek affordance at the right edge and a progress indicator at the section base. One full-width dark media section provides high-contrast atmospheric punctuation mid-page. Centered-column spacer sections (large headline, sub-headline paragraph, generous vertical whitespace) provide visual breathing room and topical transitions. A logo wall on a neutral background closes the body before the multi-column informational footer.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

The blob-shaped image frame is the site's most distinctive photographic treatment: instead of rectangular or even rounded-rectangular crops, photographic and UI content is masked into organic asymmetric shapes with four to six soft undulating lobes. This is the same formal vocabulary as the hero's decorative floating elements — here applied to contain photographic content, creating a continuous organic identity across decorative and content-bearing uses. Product screens, feature mockups, and lifestyle photography all enter through this blob frame, which functions simultaneously as visual treatment and brand marker. Section backgrounds in some zones carry very faint, large-scale abstract blob forms in a subtle secondary layer — ghost textures providing depth without competing with foreground content.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar uses a three-zone structure: wordmark far left, a cluster of primary category links centered, and a primary CTA button far right. On the hero, the bar sits over the animated gradient background as a transparent overlay — text in white for legibility against the dark hero. It does not maintain this transparency on scroll: when the user scrolls down, the nav disappears entirely. When the user reverses direction and scrolls up, the bar reappears sticky with a solid white background, a soft drop shadow elevating it from underlying content, and a backdrop blur applied to content visible through the bar's surface. All text inverts from white to near-black during this transition. This is a "scroll-up reveal" pattern — prioritizing immersive content consumption on the downward journey while ensuring consistent navigational access when the user indicates intent to re-orient.

## Overall Design Character

The site's central design argument is that organic geometry and geometric UI can share the same page without one subordinating the other. The blob system — applied at every scale, from hero floating element to image mask to section divider — unifies the interface across its varying information densities. No single section behaves like a standalone module; the scroll actors connecting hero foreground to mid-page background enforce continuity across the full page arc. The result is a site that feels designed as a single coherent world rather than a sequence of individual templates. The creative tension between the blob's organic softness and the sans-serif type system's geometric precision is the brand's personality encoded in form.
