Our approach

The annotation is
the argument.

Most annotation tools treat notes as commentary. Margin treats them as first-class records — structures with authorship, timestamp, severity, and traceable resolution. The mark on the page is the evidence.

Three principles govern every decision we make.

I

The annotation outlives the conversation.

Review comments in documents exist only while the document is open. Email threads are not part of the document. Margin's position: if an annotation matters, it belongs to the document permanently — not to a sidebar that closes or an inbox that gets archived. Every note Margin stores is owned by the document, not the session.

II

Resolution is a structured event, not a deletion.

Marking an annotation as "done" should record the resolution — who decided, when, and why — not erase the evidence that a question was raised. Margin's resolution model: a note is never deleted, only closed with attribution.

III

Structure enables export, export enables compliance.

Unstructured comments cannot be exported into compliance reports. Margin requires every annotation to carry enough structure — severity, clause tag, author, timestamp — that a machine-readable compliance export is always possible. The discipline of structured input pays off downstream, every time.

Architecture

Built on document structure, not document format.

Margin's annotation engine operates on the document's logical structure — paragraphs, clauses, items — not on the rendered layout. This means annotations survive format changes: a paragraph annotated in Markdown is still annotated after export to PDF. The anchor is structural, not visual.

Annotation data lives in a separate layer from the source content. You always have a clean version of your document and a separate, complete annotation record. Nothing is embedded in the source that the source does not control.

Every annotation carries its full context.

Author Named attribution. Every annotation belongs to someone, always.
Timestamp UTC timestamp at creation and at resolution. The record does not drift.
Severity Must-fix, should-fix, informational, or approved deviation. Drives export filtering.
Clause tag Maps to any standards taxonomy: ISO, WCAG, NIST, or your own schema.
Anchor Structural reference to the exact paragraph, clause, or item. Format-independent.
Resolution Closed with attribution: who resolved it, when, with what response. Never deleted.
