Structured annotation.
No noise.

Every mark Margin makes is traceable, versioned, and exportable. Precision is the feature, not a promise.

01

Inline annotation

Place precise margin notes directly against source text. Annotations travel with the document — in PDF export, HTML output, and version history.

02

Standards compliance tagging

Map every annotation to a standards clause — ISO, WCAG, NIST, or your own taxonomy. Compliance gaps surface automatically at the document level.

03

Version-controlled history

Each annotation is a timestamped, attributed record. Disputes about what was said, when, and by whom are answered by the document itself.

From draft to record
in four steps.

01

Import your document

Paste Markdown, upload a PDF, or connect a Git repository. Margin parses the structure and presents the document with annotation rails on every paragraph.

02

Mark with precision

Select any range — character to paragraph. Add a note, attach a standards clause, assign a severity, and route to a named reviewer. One mark, complete record.

03

Resolve in context

Reviewers respond in-thread, directly on the annotation. Resolutions are logged against the original mark — not in a separate comments panel or email chain.

04

Export the record

Generate a compliance report, an annotated PDF, or a diff-ready Markdown file. Every annotation, every resolution, every author — in one clean export.
