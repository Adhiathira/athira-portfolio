Each project below started as a working draft. Margin turned the annotation layer into the authoritative version history — so when questions arise about a decision, the answer is already in the document.

Standards alignment ISO 27001

ISMS Policy Suite — Thornwick Group

Thornwick's information security management policy required simultaneous annotation by four teams — legal, InfoSec, audit, and executive communications. Margin served as the single annotation layer across 43 policy documents, mapping every note to its ISO 27001 clause. Final export: a fully annotated policy suite with a compliance-gap register embedded in each document.

43 policy documents 4 concurrent review teams 100% clause coverage
Technical publishing WCAG 2.2

Accessibility Audit Documentation — Vere Digital

Vere Digital needed to produce client-facing accessibility audit reports traceable to WCAG 2.2 success criteria. Previous workflow: spreadsheets plus email threads. Margin replaced both — annotations attached to client-uploaded HTML snapshots, each mapped to the appropriate criterion. Reports exported as structured PDF with clause references intact.

2.3× faster report production 0 disputed annotations post-delivery WCAG 2.2 full taxonomy
Regulated industry FDA 21 CFR Part 11

Clinical SOP Library — Meridian Medical Devices

Clinical standard operating procedures require controlled annotation: every mark must carry an author, a timestamp, and an approval chain. Margin's version-controlled history satisfied Part 11's electronic records requirements without a separate EDMS. SOPs moved from draft to approved state inside Margin, with the annotation log serving as the audit trail.

87 SOPs in scope Part 11 compliant audit trail 18 days to full deployment

Developer Portal Specification — Fulcrum Infrastructure

Fulcrum's developer portal spans 12 product APIs. The annotation challenge: keeping product managers, engineers, and technical writers aligned on specification intent without three separate tools. Margin became the shared specification surface — OpenAPI YAML imported, annotated in-browser, exported with change history preserved in the YAML comments.

12 product APIs 3 teams, one annotation surface OpenAPI 3.1 native
