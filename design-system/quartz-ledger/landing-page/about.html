About Margin

We built this because the document deserved better.

Margin was built by a small team of technical writers, documentation engineers, and standards consultants. We had all spent years in the same situation: the document was the artifact, but the annotation layer was a collection of workarounds — tracked changes that disappeared, comments buried in email replies, compliance notes on sticky notes photographed and attached to tickets.

The document deserved a first-class annotation layer. That is the only thing Margin is about.

What we believe about
the annotation record.

Permanence

An annotation that can be deleted was never a record. Margin's data model treats closure and deletion as different events — always.

Attribution

Every mark has an author. Documents reviewed by a committee are still reviewed by individuals. Attribution is not optional metadata.

Portability

Your annotation data belongs to your document, not to our platform. We provide export at any tier, including after cancellation, without condition.

Precision over volume

We are not building a collaboration tool. Margin does one thing: structured annotation for documents where precision is the requirement, not the bonus.
