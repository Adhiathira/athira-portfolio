# Company — Layout Brief

---

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately **9–10 distinct sections**, arranged in a clear vertical rhythm with generous white space between each. The overall cadence alternates between: full-bleed editorial moments → contained card grids → wide promotional banners → more card grids → footer. The page breathes — sections are never densely packed, and the spatial gaps between them are as intentional as the content itself.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

**Dual-tier navigation system:**

- **Top bar:** Full-width, light background. Left-anchored logo + wordmark (separated by a vertical rule). Primary nav links spread horizontally across center-left, with a right-anchored "Sign in" + avatar icon. Links use a clean sans-serif, no weight or color differentiation — all neutral, democratic hierarchy.
- **Alert banner:** Immediately below the top nav, a narrow dark full-width strip carries a single promotional message with an inline text link (underlined). A close (×) button floats to the far right. This banner visually sandwiches between the primary nav and the hero — it's a third layer that doesn't feel intrusive.
- **Sticky secondary nav:** As you scroll, a thinner secondary tab bar (light background, no shadow initially) locks to the top. It carries 6–7 section-jump labels with an active state indicated by a small accent-color underline beneath the current section label. This nav is lighter in weight than the primary header.

---

## 3. Hero Section

**Asymmetric split-column layout with a full-bleed ambient background:**

- The hero occupies roughly the top 60–65% of the first viewport.
- **Background:** A soft, warm abstract organic-form photograph bleeds edge-to-edge behind the entire hero. There is no hard crop — the image dissolves into the section below.
- **Left column (~55% width):** Contains the editorial content stack — a small all-caps category label, followed by a very large display heading (3 lines, heavy weight, near-dark), then a 2–3 line body copy paragraph, and finally a prominent accent-color Primary CTA button with a directional icon. All text is left-aligned with generous line spacing.
- **Right column (~45% width):** Hosts a large rounded-rectangle media thumbnail card, slightly elevated (floating feel with subtle shadow). The card appears to float in front of the ambient background image. A centered play button overlays the media content.
- Vertical alignment of both columns is center-anchored — neither top-pins nor bottom-pins, creating a balanced tension.

---

## 4. Content Sections Below the Hero

### Section 2 — Category A (Horizontal Card Carousel)

A constrained-width section (not full-bleed). A centered heading + subtext block sits above. Below it: a **4-across horizontal card row**, but the rightmost card is partially clipped by the viewport edge — signaling scroll/swipe affordance. Each card is a tall portrait-format light card with:
- A square image at the top
- A small category label in the accent color
- A 2-line headline in medium weight
- A linked item title with an accent-color arrow-in-square icon

Two circular navigation buttons (prev/next) sit below the card row, left-aligned. The active button is filled accent; the inactive is outlined. Generous empty space below the nav buttons before the next section.

---

### Section 3 — Category B (Two-Column Asymmetric)

A light card panel that spans most of the content width (not full-bleed). Inside:
- **Left column (~40%):** Stacked text — headline, numbered list (4 items), then two side-by-side CTA buttons (one filled accent, one outlined accent).
- **Right column (~60%):** A large image or editorial photograph.

The two columns are roughly equal in visual weight, but the image column is wider — making the visual mass right-heavy, which keeps the eye moving rightward.

---

### Section 4 — Value Proposition (Full-Bleed CTA Banner)

A visually quiet but weighty section. A simple centered layout:
- Large centered heading (heavy, dark)
- 2-line centered subtext
- A single centered accent-color Primary CTA button

Light/off-white background. No images. Uses spatial isolation — lots of vertical breathing room above and below — to let the message land. A transitional section, not a content-dense one.

---

### Section 5 — Trust/Differentiator (Full-Bleed Immersive Image + Overlay Cards)

The most visually dramatic section:
- A **full-bleed edge-to-edge photographic image** (abstract or editorial in tone).
- Overlaid on top of this image, three **frosted glass-style cards** arranged in a 3-across grid. Each card has a light icon in a square container, a bold headline, and a short body paragraph, plus an accent-color arrow CTA. The cards have a semi-transparent light fill, allowing the image beneath to bleed through.
- This creates a layered depth effect — image behind, cards floating above — the only true overlapping-layers pattern on the page.

---

### Section 6 — Category C (Card Carousel with Gradient Background)

Similar pattern to Section 2 but with a **gradient background wash** — soft blended tones — giving this section a warmer editorial feel. Three wide portrait cards show editorial photography with category titles, body copy, and an accent-color CTA link. Again, a prev/next pagination control sits below.

---

### Section 7 — Category D (Card Carousel, Light Background)

Identical structural pattern to Sections 2 and 6 — 3-across horizontal card scroll with image, heading, and a text CTA. Clean light background with no gradient, restoring visual neutrality. Same pagination control.

---

### Section 8 — Social Proof (Card + Logo Selector, Light Background)

A two-part layout:
- **Upper:** A large single featured story card — left text block with tag links and a Primary CTA button, right side showing an editorial photograph.
- **Lower:** A horizontal logo strip — 6 partner or customer logos in equally-divided columns. The active/selected logo has an accent-color underline tab indicator. This functions as a tabbed selection mechanism disguised as a logo bar.

---

### Section 9 — FAQ (Accordion List)

Full-width section, light background. Left side has a large "Frequently asked questions" heading with two buttons (Expand all / Collapse all) aligned right of the heading. Below: a numbered accordion list — each row is separated by a thin horizontal rule, carrying a question and an accent-color toggle button on the far right. Numbers are displayed in a small secondary accent color functioning as visual anchors.

---

### Section 10 — Closing CTA (Two Split Cards, Gradient Background)

A soft gradient background. Two large horizontal cards side-by-side, each in a half-and-half layout:
- **Left half of each card:** Text block with icon, heading, body copy, and stacked CTA links
- **Right half of each card:** Photography bleed (editorial or product imagery)

The two cards are perfectly symmetrical in width and internal proportion — a clean 2-up grid that concludes the page content with visual warmth and clear action paths.

---

## 5. Footer

**Multi-tier footer, light background:**

- **Social media strip:** "Follow [Brand Name]" label followed by social platform icons, left-aligned. Sits above the link grid with a subtle visual gap.
- **6-column link grid:** Six equal-width columns, each headed by a category label, with 6–8 plain text links stacked beneath each. Completely flat hierarchy — no visual distinction between categories and links beyond a subtle weight difference.
- **Legal strip:** The bottom-most bar carries a globe icon + language selector, privacy toggle, and a horizontal list of legal links — all small, light-weight text. Right-aligned additional legal links mirror left-aligned locale links.

---

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

**Sticky secondary navigation:** The 6-tab label bar locks to the top after scroll, with an animated accent-color underline tracking the active section. This is the primary wayfinding device at scale.

**Ambient full-bleed backgrounds:** Two sections use edge-to-edge photographic or abstract backgrounds (hero, trust/differentiator section). These aren't decorative — they define the emotional register of the sections.

**Overlapping layers (Section 5):** Cards float on top of a full-bleed image with a frosted/translucent treatment — the only instance of true z-index layering on the page, used deliberately for maximum impact.

**Carousel pattern as a system:** Three sections reuse the exact same card-carousel pattern with slight content variations. This creates strong visual consistency and teaches the interaction pattern once — then reuses it across content categories.

**Partial card clip as scroll signal:** In all carousel sections, the rightmost card is intentionally cropped by the viewport edge — a classical horizontal scroll affordance that communicates "there is more" without text instructions.

**Gradient section washes:** Soft color gradients appear under certain sections, creating thermal differentiation between areas without hard-edge color blocks. These gradients feel like color temperature shifts rather than background fills.

**Spatial rhythm:** The page uses very consistent section-to-section gap heights. The breathing room between sections is generous and equal — approximately the height of 2–3 card thumbnails. This gives the page a calm, editorial pacing rather than a dense product catalog feel.
