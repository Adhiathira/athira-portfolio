# Ibotta — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A mobile-first cash-back and rewards platform. The site's central design decision is the use of a vibrant rose-pink (#df2a63) as the hero and conversion-section background — an assertive brand color that bookends the page while white informational sections handle the explanatory content between. The result is a structural rhythm familiar to consumer fintech: emotional opening → rational explanation → emotional close. The design operates within a tight, coherent shape language (pill buttons, generously rounded containers) and a single-family typographic system. A distinctive curved arc divider separates the pink sections from the white sections below, softening the color boundary with a single smooth scoop rather than a hard edge.

## Color Architecture

The token palette is comprehensive and named. Rose pink (#fceaef) is the brand's softest tone; ruby (#df2a63) and bright ruby (#ff3a78) are the primary brand assertives. A near-black (#1c1f24, labeled "cash-back-black") handles all body text and most UI text. White and five neutral grays (#d6d9dc through #f8f9fc) provide the layered surface vocabulary. A secondary teal (#0a7986) appears in the palette, with cobalt (#1e3cd6) and other accent hues reserved for categorical blog content labels. Heading text is white — confirming that primary display headings appear on the pink hero background rather than white surfaces. Body text is the near-black "cash-back-black." The color system is thorough but operates in practice as a two-register system: pink for energy, white/neutral for clarity.

## Typography System

Museo Sans (proprietary, not web-accessible) is the sole typeface throughout — used at every scale from the 72px/700 hero headline down to the 12px/300 paragraph text. The weight range is expressive: 700 for hero and section headlines, 400 for section titles and body, 300 for paragraph content. The single-family approach, running from condensed-bold display to light fine print, gives the site typographic coherence without visual variety. Nav links are set bold at 16px. The system has no editorial split — no serif counterpart, no secondary grotesque — which reads as young and friendly rather than sophisticated. The font's humanist construction at bold weights has enough personality to carry the pink-and-white brand register without feeling flat.

## Spatial System

The page operates in four spatial modes. The hero is a full-bleed pink composition: centered headline cluster flanked by asymmetrically placed floating product photography (cut-out shots without frames, with soft drop shadows), app store CTA buttons, and a single smooth concave arc at the bottom boundary where pink meets white. The app feature showcase is a self-contained large-radius card on a white background — a two-column split with text left and overlapping phone mockups right. Numbered feature explainer sections stack a text block (with pink circular step indicators) against a device mockup, repeated vertically. Blog and logo carousel sections use standard white-background three-column grids. The footer arrives on a dark background that closes the page's alternating color sequence.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

The hero uses product photography as compositional elements rather than contained illustrations — cutout product images floating freely in the negative space around the central text. These shots carry soft drop shadows that lift them off the pink background. On secondary pages, device mockups (phone, laptop) display UI screenshots, functioning as the primary media for feature explanations. Blog grid cards use lifestyle photography within rounded card containers. The overall photographic approach is commercial and approachable: high-quality product and lifestyle imagery without editorial abstraction.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar is transparent over the pink hero and remains transparent as the user scrolls — dark text (#1c1f24) throughout with no background fill change detected in the extracted tokens. The structure: logo left, four primary navigation links center, single CTA button right. A floating QR code element appears in the bottom-right corner of the viewport after the user begins scrolling — a persistent scroll actor that remains visible across conversion-focused pages but is absent from the blog section. The logo carousel section introduces a horizontal scroll interaction for partner brand logos, non-hijacking, indicated by content bleed on the right edge.

## Overall Design Character

Ibotta is a consumer fintech app with a single strong brand-color commitment. The rose-pink (#df2a63) is not used as a decorative accent — it is the hero background, the conversion section fill, the numbered step indicator, and the emotional frame of the entire page. Every design decision is organized around making this pink feel energetic without overwhelming the informational white sections. The curved arc divider is the one moment of geometric expressiveness in an otherwise rectilinear system: a single wide smooth scoop at the hero's bottom boundary, repeated at the CTA section, making those pink-to-white transitions feel organic rather than abrupt. The pill-button language and rounded card containers reinforce a friendly, accessible register consistent with a mass-market consumer brand.
