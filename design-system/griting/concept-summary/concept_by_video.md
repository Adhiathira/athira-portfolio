# Griting — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads with the hero fully rendered — the deep forest green (#003620) field fills the viewport and the headline is visible at full opacity in its bottom-left position. No loading animation, no entrance sequence. The environmental color arrives as a fact, not a transition. The first frame communicates permanence: this is infrastructure, and it was here before you arrived.

## Hero Experience

The hero's defining motion is a single, measured text reveal. The headline — typeset in 80px Helvetica Neue at the bottom-left — fades up from slight vertical offset (12–16px) over 600ms with a deceleration curve. The subheadline follows with a 150ms delay. Both CTA buttons enter together 100ms after the subheadline, also fading up. There is no parallax on the green background field. The background is a wall, not a layer — it does not move. The entire hero sequence lasts under one second and then holds completely still. The stillness is the statement: the infrastructure is ready; here is what it does.

## Section Transitions

As the user scrolls, each section transitions with a hard brightness shift — from deep green hero to light gray body, to dark green gradient, back to light gray, and finally to deep green footer. These color transitions happen at precise scroll boundaries with no blending or gradient overlap between sections. The effect is sectional clarity: each zone is a distinct environment that the user moves through, not a continuous scroll. The visual rhythm is punctuation-based — green sections are statements; light sections are elaboration.

## Content Rhythm

Individual elements within sections use a consistent stagger-fade-up pattern: opacity from 0 to 1, y from 24px to 0, over 400ms ease-out, with 80ms stagger between cards in multi-column grids. This applies to the four-card problem taxonomy (New Infrastructure section), the three-column ecosystem cards, and each product feature section. The data elements in the UI mockups — skill percentages, chart bars, roadmap weeks — count up from zero on scroll entry, reinforcing the platform's evidence-first philosophy. The motion communicates: here is the data arriving, verifiably, before your eyes. The testimonial cards in the six-column grid stagger in from left to right as a single sweep.

## Footer Approach

The footer arrives as a complete hard cut to deep forest green — the same environmental color as the hero. No fade, no gradient transition. The five-column link grid, copyright line, and social icons are all present at full opacity on entry. The footer's instant arrival closes the page's visual bracket: the visitor exits the same green environment they entered. The journey ends where it began, suggesting a system that is complete and self-contained.

## Motion Principles

The motion philosophy is deliberate, non-decorative, and evidence-oriented. Durations sit in the 400–600ms range — longer than typical SaaS animation, which communicates weight and permanence rather than agility. Easing is consistently deceleration-based (ease-out or ease-in-out), never spring or bounce. Stagger values are tight (80–100ms) so grid content arrives as a cohesive unit rather than a staggered show. The only motion that breaks this pattern is the count-up on data figures, which is slower (800ms) and synchronized to scroll entry. The design treats animation as a form of evidence delivery — things arrive when they are needed, fully formed, not in pieces.

## Video Placements

Two sections of this site would be meaningfully served by ambient looping video:

**1. Hero Background — Deep environment ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `hero-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A slow, almost imperceptible drift across a deep forest floor or dense moss surface in low, directional light — organic texture, earthy and permanent; 10s loop with a barely-visible camera movement, dark enough to preserve full text legibility over the green field
- Duration: 10s loop
- Why video: The hero currently uses a solid #003620 color field. A near-still ambient video of deep organic texture would preserve the environmental solidity of the green while adding a subtle temporal quality — the sense that the infrastructure is alive and breathing, not frozen. The motion would be subtle enough that visitors may not consciously register it as video, which is precisely the right register for a platform that positions itself as infrastructure beneath the visible.

**2. Product Feature Section — Dashboard in motion ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `product-eval-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A screen-recorded loop of the talent evaluation dashboard animating — skill percentage bars filling, evidence sources populating, graph nodes connecting — shot at a slight angle with soft studio lighting; 8s loop, slow and deliberate, suggesting a system processing in real time
- Duration: 8s loop
- Why video: The product feature sections use static UI mockup screenshots as their proof-of-concept artifacts. A slow-motion dashboard animation loop — showing the evaluation system doing its work — would make the "evidence-based" value proposition literal and kinetic. Seeing the percentages emerge and the report populate communicates, in motion, exactly what the static screenshot implies: this system produces verifiable, traceable outputs.
