How We Work

Every commission begins with listening.

We don't offer packages or standard configurations. Each piece begins as a conversation about how the object will be used, how it will age, and what it needs to survive.

01

Selection

We assess the object's current condition, discuss the commission scope, and agree on materials — leather origin, paper weight, thread color, board thickness. No detail is arbitrary.

02

Preparation

Existing bindings are carefully removed. For restorations, we consolidate loose pages, treat foxing, and stabilize the text block. For new commissions, paper and board are cut to specification and conditioned.

03

Binding

We use traditional hand-sewn structures — Coptic, longstitch, or French-link — appropriate to the commission. Leather is pared to the correct thickness for the spine and corners. Endpapers are attached and pressed.

04

Finishing

Titles are tooled by hand or gilt. Leather receives a final dressing. The completed piece is pressed, wrapped in acid-free tissue, and documented with photographs. Delivery is arranged with the collector.
