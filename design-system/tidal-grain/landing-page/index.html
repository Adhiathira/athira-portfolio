Our Belief

"Each book is a record of time made material. We work with the assumption that what we make should outlast us — the leather should soften, the thread should hold, the paper breathe for another century."

Since 1994, we have bound journals, restored first editions, and pressed custom folios for private collectors, rare book dealers, and institutional archives across the Pacific Northwest.
