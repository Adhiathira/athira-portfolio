# Griting-Voltage — Concept by Video

## Scroll Opening

The page loads with the hero fully rendered — the deep forest green field at full opacity, the headline at the bottom-left, CTAs visible. No entrance sequence. The distinction: the green field is not static in the way a flat color is static. There is a living quality to it — a barely-perceptible pulse or low-frequency ambient motion, as though current is running through the field rather than it simply existing as background. The effect is not decorative; it is atmospheric. The page does not announce itself. It operates.

## Hero Experience

The headline text reveal is the same as the primary system: fade-up over 600ms, deceleration easing. What the voltage layer adds is a directionality to the CTA button's treatment: on load, the primary CTA button carries a subtle animated sheen — a horizontal light sweep that crosses the button face once, then holds. This is not a hover state and it does not loop rapidly. It runs once on load over 800ms, establishing that the primary action is charged and ready. The button then holds still. The interaction cue happened; it will not beg to be noticed again.

## Section Transitions

The section color transitions (deep green → light gray → dark green gradient → light gray → deep green footer) occur at precise scroll boundaries with no blending. The voltage aesthetic contributes a directional charge to these transitions: rather than a pure hard cut, each section enters with the pacing of a circuit completing — a wipe or reveal that moves across the viewport in 150–200ms before the section is fully present. The motion is fast and purposeful. It does not linger. A section has arrived; the system has moved to the next state.

## Content Rhythm

Individual content elements use the primary stagger-fade-up pattern (400ms, ease-out, 80ms stagger between cards). The voltage layer's contribution appears most distinctly at data elements: when skill percentage bars, graph connections, and roadmap step nodes enter the viewport, they animate with electrical precision rather than standard progressive reveals. Graph nodes connect with fast draw-in (100–120ms per connection, sequential, no stagger — the graph builds like a circuit being laid rather than content being revealed). Percentage bars fill in one continuous motion rather than easing up gradually. The effect is computational: this system is calculating, not performing.

## Footer Approach

The footer arrives as a hard cut to deep forest green — the same environment as the hero. The voltage aesthetic adds one closing gesture: as the footer enters the viewport, the large wordmark in the footer space has a brief illumination — a fast, single-pass brightness sweep, 300ms, held to the background color so it reads as the field briefly charging up around the mark. It is a closing signal, not a decoration. The platform's name lights up once, then holds. The bracket closes with current.

## Motion Principles

The motion philosophy is deliberate, systematic, and charged. Standard durations: 400–600ms (content blocks), 100–120ms (data element draw-ins), 150–200ms (section wipes). Easing: deceleration for content reveals, linear for data/graph animations (precision, not organic). One-time charged signals for primary interactive elements (CTA button sheen on load, wordmark in footer). No loops on UI elements — loops belong to ambient video only. No spring physics, no bounce. The voltage energy is expressed through speed precision and directionality, not through oscillation or excitement.

## Video Placements

Two sections of this site would be meaningfully served by ambient looping video:

**1. Hero Background — Live infrastructure ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `hero-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A slow visualization of a talent network forming and reforming — nodes appearing, connections drawing in, graph reorganizing — all rendered in deep forest green (#003620) with white and medium green (#00693e) node labels and edges; 10s loop, slow enough to read as ambient rather than active; the motion should feel like a system operating at baseline load, not a demonstration
- Duration: 10s loop
- Why video: The voltage aesthetic adds the felt quality that the green field is live infrastructure, not passive background. An ambient network-formation loop — in the primary system's color world — would make that quality literal and visible without making it dramatic. The viewer is inside an operating system. The motion communicates that the platform is always running, always verifying.

**2. Product Feature Section — Real-time system ambient loop**
- Suggested file: `product-eval-ambient.mp4`
- Subject: A screen-captured loop of the evaluation dashboard in motion — skill graph nodes connecting with electrical speed, percentage bars filling in one fast sweep, evidence sources populating in rapid succession; 8s loop, shot at a slight angle in soft studio light; the pacing should feel like watching a computation complete, not watching a slideshow play
- Duration: 8s loop
- Why video: The product feature sections carry the "proof over promise" principle into static UI mockups. The voltage layer's contribution is speed and precision: where the primary system's dashboard would animate slowly and deliberately (to emphasize completeness), the voltage version animates with electrical speed (to emphasize capability and real-time performance). The same evidence arrives — it just arrives faster, with the confidence of a system that knows what it is doing.
