# Brand Name — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately 7–8 distinct full-viewport sections, each functioning as a discrete visual chapter. The rhythm is cinematic — one scene transitions into the next, each commanding the full screen height. There's no conventional "content column"; instead, the layout alternates between editorial grid compositions, full-bleed atmospheric photography, and typographic statement sections. A persistent email signup modal overlays the entire scroll journey.

---

## Header / Navigation

The navigation is minimal and deliberately sparse — a single horizontal bar pinned to the top of the viewport. Left side carries three text links (Shop, About, Sourcing) in small, widely-tracked uppercase or small-caps. The right side mirrors this with Contact and Cart. The wordmark/logo appears centered in the header only as the user scrolls deeper — on the hero it's absent or transparent-merged into the background. The nav has no visible background fill; it floats directly over the content beneath it, creating a transparent overlay effect. Extremely low visual weight — the entire nav is subordinate to the imagery.

---

## Hero Section

Full-bleed, full-viewport atmospheric photography of the product — dark, almost black, filling the entire screen edge-to-edge. The wordmark is set in a very large display typeface, centered horizontally at mid-screen, rendered in a low-opacity or ghosted treatment so the photography bleeds through the letterforms — the type and image are optically merged, not layered. Below this sits a short descriptor line in small text. At the bottom-left corner, a secondary CTA ("Primary CTA →") anchors in small uppercase with an arrow. A typographic nudge appears bottom-right. The hero is pure atmosphere — there are no boxes, no cards, no UI chrome. The spatial hierarchy is: image fills everything, type is overlaid at center, utility text anchors the corners.

---

## Content Sections Below the Hero

### Shop Grid Section
A three-column editorial grid that bleeds edge-to-edge. Each column is a full-height image panel — roughly equal width (~33% each), no visible gutters. The left column shows a product on dark background. The center column is framed differently — contains an origin or category badge (circular pill) centered at the top, followed by the product name and a short descriptor below. The right column shows lifestyle photography. This is an asymmetric editorial grid where the center column acts as the content/type anchor flanked by pure photographic columns.

### Product Listing / Variant Navigation
Continues the three-column structure. Center column shifts to show product variant labels and pricing. The side columns remain full-bleed lifestyle photography. As the user scrolls, the center column content cycles through different product information while the flanking images update — creating a vertically paginated product showcase within a fixed-column scaffold.

### Marquee / Statement Typography Section
Full-bleed dark section. A massive display headline occupies most of the left two-thirds. The type is enormous, spanning nearly the full section height, rendered in two weights (bold serif for the primary line, italic script for the secondary line). To the right, a circular badge/stamp graphic overlaps the text. This section is pure typographic statement with no image content — maximum negative space, maximum visual weight on type.

### About / Editorial Split Section
Two-column layout: left column is a narrow text block (roughly 40% width) with body copy and a text CTA link ("Secondary CTA →"). Right column is full-bleed photography of a flagship product. The columns sit side-by-side with no gutter visible — image bleeds to the right viewport edge. A strong asymmetric split — text column feels compressed and editorial against the expansive photography.

### Brand Statement Section
Full-bleed, full-viewport section. The left ~40% is dominated by an oversized headline in a heavy condensed display typeface set very large and low on the left half of the frame. The right half is dark atmospheric photography. This mirrors the previous split but reverses the visual weight — type dominates left, image dominates right.

### Mailing List / Join Section
The page transitions into a darker section where right-side imagery bleeds in (lifestyle photography) and a secondary email opt-in appears — "Action Button" as a small header, a sentence of copy, and an email input field with an arrow submit button. This lives in the right ~40% of the section. Left side continues the brand statement text from above.

## Footer

Four-column layout at the bottom. The columns appear to be: Brand/copyright (left, with a short brand descriptor paragraph), Navigation (primary collection links), Shop (product categories — Product 1, Product 2, Product 3), and Social/Credits (social link, Credits). Column widths feel roughly equal or slightly left-weighted. Above the footer columns, there is a large circular macro photography image filling the right ~40% of the viewport — the footer content lives in the remaining left-center space. Copyright year sits bottom-left in small type.

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Full-bleed imagery as structural columns** — photography doesn't sit inside containers; it *is* the container. Images expand edge-to-edge without padding or border radius.

**Overlapping ghost type on photography** — the hero wordmark is intentionally semi-transparent, merging with the image layer beneath. Type and image share the same visual plane rather than stacking independently.

**Persistent modal overlay** — an email signup modal (split-panel: left image, right form) floats over every scroll position across the entire page. It has its own two-column internal layout — left ~40% is a lifestyle photograph, right ~60% is the form with a dark CTA button. This modal has a secondary close button conflict: there appears to be both a native modal close (×) and what looks like a background modal behind it, creating a visual layering anomaly.

**Vertical scroll as product navigation** — the center column of the product grid functions as a content scroller while outer columns update, creating a parallax-like product browsing rhythm without sidebar navigation.

**Typographic sections as full sections** — several sections contain zero imagery and treat type as the primary visual element at display scale, using scale and negative space as the layout tool.

**Corner-anchored micro-typography** — CTAs and utility labels are pushed to the extreme corners of sections (bottom-left, bottom-right), never centered, preserving the atmospheric quality of the main visual field.
