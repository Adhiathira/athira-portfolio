# Sakari Sake — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

A premium Japanese beverage brand site with an age gate at entry. The design positions its subject through a tension between austere, disciplined restraint in its component system and expressive, organic motion in its structural transitions. The primary visual signature is a large-amplitude asymmetric sinusoidal wave deployed as the boundary between sections — a shape language that contradicts every other element's strict rectilinearity and announces the brand's aesthetic philosophy as the interplay between precision and organic form. The photography is product-first: the bottle as a sculptural object against carefully controlled studio backgrounds.

## Color Architecture

The palette operates through contrast inversion rather than chromatic variety. The primary surface is a cool light gray (`#f0f0f0`) — not warm white, but a neutral slightly-cool off-white that reads as clean without clinical harshness. Body text and headings use near-black (`#0a0a0a` and `#000000`), with no warm or chromatic variation. Link and secondary text use a medium gray (`#969696`) — subdued and functional. The structural drama comes from section-level alternation: content sections default to the light gray page background; editorial narrative sections invert entirely to a dark charcoal or near-black background with light text. This light-dark oscillation is the color system's mechanism — not accent colors, not gradient fills, just the hard binary alternation of the two registers.

## Typography System

Two typefaces, strict role division. Mirador (SemiBold and BookItalic, proprietary) is the editorial display face: h1 at 40px with tight `-0.09px` tracking, h2 at 28px, h4 at 28px — a proportionally modest scale compared to many premium sites, relying on the typeface's inherent elegance rather than sheer size. The BookItalic weight introduces a secondary tone: editorial captions or ornamental flourishes. Roboto (Regular, Bold, Medium — 400/700/500) serves all functional and body text at 16px, with no distinctive tracking. The serif-editorial / sans-functional split is classical and deliberately unobtrusive — the design does not call attention to its typographic architecture.

## Spatial System

The page organizes through several distinct zones with the sinusoidal wave as the boundary mechanism rather than straight horizontal edges. Introductory age-gate modal: full-screen overlay. Hero: full-viewport dark or textured background with floating animated decorative leaf elements at multiple depth layers, central product photography, and typographic headline. Sinusoidal wave divider: the section-boundary event — a large asymmetric wave curve (~20–25% viewport height amplitude) rendered as a shape mask or SVG path at the base of the hero; as the user scrolls, this wave translates upward, creating the appearance of a liquid surface boundary between sections rather than a cut. Editorial narrative sections: dark background, centered single-column text, small animated decorative graphic above the text block. Full-width video sections: edge-to-edge background video (black-and-white historical footage; color contemporary footage) serving as atmospheric chapter breaks. Product showcase sections: repeatable asymmetric composition — product photography one side, decorative circular graphic clip and floating animated leaf elements on the other. Footer: return to light register.

## Photography and Editorial Voice

Photography is studio-controlled and product-centric: the primary subject is the bottle as a sculptural object, presented against controlled studio backgrounds with deliberate lighting. Circular and elliptical crop shapes appear in product showcase sections as decorative containers for secondary photography — breaking the strict rectangular grid at a local scale while the overall layout remains rectilinear. The leaf motif — stylized botanical illustration rendered as an animated floating element — appears throughout hero and product sections as a recurring visual thread. All image containers and UI elements use sharp 90° rectangular corners; the circular clips are deliberate decorative exceptions, not a systemic rounding approach.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation is compositionally minimal: logo aligned left, hamburger menu icon right, nothing else visible in the collapsed state. The nav is transparent throughout — no background fill at rest, no visual treatment on scroll. The icon colors adapt to section register: dark icons over the light content sections, light icons over the dark narrative sections. This inversion-responsive behavior is the nav's only adaptive logic; there is no scroll-opacity transition or sticky solidification. The hamburger-only visible state withholds the nav's full structure from view, consistent with the site's general aesthetic of revealed-on-demand information.

## Overall Design Character

The site articulates a philosophy of disciplined form broken at the boundaries. Within sections, the design is strictly rectilinear — no rounded corners on cards, buttons, or containers — and typographically restrained. Between sections, the sinusoidal wave boundary contradicts this discipline with an organic, liquid geometry. The result is a site that feels simultaneously precise and sensory: the product's identity (a refined craft beverage with organic, natural origins) is communicated not through decorative motifs but through the architectural decision to make the section transitions themselves organic. The floating leaf elements reinforce this: they are not background decoration but foreground participants in the composition.
