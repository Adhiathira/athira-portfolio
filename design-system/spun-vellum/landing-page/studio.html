The workshop at 14 Grassmarket, Edinburgh.

A workshop, not a factory.

The building at 14 Grassmarket has housed Kesslar & Vane since the firm's founding in 1887. The ground floor is the main workshop: lathes, milling machines, the grinding room. The first floor is the office, the drawing room, and the archive. The basement is where instruments await calibration.

We have never had more than fourteen people working in the building at one time. The maximum has been reached twice — once in 1942, when the workshop was contracted for wartime optical instruments, and once in 2018, when we were simultaneously building six commissions.

The workshop is not open to visitors. Precision manufacture requires stillness, controlled temperature, and the absence of external disturbance. We do, however, welcome clients at agreed times to see their commission at a relevant stage — typically at the start of assembly and at the start of calibration.
