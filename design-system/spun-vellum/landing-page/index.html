How We Work

Each instrument
begins as a brief.

A commission at Kesslar & Vane starts with a conversation — what the instrument must measure, where it will be used, how long it must last. The brief is handwritten. It becomes the document that travels with the instrument for its lifetime.

Every component is made in-house. Optics ground on the premises. Metal turned by hand. Assembly takes weeks. Testing takes months.
