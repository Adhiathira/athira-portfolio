# Brand Name — Layout Design Brief

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately **8–10 distinct sections** from top to bottom, following a classic editorial scroll narrative. The pacing alternates between high-density hero moments and open, breathable content sections. There's a strong sense of vertical rhythm — tight, punchy hero → generous whitespace content sections → immersive full-bleed moments → dense feature grids → editorial split layouts → closing CTA. The overall composition leans light and minimal (predominantly white background) with occasional dramatic dark full-bleed sections for contrast and emphasis.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

**Sticky, full-width navigation bar** sitting at the top of the viewport. Composed of three distinct zones:

- **Left:** Brand logotype (icon + wordmark, flush left)
- **Center:** Horizontal nav links — evenly spaced, minimal weight
- **Right:** Utility actions — text link, text link, solid pill button (strong CTA weight)

Above the main nav sits a **full-width announcement banner** — a thin colored strip pinned to the very top of the page, containing a single promotional text string centered horizontally. This creates a two-tier top bar that gives the page a crisp, structured entry point.

---

## 3. Hero Section

**Full-width, centered editorial hero** — no split columns, no image. Pure typographic statement.

- The headline is massive — occupying roughly 60% of the viewport width, centered, in a heavy sans-serif at an aggressive scale. It commands immediate attention.
- Below it: a two-line body copy paragraph at a much smaller scale, still centered, creating a clear typographic hierarchy.
- No immediate CTA buttons in the hero itself — the conversion happens in the section immediately below.

**Below the headline:** A horizontal selector row splits into a full-width **3-card selector** (partially visible: one card clipped on the left, two options visible). This card row uses rounded-corner cards with equal width, each containing a label, short description, and a preview image (product UI screenshot). The leftmost card appears cut off, implying horizontal scroll or a carousel pattern.

The cards float over a **gradient background** — a soft gradient that fills the upper portion of the page, transitioning smoothly into white as the page scrolls.

---

## 4. Content Sections (Below Hero)

### Section: Product UI Immersion (Full-bleed)
A **cinematic full-width image** — a screenshot of the product interface embedded inside a dark browser chrome. The image spans the entire viewport width with no gutters, creating an immersive product moment. No surrounding text visible at the top of this section.

### Section: Two-Column Benefit Statement
**Asymmetric two-column layout:** The left column holds a large, bold typographic statement at display scale. The right column (implied) contains a product UI detail shot. The columns are not equal — the text column dominates.

### Section: Three-Column Feature Cards
A **symmetric 3-up grid** — three equal-width columns, each containing:
- A full-bleed image at the top (product UI screenshots / lifestyle photography)
- A bold label ("Feature A", "Feature B", "Feature C")
- A short body paragraph
- A text link with arrow

The cards have no visible borders — they're separated purely by gutter space. Generous whitespace above and below.

### Section: Editorial Long-form (Full-bleed Highlight)
**Full-width typographic section** on white, left-aligned display text at large scale. Followed by a body paragraph and a text link. Clean, editorial, no imagery. Purely about typographic hierarchy and breathing room.

### Section: Stats + Testimonial Carousel
**Stat headline** ("[Stat] customers achieve results with Brand Name") sits in a left-aligned large typographic lockup, with carousel navigation arrows pinned to the right edge — creating a left-heavy text / right-utility tension.

Below this: A **horizontal card carousel** of customer testimonials — wide cards (roughly 1/3 viewport each), dark background with light text, containing a stat callout, pull quote, and attribution. Cards bleed slightly off the right edge of the viewport, signaling scroll availability.

### Section: Split Feature Explorer (Accordion-style)
**Two-column asymmetric layout:**
- **Left column (~40%):** A vertical list of labeled feature categories acting as navigation anchors — minimal, secondary text weight
- **Right column (~60%):** Expanded feature detail — larger body text, then a vertical list of drill-down links with arrows

This is an **interactive accordion navigation pattern** — clicking a category on the left surfaces relevant feature links on the right, with a corresponding product screenshot appearing on the far right. Three zones total: label | links | screenshot. The rightmost screenshot zone appears as a floating elevated card, subtly separated from the page grid.

### Section: Key Feature Callout (Full-bleed, gradient)
**Full-width section with gradient background**. Large display headline centered or left-aligned. This section serves as a visual palette break — color instead of white — signaling a distinct narrative chapter.

---

## 5. Footer / CTA Section

The **closing CTA section** is a **full-bleed dark section** (near-black, with a large atmospheric background photograph at low opacity beneath the text). The composition:

- **Centered headline** at display scale
- **Centered subtext paragraph** below
- **Two CTA buttons** side by side, centered: Primary CTA (solid, filled) + Secondary CTA (dark/outlined) — equal visual weight with slight primary/secondary distinction

This is a high-contrast dramatic close to the page — dark vs. the predominantly white scroll above it.

---

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

**Sticky Navigation:** The top nav remains fixed throughout the scroll, maintaining wayfinding without interrupting content flow.

**Full-bleed alternation:** The page uses full-bleed sections strategically — not constantly — as punctuation marks. They appear roughly every 3–4 scroll lengths to reset the visual rhythm.

**Cookie consent overlay:** A small, bottom-left positioned modal (rounded-corner card) floats above all content with fixed position. It doesn't block content but asserts presence through elevation.

**Carousel overflow:** The testimonial section deliberately lets cards overflow the viewport edge — a common pattern to signal "there's more here" without requiring an explicit UI affordance.

**Typographic scale as hierarchy:** The page relies almost entirely on scale contrast (not color or weight alone) to establish section hierarchy. Display text is genuinely large — pushing toward 5–7rem — while body stays intimate at 1–1.2rem.

**Spatial rhythm:** Each section maintains generous top/bottom padding (~80–120px equivalent), creating a slow, deliberate reading pace that matches the editorial brand tone.
