Hand washing is recommended and will extend the life of the glaze significantly. That said, running a piece through the dishwasher once a week is fine — use a gentle cycle and avoid high-heat dry settings.

Season with food-safe mineral oil twice yearly — apply a thin coat to the unglazed base and foot ring, let it soak for 10 minutes, then wipe away the excess. This keeps the raw clay from absorbing moisture and preserves the matte exterior finish.
