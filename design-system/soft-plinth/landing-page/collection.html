The piece that started it all.

The Round Bowl came before the studio had a name. It was thrown in a rented space on a borrowed wheel, glazed with whatever was on hand, and fired in a communal kiln. When it came out, something clicked. A form that simple — low, wide, completely without corners — turned out to be the hardest thing to get right. We've refined it 23 times since that first pull of clay. The proportions are now fixed. We won't change them.

Care Guide

These pieces last lifetimes

Hand wash only

Warm water and a gentle cloth. No dishwasher — the constant thermal cycling dulls the glaze over years. Five seconds of care preserves decades of beauty.

Microwave safe

All our glazes are lead-free and food-safe. Heat evenly in short bursts. The stoneware body holds warmth longer than glass or thin porcelain — by design.

Season with oil

Unglazed bases benefit from occasional conditioning with food-grade mineral oil. Rub in, let absorb overnight, wipe clean. The clay darkens slightly. That is correct.
