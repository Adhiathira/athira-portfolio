Why Roundwork

The promise in every curve

Three principles guide every object that leaves the workshop in San Marcos Tlapazola.

No Sharp Edges

Every surface is curved, every edge rounded to a minimum radius of 16mm. Not as a stylistic choice — as a material commitment. Clay remembers hands.

The Workshop

The workshop in San Marcos

Nestled in the valley east of Oaxaca City, San Marcos Tlapazola has been a centre of red clay pottery for centuries. The Gómez workshop — three generations of the same family — still fires from a wood-kiln built by the founders in the 1980s. Every form thrown there carries the geological memory of Oaxacan earth.

Roundwork partners exclusively with this single workshop. No outsourcing, no factory lines — only pieces thrown, trimmed, bisqued, and glazed by the same hands that shaped the studio’s aesthetic from the beginning. Clay sourced within 40 kilometres. Glazes mixed on site. Firing schedules tied to weather, season, and instinct.
