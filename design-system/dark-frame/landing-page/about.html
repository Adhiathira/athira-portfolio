About the Agency

We represent photographers,
not photographs.

Founded 2009

Fifteen years of saying no to the wrong work.

Dark Frame was founded by Elena Marchetti and James Oduya in 2009, after a decade working at opposite ends of the same industry — she as a photo editor at three major publications, he as a photographer represented by an agency that did not understand his work.

Their shared frustration was simple: too much photography was being licensed without context, used without understanding, and paid for without respect. The archive was the asset; the photographer was an afterthought.

Dark Frame was built on the opposite premise. We represent the photographer first. The archive is a consequence of their practice — carefully maintained, precisely licensed, never cheapened.

How We Work

Three principles. Not negotiable.

01 — Integrity

No licensing without understanding.

We read every brief before recommending a photographer. We don't sell images; we match intent to practice.

02 — Precision

Clean contracts. Complete documentation.

Every license includes clear territorial limits, usage windows, and edition controls. No ambiguity, no surprises.

03 — Restraint

We accept two photographers per year.

Quality of representation declines with scale. We grow only when we can maintain the standard we've built.
