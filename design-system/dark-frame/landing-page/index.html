Our Practice

Photography as a primary language, not a supporting medium.

Dark Frame was founded on the belief that photographic images carry arguments that words cannot. We represent photographers who understand this — artists whose work has appeared in Vogue, The New York Times Magazine, and the permanent collections of major museums.

Our licensing division manages one of the most closely curated archives in the industry: 14,000 images across 15 years, available exclusively through Dark Frame.
