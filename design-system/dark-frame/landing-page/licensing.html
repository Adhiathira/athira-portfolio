How It Works

One conversation. One quote. One agreement.

We don't use pricing databases or automated rate calculators. Every license is quoted by a human who has read your brief, understood your intended use, and confirmed that the photographer approves.

Our standard turnaround is 48 hours for editorial licenses and 5 business days for advertising and commercial use. Archive requests may take longer depending on research requirements.

All licenses include full provenance documentation, credit line requirements, and territorial boundaries. We do not issue licenses that we cannot defend.

The Process

Four steps from brief to license.

  1. 01

    Submit your brief

    Use the contact form or email us directly. Include intended use, territory, duration, and any preferred photographers or subject matter. The more specific the brief, the faster we can respond.

  2. 02

    We send a curated selection

    Within 48 hours for editorial requests, we return a selection of images with preliminary usage terms. No automated database — a human made this selection against your brief.

  3. 03

    Agreement and payment

    We issue a license agreement with full usage terms. Payment is required before high-resolution delivery. We accept bank transfer and major credit cards.

  4. 04

    High-resolution delivery

    Files delivered via secure transfer with full metadata, provenance documentation, and credit line requirements. We remain available for the duration of the license for any usage questions.
