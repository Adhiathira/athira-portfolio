# Dark Frame — Visual Concept

## Photography-Forward Editorial Design System

**Dark Frame** is built on a single principle: the photograph is the primary communication. Every typographic and layout decision exists to serve the image, not compete with it.

## Visual Language

**Full-bleed imagery as architecture.** Photos are not decorative elements placed inside a layout — they are the layout. The page is a sequence of images interrupted by restrained text, not a page of text illustrated by images.

**Dark substrate between images.** Where photographs end, near-black (#0a0a0a) continues the visual field. The eye moves from image to image without jarring white-page interruptions. The darkness is the pause between frames on a contact sheet.

**Overlay as controlled legibility.** Text on images is never simply set on top. A dark overlay (60% opacity) provides a legible surface. On hover, the overlay reduces to 20% — the photo brightens, the image rewards attention.

**Gold as the prestige signal.** A single accent color — warm gold (#c9a96e) — carries all editorial weight. It appears on hover states, active navigation, decorative numerals, quote marks, and service numbers. It is used exactly where a master printer would place a registration mark: sparingly, precisely, intentionally.

**Type as caption and wayfinding.** DM Serif Display carries the editorial serif identity — its weight and elegance signal a serious photography institution. DM Sans at weight 300 handles all supporting text with deliberate lightness. Type never overpowers. Headlines are large but serve as orientation, not spectacle.

**Museum-level whitespace.** 120px section padding. 60px container margins. The breathing room of a gallery opening. Images are never crowded.

**Letterbox reveal.** When images enter the viewport, they emerge from a cinematic crop (inset 20%) expanding to full frame. This is the frame opening — a photographic gesture.

**Rectangular geometry.** Zero border-radius. No rounded corners. Photography is rectangular. Frames are rectangular. The grid is strict.

## Color Register

Dark — near-black backgrounds, near-white text, warm gold as the single accent. This is a night studio lit by a single warm lamp.

## Typographic Hierarchy

The display serif (DM Serif Display) is reserved for titles, quotes, and decorative numerals. It is never used for navigation or captions. DM Sans light carries the rest: subordinate, invisible, functional.
