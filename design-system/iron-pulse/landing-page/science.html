01 — Core Technology

Holographic diffraction gratings. Made here.

A diffraction grating separates light by wavelength through interference. The performance of any spectrometer is limited by the quality of its grating — the ruling density, the groove profile, and the surface uniformity. Prismatica designs and manufactures all gratings in our Geneva facility.

Our holographic gratings are produced by interfering two coherent laser beams across a photoresist-coated substrate. Ion-beam etching transfers the sinusoidal groove pattern to the glass with ruling densities up to 3600 grooves per millimeter and an absolute accuracy of 0.3 nm.

Blaze optimization using ion-beam ion figuring allows us to concentrate diffraction efficiency into a single order — achieving 92% efficiency at the blaze wavelength. No production grating from any other manufacturer has exceeded this figure on a commercial instrument.

Max ruling density 3600 gr/mm
Blaze efficiency 92%
Ruling accuracy ±0.3 nm
Substrate material Zerodur / UV-fused silica

03 — History

Founded by physicists.
Obsessed with optics.

Prismatica was founded in 1983 by Dr. Ingrid Solberg and Dr. Yusuf Karimov, both physicists from the European Synchrotron Radiation Facility in Grenoble. Their research into undulator beamline instrumentation had revealed a fundamental limit in commercially available diffraction gratings — and they saw a path to exceed it.

The first instrument, the Axiom-1, was built in a converted watchmaking workshop in Geneva's Carouge district. It shipped to three university customers in 1985 at a resolution figure double that of any competing system. Forty years later, nothing has changed about our ambition.
