Our Science

Where quantum optics
meets industrial precision.

Every Prismatica instrument is built around holographic diffraction gratings manufactured in-house to sub-nanometer tolerances. Our blazed grating technology achieves diffraction efficiencies exceeding 92% — a figure that defines what is measurable at the limits of optical physics.

Founded in 1983 by physicists from the European Synchrotron Radiation Facility, Prismatica has spent four decades at the intersection of fundamental research and precision engineering. Our instruments have contributed to seven Nobel Prize-winning research programs.

How We Build

Four stages from
photon to data.

01

Optical Design

Grating geometry, slit geometry, and detector geometry are co-optimized in ray-trace simulation before any glass is cut.

02

Grating Fabrication

Holographic exposure and ion-beam etching produce master gratings at 2400 grooves/mm with 0.3 nm absolute ruling accuracy.

03

Mechanical Integration

Invar-bodied housings machined to 2 μm tolerance. Thermal compensation is passive, requiring no active cooling in the 15–35°C operational range.

04

Calibration & Certification

Each instrument is wavelength-certified against NIST-traceable atomic emission lamps and ships with a full spectral response curve.
