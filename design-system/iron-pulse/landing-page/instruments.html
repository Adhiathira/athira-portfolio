PI-AX9

Axiom-9

UV-VIS Spectrometer

High-resolution dual-beam spectrophotometry for quantitative analysis across the full UV and visible range. The Axiom-9 sets the bench-top performance standard with a crossed Czerny-Turner optical design and temperature-stabilized CCD detector.

Range
190–1100 nm
Resolution
0.03 nm FWHM
Grating
1200 gr/mm holographic
Detector
2048-px back-illuminated CCD

PI-LR

Lattice-R

Portable Raman Analyzer

Field-deployable Raman spectroscopy for materials identification, gemological analysis, and pharmaceutical quality control. Battery-powered, IP65-rated, and calibrated against a neon reference lamp for wavelength stability without laboratory infrastructure.

Excitation
532 nm, 785 nm (switchable)
Raman shift
100–3200 cm⁻¹
Integration
1 ms – 60 s
Protection
IP65, MIL-STD-810G

PI-PX

Prism-X

FTIR Spectrometer

Fourier-transform infrared spectrometer optimized for polymer characterization, surface chemistry, and atmospheric trace-gas analysis. KBr beamsplitter and MCT detector deliver mid-infrared sensitivity that enables detection of sub-ppm molecular concentrations.

Range
400–4000 cm⁻¹
Resolution
0.5 cm⁻¹ max
S/N ratio
55,000:1 (peak-to-peak)
Beamsplitter
KBr broadband

PI-EA

Eclipse Array

Atmospheric Multi-Channel System

Multi-axis differential optical absorption spectroscopy system for real-time atmospheric column measurements. The Eclipse Array operates as a distributed sensor network — up to 32 telescopes simultaneously measuring trace gas profiles during twilight, zenith, and off-axis geometries.

Channels
Up to 32 simultaneous
Species
O₃, NO₂, SO₂, HCHO, BrO
Operation
−40°C to +60°C
Connectivity
4G/LTE + satellite fallback
