## Visual Identity
The visual identity leans towards understated luxury, using serif type for body copy and a proprietary sans-serif typeface for headlines. The primary color palette is based on deep forest green and warm off-white, with warm gold/amber as an accent, creating a subtle sense of richness. Subtle animations add further polish.

## Hero Section
The hero section showcases a full-viewport video in the background, creating a dynamic entry point. Centered white headline text in a large sans-serif typeface contrasts with a single white CTA "LOG IN & ORDER" underlined in warm gold and is positioned below the headline. The hero occupies the full viewport height, obscuring elements below.

## Content Sections
Content sections employ a variety of layouts, including 3-column grids, asymmetrical editorial columns, and full-width alternating sections. Whitespace is used generously to create an airy feel. Imagery includes product photography and editorial imagery, often edge-to-edge. Headings are set in solid blocky green and body copy appears in a classic serif font in dark charcoal.

## Footer Section
The footer is designed as a dark green section with four columns of links. Type size and weight are reduced compared to the body, and labels appear in small white sans-serif. A centered monogram logo, wordmark, and social icon row are included, and a copyright line is aligned to the bottom right.

## Design Principles
*   Balance of proprietary sans-serif and serif typefaces contributes to the site's established feel.
*   Consistent use of forest green and off-white creates a sense of brand unity.
*   Generous whitespace emphasizes individual elements.
*   Subtle and restrained animations add polish without being distracting.
*   Clean grid structure supports readability and scannability.

## Distinctive Qualities

*   The prominent use of a proprietary sans-serif font, though difficult to reproduce on the web, reinforces brand identity.
*   The choice of a dark green for the footer and header grounds the design and stands out from the lighter main content area.
* The Gazatte mascot adds a unique illustrated counterpoint to the design.
