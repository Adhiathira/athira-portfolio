# Opaline Draft — Concept by Image

## Brand Premise

This design system operates as a precision instrument for digital products whose interface is itself an argument: that visual clarity and typographic confidence are the tools of persuasion. The site does not perform busyness — it presents a structured catalog of evidence. Distinct section patterns, each with a discrete role, move the user from typographic opening to product showcase to social proof to conversion, building a case through accumulation rather than spectacle. The underlying proposition is that expertise speaks through organization.

## Color Architecture

The palette is a restrained, luminous system: near-white (#fbfbfd) as the primary ground, near-black (#1d1d1f) as the structural anchor, medium grey (#6e6e73) for descriptive text, and a Siri-inspired gradient range — pink (#ea3a8c), purple (#bf5af2), indigo (#5e5ce6), cyan (#32ade6) — held in reserve as an accent vocabulary for gradient wordmarks and highlight moments. The near-white ground is not stark. It has an opaline quality — a pale warmth that avoids the clinical flatness of pure white while retaining full legibility. Chromatic expression is concentrated at the headline level: gradient text passages function as the palette's chromatic statement, earning their saturation through scarcity. Elsewhere the system is achromatic by design.

## Typography System

The type system is assertive and hierarchically legible. Space Grotesk provides the full range from display headline to functional body text. At intensity 3, the heading scale has been enlarged by 15% — h1 now lands at 74.75px, h2 at 44.85px — and letter-spacing of 0.06em has been injected for all heading roles. The effect is a typeface that occupies more horizontal ground, each letter given more room to read as an individual character rather than flowing into a word-stream. This is typography treated as precision machinery: components with tolerances. Body text operates at a comfortable 19.5px with generous 35.1px line height, producing an editorial reading rhythm for long-form descriptive sections. The system's authority comes from a single family applied with structured variation across vastly different scales.

## Spatial System

The spatial logic is container-based and deliberately generous. The grid holds at 1530px maximum width, with column gaps scaled 40% wider than the source — 56px between header columns, 50.4px in the footer grid. Section horizontal padding has been opened from 54px to 86px, providing an additional 32px of air on each side of every section. This is not decorative — it is editorial: each content region is given enough room to read as a complete composition before the eye moves on. The gap increases create more pronounced negative space in the grid, making the system feel constructed from considered intervals rather than packed placement.

## Motion Character

The motion system has been given a symmetrical breathing quality. The primary easing has been swapped from the standard material cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1) — which decelerates sharply — to ease-in-out, which mirrors acceleration and deceleration. Combined with snappier hover interactions (0.35s, trimmed from 0.45s), the result is motion that feels responsive without aggression. Elements enter and leave with the same care. The parallax scroll patterns and scroll-driven tab switching from the griting-opux source add dimensional depth to longer pages without requiring scroll hijacking or complex GSAP orchestration.

## Navigation and Wayfinding

The navigation bar is transparent from load, transitioning to a frosted glass surface (rgba(251,251,253,0.85) with 20px backdrop blur) on scroll. Nav links carry a letter spacing of 0.08em — a small but perceptible precision detail that distinguishes navigation labels from body text and gives the header a measured, spaced cadence. The nav height at 48px (44px scrolled) maintains a slender profile consistent with the editorial restraint of the spacing system. The transparent-to-frosted transition allows the white page ground to show through on scroll, maintaining tonal continuity rather than introducing an opaque visual break.

## Overall Design Character

This is a light-register digital product system built around precision and restraint. Its chromatic richness is earned — gradient accents appear at moments of brand identity, nowhere else. Its structural decisions are editorial: wider gaps, taller heading sizes with tracked letter spacing, section margins that give each content unit space to resolve before the next begins. The motion is symmetrical and calm. The interactions are crisp without aggression. The whole system reads as instruments on a white surface — no decoration, no ambient noise, every element earning its position.
