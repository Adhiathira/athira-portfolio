The Forme method

Specification structured at entry, not at export.

Most tools are organized around producing a document. Forme is organized around maintaining a record. The distinction determines every design decision we've made.

Why it matters

The problem isn't the drawing. It's what happens between drawings.

Engineering specifications fail not because they're wrong, but because they're disconnected. The tolerance is set on Tuesday, the material is confirmed on Thursday, the surface finish is changed by email on Friday, and none of those decisions are linked in a single record. By the time the part reaches the supplier, the specification is a document that describes a moment that no longer exists.

A specification is not a document. It is a record of decisions — and every decision has a context, a time, and an author.

Forme treats the specification as a structured record that evolves. Every field entry has an author, a timestamp, and a revision ancestry. When a tolerance changes, the previous value is not overwritten — it is branched. The record knows its own history because it was designed to accumulate decisions, not replace them.

This isn't a technical constraint. It's a design philosophy: precision work requires a precise record. Forme is built for engineers who understand that the measurement is only as reliable as the system that contains it.

Design principles

Six decisions that define how Forme is built.

01

Structure at entry, not at export

Every value is typed and linked when it is created. Export is a view, not a transformation.

02

Revision is ancestry, not replacement

Previous values are preserved, not overwritten. The record knows where every value came from.

03

Every decision has an author

Tolerances, material changes, and surface call-outs are attributed. Accountability is structural, not procedural.

04

Geometry governs, not the other way around

Specifications attach to geometry references — faces, edges, features. Not to rows in a table.

05

Supplier views are filtered, not redacted

The supplier sees what their process requires. Nothing more. The filter is structural — not a PDF with white boxes.

06

Precision is a property of the system, not the engineer

A specification tool that can hold imprecise values is not a specification tool. Forme enforces types.

About Forme
