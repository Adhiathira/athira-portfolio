Common questions

What engineers ask before committing.

What counts as a "part" in the free trial?

One specification record per part number. A record can have unlimited revisions, unlimited fields, and multiple geometry references. Three records means three distinct part numbers.

Can I import from our existing CAD system?

STEP import is available on Studio and Program plans. We read dimensional features from STEP files and populate nominal values automatically. Other CAD formats are on the roadmap — contact us if you need something specific.

What does the supplier view expose?

Only the call-outs relevant to the supplier's process scope. Machining suppliers see dimensional tolerances and surface finishes. Finishing suppliers see coating specifications and surface requirements. Internal revision history, material costing data, and design intent notes are never included.

Does Forme work offline?

The web application requires an internet connection. The API is available on the Program plan for integration into local build systems. Offline desktop clients are on the enterprise roadmap.

How is data stored? Where?

Specification records are stored encrypted at rest in ISO 27001-certified infrastructure. US and EU regions available. Enterprise customers can specify data residency requirements. We never share specification data with third parties.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes, on monthly plans. Annual plans are cancellable with 30-day notice. All your data exports in full before cancellation — specification records in JSON and PDF, revision history included.
