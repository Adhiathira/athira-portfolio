# Brand Name — Layout System Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page is composed of roughly **9–10 distinct sections**, each defined by a strong background color shift rather than borders or card containers. The rhythm is deliberately chromatic — each section announces itself with a flat color fill (dark background, accent, light panel, black, secondary accent, muted tone, neutral), making color the primary structural separator. There is no sidebar; everything is full-width, but most sections split horizontally into a precise **50/50 two-column system** that runs almost the entire page length. This is the single most defining layout rule on the site.

---

## Header / Navigation

The navigation is **minimal and edge-anchored**. The wordmark sits flush to the top-left in bold lowercase. A small stacked-bars icon (hamburger or app icon) floats to the top-right. There is no full nav bar — no visible links, no background fill, no underline. The nav floats transparently over the hero background, creating almost zero visual weight. This is a "ghost nav" pattern — the brand name is the only anchor.

---

## Hero Section

The hero is a **strict vertical split**: left half accent color, right half near-black. These two panels are equal width and fill the full viewport height.

- **Left panel:** Contains the wordmark at top-left, large bold headline at mid-left ("Primary CTA"), and a small body text block at the bottom-left. Typography is stacked vertically with generous breathing room.
- **Right panel:** Houses the signature UI element — **6–7 horizontal color bars**, each containing a floating dark square "key cap" with a single character. The key caps are offset from the left edge of each bar, and alternate bars have the key cap pushed further right, creating a **staircase/cascade rhythm**. The bars are stacked with no gap between them, spanning the full height of the right panel.

This right panel is the site's most distinctive layout element — it's a **data-visualization-as-illustration** pattern, where the color palette itself becomes the hero image.

---

## Section 2 — Content Catalogue / Category Browser

Another **50/50 horizontal split**, this time on a light background.

- **Left panel:** A vertically scrollable category list. Each item is a full-width row with an icon on the left and bold label text. The selected item has a rounded-rectangle highlight fill. The list has generous row height and clear vertical rhythm — it reads like an app sidebar.
- **Right panel:** Divided into a top half and bottom half with contrasting fills, with a large illustration or mascot centered in the top area. Below that, on the lower panel, a waveform or data visualization is centered horizontally. A content label appears in a small pill at the bottom-right.

---

## Section 3 — Talent / "We Work With the Best"

**50/50 split**, with a light neutral background for the full section.

- **Left panel:** Very large, heavy headline ("We work with the best talent across the globe.") with small subtext below. The type is extremely large — this is a display-scale statement, not a body block.
- **Right panel:** A **2×2 image grid** of square or portrait photos on colored backgrounds. Each photo has a caption label below it (Role A, Role B, Role C, Role D). The grid is tight with minimal gaps. Photos are cut-out style — subjects photographed against solid color backdrops that echo the site palette.

---

## Section 4 — Distribution / "Our Content Is Already Everywhere"

**50/50 split**, dark left / light right.

- **Left panel:** Dark fill, used as a visual pause — completely empty in the visible frame, or containing minimal content below the fold.
- **Right panel:** Light gray/white. Contains a large bold statement headline. Below it, a small 2-column body text block. Below that, a **circular logo parade** — partner or platform logos rendered as colored circles, arranged in an organic cluster or grid. The circles overlap slightly, creating a playful collage effect.

---

## Section 5 — FAQ

**50/50 split**, with a different color treatment per panel.

- **Left panel:** Dark background. Vertical list of FAQ question rows — each row is a full-width text link with a subtle divider line. The active/selected question has an inverted dark fill with the question text in a light accent color. This is a classic **accordion-list pattern**.
- **Right panel:** Muted accent fill. The answer to the selected question is rendered in a very large, loose body type — not a small paragraph, but almost headline-scale body text. The panel is mostly text with a subtle "Answer" label at top.

The spatial contrast is intentional: tight list on left, expansive answer on right. Density versus breath.

---

## Section 6 — Mission / "Driven By Purpose"

**50/50 split**, light neutral background throughout.

- **Left panel:** A photo collage — a subject positioned over an abstract circular shape on a light background. The photo bleeds off the bottom edge, and there's a decorative circle element layered behind the figure, creating a **layered, scrapbook-style composition**.
- **Right panel:** Large display headline ("Driven by the desire to [key differentiator]."). Below it, a small body text block. Classic editorial-magazine two-column layout.

---

## Section 7 — Contact Form ("Send Us a Message")

**50/50 split**, accent left / light right.

- **Left panel:** Accent fill. Large headline ("Primary CTA") with a typographic accent color on one word. No other elements.
- **Right panel:** Light gray. Contains a **vertical form stack**: Name field, Email Address field, Message textarea (taller). Below the form, a checkbox with legal copy on the left and a large action-color CTA button ("Action Button") on the right. The button has full rounded corners and heavy weight — very tactile.

---

## Section 8 — Contact Details / Social ("Reach Us Anywhere")

**50/50 split**, muted left / warm neutral right.

- **Left panel:** Muted fill. "Contact us anytime" headline + contact address in large text. A two-character icon sits at the bottom-left as a visual symbol.
- **Right panel:** Warm neutral fill. "Contact us anywhere" + a row of **social platform icons** inside square border-boxes. Below that, a cursor or click icon in the lower area. Icon grid is evenly spaced, all uniform size.

---

## Section 9 — Platform CTA ("Find Us On [Platform]")

**Full-width dark panel** — breaks the 50/50 pattern.

- Left side: Platform logo at top, large "Secondary CTA" headline, platform text link at bottom-left.
- Right side: A large cut-out photo of a subject, positioned against a muted rectangle that bleeds off frame, with an accent background behind it. The figure overlaps the edge of the image area, creating a **layered overflow** effect — the photo extends beyond its container.

This section feels like a CTA hero — full-bleed, dramatic, with the figure popping out of the panel boundary.

---

## Footer

**Full-width, two-row footer** on a light neutral background.

- **Top row:** Six navigation links spread evenly across the full width. Equal spacing, bold underlined links.
- **Below that:** The wordmark rendered at **massive display scale** — the text fills the entire viewport width, cropped at the edges. This is a typographic footer-as-visual-statement, a common technique in premium brand sites. The letterforms are so large they become geometric shapes rather than text.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

| Pattern | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| **Strict 50/50 column split** | Every major section — this is the governing grid |
| **Color-as-section-divider** | No borders; background fill changes are the only section markers |
| **Staircase/cascade key-cap layout** | Hero right panel — offset characters create diagonal motion |
| **Cut-out photography on solid fills** | Talent grid, platform CTA — subjects photographed against brand colors |
| **Layered overflow figure** | Section 6 (subject photo) and platform CTA — figures bleed outside their containers |
| **Oversized typographic footer** | Brand name at display scale fills the full page width |
| **FAQ accordion with spatial contrast** | Dense list left, expansive answer right |
| **Icon parade / collage** | Partner or platform logos as overlapping colored circles |
