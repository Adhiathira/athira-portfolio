RESEARCH FOCUS

Active Research Programs

Fault-Tolerant VQE

Variational quantum eigensolver algorithms adapted for error-corrected logical qubits. Active research on resource-efficient ansatz design for molecular simulation at pharmaceutical-relevant scale (100+ electron systems).

Quantum Fourier Transform

Optimized QFT circuit decompositions for fault-tolerant execution. Current benchmark: 512-qubit QFT in under 10,000 logical gate operations, enabling practical Shor's algorithm variants for cryptanalysis research.

Quantum Simulation

Digital simulation of strongly correlated electron systems using logical qubit arrays. Demonstrated Hubbard model simulation at 64-site resolution, exceeding classical tensor network methods in circuit depth and accuracy.

Quantum Machine Learning

Quantum kernel methods and parameterized quantum circuits for classification tasks. Benchmarked against classical neural networks on structured datasets: 2.7× speedup in training convergence at 512-feature input dimension.

Combinatorial Optimization

QAOA variants for portfolio optimization, route planning, and supply chain problems. Published benchmark showing 15% improvement over best-in-class classical solvers on QUBO instances with 256 binary variables.

Open Benchmarking Suite

Publicly available circuit benchmarks for quantum volume, randomized benchmarking, cross-entropy benchmarking, and application-specific metrics. Reproducible methodology accepted by the quantum computing community as a reference standard.

RECENT PUBLICATIONS

Selected Research

2025 · NATURE

Logical Qubit Fidelity Above 99.9% at Distance-7 Surface Code

Demonstration of sustained logical qubit operation above the fault-tolerance threshold in a distance-7 surface code array. 100,352 physical qubits. Continuous operation over 72-hour window. Nature Physics, Vol. 21, pp. 847–859.

2025 · PRX

Sub-Microsecond Classical Feedback for Real-Time QEC

FPGA-based decoder achieving 700ns end-to-end latency from syndrome extraction to correction pulse output. Physical Review X Quantum, Vol. 6, 030312.

2024 · SCIENCE

Quantum Advantage in Molecular Energy Estimation

First demonstration of computational quantum advantage in a practically relevant chemistry problem: FeMoco active space simulation at chemical accuracy using 256 logical qubits. Science, Vol. 387, pp. 1204–1211.

2024 · PRL

Leakage Suppression in High-Fidelity Superconducting Qubit Arrays

Active leakage reduction protocol maintaining transmon leakage below 0.01% per gate across a 100K qubit array. Physical Review Letters, Vol. 133, 210502.
