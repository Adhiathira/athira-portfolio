# Brand Name — Layout Brief

---

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page follows a classic editorial e-commerce flow with approximately **8–9 major sections**, moving top to bottom:

1. Announcement bar
2. Navigation header
3. Hero (split-screen)
4. Category grid
5. Editorial duo (two full-width editorial panels)
6. Brand statement / interstitial
7. Featured product carousel ("Primary Collection")
8. Editorial trio (three equal editorial tiles)
9. "Our Favorites" product carousel
10. Footer

The overall rhythm alternates between **tight product grids** and **expansive editorial moments** — creating a breathing pattern that feels both commercial and brand-forward. The page has generous vertical whitespace between sections, giving it a premium, unhurried pace.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

The nav sits as a **fixed or sticky horizontal bar** at mid-scroll (visible floating over content in several screenshots), consisting of:

- **Left:** Brand wordmark logotype in a bold, spaced typeface
- **Center:** Three primary nav items — Category A, Category B, Category C — evenly spaced
- **Right:** Utility icons — Search, Service, My Account, Cart (with badge)

The nav is **light/white background**, creating a clean contrast strip against both the content below and the hero. It uses generous horizontal padding and sits at a low height — understated and functional. The overall weight is balanced: logo anchors left, commerce utilities anchor right, navigation floats center.

---

## 3. Hero Section

The hero is a **two-column split layout**, roughly **70/30** proportions:

- **Left column (~70%):** A large, almost full-height product photograph on a warm, natural surface. This image bleeds to the top and left edges of the viewport, creating a full-bleed left panel.
- **Right column (~30%):** Clean white background with vertically centered text copy. The copy is minimal: a small descriptor line, a large bold headline (with a small square bullet accent `■`), and a subtle underlined CTA ("Primary CTA").

The split feels asymmetric and editorial — not a traditional centered hero. The right panel acts as a **floating caption** against the dominant image. There is no overlap between the two panels; they sit flush side by side.

Above the hero: a **full-width announcement bar** in a vivid accent color — a deliberate high-contrast accent that bookmarks the very top of the page.

---

## 4. Content Sections

### Category Grid
A **4-column product grid** — equal-width columns, no gutters or minimal gutters — displaying product category thumbnails. Each cell is image-dominant with a small category label beneath. The grid fills the full page width and feels dense but orderly.

### Editorial Duo
Two **equal-width editorial panels** side by side, each taking 50% of the page width. These are full-bleed lifestyle images (one close-up, one wider environmental shot). They bleed edge-to-edge with no outer margins, creating a bold horizontal band. Minimal text overlays appear bottom-left of each panel in white.

### Brand Statement / Interstitial
A **narrow single-column centered block** — just a small square mark (`■`) centered on a neutral mid-gray background. This acts as a visual breath, a section divider with almost no content. It creates a moment of pause between editorial blocks.

### Featured Product Carousel
A standard **horizontal scrolling carousel** with:
- A section header in large bold type, left-aligned
- Arrow navigation on the far right (previous/next chevrons)
- **4 product cards visible** simultaneously, each with: product image, collection label, product name, material, original + sale price

Cards are equal width, evenly spaced. Product images are on white/neutral backgrounds — clean and catalog-like, contrasting the more dramatic editorial panels.

### Editorial Trio
**Three equal-width editorial columns**, each a full-height lifestyle image with text overlaid at the bottom. Text appears in white against the dark image areas. The three panels together form a near-full-bleed band across the page, edge to edge.

### Secondary Product Carousel
Identical structure to the Featured Product carousel above — **4 product cards**, arrow navigation, left-aligned header. This reinforces a consistent rhythm for commerce sections. Pagination arrows appear **below the carousel** as well, centered.

---

## 5. Footer

The footer uses a **4–5 column horizontal layout** on a white background with generous top padding:

- **Column 1 (far left):** "About [Brand Name]" — paragraph text describing the brand, followed by a "Read more" link. Below this, an email signup CTA with an inline input + Submit button.
- **Columns 2–3 (center):** Navigation links — structured as labeled lists (no visible headers in the screenshots, but clearly grouped)
- **Column 4:** Customer service links (Customer Service, Shipping & Delivery, Returns & Exchanges, Size Guide, Terms of Service)
- **Column 5 (far right):** "Follow us" with social links

Below the main footer columns: a **utility strip** containing payment method logos, legal links (Terms & Conditions, Privacy Statement), and **country/language selectors** on the far left.

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

### Persistent Modal Overlay
A **popup/modal** is layered over the entire page — a split-panel modal with a lifestyle photo on the left and a form on the right. It uses a semi-transparent backdrop to dim the page content behind. The modal is centered in the viewport and appears persistent through scroll, indicating it was triggered on load and not yet dismissed. The modal itself is a clean two-column card with a close `×` icon top-right.

### Full-Bleed Images
Multiple sections use images that run edge-to-edge with **zero horizontal margin** — particularly the editorial duo and trio. This creates a cinematic, poster-like quality that's central to the brand's visual identity.

### Overlapping / Layered Elements
The modal overlay demonstrates the site's willingness to use **z-axis layering** — content beneath is visible but grayed. The hero's split-column also creates a slight layered illusion where the image panel feels dominant and the text panel recedes.

### Announcement Bar as Accent
The accent-colored announcement bar is a deliberate **color interrupt** in an otherwise near-monochromatic palette (whites, grays, blacks). It draws the eye immediately and anchors the very top of the page without competing with the hero.

### Consistent Section Rhythm
The alternation of **wide editorial bands → compact grid/carousel** repeats throughout the scroll, creating a reliable visual cadence. Section spacing is generous — each block has breathing room above and below, reinforcing the premium, editorial character of the brand.

### Typography as Layout
Headlines are used structurally — large, bold type acts as a spatial anchor in sparse sections, carrying compositional weight that images carry elsewhere. The hero right column is a prime example: almost entirely white space with three short lines of type, yet it reads as visually complete.
