# Layout Brief

---

## Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately 8–10 distinct sections, scrolling vertically from a utility announcement bar at the top through hero, category grid, editorial imagery, brand statement, product carousels, editorial tiles, and a multi-column footer. The dominant rhythm alternates between full-bleed image moments and structured grid layouts, creating a breathing cadence of tension and release. A persistent modal overlay (email capture popup) floats above all content throughout the scroll — a deliberate interruption pattern used aggressively across all page states.

---

## Header / Navigation

A clean, minimal navigation bar sits at the very top beneath a slim announcement bar. The announcement bar is a single full-width horizontal band in a warm accent color, containing centered micro-copy — functionally a persistent utility strip.

The nav itself uses a classic split layout: **logo anchored left** (the brand wordmark in sparse letterforms), **primary navigation links centered** (Category 1, Category 2, Sale), and **utility icons right-aligned** (Search, Service, Account, and a circular cart counter badge). The bar is white/light, maintaining maximum contrast against the content below. Spatial rhythm is tight and precise — no wasted space, no decorative elements.

---

## Hero Section

The hero is a **split asymmetric composition**: a large full-bleed editorial photograph occupies roughly 75% of the viewport width on the left, cropped tightly to focus on a product/lifestyle detail from a low angle. On the right, a narrower vertical panel (approximately 25% width) sits in a neutral warm gray, containing the brand tagline and a text CTA ("Primary CTA" with an understated underline). The text in the right panel is vertically centered and uses a heavy editorial headline paired with a small descriptor above it. This left-heavy / right-sparse split creates strong visual weight contrast — the image does the emotive work, the text panel is the quiet editorial counterpoint.

---

## Content Sections (Below Hero)

### 1. Category Grid
A **4-column product grid** fills the full viewport width. Each cell is a square product tile with a light gray background, showing a single product on a clean ground. Product names appear as small labels directly below each image. The grid is tight with minimal gutters, giving a catalog-like density. No decorative elements — pure product focus.

### 2. Editorial Two-Column Feature
A **50/50 full-bleed split**: two large editorial photographs side-by-side, each occupying half the viewport width. Both images are dark-toned, atmospheric lifestyle photography. No text overlaid — pure visual statement, functioning as a visual palette cleanser between product grids.

### 3. Brand Statement / About Block
A **centered text block** on a mid-gray background. A small filled black square (bullet marker) anchors the top-left of the text column, serving as a typographic accent rather than functional navigation. The copy block is wide (approximately 60% of viewport) and left-aligned, using a generous type size. This section is intentionally sparse — open breathing room above and below, letting the words carry weight.

### 4. Full-Bleed Split Photography (Two Asymmetric Columns)
A **2-column asymmetric layout** where one image is portrait-oriented (taller, narrow), and the other is landscape (shorter, wider). The two images sit flush together edge-to-edge with no gap, creating compositional tension from the differing aspect ratios. Close-up product details at macro scale.

### 5. Full-Bleed Single Image with Inset Brand Statement
A **full-width macro photograph** (extreme close-up of product construction detail) serves as a visual backdrop. Below it, a **centered text block** reappears — same small black square bullet accent, with a wide body copy paragraph. The photography here bleeds completely edge to edge with no margin containment.

### 6. Horizontal Product Carousel (Editorial Tiles)
Three **equal-width editorial tiles** sit side-by-side in a horizontal row, each containing a lifestyle photograph with text overlaid at the lower left (white type on dark imagery). Each tile has a small "Read more" CTA link. This functions as an editorial/journal preview section — content tiles rather than product tiles, distinguished by the text overlay and narrative copy.

### 7. "Featured Products" Product Carousel
Section header sits left-aligned at full width. Below it, a **4-column product grid carousel** similar to the category grid but with additional metadata: product labels ("Most Wanted", "Promotion Label"), product names, material descriptors, and pricing (with strikethrough for sale items). The accent color for the promotion label is the only brand color departure from the neutral grayscale system.

### 8. "Featured Collection" Slider
A **labeled horizontal carousel** with navigation arrows (← →) positioned at the top-right. Product cards are consistent with the grid above. This section introduces a horizontal scroll pattern rather than static grid, signaled by the visible arrow controls.

### 9. Close-Up Detail Photography (3-Column Grid)
Three **equal-width macro photography tiles** fill the full width — extreme detail shots of product materials and construction. These carry no text. Functions as a material/craft storytelling section, all visual, no copy.

### 10. Full-Bleed Brand Statement (Repeat)
The brand statement pattern recurs — small black square bullet, centered copy block — this time on a dark background with light photography surrounding it, creating a dramatic tonal shift before the footer.

## Footer Layout

The footer uses a **5-column layout** across the full width on a light background:

- **Column 1 (left):** "About [Brand Name]" heading with a paragraph of brand copy and a "Read more." CTA link
- **Column 2:** Address block (physical address)
- **Column 3:** Contact block (email link, phone numbers)
- **Column 4:** Info links (Customer Service, Shipping, Returns, Care Guide, Terms, Jobs)
- **Column 5 (right):** Social links (Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, TikTok)

Below the main footer columns, a **sub-footer strip** runs full-width containing: a newsletter signup field on the left with a "Submit." text CTA, a horizontal row of payment method icons, and legal links (Terms & conditions, Privacy statement) right-aligned.

At the very bottom, a final utility strip holds locale/language selectors on the left.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Floating Modal Overlay**
The email capture modal is a persistent floating layer that appears fixed-center regardless of scroll position. It uses a **split-panel modal** design: left half is a full-bleed lifestyle photograph, right half is a white form panel with headline, body copy, email input field, and a full-width dark CTA button. The modal sits above a semi-transparent dark overlay that dims the page content behind it, creating depth separation. The close button is a minimal × mark top-right.

**Full-Bleed Imagery as Structure**
Repeatedly, full-bleed photographs function as structural section dividers rather than decorative elements. They carry no frame, no border, and bleed edge-to-edge, creating a magazine-editorial spatial rhythm.

**Consistent Typographic Accent**
A small filled black square (▪) appears multiple times as a section-opener — an unconventional but highly intentional design choice that creates visual continuity across otherwise disparate content sections. It functions as a quiet brand marker.

**Color as Signal**
The entire system is near-monochromatic (white, light grays, charcoal, black) with a single strategic accent color used exclusively for the announcement bar and promotion product badges. This restraint makes the accent color carry enormous signal weight when it appears.

**Spatial Rhythm**
The page alternates between high-density (4-column grids) and low-density (single text blocks, editorial photography) sections with remarkable consistency. This creates a deliberate breathing pattern — compress, release, compress, release — that mirrors a "less is more" brand positioning.
