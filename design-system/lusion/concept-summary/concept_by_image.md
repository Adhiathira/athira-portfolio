## Brand Overview

Lusion is a digital experience studio that partners with brands to build high-craft interactive products — the kind of work that sits at the intersection of 3D, motion, and web. Their audience is not end consumers but rather brand decision-makers and creative directors at companies looking to commission something genuinely distinctive. The site is itself the portfolio and the pitch.

## Visual Identity

The palette is restrained to the point of severity: an off-white page (`#e8e8ed`) against a near-black hero (`#0d0d0d`), with a single electric blue (`#2b2bff`) doing all the emotional lifting — present in the 3D objects but absent from the UI chrome. Aeonik carries the entire typographic system at a normal weight of 400, creating a calm, undecorated voice that lets scale alone create hierarchy (body at 16px, display at 128px with negative tracking). Motion is precise and brief — transitions cluster around 0.3–0.4s with custom cubic-bezier curves (`0.4, 0, 0.1, 1`) that feel intentionally engineered rather than default.

## Emotional Tone

Cool, unhurried, quietly confident. The site doesn't chase you — it waits. The vast off-white negative space feels like a gallery with the lights dimmed slightly, the hero image a lit vitrine. There's a controlled tension between the soft lavender-gray atmosphere and the hard black 3D objects tumbling in the dark — precision and mass coexisting. It reads as serious without being cold, premium without being decorative.

## Target Audience

Senior creative and marketing decision-makers at mid-to-large brands — CMOs, creative directors, heads of digital — with budgets for bespoke web experiences. They are visually literate, fatigued by template culture, and are specifically looking for a studio that operates at the level of craft they can feel before they read a word. Secondarily: recruiters and other studios benchmarking the top of the industry.

## Design Principles

- **Restraint as luxury** — the 12-column grid (`2vw` gap, `max(5vw, 40px)` horizontal padding) is generous to the point of extravagance; whitespace is the primary design element
- **Monochrome foundation with a single chromatic bet** — all UI is black and white; the electric blue exists only in the 3D render, making it feel earned rather than branded
- **Scale over decoration** — typography ranges from 14px utility labels to 128px display; the `h4` with `-2.56px` letter-spacing replaces any need for ornamental detail
- **Pill buttons as soft counterpoint** — the fully-rounded CTAs (`border-radius: 100px`) introduce the only soft geometry in an otherwise angular system, making them feel like physical objects
- **Motion that communicates engineering** — the custom easing curves and stacked `color, transform` transitions on nav elements signal that someone wrote these by hand, not from a library preset

## Distinctive Qualities

The sound button (`#header-right-sound-btn`) reveals that the site has an audio layer — a rare commitment that implies a fully art-directed sensory experience, not just visual design. The `LusionMono` custom typeface is a proprietary font named after the studio itself, a subtle but telling investment that says the brand extends to every typographic detail. The hero is not a video loop or a hero image — it's a real-time 3D scene, which means every visitor's view is slightly different depending on timing. The footer bottom bar with four `+` markers distributed at grid extremes is a spatial signature borrowed from technical drafting, turning a throwaway element into a brand tic.
