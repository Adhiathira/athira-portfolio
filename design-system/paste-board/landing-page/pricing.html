Questions

Frequently asked

Can I try Studio before paying?

Yes — Studio comes with a 14-day free trial. No card required until the trial ends. If you decide not to continue you stay on the Free plan automatically.

What happens to my boards if I downgrade?

Your boards stay intact and fully readable. You lose editing access to boards above the free limit until you upgrade again.

Does the Figma plugin need a paid plan?

The Figma plugin requires Studio or Team. It lets you push colour tokens and type specimens directly into your Figma file variables.

Is there an enterprise plan for larger teams?

Yes — for teams of 6 or more, contact us for custom pricing. We offer SSO, audit logs, and a dedicated account manager.

Can clients comment without signing up?

Yes. Studio and Team plans let you share boards with a comment-only link. Clients leave notes directly on the board — no Clippings account needed.
