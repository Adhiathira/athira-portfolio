Our story

Built by designers,
for designers.

Clippings started as a personal tool. We were tired of screenshot folders, Pinterest boards that looked like everyone else's, and mood decks that took longer to design than the actual brand. So we built something that works the way designers actually think — messy first, curated second, always visual.

What we believe

The Clippings values

01

Craft over convenience

The easiest path — dumping images into a folder — is not the right one. We optimise for boards that feel considered, even when you are in a hurry.

02

Personal over generic

Your reference library should look different from every other designer's. We build tools that express your specific taste, not a template version of it.

03

Context is meaning

An image without annotation is just a file. We build annotation into the product's DNA — not as an afterthought, but as the primary act.
