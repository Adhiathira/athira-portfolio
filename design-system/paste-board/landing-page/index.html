Why Clippings

Everything your references deserve

Not a folder. Not a screenshot dump. A living, annotated board that thinks like a designer.

✂

Clip from anywhere

Browser extension, mobile share sheet, drag-and-drop. If you can see it, you can clip it. Images, video, colour palettes, type specimens.

✏

Annotate in your hand

Add handwritten notes, circle details, draw arrows. Annotations render in Caveat so they feel like your actual handwriting on a printed image.

⊞

Board layouts that stick

Free-arrange on a canvas or use smart grid layouts. Cards tilt at organic angles. The board looks assembled, not generated.

◎

Extract colour tokens

Click any image and Clippings extracts the dominant palette as usable tokens — hex, HSL, and CSS custom properties ready to copy.

⇄

Share with your team

Send a live board link or export a PDF mood board with cover page. Clients can comment without needing an account.

◈

Connect to Figma

Push colour tokens and typography specimens directly into your Figma file. Design system seeded from real-world references.

The process

Three steps. One beautiful board.

01

Clip your references

Drag images from anywhere, paste URLs, or use the browser extension. Everything lands in your board in seconds.

02

Arrange and annotate

Move cards freely or snap to grid. Add handwritten notes, colour tags, and source labels directly on each piece.

03

Share and export

Send a live link, present in fullscreen, or export a polished PDF. One click to push tokens to Figma.
