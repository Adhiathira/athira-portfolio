Our Method

A Science of
Measured Lines

Calibration is not an opinion. Every instrument enters the Quill Meridian registry through a documented measurement protocol — optical linewidth analysis, cross-sectional geometry, and material condition assessment — producing a certificate that is unambiguous and reproducible.

01

Optical Measurement

Every nib and ruling edge is measured under collimated light at 40× magnification. Linewidth is recorded in five positions along the stroke path. Variance is documented alongside the mean.

02

Condition Assessment

Material condition — tip wear, slit geometry, tine symmetry — is recorded against a standardized five-point scale. Photographs accompany every condition record in the certificate package.

03

Sealed Registration

Calibration results are entered into the ledger with a cryptographic signature. The record is permanent, non-editable, and accessible to designated parties with the instrument's registration identifier.

Services

What the Registry
Provides

Quill Meridian's calibration services are organized into four disciplines. Each produces a distinct certificate type and a corresponding entry in the sealed ledger.

CALIBRATE
01

Nib Calibration

Optical linewidth measurement and tipping geometry analysis for steel, gold, and iridium nibs. Certificate includes mean linewidth, tolerance band, and condition grade.

How It Works

From Submission
to Certificate

The calibration process follows a defined protocol. No instrument leaves the registry without a complete measurement record, a sealed certificate, and a registered provenance chain.

01

Submit Your Instrument

Complete the submission form with instrument description, known provenance, and current condition assessment. A registry coordinator confirms eligibility within five business days.

02

Secure Shipping Protocol

Instruments are shipped using the registry's insured carrier arrangement — custom packing materials provided, chain-of-custody documentation generated for every transit segment.

03

Measurement and Documentation

The calibration lab conducts optical measurement, condition assessment, and cross-reference against prior records if the instrument has been registered before. Typical turnaround: 14–21 days.

04

Certificate Issued

A signed certificate and sealed ledger entry are delivered with the returned instrument. The certificate includes the measurement data, condition grade, calibrator signature, and registry identifier.
