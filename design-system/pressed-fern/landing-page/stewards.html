Stewards
Steward Network · 312 Active

The archive grows only
because stewards grow it.

Stewardship is a reciprocal agreement. The archive allocates rare seed to you. You grow it out, save seed from the best plants, and return a portion to the library each autumn. The knowledge travels in both directions.

The Steward Agreement

A reciprocal custody.

01

You receive seed

Each spring, stewards receive a parcel of 3–5 varieties selected by the archive for their region, growing experience, and the archive's current preservation priorities. We send enough for a meaningful grow-out — not a token amount.

02

You grow it out

Grow all three to five varieties in your garden or allotment. Keep the varieties separated according to isolation guidelines provided with each seed parcel. Observe and note what you find. The archive's knowledge grows from what stewards observe.

03

You save seed

Select seed from the plants that most faithfully show the variety's original character. Save from a minimum of five plants per variety to maintain genetic diversity. Process according to the cultivation guides in the Wisdom section.

04

You return a portion

Send half your saved seed back to the archive each autumn. Keep the other half for your own use and to share with your community. The returned seed, with your growing notes, refreshes the living stock and funds the next season's distributions.
