Journal Entries · 2024

Notes from the archive.

April
2024

Tomato trials: the Mortgage Lifter continues to hold its own.

The 2024 Mortgage Lifter grow-out at the Thornfield reference plot produced an exceptional example of why this variety endures. Fruit weights consistently between 450g and 680g, with the characteristic meaty interior and low seed count that define the variety's original character. We confirmed genetic stability after twenty consecutive saved-seed cycles by visual comparison with our 1994 archive specimen.

The key observation this season: Mortgage Lifter sowed two weeks later than optimal (wet April delayed planting) still produced the first ripe fruit before the Green Zebra planted on schedule. The variety's vigour overcomes timing errors. This is the kind of knowledge that does not appear in seed catalogs.

Thornfield Farm, Shropshire · P. Whitmore, Head Steward
February
2024

Champion of England pea: germination rates from six-year-old stock remain at 94%.

Six-year-old Champion of England seed stock, stored under archive conditions (35°F, silica-sealed borosilicate glass), germinated at 94% in the January test. This result — higher than most commercial sources cite for fresh pea seed — confirms our assertion that controlled-atmosphere storage radically exceeds the published longevity expectations for legumes.

For comparison, the same 2018 Dragon Tongue bean seed germinated at 78% — below our 80% return-to-archive threshold. We are initiating an emergency grow-out at three steward sites to refresh this stock before the 2025 distribution cycle.

Seedkeep Archive, Herefordshire · Archive Team
October
2023

Forellenschluss lettuce: new provenance documentation received from Vienna.

A steward in Vienna — a retired market gardener whose family has grown Forellenschluss for four generations — provided us with documentation tracing the variety to the 1640s, predating our previous earliest documentation by nearly a century. He also provided seed from his family's own line, which shows distinctly more intense freckling than our current archive stock.

We are conducting a comparative grow-out in 2024 to determine whether the two lines should be held separately as distinct regional variants, or merged. This is the kind of question that a purely commercial seed library never has to answer — but we do, because our purpose is provenance.

Vienna correspondence · H. Gruber, Contributing Steward
