Studio Method

Process

Handled once. Scanned once. Delivered without shortcuts.

Every project begins with a condition assessment. We do not accept unsleeved originals or wet-damaged film without prior discussion. Intake is logged, each frame or sheet numbered, and the batch assigned to a single operator for the full duration. Nothing enters an automated queue.

Workflow

From intake to delivery.

  1. 01

    Intake & Assessment

    Originals are received by appointment or via insured transit. Every sheet or roll is catalogued on arrival: format, emulsion type, condition notes, any visible damage. Clients receive an intake receipt before work begins.

  2. 02

    Cleaning & Preparation

    Large-format negatives are cleaned dry with a Staticmaster brush, then wet-mounted on mylar for drum work. Medium-format rolls receive ultrasonic cleaning. No originals are touched barehanded at any stage.

  3. 03

    Scanning

    The Heidelberg Tango runs at the resolution appropriate to the final use: 2000 dpi for proof, 4000–6000 dpi for exhibition, 8000–11000 dpi for archival or reproduction work. Each scan is profiled with a Macbeth ColorChecker under controlled illumination.

  4. 04

    ICC Profiling & QC

    Every file is exported as a 16-bit TIFF with embedded ICC profile. We do not apply curves, sharpening, or noise reduction unless explicitly requested. What you receive is the film — not an interpretation of it.

  5. 05

    Delivery & Return

    Files are delivered via encrypted transfer link. Originals are returned in archival sleeves by insured courier, or collected at the studio. Turnaround is 5–10 working days for standard batches; express service available by arrangement.

Equipment

The instruments we use.

Drum Scanner

Heidelberg Tango · PMT array

Photomultiplier tube drum scanner. Maximum optical resolution 11,000 dpi. Wet-mount capable. The reference standard for large-format archival work.

Flatbed Scanner

Hasselblad Flextight X5

Virtual drum flatbed for 35mm through 4×5. CCD linear array at 6300 dpi maximum. Ideal for medium-format and roll film collections.

Cine Digitizer

Wolverine 8mm / 16mm · Kino

Frame-by-frame registration pin advance. Each frame treated as an independent negative. Output: DNG sequence or ProRes 4444 with per-frame metadata.
