What We Do

The light that made the exposure deserves to survive it.

Filum operates one of Europe's few remaining drum scanners — the Heidelberg Tango — alongside a Hasselblad Flextight X5 for medium-format work. Every scan is delivered as a 16-bit TIFF with a calibrated ICC profile. We do not offer automated pipelines. Each batch is handled by a single operator from intake to delivery.

Clients include the Rijksmuseum photographic archive, the Nederlands Fotomuseum, independent fashion photographers, and private collectors with irreplaceable originals.

Services

Every format. Every emulsion.

01

Drum Scanning

Large-format negatives and transparencies up to 8×10 inch, wet-mounted on the Heidelberg Tango. Maximum optical resolution, minimum grain aliasing.

4×5 · 5×7 · 8×10
02

Flatbed & Flextight

35mm and medium-format scanning via the Hasselblad X5. Contact sheets processed in batches. Ideal for editorial archives and roll film collections.

35mm · 6×6 · 6×9 · 6×17
03

Cine & Motion Film

16mm and Super 8 frame-by-frame digitization. Each frame treated as a still negative. Delivery as DNG sequences or ProRes with per-frame ICC tagging.

16mm · Super 8 · DNG
04

Archive & Institutional

Long-form digitization projects for museums, estates, and photographic foundations. Metadata schema mapping and TIFF delivery to DAM specifications.
