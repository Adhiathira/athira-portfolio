Featured Project

Rijksmuseum Photographic Archive

Twelve months of ongoing work digitizing the Rijksmuseum's photographic collection: 4×5 glass plates, albumen prints, and early gelatin-silver negatives from the 19th and early 20th century. Each piece assessed individually, scanned at archival resolution, delivered with full XMP metadata mapped to their DAM schema.

Format 4×5 Glass Plate
Resolution 8,000 dpi
Output 16-bit TIFF
