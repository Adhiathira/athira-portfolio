The Setup

Establish your monitoring baseline.

SONOR begins every measurement session with a full-bandwidth sweep — a logarithmic sine wave from 20Hz to 22kHz — played through your main monitors at a calibrated SPL. Up to four calibrated reference microphones capture the response at your mix position simultaneously, giving the engine a spatial view of the room's behavior rather than a single-point snapshot.

From that raw capture, SONOR's acoustic model identifies modal resonances, early reflections, comb-filtering artifacts, and decay irregularities across the frequency spectrum. The resulting correction filter is generated in under 90 seconds and is immediately deployable — either as a phase-linear linear-phase FIR plugin or as an external hardware insert.

  • Full-range logarithmic sweep from 20Hz to 22kHz at user-defined SPL
  • Simultaneous multi-mic capture — up to 4 positions for spatial averaging
  • Phase-linear FIR correction — zero pre-ringing, zero transient smear
  • Sub-90 second generation from sweep capture to deployable correction filter

From sweep to session in four steps.

A process calibrated for working engineers — not acoustic researchers. From uncorrected room to trusted reference in the time it takes to brew a coffee.

01

Place

Position SONOR's reference microphone at your primary mix position — the sweet spot where critical decisions are made.

02

Sweep

SONOR plays calibrated logarithmic sweep tones from 0.8Hz to 22kHz through your monitors at reference level. The full spectrum is captured.

03

Analyze

The acoustic model is generated in real time. Problem frequencies, modal nodes, decay tails, and reflection patterns are identified and mapped.

04

Correct

A precision correction filter is applied — as a native DAW plugin insert or external hardware processor. Your room is now a trusted reference.
