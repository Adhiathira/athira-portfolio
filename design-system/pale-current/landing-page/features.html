01

Real-Time Spectrum Analysis

SONOR captures the complete frequency response of your environment across the full audible spectrum — 20 Hz to 20 kHz — with sub-millisecond temporal precision that reveals anomalies invisible to less capable analyzers. Whether you're tracing comb-filtering artifacts from parallel walls or identifying modal buildups in a small mix room, the engine identifies every irregularity in a single automated pass. Simultaneous multi-channel measurement means you see the complete acoustic picture of a room in one continuous session, with no patch-and-stitch limitations.

  • Impulse Response Capture
  • Phase Coherence Detection
  • Third-Octave Band Display

02

Adaptive Room Correction

SONOR's correction engine applies dynamic EQ filters derived directly from your room's measured impulse response — not from a generalized correction curve, but from precise acoustic data captured in your specific space. Standing wave suppression targets resonant modes at their exact frequencies, deploying minimum-phase filters that correct the problem without smearing transients or introducing pre-ringing artifacts. Every correction can be adjusted in real time and toggled with a low-latency A/B bypass, so the impact of each filter decision is immediately audible in your monitoring chain.

  • Mode Targeting
  • Filter Visualization
  • A/B Bypass
