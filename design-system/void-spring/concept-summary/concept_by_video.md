## Scroll Opening

The page opens without preamble: the viewport is already filled with deep forest green (`#09543d`) before the first frame settles, the nav materializing at the top edge as a horizontal strip — three links, centered wordmark, three links — lightweight and geometrically exact. There is no fade-in of the background field; the color is present from frame zero, establishing the site's dominant surface as a given condition rather than a reveal. The scroll cursor has not yet moved and the design is already making a claim about its tone: dense, direct, chromatic.

## Hero Experience

As the page finishes loading and Locomotive Scroll initializes, the cartoon hands enter from both horizontal edges in parallel — a peace sign drifting in from the left, a thumbs-up from the right — their motion governed by `cubic-bezier(0.17, 0.67, 0.3, 1.1)`, which gives each illustration a slight overshoot before settling, so they feel pushed into frame rather than floated. The center column loads its type in vertical sequence: the Poppins label appears first at small scale, then the three-line Anton headline stacks downward at 75px, and the final two lines resolve into their inline pink color block (`#ffa9e9` background, `#461e10` text) as a distinct second beat. The two CTAs appear below as a locked pair. The total elapsed time from page-ready to fully-composed hero is under one second — the experience is a rapid assembly, not a slow introduction.

## Section Transitions

The first scroll gesture drops the user into the warm off-white field (`#fffdf7`) abruptly: there is no crossfade, no parallax gradient between the green hero and the body. The transition is a hard cut controlled by scroll position rather than blended by opacity — the green disappears as the hero exits the viewport top, and the light field replaces it at full value. Within the content sections, cards enter on `data-scroll-offset: 300,0` thresholds: each card waits until its top edge is 300px into the viewport before triggering its reveal animation, creating a staggered cadence in which the page appears to build itself incrementally as the user descends. The dark green `#09543d` reappears on card surfaces mid-scroll — mission cards, section backgrounds — like a color that was not gone, only waiting.

## Content Rhythm

The scroll pacing is medium-density: sections are long enough to require deliberate movement through them but not so long that momentum breaks. The search/filter grid (two 720px columns) presents as a single wide interactive surface that the user reads left-to-right before scrolling past. The mission section shifts to a flex-wrap row of rounded cards — visually lighter, more scattered — creating a change in reading tempo from the tighter grid above. Anton headings appear at 36px (section level) and 75px (hero/CTA level), and the size differential alone signals the hierarchy: when Anton appears at full display scale mid-scroll, it functions as a reset, snapping attention back to the primary narrative thread after the card-density sections.

## Footer Approach

Approaching the terminal zone, the two full-width dark green rounded cards appear side by side — the last high-energy surface before the page closes. These cards carry the final CTAs, absorbing the site's remaining conversion pressure in a contained burst. Then the true footer arrives: six Poppins navigation links in a single centered row, followed by two lines of legal copy, all in the off-white field with no colored surface beneath them. The scroll ends not with emphasis but with exhale — the terminal typographic weight drops to near-zero, and the page simply stops. There is no sticky footer CTA, no dark background closing beat, no logo repetition. The green cards above did the work; the footer just closes the file.

## Motion Principles

The motion vocabulary is built on two easing signatures: `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` for UI transitions (link color changes, 0.4s) and `cubic-bezier(0.17, 0.67, 0.3, 1.1)` for spatial reveals (illustration entries, image appearances). The first is a standard ease-out — fast start, soft landing — and functions as a responsiveness cue on interactive elements. The second has a slight overshoot factor (the `1.1` output value exceeds 1.0), giving physical objects a weighted arrival rather than a clean stop. Together, these two curves define the site's kinetic register: interactive elements feel responsive and immediate; spatial elements feel present and grounded. Locomotive Scroll handles the scroll container itself, applying momentum and smoothing to the page's vertical movement — the result is a continuous drift rather than a stepped page snap, which keeps the card-reveal stagger legible as a time-based sequence rather than a positional one.
