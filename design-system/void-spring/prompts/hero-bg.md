## Prompt

A vast, near-black (#0f0f10) digital landscape viewed from above at a slight angle — an abstract network of ultra-thin circuit-like lines and nodes rendered in deep graphite and charcoal tones, with one dominant thread of electric pink-red (#fd356e) pulsing through the geometry like a live data signal. The composition is asymmetric, technical, and architectural — geometric precision with no organic curves. The right two-thirds of the image is denser with data geometry; the left third fades to near-solid black to accommodate foreground text overlay. Subtle light blooms of pale grey (#e8e8ea) mark routing intersections. Cinematic depth of field, ISO-clean render, no lens flare. Dark precision, industrial elegance.

## Usage

Used in `landing-page/index.html` as the full-bleed hero background image.
HTML: `<img src="../assets/hero-bg.jpg" alt="Abstract dark technical interface with electric pink-red data flows">`
