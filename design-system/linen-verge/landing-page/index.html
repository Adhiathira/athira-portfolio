Principle

Documentation is not administration. It is design.

The gap between intent and execution in architecture lives in the record — in the annotation missed, the spec revised without notation, the version no one can trace. Ledgerline closes that gap. It treats every specification as a designed artifact: something with authorship, history, and consequence.

Built for how buildings are made

03 capabilities
01

Contextual annotation

Attach notes, dimensions, and material calls directly to drawing coordinates. Annotations travel with the document — no floating sticky notes, no external spreadsheets.

02

Version-aware specifications

Every spec entry carries a full audit trail: who wrote it, when it changed, what preceded it. Review sessions become conversations with the record rather than reconstructions from memory.

03

Contractor handoff

Issue packages that arrive complete: drawings, specs, and RFI histories bundled by trade. No missing attachments. No ambiguous revision marks. The contractor receives what was actually intended.
