# Brand Name Hackathon — Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately **9 distinct sections**, all set against a consistent pure black canvas. The rhythm alternates between **full-bleed atmospheric moments** (hero, CTA closing) and **content-dense reading sections**. Negative space is used aggressively — sections breathe with substantial padding top and bottom, giving the page a slow, editorial cadence rather than a packed marketing feel.

---

## Header / Navigation

**Fixed top bar, full viewport width.** Horizontally divided into three zones:

- **Left:** Logo mark (small icon) + wordmark — anchored left with tight pairing
- **Center:** Four text nav links — evenly spaced, centered in the viewport
- **Right:** Two CTAs — Secondary CTA (pill with dark fill, muted border) + Primary CTA (pill with bright accent fill, high contrast) — right-aligned as a pair

The nav sits on a near-transparent background, floating directly over the hero's ambient video/animation. No visible divider line — the header is part of the full-bleed experience.

---

## Hero Section

**Full-viewport, full-bleed atmospheric layer.** The entire first screen is consumed by a dark, iridescent animated background — light refracts in slow arcs against absolute black, creating a cinematic, almost liquid depth.

Content floats centered over this background in a **single-column, centered vertical stack**:

1. A small pill/badge at top — status indicator text with a colored dot — rounded capsule shape, dark fill with subtle border
2. A massive typographic lockup — two lines, display-weight, light, very large point size (~120–140px feel). Centered. Occupies the visual center of gravity of the entire viewport
3. A subtitle line in smaller weight, muted off-white, centered — a single sentence
4. A single CTA button — Primary CTA — dark pill shape, centered below subtitle

There are no columns, no image panels. Pure atmospheric full-bleed with centered text hierarchy. The hero reads as a **poster**, not a split-screen.

---

## Content Sections

### Section 2 — Editorial Intro

**Asymmetric two-column layout, left-weighted.** A large heading occupies the left ~55% of the viewport. A key figure or phrase is rendered in a bright accent color, remaining text in light — typographic color split used as the only visual accent.

Below the heading, two short paragraphs in small body text sit in a narrower left column (~45% width), while the right half of the viewport is deliberately left **empty**. This asymmetry creates tension and implies breathing room rather than filling space.

Then, a **vertical reading column** begins: two large body paragraphs (~60% width, left-aligned but centered within a constrained reading column) fill the lower portion of this section. The second and third paragraphs use a **progressive opacity fade** — text starts at full opacity, middle paragraph becomes medium gray, final paragraph becomes very light gray — a sophisticated typographic device communicating narrative recency/relevance.

---

### Section 3 — Features / Tiers (Three-Column Cards)

**Centered section header** — large display heading, centered, full-width treatment. Below it, a short subtitle paragraph, also centered.

Then: **a three-column card grid**, all columns equal width, contained within a card group that has a very subtle dark-gray fill and rounded corners. This is the only section where a visible container boundary appears — the cards are grouped inside a shared rounded rectangle that reads as a single panel with internal divisions.

Each card column contains:
- Icon at top (line-style, small)
- Colored heading (accent color for all three — Feature A, Feature B, Feature C)
- Body description text
- A typographic "progression notation" in bold display size
- An "Expectations:" label with bullet checklist
- Action Button pill at bottom

The three cards are equal-height, aligned at the top. The card group has generous internal padding. No shadows — differentiation comes from the group container background vs. the page black.

---

### Section 4 — Key Differentiators (Scrolling Feature Rows)

**Full-width centered heading** over a wide content area, then three **asymmetric two-column feature rows** stacked vertically.

Each row:
- One column is a **floating product UI mockup** — rendered as a rounded-corner card with a vivid gradient background. These gradient cards feel like product screenshots, elevated off the page
- The other column is **a label + heading + body copy** in text

The layout **alternates left/right placement** of text and image across rows:
- Row 1: Text left, mockup card right
- Row 2: Mockup card left, text right
- Row 3: Text left, mockup card right

The mockup cards are noticeably **larger than the text columns**, creating visual weight imbalance in favor of the product. They bleed slightly toward their respective edges, giving a slightly cropped, "peeking in from the side" quality.

Between rows, generous vertical whitespace (roughly 1.5–2 viewport heights) creates a slow scroll pacing, as if each feature gets its own contemplative moment.

---

### Section 5 — Quote / Testimonial

**Single-column, centered reading layout.** A large pull-quote text in bold, ~60% viewport width, left-aligned within a centered column. Below it, a small avatar image (circular, ~40px) paired with name and title in small gray text — standard attribution layout.

This section is minimal and has no background treatment — it reads against pure black, making the words themselves carry all the weight.

---

### Section 6 — Timeline

**Centered section heading**, then a **two-column timeline layout**:
- **Left column (narrow, ~20%):** Date badges — pill-shaped labels with rounded corners. Past/current dates have an accent fill; future state renders in light outline/transparent fill
- **Right column (wide, ~65%):** Event title in bold, then descriptive body text below

Rows are separated by thin horizontal dividers (hairline rules) spanning most of the content width. The dates and event titles align horizontally across each row, creating a clean ledger-like rhythm. Vertical spacing between timeline entries is generous — each entry breathes.

The progressive state of dates (accent filled = past, light outline = future) communicates status through color and fill without any additional iconography.

---

## Closing CTA Section

**Full-bleed, full-viewport atmospheric return.** The closing section mirrors the hero — the same iridescent animated background fills the entire viewport again. Centered over it:

- Large display heading in light
- Primary CTA pill button in dark fill

This creates a **bookend structure** — the page opens and closes with the same visual language, giving the layout a composed, intentional arc.

---

## Footer

**Three-column layout** on a dark ground, separated from the CTA section by a generous whitespace gap:

- **Left column (~30%):** Logo + wordmark, then two short paragraphs of descriptive body text in small type
- **Center column (~25%):** Link list — section heading in light, then nav links below in smaller muted type
- **Right column (~25%):** Link list — contact heading, then social/contact links

Below all three columns, a thin hairline divider, then a single bottom bar: copyright left-aligned, legal link right or inline — standard legal row.

The footer is spatially generous — it does not feel cramped. The three columns have substantial gutters between them.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Progressive text opacity fading** — body copy visually "fades out" across consecutive paragraphs, suggesting narrative depth without relying on section breaks. Highly unusual and worth preserving.

**Atmospheric full-bleed bookends** — the hero and closing CTA use the same animated gradient-on-dark treatment, creating visual brackets around all content between them.

**Floating product UI cards with gradient fills** — the feature section mockups are not screenshots constrained to a box; they feel like physical objects resting on the page, with their own light and material quality distinct from the flat dark ground.

**Alternating two-column rows without grid rigidity** — the feature section does not feel like a locked grid; the proportions of image vs. text feel slightly improvised, which adds energy.

**Date badge state system in timeline** — the filled/outlined pill badge is a compact, elegant status indicator that avoids the verbosity of "completed / upcoming" labels.

**No decorative dividers between major sections** — sections are separated entirely by whitespace. The visual rhythm is controlled through vertical spacing, not rules or background color changes (except for the card group in Section 3 and the atmospheric sections).
