Common Questions
What counts as a simulation run?

A simulation run is one complete FEA pass on a single material specimen under a defined load condition. Multi-axis runs (combined tensile + shear) count as a single run. Fatigue curve generation counts as one run regardless of cycle count.

How does the access review work?

After submitting your request, our team reviews your engineering context within 48 hours. Access is prioritised for structural, aerospace, advanced composites, and materials science teams. We'll ask about your primary use case before issuing a seat.

Can I bring my own material data?

Yes. On Team and Enterprise plans, you can ingest proprietary material specs directly into your workspace. Custom material profiles are stored in your private library and are not shared with the Torq material database.

Are compliance outputs accepted by accreditation bodies?

Torq's ISO 6892, ASTM E8, and EN 10002 reports are structured to meet the documentation requirements of standard accreditation bodies. We recommend verifying acceptance with your specific body before submission in a regulated context.

Is there an annual contract requirement?

All current pricing is billed annually. Monthly billing will be available after general access opens. Enterprise contracts are structured on a 12-month or 24-month basis with custom volume pricing.

What security certifications does Torq hold?

Enterprise accounts are provisioned on SOC 2 Type II compliant infrastructure. All simulation data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Data residency options (US, EU) are available on Enterprise plans.
