The Company

Built by
Engineers.

Torq was founded by materials scientists and simulation engineers who spent years working around inadequate tooling. We built the platform we couldn't find.

Our Mission

Precision over
approximation.

The materials simulation industry has relied on regression-table approximations for decades — population-average data fitted to generic curves, handed to engineers as if it were specific to their material. It is not. The gap between approximation and measurement is where structural failures live.

Torq was built on a single premise: every material deserves a simulation derived from its actual geometry, not borrowed from a population average. Our FEA engine resolves at the microstructure level because that is the level at which composite materials actually behave differently from each other.

The result is simulation output that engineering teams can rely on — not as an estimate, but as a measurement-quality prediction they can put in front of an accreditation body without caveat.

What We Hold To

Principles.

01

No Rounding

Every threshold value in a Torq simulation is derived from the input material geometry. We do not round to the nearest standard value, and we do not substitute population-average data when material-specific data is unavailable. We tell you what we know and what we don't.

02

Output First

Simulation that does not produce usable documentation is incomplete work. Every Torq run ends in structured, standards-compliant output — not a data dump that requires interpretation. The report is part of the simulation, not an afterthought.

03

Engineer-Paced

We do not design for analysts who need visual dashboards. Torq is built for engineers who know what they are looking for and need infrastructure that does not slow them down. The interface surfaces precision, not decoration.
