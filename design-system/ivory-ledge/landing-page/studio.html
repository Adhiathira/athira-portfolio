The practice

A small practice that doesn't work small

Who we are

Specialists, not generalists

Lattice Bureau was founded in Edinburgh in 2007 by two structural engineers who believed that deep specialism produced better buildings than broad practice. Seventeen years later that conviction runs through everything we do.

Completed projects

Across civic, residential, research, and cultural sectors

"A structural engineer who doesn't care deeply about architecture will never understand the problem they're being asked to solve."

We keep the practice intentionally small. Every project is led by a director who remains involved from first conversation to practical completion. There are no accounts managed by juniors. There is no such thing as a low-priority project.

Our materials science capability is unusual in a structural practice of our scale — but it has become central to the type of work we attract: buildings where the structural system and the material palette are inseparable.
