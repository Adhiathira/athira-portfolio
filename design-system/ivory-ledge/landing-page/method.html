How we work

The method behind every structure

Structure is not separate from design — it is design.

Most structural engineering practices begin with a set of drawings and ask: how do we make this stand? We begin much earlier — in the conversation between structural logic and architectural ambition, where the most interesting decisions are made.

The result is structures that express their own reasoning — where the way a building carries load is also the way it holds space.

Our process

Four phases, no shortcuts

01

Discovery

We read every condition before we calculate anything. Site geometry, geology, adjacencies, climate, and the architect's formal intent — these are the inputs that determine what kind of structure a building actually needs.

Deliverables

  • Site appraisal report
  • Structural strategy options
  • Materials shortlist
02

Structural Concept

We develop one or more structural concepts that respond directly to the architectural intent. At this stage we are still making design decisions, not just engineering ones — and we invite the architect deep into that process.

Deliverables

  • Concept structural drawings
  • Preliminary sizing
  • Comparative cost indication
03

Analysis & Detail

Full analytical models — linear, nonlinear where needed, dynamic where required. Every connection is designed with the fabricator in mind. Our details are buildable, not just calculable.

Deliverables

  • Full calculations package
  • Structural drawings set
  • Connection details
  • Specification
04

Construction Support

We remain involved on site — answering RFIs with precision, reviewing shop drawings, and visiting at critical structural stages. A building's structure is still being designed when it is being built.

Deliverables

  • RFI responses
  • Shop drawing review
  • Site inspection reports
Materials

We work with materials on their own terms.

Every structural material has a grammar — a set of conditions under which it performs with intelligence, and a set under which it fights you. We study these conditions rigorously, and our specifications reflect what the material actually wants to do.

This is what separates a detail that endures from one that corrodes, creeps, or cracks at year seven.
