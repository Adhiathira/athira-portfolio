Our disciplines

What we bring to each project

  1. 01

    Structural Analysis

    Linear and nonlinear analysis for buildings where standard codes meet their limits — tall, slender, laterally complex, or materially hybrid.

  2. 02

    Adaptive Retrofitting

    Existing structures reimagined for new programs — seismic upgrades, vertical extensions, and the precise surgery of removing load-bearing elements without disturbing the whole.

  3. 03

    Connection Design

    Bespoke connections between dissimilar materials, exposed structural nodes that satisfy both engineering and the architect's eye, and the detailing that makes a building's joints an expression of its design intent.

  4. 04

    Materials Science

    Specification and testing of emerging structural materials — mass timber, cross-laminated bamboo, ultra-high-performance concrete, and hybrid composites at the frontier of what can be built.
