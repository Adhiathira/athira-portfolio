# Website Layout Brief

---

## 1. Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately **10–12 major sections** in a single-column, full-width scroll narrative. The overall rhythm alternates between **text-centered storytelling** moments and **product showcase** moments (app screenshots, feature grids). The background is a near-black dark base throughout, creating a unified canvas. Color temperature shifts subtly — warm accent gradients pool behind the hero and certain feature sections, giving depth without breaking the dark base. The spatial cadence is generous: sections breathe with substantial vertical padding, never feeling compressed.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

Not fully visible in the screenshots, but the top of the first screenshot confirms a **minimal, transparent fixed header**. It sits flush against the dark background with no visible border or shadow. Navigation items are sparse — a logo on the left, a short link list centered or right-aligned, and a CTA button on the far right. The header contributes zero visual weight; it recedes, letting the hero dominate.

---

## 3. Hero Section

**Full-width, center-aligned, two-part vertical stack.**

- The upper half is pure typographic — a massive display headline dominates the viewport. The headline is **center-aligned**, broken across 2–3 lines, with maximum weight. Key emotional words are colored in a brand accent, creating a highlight-word technique that breaks the all-white monotony.
- Below the headline sits a single short body paragraph — narrow, center-constrained (roughly 40% of viewport width), creating a visual tightening of hierarchy.
- A single pill-shaped Primary CTA button in the brand accent color sits below the body copy, centered. It floats with generous whitespace above and below.
- The lower portion of the hero bleeds into a **product screenshot** — a large, slightly perspective-angled or flat browser window mockup of the app UI. This mockup is **cropped at the bottom**, implying it continues beyond the fold, creating scroll invitation. The mockup is wide (~70–80% viewport width), centered, floating on the dark background with no card borders or shadows needed — the UI itself has a dark chrome that merges naturally with the background.
- Behind all of this, a **soft radial gradient** — warm accent tones — pools in the upper-left and center, functioning as an atmospheric depth layer, not a geometric shape.

## 4. Content Sections Below the Hero

### Section 2 — Key Statement (Full-Width Text Block)
A **center-aligned, narrow text column** against the dark background. The headline is large, multi-line, bold white — approximately 60–70% viewport width constrained. Below it, a social proof bar: a label "Used by professionals at:" followed by a horizontal row of **logos** in two rows of 4, rendered in white/light gray at uniform size. This is a clean symmetrical grid — 4 columns, even gaps, no variation in logo sizing to avoid hierarchy conflicts.

### Section 3 — Feature Highlight: Tab-Driven Content (Left-Right Split)
An **asymmetric two-column layout**. The left column (~40% width) contains a vertical stack of 3–4 feature items — each a short bold title + 1-line description. The currently selected item has a left accent bar (thin vertical accent-color line) and full white text; inactive items are muted gray, creating a tab-like hierarchy without traditional tab controls. The right column (~55–60% width) shows a large **mobile app screenshot** placed on a vivid color block background, creating a strong color contrast against the page dark. The screenshot is inset into the color block, clipped at top and bottom, implying fullness without showing every pixel.

### Section 4 — Feature Grid: Primary Features (Two-Column Cards)
A **2-column grid** of large feature cards, each occupying roughly half the viewport width. Each card has:
- A feature title + short description at the top in white/light text
- A large product screenshot below, placed on a colored rectangle background
The cards are equal height, side-by-side, with minimal gutter. This creates a dense, magazine-style feature showcase.

### Section 5 — Feature Mini-Cards (Three-Column Grid)
Below the two large cards, three **equal-width smaller cards** sit in a row — each with a feature name + 1-line description at top, and a small cropped app screenshot below on a colored rectangle. This creates a visual rhythm: 2 large → 3 small, implying decreasing feature hierarchy as you scan down.

### Section 6 — Differentiator Callout (Left-Aligned, Logo + Headline)
A **left-aligned two-line headline section**. A small branded icon/logo mark appears top-left, followed by a large bold headline in two tiers — the primary statement white, the secondary statement in a muted gray. Both lines are the same large size but the gray creates a visual fade — a "headline + subhead as equal-weight layered statement" technique. This section has significant vertical breathing room above and below, acting as a **palette-cleansing pause** between dense feature grids.

### Section 7 — Social Proof Awards (Center-Aligned Badges)
Three centered award badges arranged in a horizontal row with equal spacing. This is purely symbolic, light, and takes minimal vertical space.

### Section 8 — Marquee / Feature List (Full-Width Vertical Typography Scroll)
A **full-bleed typographic section** where feature names are stacked vertically, center-aligned, in an enormous display size. Items near the top are bright white; items further down progressively fade to gray, implying a **gradient opacity fade downward** — creating a scrolling marquee illusion or a "features receding into the distance" effect. This is a bold, unconventional layout that uses type-as-texture.

### Section 9 — Editorial Quote (Center-Aligned Display Text)
A **centered editorial quote section**. A large, multi-line quote in a **display/decorative-style font** (distinct from the rest of the page) fills most of the viewport. The text uses a **multi-color gradient** across individual words, making this the most visually expressive section. Below sits a publication attribution with a hand-drawn arrow. Large vertical padding above and below.

### Section 10 — Testimonials (Three-Column Masonry-Style Cards)
A **3-column asymmetric card layout**. Review cards are white-bordered on a slightly lighter dark background, containing long-form user review text. The cards are **offset vertically** — the center column appears to start lower or be taller than the side columns, creating a **masonry / staggered grid** pattern. Cards fade out at the bottom (opacity gradient), implying continuation. The right column's bottom cards appear faded/dimmed (lower opacity), suggesting a depth fade or "load more" visual convention.

## 5. Footer

A **multi-column link grid** on a dark background, using **colored category labels** as column headers — each in a distinct accent color. Below each colored header sits a plain white text link list. The columns are unequal width — the first column is wider (two sub-columns of links side by side), while remaining columns are narrow single-column lists.

The **Updates column** on the far right breaks the link-list pattern: it shows a card widget (version number, update title, date) — a mini changelog embedded in the footer. This asymmetry adds editorial texture.

The footer baseline is a centered copyright line on a slightly darker strip in small muted text. A settings icon sits bottom-right.

A **persistent consent bar** floats in the bottom-left corner across all screenshots — a small rounded dark card with text and an Action Button. It never breaks the layout, sitting as a floating overlay layer above the page.

---

## 6. Notable Layout Patterns

**Atmospheric gradient layering** — The page uses soft radial gradients pooled behind content as a depth signal, not as structural backgrounds. These gradients are visible through multiple sections, creating environmental continuity.

**Scroll-reveal depth fade on testimonials** — The masonry card section uses a vertical opacity gradient (fully opaque top → transparent bottom) to truncate content, managing density while implying abundance.

**Type-as-marquee / vertical feature cascade** — The feature list section uses typography itself as the visual medium, abandoning icons or UI chrome entirely.

**Color-blocking for screenshots** — App screenshots never float naked; they sit on vivid solid-color rectangles which function as virtual "device bezels" and color anchors. This grounds the screenshots in a designed context rather than cutting them out.

**Progressive feature hierarchy via card sizing** — The transition from 2 large cards → 3 small cards creates natural information hierarchy, implying primary features vs. secondary features without needing explicit labeling.

**Editorial pause sections** — Several single-headline sections with no supporting UI/image content function as spatial and typographic breath marks, resetting the user's attention before the next dense section.
