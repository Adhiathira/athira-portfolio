Archival bathymetric charts on cotton-fibre baryta paper. Precision survey instruments machined in brass and stainless steel. Everything Sounding Line makes is built to a working lifespan measured in decades.

Archival charts

Depth cartography.

Standard Bathymetric Chart

Single-sheet archival depth chart produced from survey data. Hand-finished on 160gsm Fabriano cotton-fibre baryta paper. Available at 1:2500, 1:5000, or 1:10000 scale. Supplied rolled in a conservation tube.

  • Sheet size: 900 × 630mm
  • Paper: 160gsm baryta cotton fibre
  • Ink: Pigmented survey grade
  • ISO 9706 permanence rated

Survey instruments

Twelve models. Continuous production since 1972.

All Sounding Line instruments are produced in our Galway workshop from machined brass and stainless steel. No plastics. No electronics that will fail. Each instrument ships with a calibration certificate and a five-year service guarantee.

SL-01

Graduated Sounding Rod

Brass-tipped ash rod, 3m in 0.5m screw-threaded sections. Metric graduations at 1cm intervals, engraved and paint-filled. Standard for shallow-draft work to 3m.

Enquire
SL-03

Tide Staff

316-grade stainless steel graduated staff, 4m in 1m bolt-connected sections. Reads to 10mm. Designed for semi-permanent installation on harbour walls and bridge piers.

Enquire
SL-07

Mechanical Tide Gauge

Float-operated mechanical gauge with chart recorder. No batteries. The clock mechanism is wound every seven days. Produces a continuous paper tape record of tidal height at ±5mm accuracy.

Enquire
SL-11

Lead-Line Sounding Kit

Traditional lead-line kit with cast lead plummet (2kg), 50m of marked sounding line, and a brass reel with brake. Marks at standard fathom intervals with secondary metre markings. The instrument that started the practice.
