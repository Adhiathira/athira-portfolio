Survey work.

Bathymetric surveys, tidal studies, and precision chart commissions across the Atlantic coast of Europe. Each project is unique to its site conditions, tidal regime, and engineering brief.

01

Galway Commercial Port Approach — Phase III

Galway Bay, Ireland 2024 Bathymetric Survey

Full dual-frequency echo sounder survey of the outer approach channel and inner harbour basin. Rock-shelf transects at 2m spacing. Chart series produced in six sheets at 1:2500 scale. Phase IV planning underway for the southern breakwater extension.

02

Kenmare River Tidal Model

Kerry Estuary, Ireland 2023 Tidal Study

Six-month pressure gauge deployment at four stations across the Kenmare River estuary. Combined with 40 years of Admiralty tidal records to produce a site-specific tidal range model for a proposed salmon farm infrastructure installation. Delivered as a 180-page tidal study report with design tidal curves.

03

Lerwick Harbour Depth Chart Series

Shetland Islands, UK 2023 Precision Chart Production

Complete reworking of the existing Lerwick Harbour navigation chart for Shetland Harbour Authority. Survey data reconciliation across twelve historical soundings, plus new line-sounding transects in the north basin. Produced as a twelve-sheet archival chart series with full metadata annotation and ISO 9706 permanence certification.

04

Brest Roadstead Shallow-Draft Survey

Brittany, France 2022 Bathymetric Survey

Line-sounding survey of the Brest roadstead shallow zone for a proposed marine research station platform. Coverage of 14km² at 1m sounding grid, with particular attention to the rock exposure pattern in the eastern sector. Chart produced at 1:5000 scale in three sheets.

05

Dingle Bay Navigational Survey

County Kerry, Ireland 2022 Bathymetric Survey

Comprehensive navigational survey of Dingle Bay for the expansion of Dingle Harbour's commercial pier. Tidal correlation stations at four points around the bay, with full bathymetric coverage of the proposed vessel approach corridor. Delivered in partnership with a marine civil engineer.

06

Bantry Bay Offshore Platform Siting

County Cork, Ireland 2021 Bathymetric Survey + Tidal Study

Combined bathymetric survey and tidal study for the siting of a floating wind energy platform in Bantry Bay. Full seabed characterisation across three candidate sites, with tidal stream measurements at depth from moored ADCP instruments. Report delivered to Orsted offshore wind energy team.
