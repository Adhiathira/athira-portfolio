About the practice

A private hydrographic practice for coastal engineers.

Sounding Line was founded in Galway in 1967 by hydrographer Ciarán Ó Muircheartaigh to address a gap in the coastal survey market: the absence of instruments precise enough for shallow-draft harbour engineering. For nearly sixty years, we have produced bespoke bathymetric charts using a combination of traditional line-sounding methods and contemporary echo-sounder data. Our charts carry a resolution and depth accuracy that no automated survey vessel has yet matched in tidal estuaries and rock-shelf approaches.


Each chart is hand-finished on Sounding Line archival baryta paper — an 160gsm cotton fibre base that accepts our survey ink without dimensional change. The paper is produced by our partner mill in Fabriano to our specification. It does not warp, fade, or delaminate over a working lifespan measured in decades, not years.

What we do

Survey. Instrument. Chart.

01

Bathymetric Survey

Line-sounding and dual-frequency echo sounder surveys of harbours, estuaries, tidal channels, and rock-shelf approaches. Depth accuracy to ±0.02m at calm-water reference datum.

02

Tidal Study & Modelling

Multi-cycle tidal pressure gauge deployments combined with historical Admiralty tidal prediction tables to produce site-specific tidal range models for infrastructure design.

03

Precision Chart Production

Hand-finished bathymetric charts on archival cotton-fibre baryta paper. Produced in our Galway workshop to client specification with full metadata annotation and ISO 9706 permanence rating.

04

Survey Instrument Manufacture

Twelve instrument models in continuous production since 1972 — sounding rods, tide gauges, current meters, and depth loggers — built to our own specifications in machined brass and stainless steel.
