We Were the Red Team

Vortex was built by offensive security practitioners who got tired of watching organizations fail the same tests year after year because they could only afford one engagement per year.

Origin

The Problem We Kept Hitting

For six years, our founding team ran red team engagements for Fortune 500 companies, government agencies, and high-growth startups. The pattern was always the same.

We'd find critical vulnerabilities. The client would patch them. We'd come back 12 months later and find new critical vulnerabilities — different CVEs, same root causes. The cycle never broke because security testing was too expensive to run continuously.

In 2024, we decided to automate what we knew how to do manually. Vortex is the red team toolkit we wish we'd had access to — running continuously, at machine speed, for every organization regardless of budget.

Principles

Operating Doctrine

// Offense Informs Defense

You cannot defend what you don't understand. We teach defensive teams to think offensively by showing exactly how an attacker would move through their environment.

// Continuous Over Periodic

Annual penetration tests are a snapshot. Your infrastructure changes daily. Security posture must be measured continuously — not photographed once a year.

// Signal Over Noise

Every false positive erodes trust in security tooling. We hold ourselves to a 91%+ true positive rate. If we're not sure, we don't surface it.

// Responsible Disclosure

When Vortex discovers a novel zero-day through research or customer scans, we follow coordinated disclosure. We have reported 23 CVEs to date.
