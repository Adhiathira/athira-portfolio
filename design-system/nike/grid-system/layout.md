# Brand Name Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately 6–7 distinct sections, progressing from a commanding full-viewport hero carousel down through editorial split panels, a horizontal image trio, a product spotlight grid, and finally a two-tier footer. The rhythm alternates between immersive full-bleed moments (hero, split panels, sport cards) and structured grid sections (spotlight icons, footer columns). Vertical pacing feels generous — sections breathe with implied white space between them.

---

## Header / Navigation

Two-tier header stacked vertically:

- **Utility bar** (top): Slim strip, light background. Left-aligned secondary brand marks, right-aligned utility text links (Store Locator | Help | Join | Sign In). Minimal, fine typographic weight — purely functional.
- **Primary nav bar**: Light background, centered logo anchored far left. Main nav links (Category A, Category B, Category C, Sub-Brand, Collaboration) centered horizontally across the bar. Right side carries icon cluster: search field (pill-shaped with magnifier icon), wishlist icon, bag icon. The bar has generous vertical padding, giving it authority without bulk.

Together the two bars read as a unified top system — the utility bar recedes, the primary nav commands.

---

## Hero Section

Full-viewport-width carousel. Single-slide format taking the entire browser width and approximately 85–90% of viewport height. Key layout decisions:

- **Full-bleed editorial photography** — the image bleeds edge-to-edge, no frame, no padding.
- **Text overlay, bottom-left anchored**: Large display headline in heavy condensed uppercase, with a subline below. The text sits low, approximately 70% down the frame, leaving vast negative space of image above.
- **CTA pair** floats just below the headline: two pill-shaped buttons (Primary CTA and Secondary CTA), left-aligned, modest in size — they don't compete with the imagery.
- **Carousel controls** sit at the bottom-right corner: pause/play, prev/next chevron buttons in circular frames. Dot indicators centered at the very bottom edge.
- **Vertical crop framing** is extreme — the photo is cropped to show a single dynamic moment, creating tension and energy through partial framing.

## Content Sections Below Hero

### Section 2 — Editorial Split Panel (50/50 Two-Column)

Two equally wide columns occupying the full page width, no gap or gutter between them — panels abut flush. Each panel:
- Full-bleed editorial photo filling the column height
- Bottom-left overlay text: eyebrow label, headline copy
- Pill CTA button at bottom-left

The two panels feel like a diptych — side by side but editorially independent. Height is roughly 55–60% of viewport. This creates a second "billboard" moment that sustains the hero's energy.

### Section 3 — Three-Column Category Cards (Horizontal Image Trio)

Three equal-width editorial photos in a single horizontal row, full bleed across page width. Each card:
- Full-bleed photo (category-specific imagery)
- Bottom-left pill CTA (Action Button)
- No text headline — purely image + action label

Cards share equal height. This section is pure editorial rhythm — three beats, equal weight, no hierarchy among them.

### Section 4 — Spotlight Section (Centered Headline + Product Icon Grid)

Opens with a bold centered typographic lockup:
- All-caps display headline in heavy condensed display weight, very large — takes commanding vertical space
- Single-line descriptor sentence below, centered, light weight

Below the headline: a **dense icon-grid** of product categories. Two rows of approximately 8 items each. Each item is:
- Small circular/square product cutout image (~60–70px implied)
- Product name label below in fine type

Items are evenly spaced in a wide horizontal band. The contrast between the massive display headline and the small dense icons creates sharp typographic hierarchy — a vertical drama from loud to quiet.

## Footer Layout

Two-tier footer structure:

### Upper Footer — Sitemap Columns

Four equal-width columns, full-width light background with generous top and bottom padding. Column headers in medium weight, links below in muted fine type. Generous line-height between links. Clean, airy, utilitarian.

A thin horizontal rule separates the sitemap from the lower footer.

### Lower Footer — Meta Links + Localization

Four columns (Resources / Help / Company / Promotions), with a region/language selector anchored far right. Same visual treatment as upper footer — fine muted type, generous spacing.

Bottom edge: a final single-line strip with copyright, legal links (Terms of Sale, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy), a branded privacy toggle, and regulatory links. This strip is the typographic floor of the page — smallest type, least visual weight.

## Notable Layout Patterns

- **Full-bleed as the default** — almost no section uses contained widths or side padding. Content extends edge-to-edge throughout, giving the page an expansive, gallery-like feel.
- **Bottom-left overlay anchoring** — repeated across hero and split panels. This creates a consistent compositional signature: photography dominates, typography grounds itself modestly at the bottom-left corner.
- **Pill-shaped CTAs** — universally used across all editorial sections. Consistent visual grammar: light fill, dark border or filled dark, rounded ends. They float above photography without disrupting it.
- **Typographic scale contrast** — sharp oscillation between enormous display type and fine body/label type. The page never sits at a medium scale — it goes big or small.
- **No sticky header visible** — the nav appears to scroll away as you move down, giving the content sections full screen real estate.
- **Zero gutters on image grids** — split panels and image card trios have no gaps. Images touch edge-to-edge, creating seamless panoramic continuity.
- **Photography does the spatial work** — sections don't rely on colored backgrounds, dividers, or decorative elements to create rhythm. The imagery itself provides all visual punctuation.
