# Brand Name Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page flows through approximately 6–7 distinct sections from top to bottom, following a vertical editorial narrative. The rhythm alternates between high-density visual moments (the hero grid, split-column imagery) and lower-density product/navigation sections. The pacing feels magazine-like — immersive full-bleed visuals followed by breathing room before the next content hit.

---

## Header / Navigation

Two-tier horizontal nav pinned to the top of the page:

- **Top utility bar:** Full-width, right-aligned, with text links (Find a Store, Help, Join Us, Sign In) flanked on the far left by secondary brand logos. Lightweight, small type, low visual weight.
- **Primary nav bar:** The brand logo anchors the far left. Center-aligned primary navigation items (Category 1, Category 2, Category 3, Category 4, Category 5, Category 6) with generous horizontal spacing between each. Far right houses a search bar (pill-shaped, medium width), a wishlist icon, and a bag icon.

The two bars create a clear visual hierarchy: utility tasks sit above, primary shopping tasks sit below. The brand logo is large enough to dominate the secondary bar without competing with nav items.

---

## Hero Section

A **full-bleed asymmetric mosaic grid** — the most visually dominant section on the page. It occupies essentially the full viewport height on load and spans edge-to-edge with no margins.

The grid is structured as a **3×3 tile matrix of product images**, each with a colored background that creates a vibrant patchwork. The tiles appear roughly equal in size.

Overlaid **centered on the grid** is a large white headline in compressed, heavy uppercase type. Below it, a short descriptor line and a single pill-shaped CTA button ("Primary CTA"). This overlay creates a **floating editorial card effect** — text and button float above the mosaic with no containing box, just typographic hierarchy against the colored image tiles.

Bottom center of the hero has a **dot pagination indicator** (4 dots, suggesting a carousel/slideshow), and bottom-right hosts playback controls (pause, previous, next arrows). This is a **full-bleed autoplay carousel**.

---

## Content Sections Below the Hero

### Section 2 — Split Two-Column, Full-Bleed
A **50/50 horizontal split** taking full viewport width. Left panel: a product lifestyle image. Right panel: a second product lifestyle image. Each panel has a **bottom-left text overlay** (small category label above a larger product name) with a pill-shaped Primary CTA beneath. The two panels feel equal in weight, creating a strong bilateral symmetry. The images bleed to the edges with no gutters between panels.

### Section 3 — Three-Column Equal Grid
Three equal-width image panels side-by-side, each with a **centered bottom CTA button** ("Action Button 1," "Action Button 2," "Action Button 3"). The images bleed top and bottom. The three-column structure at full viewport width creates a pronounced horizontal band of editorial content.

### Section 4 — Editorial Spotlight (Centered, Contained)
A significant **vertical breathing space** precedes a centered headline section: a section label in massive, heavy compressed uppercase type, with a two-line descriptor beneath in normal weight. This section functions as a typographic divider — no imagery, just white space and bold type, centering the reader's attention before the product carousel below.

Below the headline: a **horizontal scrolling product strip** — eight equal-size product tiles arranged in a single row. Each tile is small (icon-scale), showing product image above a product name label beneath. The strip feels like a navigation/wayfinding row rather than a full product grid.

## Footer Layout

The footer breaks into two distinct sub-sections:

### Upper Footer
**Four-column link grid** on a light background. Each column has a bold category header ("Category A," "Category B," "Category C," "Category D") above 4–5 grey text links. Columns are evenly spaced with generous vertical rhythm between header and links. Above the four columns, centered on the page, sits the brand logo at medium scale — acting as a visual anchor before the link columns.

Directly above the four-column grid (between it and the logo), four utility text links appear centered — mirroring the top utility bar and bookending the page with consistency.

### Lower Footer
**Full-width secondary footer** separated from the upper section by a hairline rule. Five loosely spaced column groups (Resources, Help, Company, Promotions, Locale selector) each with a bold header and multiple grey sub-links below. Columns are left-aligned within their zones and span the full page width with even proportional spacing.

**Bottom bar:** A single row below the columns with copyright text on the far left, followed by legal links spaced evenly across the row.

## Notable Layout Patterns

- **Full-bleed images everywhere above the fold** — the page treats the browser edges as hard margins that images press against, not a container to sit within. No padding or inset visible on hero or split-column sections.
- **Persistent sticky nav** — the two-tier header remains fixed throughout scroll, creating a constant 60–80px reserved zone at the top.
- **Text overlays floating on imagery** — both the hero mosaic and the split-column panels use text-on-image without containing boxes or cards. Type floats directly over photography, relying on color contrast and size to maintain legibility.
- **Alternating density rhythm** — the page breathes: dense mosaic → spacious split columns → three-column grid → large typographic pause → small product strip → generous footer. This cadence prevents fatigue while maintaining momentum.
- **Consistent pill-shaped CTAs** — every call-to-action button across all sections uses the same pill shape with consistent color treatment, creating a unified interactive signature regardless of section context.
- **Footer mirroring header** — the brand logo and utility links reappear at the top of the footer, creating a deliberate bookend structure that reinforces brand presence at both entry and exit points of the scroll journey.
