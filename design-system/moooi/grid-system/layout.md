# Brand Name Website Layout Brief

## Overall Page Structure

The page is a long-scroll editorial experience with approximately 8–10 major sections. The visual rhythm alternates between full-bleed imagery and contained editorial panels, creating a cinematic push-and-pull as you scroll. A persistent modal overlay interrupts the scroll at every viewport — it never dismisses — suggesting it was captured mid-interaction. Despite the overlay, the underlying page structure is readable.

---

## Header / Navigation

The header is a slim, fixed bar sitting at the very top of the viewport. It splits into three zones:

- **Left:** A hamburger icon + "Menu" label, and a magnifying glass + "Search" label — both sparse and text-accompanied
- **Center:** The brand wordmark in a lightweight, spaced-letter typeface, centered precisely
- **Right:** Language selector (EN ▾), account icon, wishlist icon, and cart icon — four utility icons in a tight cluster

Above this nav bar is an even thinner announcement strip — full width, dark background — with a promotional message in uppercase small caps. This creates a two-layer top system: utility strip → brand nav.

The nav sits with generous white space on either side, making the wordmark feel like it floats.

---

## Hero Section

Full-bleed, edge-to-edge video or photographic background — no padding, no margins. The hero occupies 100% of the viewport height. Content overlays are minimal: a small pause button in the lower-left corner is the only visible control.

A **newsletter modal** floats centered over the hero. It's a split-panel box:
- **Left half:** Dark background with a stylized editorial cover image (brand pattern), slightly tilted at ~15° for dynamism
- **Right half:** Light panel with an eyebrow label, large editorial headline, short body copy, a dark pill-shaped Primary CTA button, and a small Secondary CTA text link

The modal uses hard geometric split — left panel bleeds no further than 50% of the box width. The box itself is roughly 700px wide, vertically centered, with a drop shadow and slight border-radius. An × close button sits top-right of the right panel.

This floating modal creates a **deliberate layer hierarchy**: background video → dimmed overlay → modal card.

---

## Section: Category Row

Below the hero: a centered section header in small uppercase tracking. Below it, a horizontal row of 6 category items — equal-width columns with generous breathing room between them. Each item is:

- A transparent/cutout product photo (no background — objects float on white)
- A short category label below in serif body text

This is a **6-column equal grid** with no borders or card backgrounds — pure whitespace separation. The floating objects give it a gallery-like lightness.

---

## Section: Full-Bleed Editorial Pairs (Alternating Two-Column Splits)

Multiple sections follow a **50/50 vertical split** pattern — the viewport is divided cleanly down the middle:

- **Left half:** Full-bleed photographic image cropped to the left 50% of the screen
- **Right half:** Contrasting background (dark panel, muted panel, warm panel, or near-black) with text content positioned toward the center of its column

The text blocks in the right panels are modestly sized and vertically centered, typically containing:
- A section label or product name (uppercase or serif large)
- A short descriptive paragraph
- An underlined text CTA link ("Discover more...")

These pairs create strong **bilateral symmetry broken by color contrast** — one side is always photographic, the other typographic. The horizontal centerline acts as a precise axis.

---

## Section: Large Brand Typography Overlay

One section uses a **full-bleed dark image** with the brand name rendered in massive, low-opacity letterforms — nearly as wide as the viewport, sitting in the lower third of the image. It functions as environmental texture rather than legible headline, creating depth and brand presence simultaneously.

A downward arrow/chevron appears centered just below this, inviting continued scroll.

---

## Section: Product Story — Asymmetric Two-Column

A section labeled "PRODUCT STORY" uses a **left-heavy 60/40 split**:
- Left: Full-bleed atmospheric product image
- Right: Light background with eyebrow, large serif headline, body paragraph, and CTA link

The text column is right-aligned to the content, not flush to the edge — generous internal padding.

---

## Section: Editorial Feature

Another **50/50 split** at the bottom of the scrollable content, this time with:
- Left: Muted background with section label and a large serif headline beginning to appear
- Right: Warm dark full-bleed image

The headline is cropped by the viewport bottom, implying it continues — a deliberate tension device to pull the user to keep scrolling.

---

## Footer

The footer is a multi-zone dark section (near-black background):

**Upper footer zone:**
- Left: A region selector pill (rounded rectangle with flag + region label + chevron)
- Right: An upward arrow icon for scroll-to-top

**Mid footer — Two-column link grid:**
- Left column: Site utility links (Store locator, Resource Library, Shipping & Returns, FAQ)
- Right column: Brand/community links (Newsletter, Press Area, Trade Program)

Links are in a warm light color, medium weight, no separators — vertical list format.

**Lower footer bar:**
- Left: Social media icons row — multiple icons, equal spacing
- Right: Legal text links (Disclaimer, Cookie Policy, General Conditions, Privacy Statement) + copyright notice in small text

The footer uses strong horizontal banding — each zone is clearly stacked with ample vertical rhythm between them.

---

## Notable Layout Patterns

**Full-bleed photography:** Every major section uses edge-to-edge imagery with zero margin. The browser chrome is the only boundary.

**50/50 editorial splits:** The dominant compositional device on this page. Hard vertical midline divides image from text in at least 4 sections. This creates a strong editorial cadence — almost magazine-like.

**Layered depth / floating elements:** The modal demonstrates the site's comfort with floating layers over content. Even product photos in the category row appear to float with no containers.

**Persistent announcement strip:** The thin promotional bar above the nav creates a 3-level top system that compresses the actual nav zone — a common e-commerce pattern that maintains conversion surface without sacrificing brand presence.

**Color-blocking as navigation:** Background colors in the editorial splits (muted panel, warm panel, dark panel, light panel, near-black) act as sectional dividers — no horizontal rules or whitespace separators needed. The user reads color transitions as section changes.

**Typography as texture:** The oversized watermark-style brand name in the mid-page section treats type as an image element, not just a communication vehicle — characteristic of high-design editorial brands.
